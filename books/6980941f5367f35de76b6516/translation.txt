I very gracefully pass over your greatest praises here; for it is a foolish thought to attempt to seal within these small pages your works, so filled with glorious deeds that they are capable of filling the entire universe. The Fame of your Glory flies through every land; being held upon the lips of all people, they build a temple to your eternity. In you, <term>Bellona</term> <note>The Roman goddess of war, representing military might.</note> and <term>Pallas</term> <note>Pallas Athena, the goddess of wisdom, representing the arts and intellect.</note> have shared a kiss, proving to all people that in a true Prince, a Throne can be firmly established upon both arms and virtues.
