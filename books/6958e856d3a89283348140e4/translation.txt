->KIRANUS<-
<note>The name "Kiranus" refers to the legendary author or compiler of this magical-medical text, often associated with Hermes Trismegistus.</note>

->KIRANIDES<-
<note>The "Kiranides" (or Cyranides) is a collection of books detailing the "sympathies" and "antipathies" of nature—how specific animals, plants, and stones can be used in healing and magic.</note>

---

->1638<-
<note>The year of publication or binding, during the height of the 17th-century interest in "natural magic" and early chemistry.</note>
