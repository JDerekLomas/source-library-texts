## To the Reverend & Very Learned Sir
## Mr. B. van der Feen.

Minister of the Dutch Reformed Congregation <note>Dutch: *Nederduitsche Hervormde Gemeente*, the historically dominant Protestant church in the Netherlands</note>
of Middelburg in Zeeland; this copy
of Moubach’s Religious Customs of all the Peoples
of the World <note>original: *Moubachs Godsdienstpligten van alle volken der Wereld*; this refers to the Dutch edition of a famous 18th-century encyclopedia of world religions, originally illustrated by Bernard Picart</note> is respectfully offered and gifted
by His Friends, on the occasion that His Reverence
has declined the call to the Haarlem Congregation <note>In the Dutch Reformed tradition, a "call" (beroeping) is a formal invitation for a minister to leave their current post and lead a new congregation; here, the friends are celebrating his decision to stay in Middelburg</note>,

->Middelburg, August 14, 1823.<-
