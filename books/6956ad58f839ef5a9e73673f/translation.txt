We might clearly infer from these words that the writer was copying an older text.

Our manuscript consists of 72 quarto <note>a book size where each sheet is folded twice to create four leaves</note> leaves of one pagination and 7 leaves of a second pagination, 157 pages in all. The writing is in the Hebraic-Italian hand, the manuscript itself being supplied with numerous illustrations.
