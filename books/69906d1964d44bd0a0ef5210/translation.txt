Now again, the **Chapter on Auspiciousness and Inauspiciousness** is explained. First of all, the auspicious and inauspicious outcomes are declared:

**In the Quadrants and Trines... (Verse 30)**

<term>Kendra</term> <note>These are the "pivotal" or "angular" houses in a birth chart—the 1st, 4th, 7th, and 10th houses—considered the strongest areas of the sky.</note> refers to the Ascendant (1st), the 4th, the 7th, and the 10th houses. <term>Trikona</term> <note>These are the "trine" houses—the 1st, 5th, and 9th houses—associated with luck and merit.</note> refers to the 5th and 9th houses. 

When <term>Benefic</term> <note>original: "Saumya"; literally "gentle" planets like Jupiter, Venus, and Mercury.</note> planets are situated in these Quadrants or Trines, it indicates an auspicious outcome. Likewise, if <term>Malefic</term> <note>original: "Papa"; literally "sinful" or "cruel" planets like Mars and Saturn.</note> planets are placed anywhere else—specifically excluding the Quadrants and the 8th house <note>The 8th house is traditionally associated with death and obstacles; here the text suggests that if malefics are kept out of the vital centers and the house of death, the outcome is positive.</note>—then one should declare <term>Success in all Pursuits</term> <note>original: "Sarvartha Siddhi".</note> for the person asking the question. 

One should declare the achievement of success for men. If the opposite occurs <note>meaning if malefics are in the centers and benefics are in weak houses</note>, then the result will be the opposite. There is no doubt; it will certainly be the reverse. This is what is meant: when malefic planets are in the Quadrants, Trines, or the 8th house, and the Benefics are excluded from the Quadrants and Trines, one should understand this as a sign of failure. Otherwise, one should declare the fulfillment of all goals.

Now, he explains the positions related to gains:

**In the Third, Fifth, and House of Gains... (Verse 31)**

In the 3rd, the 5th, the famous House of Gains (11th), or the 7th—if <term>Benefic</term> planets are situated in these houses, they are great givers of gains. They grant prosperity in abundance. 

Similarly, in the 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 11th houses, whether the planets are Benefic or Malefic, they are all fruitful. They do not produce an inauspicious result...
