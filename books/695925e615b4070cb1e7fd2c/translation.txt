### 1655

<note>This date indicates the book is a copy of the first English translation of "De Occulta Philosophia libri tres," which was originally published in Latin in 1533. The 1655 edition brought Agrippa's complex system of "natural magic" to a broader English-speaking audience during a period of intense interest in the hermetic arts.</note>
