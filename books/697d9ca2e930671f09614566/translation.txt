<header>APPENDIX TO THE APOLOGY FOR THE HARMONIES</header>

We rightly attribute our mind's natural aptitude for perceiving Geometrical things to all our innate and accepted knowledge. Therefore, the Geometrical foundations of our arguments are not weak or invented simply because they exist within the human mind; rather, they are firm because they depend on a natural participation in the image of God. <note>Kepler refers here to the concept of *Imago Dei*—the belief that humans are created in God's image, allowing them to understand the mathematical "language" God used to design the universe.</note> Nor is it the case that the Architect of the heavens looks to human rules when a man reasons from the light of nature <note>The "light of nature" is a philosophical term for human reason and observation independent of divine revelation.</note> about what fits the structure of the heavens. Instead, because God Himself drew those rules from within Himself to build the world, His own image—the human mind—finds no other rules when it reasons.

Even though in this last repetition of your philosophy, which you have stretched over nine full pages, I find my name or my "crimes" inserted more than ten times, I did not think that was reason enough to be more long-winded in examining it. For I believe that no reader, whether he be a mathematician or a Hermeticist, <note>A follower of Hermeticism, an esoteric tradition of philosophy based on writings attributed to Hermes Trismegistus. Indeed, it was not my intention in this writing to sample anything more from your philosophy than what serves to explain to readers the difference between your Harmonics and mine.

And as for your ending—where you, not without gall, <note>original: "non sine felle." This phrase implies Fludd's writing was bitter or spiteful.</note> attribute it to the *preceding graces of God* that you successfully finished your work while being spurred on by "envy" toward me—I vigorously deny that envy. I appeal to the judgment of a skilled reader to see if he finds any enviable qualities in your work that would support the charge you've leveled against me. For the rest, I candidly excuse myself to you: except for the fact that I have admitted it in almost every line, I sought nothing else but this one goal: that unlearned readers, who are easily deceived by titles, should know what they can find with each of us, and what the difference is. As far as this present apology <note>In this context, an "apology" is not an admission of guilt, but a formal defense of one's positions (from the Greek *apologia*).</note> is concerned, although I have had cause to flare up here and there, provoked by frequent insults, I hope that nothing has been said against you or your honor and dignity that is too grave, or anything that could be compared with your own bitterness. If you are softened by this equanimity of mine, nothing shall prevent us from cultivating a literary friendship in the future; and let each man contribute the talent he has received from God to the praise of the Creator and the education of his neighbor, without disparaging the other. May each man receive and enjoy with a grateful heart, and with reverence for the Giver of the Spirit, the well-written things he finds in the other. Farewell.

And how did I myself depict this very thing in my book on the **New Star**, <note>Kepler refers to his 1606 work *De Stella Nova*, which discussed the supernova of 1604 and its astrological and political implications.</note> long ago in vivid colors? But what will be the reward for the effort if, while striving for **Harmony** with a private sound, I fail to overcome the public clamor because of the weakness of my lungs, <note>original: "imbecillitate laterum," literally "weakness of the sides," a common idiom for a weak voice or physical frailty when speaking or singing.</note> and instead only increase the annoyances of an absurd discord in my own ears? 

It must indeed be confessed, alas, that the crossed wound—or should we prefer the more sacred and auspicious word, **cruciform**—is still swelling with many lips; <note>Kepler uses "cruciform" (cross-shaped) to describe the religious wars tearing Europe apart, implying the conflict is centered on the Cross of Christ itself.</note> and with none of them closing together, the medicine has so far been in vain and mocked on all sides. This is because the Physician, in order to administer a deceptive remedy to a delirious patient, adds many things at once that seem to depart greatly from sound reason. Yet I am refreshed by this very thought: that the supreme **Healer** of our wounds is certain of His art, and applies nothing in vain. 

Therefore, He who has already begun the cure, who has already prepared and shown to the world these healing remedies; <note>original: "consolidantia," referring to medicines that cause a wound to close and scar over.</note> meanwhile, however, He uses corrosive agents through public calamities, until—once the putrid and wild flesh of extinguished charity is consumed—some sense of pain descends to the depth of the living flesh. He will undoubtedly also use soothing remedies soon to reduce the swellings, so that there may finally be a place for those healing ones; and at last this long-lasting **Dissonance** (to return to the proposed example) may end in a pure and lasting **Harmony**. 

In this hope, even against hope, I am confirmed not only by the success of my **Harmonic** speculations—the success of which far exceeds my boldness in searching—but also by this very fact: that among the other things necessary for the completion of the Work, <note>His book, *Harmonices Mundi* (The Harmonies of the World).</note> which have remained in their original state for so many years, I have seen even Your **Royal Majesty**—to whom I had destined the patronage of this work before it was even begun—safe and flourishing up to this point.
