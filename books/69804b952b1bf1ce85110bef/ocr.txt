A vertical symbolic diagram contained within a double-line rectangular border. At the top center, the Agnus Dei (Lamb of God) holds a cruciform banner within a radiant oval glory. 3. "Aevum mortuorum" is a darker circle with dense concentric hatching.

Four phrases curve around the perimeter of these circles. On the left side, in German: "erschaffene Ewigkeit: Ebr. 1. 2." (top) and "Die durch den Sohn Gottes" (bottom). On the right side, in Latin: "Aeones per" (top) and "Filium Creati." (bottom).
