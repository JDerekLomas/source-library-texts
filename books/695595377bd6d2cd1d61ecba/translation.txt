# VOLUMES IX, X, AND XI OF THE ROTCH EDITION

<note>The Rotch Edition is a well-known 19th-century American translation of Emanuel Swedenborg’s theological writings.</note>

->TREAT OF<-

THE TENDENCY OF THE CHURCH TO TRUST IN FAITH OR KNOWLEDGE ALONE; AND THE SUBORDINATION OF ALL THINGS OF KNOWLEDGE AND INTELLIGENCE TO THE DIVINE LOVE, IN THE LORD, AND FROM HIM IN THE SPIRITUAL CHURCH.

<note>In this context, "the Lord" refers specifically to Jesus Christ as the manifestation of God, and the "Spiritual Church" refers to a state of humanity guided by conscience and truth derived from goodness.</note>

<summary>This introductory page outlines the themes of three volumes regarding the danger of prioritizing intellectual knowledge over love, and the necessity of aligning all intelligence under Divine Love.</summary>
