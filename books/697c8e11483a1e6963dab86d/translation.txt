# Dedication.

## Dear Brothers in the Grace of GOD through JESUS Christ!

Those prototypes <note>German: *Fürbilder* — refers to "types" or biblical patterns that prefigure future events</note> of the deviations of that Israel according to the flesh were written by Paul and dedicated as a warning to all those Israelites according to the Spirit upon whom the ends of the world-ages <note>German: *Welt-Ewigkeiten* — a term suggesting the final eras of time or aeons</note> have come. But who has come closer to the end of the world, and become more similar to those prefiguring Israelites, than all of us? For we have been called in the last of these days through the living and inwardly powerful word of GOD to a change of heart <note>German: *Aenderung unseres Sinnes* — a translation of the Greek *metanoia*, or repentance</note>, and have been drawn and led by our dear LORD toward the true Sabbath and the essential rest with GOD. 

Therefore, no one should more rightly take Paul’s faithful warning to heart and examine themselves with sincerity than we, who have inwardly experienced almost all the powers of GOD’s grace and the secret work of redemption. We have done so in order to depart from the spiritual Sodom and Egypt of our corrupted nature and to strive for the true rest of union with GOD. Yet, on the contrary, we have mostly followed those prefigured footsteps of evil desires, idolatry, and deviations through our own disobedience, unfaithfulness, and apostasy. Therefore, no one has better or greater cause to take the dedicated warning of Paul to heart, for the examination of his own conscience—

<summary>In this dedication, the author compares the spiritual journey of contemporary believers to the biblical Israelites, warning that those who have received God's grace are just as prone to the sins of idolatry and disobedience.</summary>
