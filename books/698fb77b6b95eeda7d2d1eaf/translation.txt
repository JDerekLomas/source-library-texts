## General Overview of Phenomena.

When the human spirit ventures to master matter—that is, the world of physical phenomena—and when, through the thoughtful contemplation of all that exists, it strives to penetrate the rich abundance of natural life and the governance of both free and bound forces; it feels itself lifted to a height from which, as the horizon recedes into the distance, individual details appear only in grouped distributions, as if enveloped in a light mist.
