### Divination via the Messenger
Regarding an inquiry brought by a messenger: if the person speaks from the East or the West, one should know there will be attainment and the successful completion of the work. If they speak from the North or the Northeast, and then the messenger departs, the result is favorable. If they speak from the Southwest—the "fierce" direction—then the matter is <gloss>on the path</gloss> <margin>to the wind</margin>. ### Divination via Body Contact
Regarding the question of touching the limbs: if a person touches their head, face, ears, or eyes, they will surely obtain gold. 4

Touching the hair brings sorrow. Touching the ears and similar parts leads to an end. For men, if they touch charcoal and such things, the successful completion of the work does not occur. 6

If one touches wood, mud, or a screen, the success of the work is far away. However, if one touches clothing or ornaments, success is found at every step. 7

If the inquiry happens in a temple or on the banks of a river, it will be auspicious. Success occurs if one is in their own home, but it does not occur if one is in a foreign land. 8
