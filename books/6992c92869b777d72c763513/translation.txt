<vocab>
**Qian is Heaven (乾爲天):** The first hexagram, representing pure Yang energy, leadership, and the celestial.
**Six Relations (六親):** A system in King Wen's divination that assigns familial and social roles (e.g., "Parents," "Wealth") to the lines of a hexagram.
**Mount Mangdang (芒碭山):** A historical site associated with the desperate beginnings of the Han Dynasty.
**Cloud Ladder (雲梯):** A metaphor for rapid social advancement.
**Polishing Jade (琢玉):** The process of self-improvement or hard work needed to reveal inner worth.
**Six Dragons driving Heaven (六龍御天):** A classic description of the sun's path or the ruler's movement, corresponding to the six solid lines of the Qian hexagram.
**Rank and Salary (秩祿):** The rewards of a successful career in the imperial bureaucracy.
**Cinnamon Branch (丹桂):** Refers to the legendary tree in the moon; "plucking the cassia" means achieving the highest academic honors.
</vocab>
