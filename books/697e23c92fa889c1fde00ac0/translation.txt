<meta>This page contains the formal legal privilege (an early form of copyright) granted in 1670 by the provincial government to the publisher Jan Rieuwertsz. # PRIVILEGE. <note>A "Privilege" was a legal grant of exclusive printing and selling rights, an early form of copyright protection issued by the government.</note>

The States of Holland and West Friesland make known: Whereas it has been shown to us by *Jan Rieuwertsz*, Citizen and Bookseller at *Amsterdam*, how *Gerard Brandt*, some years ago, had composed and published the *History of the Reformation* and other *Ecclesiastical Histories* occurring in and around the *Netherlands* since the year 712; which *History* the same *Author* had expanded with significant additions and continuations, divided into various volumes, and intended to have printed and published by the *Petitioner* <term>Petitioner: translated from 'Suppliant', the person making the formal request to the government</term>; but since he, the *Petitioner*, was fearful that some profit-seeking individuals might make the labor of the new printing fruitless, he, the *Petitioner*, turned to us, humbly requesting that it might please us to grant him, the *Petitioner*, letters of *Patent* <term>Patent: translated from 'Octroy', a formal document granting exclusive rights</term> in the usual form for a period of fifteen years, to the end that he, the *Petitioner*, might print and have printed, publish, and sell the same *History of the Reformation*, with its additions and continuations, during the aforementioned time, within the aforementioned lands; forbidding any and all persons to reprint the same in any manner, directly or indirectly, in whole or in part, in large or in small format, or to bring into the aforementioned lands, sell, or trade any copies reprinted elsewhere, without the consent of the *Petitioner*, on pain of forfeiture of the reprinted, imported, or sold copies, and such penalties as it should please us to find good and to decree: **SO IT IS**, that we, having considered the matter and the request aforementioned, and being inclined toward the prayer of the *Petitioner*, out of our certain knowledge, sovereign power, and authority, have authorized and granted patent to the *Petitioner*, and do authorize and grant patent to the same by these presents, to the end that he, during the period of fifteen years, shall alone be allowed to have printed, publish, and sell the aforementioned book within the aforementioned lands of *Holland* and *West Friesland*; forbidding any and all persons to reprint the same book in any manner, in whole or in part, in large or small format, or [to trade] copies reprinted elsewhere, imported, traded, or sold, under a fine of three hundred guilders, one-third of which is to be applied to the *Officer* of the place where the case occurs, one-third for the *Poor*, and the remaining third for the *Petitioner*. Providing it is understood that we, wishing to gratify the *Petitioner* with this our *Patent* to prevent his loss through the reprinting of the aforementioned book, do not thereby in any way intend to authorize or endorse the contents thereof, and much less to give the same any further credit, status, or reputation under our protection and defense, but rather that the *Petitioner*, in case anything improper should be included therein, shall be held responsible to answer for all of it at his own expense. <note>This disclaimer was standard; the government protected the publisher's commercial rights without necessarily agreeing with the religious or political views expressed in the book.</note> And to the end that the *Petitioner* may enjoy the effect of this our consent and patent as is proper, we charge any and all whom it may concern to let the *Petitioner* enjoy and use the contents of this peacefully, quietly, and fully, ceasing all hindrance to the contrary. Done in *The Hague* under our great Seal appended hereto, the *21st of April*, in the year of our Lord and Savior sixteen hundred and seventy.

->JOHAN de WITT. vt. <note>original: "vt." is an abbreviation for "vidit," Latin for "he has seen [it]," indicating official approval by the Grand Pensionary Johan de Witt.</note> 1670.<-

->By order of the States<-

->HERBERT van BEAUMONT. 1670.<-
