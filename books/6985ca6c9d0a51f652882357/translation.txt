# THE GATES
# OF LIGHT

<term>The Gates of Light</term> <note>Original Latin: "Portæ Lucis." This is the Latin translation of the Hebrew title *Sha'are Orah*, a foundational 13th-century text on Kabbalah (Jewish mysticism) written by Joseph Gikatilla. This Latin version, likely translated by the scholar Paulus Ricius, played a crucial role in introducing Christian audiences to Jewish mystical thought during the Renaissance.</note>

<summary>This title page introduces the "Gates of Light," a primary text of the Kabbalah that explains the ten divine attributes through which the infinite interacts with the world.</summary>
