The centerpiece of the front cover is a deeply recessed <term>mandorla</term><note>An almond-shaped decorative motif, often referred to in this context as a "shamsa" or sunburst.</note> medallion. This central ornament is decorated with:

*   **Arabesque designs** consisting of intertwining flowing lines.

The entire cover is framed by a series of subtle, ruled lines that form a border, focusing the eye on the central <term>medallion</term>.
