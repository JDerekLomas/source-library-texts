# The Birth of the Elements

->**Shiva’s Breath**<-

Meaning—Mahadeva <note>The Great God, Shiva</note> said: This universe is born from the elements <term>Tattvas: the fundamental building blocks of reality</term>, it is sustained by the elements, and it dissolves back into the elements. Therefore, understand that the ultimate determination of all things lies within these elements. || 4 ||

**The Goddess spoke:** The elements alone are the supreme root, as determined by those who speak of truth. O Lord, what is the true nature of these elements? Please reveal this to me. || 5 ||

**The Lord spoke:** The Supreme Lord is the one stainless, formless Divinity. From Him, space <note>original: "Akasha"</note> was born, and from space, air was produced. || 6 ||

From air came fire, from fire came water, and from water, the earth was born. These are the five elements, which expand fivefold. || 7 ||

Meaning—Mahadeva said: From air came fire <note>original: "Tejas"</note>, from fire came water, and from water, the earth was produced. These five elements each expand in five ways; that is to say, through the process of fivefold quintuplication <term>Panchikarana: the Vedantic theory of how subtle elements combine to form the material world</term>, they become twenty-five variations. || 7 ||

From these, the universe was born; by them, it is sustained. It dissolves back into them, and within them, it rests again. || 8 ||
