## The Sixth Month (Large)
<note>original: "六月大"; a "Large" month in the lunar calendar contains 30 days.</note>
The first day of the month is **Jiawu** <note>original: "甲午"; the 31st day in the 60-day cycle, associated with the Wood Horse.</note>
On the **13th day**, **Bingwu**, at the **5th quarter**, it is **Major Heat**, the midpoint of the sixth month.
<note>The quarter, or <term>ke</term>, was a unit of time measurement; in the Ming Dynasty, there were 100 quarters in a single day.</note>
On the **28th day**, **Xinyou**, at the **5th quarter**, it is the **Beginning of Autumn**, the node of the seventh month.

# The First Month (Long)
<gloss>The <term>Beginning of Spring</term><note>Lichun: the first of the 24 solar terms, marking the start of the astronomical spring</note> occurs on the 28th day of the second month of the previous year, a <term>Wu-Wu</term> day, at the first mark of the <term>Shen</term> hour (approximately 3:15 PM). The Heavenly Way travels south; Fortune travels south. It is suitable to build or repair in the south. Auspicious colors: White, Green, and Jade.</gloss>

# Established in the Month of Bing-Yin
<gloss>The Heavenly Virtue resides in <term>Ding</term>; the Month’s Detestation resides in <term>Xu</term>; the Month’s Killing resides in <term>Chou</term>. <margin>
