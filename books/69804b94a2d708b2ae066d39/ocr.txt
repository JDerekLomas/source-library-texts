<image-desc>
A full-page engraving of a standing male figure representing the "Inwendige Mensch" (Inner Man), framed within a rectangular border. The figure is inscribed with various mystical terms:
- **Head**: On the forehead is written "H.GEIST" (Heiliger Geist/Holy Spirit). On the left and right temples are the syllables "Ver" and "stand" (Verstand/Understanding).
- **Throat**: A circle contains the text "SOPHIA Spiegel der Gottheit" (Sophia, Mirror of the Divinity).

- **Solar Plexus**: A smaller radiant sunburst below the heart is labeled "JEHOVA".
- **Abdomen**: A large circular diagram containing a tight spiral. Text on the upper arc reads: "Das Liecht der Natur in der Seelen u: 5 Sinnen" (The Light of Nature in the Soul and 5 Senses). The center of the spiral is inscribed "Finsternuß WELT" (World of Darkness). The bottom arc of the circle reads: "Der Abgrundt der Hellen" (The Abyss of Hell).
The figure stands with the left hand on the hip and the right arm at the side. The ground is indicated by horizontal hatching.
</image-desc>
