# 1. Salutations to the Illustrious Lord Ganesha
<note>original: "श्रीगणेशाय नमः". It is traditional in Sanskrit manuscripts to begin with an invocation to Ganesha, the elephant-headed deity who removes obstacles and presides over beginnings.</note>

Bowing to the lotus-like feet of the Illustrious Lord of the Divine Hosts <note>Ganesha</note> and other deities, I here skillfully compose this **Bouquet of Auspicious Moments** (<term>Muhurta Manjari</term>). It is written for the enlightenment of students <note>original: "bala-prabodhaya," literally "for the awakening of children," referring to those new to the study of astrology</note>, having investigated the entire science of time and presenting it in a manner that is easy to understand. || 1 ||

->**Now begins the Chapter on Constellations**<-
<note>original: "अथ नक्षत्रप्रकरणम्". Constellations, or <term>Nakshatras</term>, are the 27 (sometimes 28) lunar mansions that form the basis of the Indian zodiac.</note>

In this system, the constellations are classified into seven distinct categories based on their inherent qualities and the types of activities they favor:

| Category Name | Constellations Included |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Fixed** (<term>Dhruva</term>) | The three "Latter" stars <note>Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha, and Uttara Bhadrapada</note> and Rohini. |
| **Soft** (<term>Mridu</term>) | Revati, Mrigashira, Anuradha, and Chitra. |
| **Swift** (<term>Kshipra</term>) | Hasta, Ashvini, Pushya, and Abhijit <note>Abhijit is the intercalary 28th constellation used in specific calculations</note>. |
| **Sharp** (<term>Tikshna</term>) | Mula, Jyeshtha, Ardra, and Ashlesha. |
| **Movable** (<term>Chara</term>) | Swati, Punarvasu, Shravana, Dhanishtha, and Shatabhishak. |
| **Mixed** (<term>Mishra</term>) | Krittika and Vishakha. |
| **Fierce** (<term>Ugra</term>) | The three "Former" stars <note>Purva Phalguni, Purva Ashadha, and Purva Bhadrapada</note>, Magha, and Bharani. |

<note>These classifications are essential for <term>Muhurta</term> (electional astrology). For example, "Fixed" stars are chosen for foundation-laying, while "Swift" stars are better for travel or commerce.</note>
