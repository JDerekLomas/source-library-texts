He fled from the idea of multiplicity, for it has been established that the "First" is always one. His use of the word "always" in this copy—referring to whoever used a term for a name—serves to support what was previously mentioned in the introductory line. The intention is for an essential image to be formed in the mind. The Sacred in prayer, whose grace from God is known to the <term>intellects</term> <note>original: "’uqul," plural of 'aql; refers to the faculty of reason and the cosmic intellects</note>, understands God's command as the apple of his eye, his greatness, and the joy of his innate essence and his entire state. This flows among them like the generosity of the holy, encompassing sea.

In this, there is a narrow closing for souls other than their own, like the relationship of hearing to sight. What he attains with his tongue is his understanding of his own standing; he was overwhelmed, and there is none other than He.
