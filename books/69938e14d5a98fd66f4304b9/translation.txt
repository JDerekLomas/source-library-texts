->**FRO BEN.**<-
<note>The name refers to Johann Froben (c. 1460–1527), a celebrated Swiss printer and close friend of the scholar Erasmus. His printer's mark is a visual representation of a biblical proverb.</note>

<note>The imagery of the crowned serpents and the dove symbolizes the instruction from the Gospel of Matthew 10:16: "Be wise as serpents and innocent as doves." The caduceus—the staff of Hermes/Mercury—traditionally represents commerce and peace, appropriate for a printer who distributed wisdom across borders.</note>
