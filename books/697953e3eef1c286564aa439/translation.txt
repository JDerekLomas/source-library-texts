They fix their tiny "inch of reason" onto the point

“Look! Behold the sun!” and worship it like sun-worshippers <note>original: "Indian-like." A period-typical reference to indigenous peoples of the Americas or East who were thought to worship the sun.</note>.

To be "as wise as Socrates" might now rightly be

You who are sold to your physical senses! You citizens of earth!

Bathing forever in the fountain <note>original: "font."</note> of bliss,
