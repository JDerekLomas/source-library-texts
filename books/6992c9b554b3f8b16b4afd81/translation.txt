## Section 19: Explaining the Mutual Aspects of the Seven Luminaries

Generally, when the <term>Seven Luminaries</term> <note>the Sun, the Moon, and the five visible planets: Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn</note> aspect one another, there are eight classifications based on their <term>longitude</term> <note>the position of a celestial body along the ecliptic, measured in degrees</note>. These are: one <term>conjunction</term> <note>original: 相會 (xiāng huì); when two planets meet at the same degree</note>, one <term>opposition</term> <note>original: 相衝 (xiāng chōng); when planets are 180 degrees apart, facing each other</note>, two <term>squares</term> <note>original: 弦 (xián); literally "bowstrings," referring to a 90-degree angle</note>, two <term>sextiles</term> <note>original: 六合 (liù hé); literally "six harmonies," a 60-degree angle</note>, and two <term>trines</term> <note>original: 三合 (sān hé); literally "three harmonies," a 120-degree angle</note>. In total, there are eight types of aspects.
