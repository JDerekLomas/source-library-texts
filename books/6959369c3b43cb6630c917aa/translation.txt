The Master <note>Latin: Magister; the leader of the ritual</note> must therefore be a man of serious mind, steadfast and undaunted, who suffers no defect in his speech or pronunciation. All the Associates <note>Latin: Socii; the assistants or participants helping the Master</note> shall not speak a single word, but the Master alone dares to manage the business and speak with the Spirits.
