# Om! Salutations to the Lord Ganesha. Now begins "The Lamp of Inquiry."

<note>Ganesha is the elephant-headed deity traditionally invoked at the start of Hindu texts to remove obstacles.</note>

Through this text, "The Lamp of Inquiry," one may understand questions regarding the past and the future, and discover that which is desired by the seeker. This work is profoundly miraculous. (1) By identifying the first letter of the name or the spoken word, and by understanding the groups starting with the letter 'A' and the letter 'Ka,' one determines the eight symbolic categories beginning with the "Flag." (2) 

One must calculate these categories and then determine the specific results from them. This path of divination relies upon the eight groups, such as the "Flag" and those that follow. (3) These eight categories—the Flag, Smoke, Lion, Dog, Bull, Donkey, Elephant, and Crow—are used to assign auspicious and inauspicious outcomes. (4)

<note>The system described here is the "Ashta-Varga" (Eight-Group) method, where the sounds of the alphabet are mapped to symbolic animals to predict the outcome of a question.</note>

### 5. Determination of Existence or Non-Existence
<note>original: "asti-nāsti-nirṇayaḥ." This refers to a "Yes or No" question, such as "Is he alive?" or "Does this thing exist?"</note>

If the result falls into the groups of **the Flag**, **the Elephant**, **the Lion**, or **the Bull**, it is certain that the answer is "Yes, it exists." If it falls into **Smoke**, **the Dog**, **the Donkey**, or **the Crow**, then the answer to the inquiry is certainly "No, it does not exist."

### 6. Inquiry on Profit and Loss
Now, regarding the question of profit and loss: with **the Flag**, **the Elephant**, **the Bull**, or **the Lion**, profits will occur quickly; this is certain. However, **the Crow**, **the Dog**, **the Donkey**, or **the Smoke** indicates a question of loss or the coming of quarrels.

### 7. Inquiry on the Well-being of a Traveler
Now, regarding the well-being of one who is away:
<note>original: "pravāsī kuśala praśna." A common question in historical astrology regarding the safety of relatives traveling for trade or pilgrimage.</note>

With **the Lion**, **the Bull**, **the Flag**, or **the Elephant**, safety and well-being are granted to him. **The Crow**, **the Dog**, **the Donkey**, or **the Smoke** says there is no well-being.

### 8. Inquiry on Gain or Loss of Lost Objects
Now, regarding the inquiry of whether a lost object will be found: with **the Flag**, **the Elephant**, **the Bull**, or **the Lion**, its recovery is certain. With **the Crow**, **the Smoke**, **the Donkey**, or **the Dog**, the loss of the missing item is certainly permanent.

### Knowledge of the Location of Lost Objects
Now, regarding the specific knowledge of where a lost object is located:
If it falls under **the Flag**, it is located in the heavens <note>likely meaning a high place or an upstairs floor</note>; and if it is under **the Smoke**, it has gone to the South-East...
<note>original: "āgneyagāsya." The South-East direction is associated with the fire element (Agni).</note>
