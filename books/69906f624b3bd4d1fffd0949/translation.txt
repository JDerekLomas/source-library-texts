# The Book of Shem

<vocab>Book, Shem</vocab> <note>original: "Liber Sem." The name "Sem" is the Latin form of Shem, the eldest son of the biblical patriarch Noah. In the Middle Ages, many texts concerning astrology, alchemy, and "secret" wisdom were attributed to Shem to provide them with the weight of ancient, divinely inspired authority.</note>

<summary>This page contains the formal title of the manuscript's main work, attributing it to the biblical figure Shem. Such attributions were common in medieval esoteric texts to suggest the contents were part of a long-preserved tradition of ancient knowledge.</summary>
