# Royal Privilege. <term>Privilege du Roy: A legal grant from the monarch providing a monopoly on the printing and sale of a specific book, serving as an early form of copyright protection.</term>

LOUIS, by the grace of God, King of France and of Navarre. To our beloved and faithful Councilors, the members of our Courts of Parliaments, Bailiffs, Seneschals, Provosts, their Lieutenants, and all our Justices and Officers to whom it shall concern, GREETINGS. Our well-beloved Pierre Drobet, Bookseller in our city of Lyon, has represented to us that he has recovered a book titled *Oswald Croll's Chemical Basilica* <note>original: "Osualdi Crolij Basilica Chymica." This was one of the most influential works of paracelsian medicine/alchemy of the era.</note>, which book he has had translated into French, titled *The Royal Chemistry of Crollius* <note>original: "La Royalle Chymie de Crollius"</note>, translated by Jean Marcel. The said petitioner would like to print this work for the utility and satisfaction of our subjects; but he fears that someone else might wish to print it or have it printed after he has incurred great expense for the said translation and printing, unless he possessed our letters of Privilege and Permission necessary for this. FOR THESE REASONS, we have permitted and do permit by these presents the said Drobet to print or have printed the said book, as many times and by such printer as he shall see fit, and to put and expose it for sale and distribute it, during the time and term of ten years. We forbid all printers, booksellers, book-vendors, and all our subjects of whatever quality and condition they may be, from printing or having printed the said book, both inside and outside the Kingdom, under the guise of any false mark, change of translation, or disguise whatsoever, without the consent and permission of the said Drobet or his representatives, under penalty of a fine of three hundred livres <note>The "livre" was the currency of France until 1795.</note>, half of which shall be paid to us and the other half to the said petitioner, along with the confiscation of all copies and all costs, damages, and interests owed to the said petitioner. This is granted on the condition of placing one copy in our public library in our city of Paris, according to our regulations. WE THEREFORE COMMAND YOU that you allow the said Drobet to enjoy the contents of these presents fully and peacefully, and to ensure that all others obey this decree. By placing these presents, or an extract thereof, at the beginning or end of the book, WE WILL that they be held as duly notified. And let full faith be given to copies signed by one of our beloved and faithful Councilors, Notaries, and Secretaries, as if it were the original. FOR SUCH is our pleasure. GIVEN at Saint-Germain-en-Laye, the seventeenth day of October, the year of Grace one thousand six hundred and twenty-three, and of our reign the fourteenth.

->By the King in his Council.<-
