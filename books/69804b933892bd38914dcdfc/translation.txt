I must not only precisely and thoroughly refute the ugly accusations and charges that have been scattered against me; but also investigate and bring to light everything that the **Berlin Monthly writers** <note>The *Berliner Monatsschriftsteller* were contributors to the *Berlinische Monatsschrift*, the premier journal of the German Enlightenment (Aufklärung), known for its rationalist critiques of religion.</note> have seen fit to spread throughout the world regarding the secret <term>machinations<gloss>original: Machinationen; schemes or underhanded plots.</gloss></term> of the **Catholics** intended for the ruin of our church. 

I must also examine, as thoroughly and exactly as I am able, the consequences to be expected from this, and the intentions they hope to achieve through this new **heresy hunt** <term><gloss>original: Ketzerjagd; a metaphorical "witch hunt" or aggressive persecution of those with differing religious views.</gloss></term>—which is so striking for our century <note>The 18th century, the "Age of Enlightenment," was seen by contemporaries as an era of tolerance; the author finds a "heresy hunt" particularly out of place in such a "civilized" time.</note>. This was, as anyone must realize, an undertaking that has cost much time, effort, and patience. But a **peddler of anecdotes** <note>original: *Anekdotenkrämer*; a derogatory term for someone who deals in trivial stories or gossip rather than substance.</note> who also traveled—although he was not as famous a traveler as Mr. Nikolai <note>Friedrich Nicolai (1733–1811) was a leading figure of the Berlin Enlightenment, famous for his extensive travel writings and rationalist critiques.</note>—and who, as "thanks" for the kind hospitality enjoyed in my home, had all sorts of things—true, false, and distorted—about me inserted into Mr. <term>Court Councilor<gloss>Hofrath; a title given to high-ranking officials or distinguished scholars in German-speaking lands.</gloss></term> Meusel’s *Historical Literature* <note>Johann Georg Meusel (1743–1820) was a renowned bibliographer; his *Historische Literatur* was a major scholarly journal of the time.</note>, nonetheless said of me that I am very painstaking in my work.
