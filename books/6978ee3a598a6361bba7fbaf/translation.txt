It is an ancient question what one should think of <term>Alchemy</term> <note>A philosophical and protoscientific tradition aimed at the purification and perfection of objects, most famously through the transformation of base metals into gold.</note>, which has been so widely celebrated for many centuries past because of the art of <term>transmuting metals</term> <note>original: "transmutandi metalla"; the chemical process of changing one element into another.</note>. Most people regard it as a vain and deceptive fiction created by certain impostors, or by the simple-minded and the ignorant. I will not now gather together the reasons with which they attack it, nor is it worth the effort to recount the arguments of its defenders, as this has already been done by others long ago and at great length. Instead, I shall casually and briefly collect a few examples from history, as much as is sufficient for the matter.
