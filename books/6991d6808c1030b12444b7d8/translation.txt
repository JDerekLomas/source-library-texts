# CHAPTER I.

### *Discussing certain matters that prepare the soul for the Spirit of PRAYER.*

The majority of mankind, and even of Christians, may be said to be asleep. That specific way of life which occupies each person's mind, thoughts, and affections may very well be called their own particular dream. This degree of vanity <note>original: "Vanity"; here meaning the biblical sense of futility, emptiness, or the temporary nature of worldly pursuits.</note> is equally visible in every form and station of life. The learned and the ignorant, the rich and the poor, are all in the same state of slumber; they are merely passing away a short life in a different kind of dream. 

But why is this so? It is because humanity has eternity within it. A person is born into this world not for the sake of living here, nor for anything this world can give them, but only to have the time and place to become either an eternal sharer of a divine life with God, or to experience a hellish eternity among fallen angels.
