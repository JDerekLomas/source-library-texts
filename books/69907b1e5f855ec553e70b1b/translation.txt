# RESEARCHES
->ON THE<-
# SUPERSTITIONS IN CHINA
<note>While Doré uses the term "superstitions," modern scholars generally view this work as a foundational study of Chinese "folk religion" or "popular beliefs" seen through the lens of a early 20th-century Jesuit missionary.</note>
