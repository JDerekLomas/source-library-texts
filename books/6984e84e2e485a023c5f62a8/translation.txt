# THE TOUCHSTONE,

->To distinguish<-

## the Gold of true Charity,

->from the Metal of the gilded Copper of apparent Charity.<-

And to explain what the Merits of our Savior Jesus Christ and the true Love of God consist of. Declaring also the Glorious state in which all men were created in Adam. And how they must live to return to the same perfection. With many wonderful divine Mysteries.

->By<-

### ANTHOINETTE BOURIGNON.

*To contradict the lies & slanders made of her and her writings by a certain Mr. <term>GEORGE HENRY BURCHARDUS</term> <note>Georg Heinrich Burchard was a Lutheran pastor who opposed Bourignon’s mystical teachings, which emphasized direct inspiration over institutional dogma.</note> Minister of the reform of Luther, in the Cathedral Church of Slesvic <note>Now Schleswig, Germany.</note>. I. 2

### St. Peter in his second Epistle, chapter II, verses 1, 2, 3.

Just as there were formerly false prophets among the people of Israel, so there will likewise be false teachers <note>original: "faux Docteurs"</note> among you, who under plausible pretenses will imperceptibly introduce deadly errors, and who, effectively denying the Lord after he had redeemed them, will bring upon themselves plagues that will destroy them in a short time.

Many people will follow them in their laxity <note>original: "relaschemens," implying a loosening of moral or spiritual discipline</note> and their perdition, and the way of truth will be slandered and blasphemed because of them.

They will trade in your souls with well-studied words of beautiful appearance to satisfy their greed. But their sentence, pronounced long ago, will soon be executed, and the hand that must destroy them is not asleep.
