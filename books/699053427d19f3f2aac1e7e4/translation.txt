*All things must be delivered in their proper stages: so that when everything stands*
*with certain knowledge, they may be recalled for their specific uses.*

*and they leave behind their ancient homes and familiar dens.*
<margin>10</margin> *But others search for flint for the walls, and marble for the temples,*

*to this end the arts and every utility converge.*
