<insert>Happiness and Prosperity.</insert>

## The Mystery
### of the sorrowful and comforting, the killing and life-giving, the humbling and triumphing
# Cross of JESUS Christ
## and its members.

->Written in the midst of the Cross, from within and without, by<-
->a disciple of the Cross of JESUS. Along with several added<-
->Latin poems on various subjects, which were likewise<-
->composed in the solitude of the Sonnenstein<- <note>Sonnenstein is a fortress in Pirna, Saxony; at the time of this publication, it served as a state prison and garrison.</note> ->castle.<-

->Dear Cross, be my guide.<- <note>original Latin: "Cara CrVX, MIhI DVX." The capitalized letters are a chronogram, a common 18th-century word puzzle where Roman numerals add up to a specific year.</note>
->\* \* \*<-
->Dear Cross, sure guide,<- <note>original French: "Chere CroIX, gVIDe asfVre"</note>
->Lead me to the azure port!<- <note>original French: "Menez aV port azVre!"</note>
