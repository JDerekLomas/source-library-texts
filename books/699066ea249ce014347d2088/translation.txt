...doubt [is removed] over a long period of time. With these twenty-two verses, this *Manorama* <note>original: "Manorama," meaning "that which delights the mind."</note> is brought to life for the world. || 21 ||

This **science of divination** <note>original: "Prashna-vidya." This refers to Horary Astrology, where a map of the heavens is cast at the specific moment a question is asked to find the answer.</note> has been spoken by me; it should be given only to a virtuous disciple. If anything has been left unsaid by me, the wise should always add to it from the knowledge they have gained from their own intellect. || 22 ||

Thus ends the **Prashna Manorama**.

<margin>The classes of letters are Va, Ta, Ta, Pa, Ya, and Sha.</margin>

In this order, in whichever **class** <note>original: "Varga." Sanskrit letters are traditionally grouped by their phonetic properties; here, they are assigned numerical values.</note> the word of the result resides, the numerical value of that specific class should be established. This is the system for the **numerical expansion of letters**:

*   The nine letters beginning with **Ka** <note>K, Kh, G, Gh, Ng, Ch, Chh, J, Jh</note> 
*   The nine letters beginning with **Ta** <note>T, Th, D, Dh, N, t, th, d, dh</note>
*   The five letters beginning with **Pa** <note>P, Ph, B, Bh, M</note>
*   The eight letters beginning with **Ya** <note>Y, R, L, V, Sh, sh, S, H</note>

Following this method, the numerical values of the letters are to be determined. One should be assigned to them, and the **numerical values for the vowels** <note>original: "Svaranka."</note> should also be added. One should multiply the class value of the letter (including the vowel) by the value of the vowel-letter itself. In this way, through both methods, the same result is produced, and only one such calculation should be performed.

For example, take the word **Supari** <note>Betel nut. Often used as an example in divination texts.</note>. The letters of *Supari* [yield] 672. For the name **Shri Ram**, the value is 763. <gloss>|| 72 ||</gloss> <gloss>|| 5 ||</gloss> <gloss>|| 2 ||</gloss>
