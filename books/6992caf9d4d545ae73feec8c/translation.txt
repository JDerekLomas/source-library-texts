### On the Ebbing and Flowing of the Two Numbers of Yin and Yang according to the Monthly Ordinances

<note>"Ebbing and Flowing" (xiaoxi) refers to the natural growth and decay of Yin and Yang energies throughout the year. The "Monthly Ordinances" (Yue Ling) is a traditional ritual calendar that aligns human activity with the cosmic cycle.</note>

Before the Two Primordial Forces <note>Yin and Yang</note> were divided, the divine light was hidden deep within, and the original vital energy was a singular, undifferentiated whole. Then, a Dragon-Horse emerged from the river bearing a Diagram on its back, and the Sovereign Fu Xi <note>original: Xihuang, the legendary first emperor of China</note> drew the trigrams; thus, the <term>Numbers of the Changes</term> <note>the mathematical basis of the I Ching</note> came into existence.

However, the word "Change" signifies transformation. It is the Way of mutual shifting and exchanging, flowing alongside the four seasons in a cycle without end. Therefore, the ultimate mystery of numbers lies in the value of "acting in accord with the time." To follow this timing is auspicious; to go against it is inauspicious. This supreme principle is natural and immutable. The details of the Monthly Ordinances according to the seasons are provided below:

**The Three Months of Spring**
During these months, Yin is still abundant while Yang is scarce; this is referred to as the "Spring Chill" which can harm growing things. 
*   Those with **Fire** in their charts may nearly be able to establish themselves <note>Fire provides the warmth needed to counter the "Spring Chill."</note>.
*   Those with **Metal** will suffer "punishment of the wife and overcoming of the children" <note>In the Five Elements cycle, Spring is Wood; Metal attacks Wood, creating a conflict of energies often interpreted as family misfortune.</note>.
*   Those with **Water** will be prone to wandering and drifting.
*   Those with **Wood** will be characterized by a singular focus on benevolence and will attain wealth.
