## Flesh. Affection. Reason:

**F**lesh <note>original Latin: "Caro"</note> is nothing other than a beastly essence, which can be perceived in all animals.

Affection <note>original Latin: "Affectus"</note> or the inclination of the spirit, provides all reason and the senses, whether it be in all kinds of arts, subtleties, the playing of instruments, or otherwise—in short, all arts directed toward good or evil.

Reason <note>original Latin: "Ratio"</note>. That is the rational soul, through which the inspiration or breathing-in of GOD occurs within our fleshy body; it performs nothing but Divine, Heavenly things, because the soul has come from GOD. Therefore, we must be very cautious that the soul is not overcome by the bestial flesh and the evil spirit or inclination; otherwise, we will not be reborn <note>A reference to "Wiedergebohren," the spiritual transformation or "New Birth" central to Christian mysticism.</note>.

## All Things are Threefold.

**G**OD is threefold, as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Flesh, Affection, and Reason. Likewise, the body is created from the earth and from the four elements <note>The traditional elements of Fire, Water, Air, and Earth.</note>, which GOD brought forth from the stars <note>original Latin: "Astris"</note>, while the soul is from GOD; these together make a human being. The spirit, which is created from the stars <note>original German: "Gestirn"</note>, practices and thinks of nothing other than artistic things, such as skillful crafts and all worldly subtleties. This is seen and felt through daily experience: that the spirit of one person excels much higher in the gifts of the arts and learning than another.
