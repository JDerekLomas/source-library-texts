# Front Cover

The exterior of this manuscript is protected by a cover featuring a classic **marbled paper** design. The pattern is composed of organic, rounded shapes—resembling natural stones or bubbles—in varying shades of tan, ochre, and light brown. These earthy tones are accented with small, delicate spots of blue. The entire design is defined by fine, dark veins of black or dark gray ink set against a cream-colored background.

This specific aesthetic is known as a <term>shell pattern<note>A decorative marbling style, often called "stone marble," where pigments are dropped onto a bath of thickened liquid to create organic, overlapping circles that mimic geological or aquatic textures.</note></term>. This style was a hallmark of high-quality bookbinding during the late 18th and 19th centuries. On the left side, the paper shows slight wear along the hinge of the spine, suggesting the volume was frequently handled.
