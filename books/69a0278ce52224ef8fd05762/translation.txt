## The Resurrection <note>original Hebrew: קוּם (Qum), meaning "to arise"</note> will be followed by *Judgment* <note>original Ge'ez: ኵነኔ (Kunané)</note>,

The term commonly signifies *taking up and receiving something,* but in other contexts it also denotes *rousing and awakening a sleeper,* as it is understood in this sense in *Matthew 8:25* and elsewhere.
