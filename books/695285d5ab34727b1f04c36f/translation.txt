I am not concerned to deny the objective reality of all "magical" phenomena; if they are illusions, they are at least as real as many unquestioned facts of daily life; and, if we follow <term>Herbert Spencer (a 19th-century philosopher who argued that every effect must have a cause)</term>, they are at least evidence of some cause.^1
