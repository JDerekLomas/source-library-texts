## THE
# SACRED BOOKS OF THE EAST

<vocab>Sacred Books of the East</vocab> <note>This refers to a monumental 50-volume set of English translations of major religious texts from Asia, including works from Hinduism, Buddhism, Taoism, Confucianism, Zoroastrianism, and Islam. Edited by the scholar Max Müller and published by Oxford University Press starting in 1879, it was a foundational project for the modern scientific study of comparative religion.</note>

<summary>This is the title page for "The Sacred Books of the East," a landmark 19th-century series of translations that brought primary religious texts from across Asia to the English-speaking world.</summary>
