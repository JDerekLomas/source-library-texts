# Rituals of Shaving and the Commencement of Knowledge

The rituals of shaving, beginning education, the ritual fire-circle, and piercing <note>likely referring to ear-piercing or *Karnavedha*</note>—with the exception of marriage—are not recommended during the month of one's birth. Travel is also certainly not auspicious during this time; this is the truth. 51 Auspicious works and ceremonies performed in the month of one's birth constellation or birth ascendant are considered beneficial; thus says the sage Garga. However, travel is never recommended during these times. 52

### Auspicious and Inauspicious Days for Shaving

The fifth, seventh, tenth, eleventh, and thirteenth lunar days, as well as the second and third, are considered to bring good fortune. 53 One should avoid the twelfth, the eighth, the "empty" days <note>original: *rikta* — the 4th, 9th, and 14th days of the lunar fortnight</note>, the sixth, and the first day of the fortnight. Shaving performed on the remaining days brings auspicious results. 54

One should also avoid shaving on the sixth, eighth, and fourth lunar days, the day of the new moon <note>original: *sinīvālī* — the day when the moon is invisible</note>, the fourteenth, and the ninth. Among the days of the week, Sunday, Tuesday, and Saturday should be avoided for these rituals. 55 Traditional law states that Sunday and Tuesday are particularly dangerous, as they may bring death to a child. From the words of the Master, the <term>true position</term><note>original: "sphuta"; the mathematically corrected longitude of a planet.</note> is made clear. **12** The knowledge of rising, setting, and other phenomena, as well as the <term>correction for conjunction</term><note>original: "shighra-phala"; the second correction applied to the planets to account for the Earth's orbit relative to them.</note>, shall become evident. **12** The degrees of the <term>anomaly</term><note>original: "kendra"; the angular distance of a planet from its apogee or perigee.</note> are roughly written based on the time and the zodiacal signs... by these multipliers, the difference in the results of all planetary movements is found. **13**

If the result is greater or less than the intended value... calculate the <term>daily motion</term><note>original: "bhukti"</note> after dividing by <term>sixty</term><note>original: "shashti"; the base of the sexagesimal system used in Indian astronomy where 60 minutes make a degree.</note>. When the result is in the middle, hail! The motion of the anomaly and others remains "un-true" (<term>aspashta</term><note>meaning the mean or uncorrected motion</note>) until corrected again for the fast motion.

Multiply by the segment of the fast-motion table, divide by sixty, and find the difference in the result. From the <term>motion corrected for the first anomaly</term><note>original: "manda-spashta-gati"</note>, whichever is greater on the left in sequence, or by reversal, the motion of the planet becomes **retrograde** (<term>vakra</term><note>The apparent backward motion of a planet as seen from Earth.</note>).

For Mars and the other planets, using the <term>tropical Sun</term><note>original: "sayanarka"; the Sun's position adjusted for the precession of the equinoxes.</note> and the rising times of the signs... at the desired time in <term>palas</term><note>A unit of time; 60 palas equal one ghatika (24 minutes).</note>. If it is greater than sixty, one should subtract the signs beginning with <term>Aries</term><note>original: "Meshadikam"</note>. Multiply the remainder by the planet’s motion and divide by the <term>rising time</term><note>original: "ashuddhodaya"</note>. The resulting signs, starting from Aries, should be added. This, combined with the <term>precession of the equinoxes</term><note>original: "ayanansha"</note>, becomes the fourth house.

<gloss>From Pisces, Aries, and Taurus... the declination must be calculated... combined with the rays of the Sun...</gloss>

...at the time of birth, the connection called the "Great Lord's Yoga." The time elapsed in the <term>lunar mansion</term><note>original: "nakshatra"</note> should be calculated in <term>nadikas</term><note>A unit of time equal to 24 minutes.</note> as before. When the divisor is greater, the seventh part... the position of <term>Rahu</term><note>The North Node of the Moon.</note>... divided by the day or 5 <term>ghatikas</term><note>24-minute intervals; 5 ghatikas equals 2 hours.</note>.

When the motion corrected for the first anomaly is greater than the result of the fast correction, the motion is not yet determined. If the result of the fast correction is less than the motion corrected for the first anomaly, then by the four-fold path...
