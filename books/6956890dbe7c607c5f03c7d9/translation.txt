# INTRODUCTION

**T**HE author of *The City of the Sun* <note>Original Latin title: Civitas Solis; a famous early 17th-century work describing an ideal utopian society based on reason and communal property.</note> was born in Stilo, Calabria, September 5, 1568. He became a student, a Dominican <note>A member of the Order of Preachers, a Catholic religious order founded by Saint Dominic.</note>, and a philosopher, and at the age of twenty-three published a work entitled *Philosophy Demonstrated by the Senses* <note>Original Latin: Philosophia sensibus demonstrata.</note>, which roused the ire of the schoolmen <term>schoolmen: traditional scholars of the Middle Ages and Renaissance who focused on logic and the authority of established texts</term>, who were blindly following the Aristotelian philosophy. In 1599 he was accused of heresy and of conspiracy against the Spanish Government, and was imprisoned in Naples, where he remained twenty-seven years. While he was an Italian patriot, and did set forth the new philosophy that is commonly associated with Bacon <note>Referring to Francis Bacon, the English philosopher credited with developing the scientific method.</note>, he was condemned for books that he did not write and theories that he did not advocate. He was cross-questioned and tortured several times, and was finally released at the instance of Pope Urban VIII. He lived a few years in Rome, and then, when renewed persecution was threatened, fled to Paris, where Richelieu <note>Cardinal Richelieu, the powerful chief minister to King Louis XIII of France.</note> befriended him. The Sorbonne <note>The famous theological faculty of the University of Paris.</note> pronounced his works orthodox, and the King pensioned him. He died in a convent in Paris, May 21, 1639. *The City of the Sun* is one of the works that he wrote in prison. There is a biography of him by Baldacchini, and a later one by Amabile.

**Captain:** The supreme ruler among them is a priest whom they call by the name **Hoh**, though in our language we would call him **Metaphysic** <note>The term "Metaphysic" here refers to a leader who understands the fundamental nature of reality and existence</note>.
