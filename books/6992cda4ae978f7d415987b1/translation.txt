### Glossary of Terms

<term>Inauspicious Residence</term> (凶宅, *xiongzhai*): A dwelling with poor geomantic alignment that brings bad luck or tragedy to its inhabitants.

<term>Mulberry</term> (桑, *sang*): While vital for the silk industry, these trees are considered unlucky in certain positions around a home due to linguistic associations with death.

<term>Woods</term> (林, *lin*): Forests or dense clusters of trees which, if poorly placed, can block positive energy (*qi*) or harbor negative influences.

<term>Grave</term> (墳, *fen*): A burial mound. Living too close to the dead was believed to cause an imbalance between *yin* (the world of the dead) and *yang* (the world of the living).

<term>Enlightened Master</term> (明師, *mingshi*): An expert in the laws of heaven and earth capable of diagnosing the spiritual health of a property.

<term>Distress and Anxiety</term> (栖惶, *qihuang*): A state of being unsettled, wandering, or living in constant fear and hardship.

<term>Unexpected Disaster</term> (非灾, *feizai*): Calamities that are seen as unnatural or arising from spiritual disharmony rather than common accidents.

# 21: The Source of Fortune

The **Source of Fortune** <term>Fu Yuan: A fundamental concept in Eight Mansions Feng Shui representing an individual's innate energetic alignment based on their birth year.</term> is located in the palaces of <term>Zhen (Thunder/East)</term>, <term>Xun (Wind/Southeast)</term>, <term>Kan (Water/North)</term>, and <term>Li (Fire/South)</term> for those born into the **Four Eastern Directions** group. Their auspicious stars are all situated in these four directions: Zhen, Xun, Kan, and Li. 

In these directions, it is ideal to place the main gates, design the paths of travel, and construct tall, substantial rooms or buildings. These are the areas where the master of the house should reside. If one mistakenly utilizes the directions of <term>Qian (Heaven/Northwest)</term>, <term>Kun (Earth/Southwest)</term>, <term>Gen (Mountain/Northeast)</term>, or <term>Dui (Lake/West)</term>, they will encounter inauspicious stars. This error is known as "an Eastern person renovating according to Western patterns," which results in much misfortune. For this reason, I have set forth these **House Diagrams for the Four Eastern Directions**.

Suppose a husband has a life destiny belonging to the **Four Eastern Directions**, while his wife belongs to the **Four Western Directions**. Unlike fathers, sons, or brothers, who can be assigned to live in separate courtyards, a couple resides together. How, then, should their living quarters be arranged? 

*   If they live in a **North Building**, the husband should reside in the center room, while the wife resides in the western or eastern rooms; the <term>Qian (Northwest)</term> and <term>Gen (Northeast)</term> directions are suitable for her.
*   If they live in a **South Building**, the husband should reside in the eastern or center rooms, while the wife resides in the western room; the <term>Kun (Southwest)</term> direction is suitable for her.
*   If they live in an **East Building**, the husband should reside in the southern or center rooms, while the wife resides in the northern room; the <term>Gen (Northeast)</term> direction is suitable for her.
