The Art, therefore, always proceeds from those things which are more certain regarding its own nature, since uncertain things cannot be proven except from those which possess certainty, at least in some manner or for the most part. Nor can the Art exist unless it begins from its stated principles <note>original: "principiis"; the fundamental building blocks of Lull's system</note>, some of which are material and exist as more or less purified; and according to this degree of purity, they are nearer to or further from the work <note>the "Work" (opus) in this context refers to the alchemical or philosophical process of transformation</note>, as we believe you, dearest one <note>the author addresses the reader or a specific student directly</note>, are not ignorant of.
