->*In which not only*<-

## THE GOLD-MAKING ART
And the history of its more noble WRITERS is handed down; the truth of the <term>Stone</term> <note>The Philosopher's Stone (Lapis Philosophorum), a legendary substance supposed to be capable of turning base metals into gold or silver.</note> is proven by countless arguments and experiments, and even by the judgments of legal experts; obscure terms are explained; and warnings against impostors, as well as the difficulties occurring in the preparation of the <term>Universal Tincture</term> <note>Another name for the Philosopher's Stone or the Great Elixir, capable of "dyeing" or transforming metals.</note>, are set forth:
