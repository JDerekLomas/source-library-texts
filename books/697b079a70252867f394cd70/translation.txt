# Preface.

## Beloved Reader/

->I.<-

However unnecessary and insignificant the subject matter described here might seem in the eyes of reason, it has nonetheless always been important and venerable according to the judgment of the **Holy Spirit** <note>original: "H. Geistes"</note>, as He has dealt with it so minutely and extensively in the writings of the Apostles and Prophets. It is indeed well known enough what frivolous and shameful thoughts and words regarding <term>chastity<note>The term "Keuschheit" here refers to moral and sexual purity as defined by religious law.</note></term> and marriage are common among the children of this world <note>A phrase used to describe those who prioritize secular life and physical pleasures over spiritual duties.</note>—so much so that even the most holy expressions of the Spirit of **God** cannot remain unreviled by the impure spirit of this world. Experience also shows that at times an impure mindset regarding such important matters has even prevented well-meaning souls from contemplating and honoring them according to their pure and innocent foundation.

<summary>In this preface, the author defends the spiritual dignity of discussing marriage and purity, arguing that despite worldly mockery, these topics are treated with great reverence in the Holy Scriptures.</summary>
