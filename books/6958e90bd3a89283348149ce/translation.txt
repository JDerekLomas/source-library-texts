*Natural History is, as it were, a most richly stocked storehouse. *Once I had obtained these from the gardeners <note>original: "ab Hortulanis"; referring to the professional keepers of botanical gardens</note>, nothing was of greater concern to me than that my collected plants should be marked with their proper names.
