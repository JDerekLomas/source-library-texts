# The Birth of Christ

<page-type>illustration</page-type>

This woodcut illustration depicts the **Nativity of Jesus**, the scene of his birth, set within a grand but decaying structure of stone. 

On the left side of the scene, the **Virgin Mary** kneels in humble prayer, adoring her newborn son. Her head is encircled by a radiant halo of light, a traditional artistic symbol of her holiness and divine grace. In the center, the **infant Jesus**—the *Christ Child*—lies peacefully upon a simple cloth spread on the bare ground.

To the right, **Saint Joseph** leans forward, supporting himself with a shepherd’s staff as he gazes down at the child. Behind the holy family, the heads of an **ox** and a **donkey** <note>The presence of these animals is a traditional detail based on the prophecy in Isaiah 1:3: "The ox knows its master, the donkey its owner’s manger," signifying that even the animals recognized the arrival of the Lord.</note> emerge from the shadows of the stable. In the background, a third figure, likely a **shepherd**, stands leaning against a massive stone pillar.

The setting is notable for its **ruined architectural elements**, including heavy masonry, a wide rounded arch, and a classical column with a decorative top. <note>In Renaissance art, the Nativity is often shown among ruins to symbolize the "Old Law" or the ancient world falling away to make room for the "New Law" brought by the birth of Christ.</note> The artist used fine parallel lines and cross-hatching to create a sense of deep shadow and three-dimensional space within the crumbling stone walls.

At the highest point of the image, the **Holy Spirit** <note>original: "Spiritus Sanctus"</note> descends in the form of a dove within a brilliant sunburst, signifying the divine blessing and the presence of God.
