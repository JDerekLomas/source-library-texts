# Chapter I.

### On the Origin and Foundation of <term>Geomancy</term> <note>original: "Geomantiæ."</note>.

Although Adam, due to the eating from the <term>Tree of Knowledge</term> of Good and Evil, brought the curse of GOD upon himself, nevertheless, because of being created in the <term>Image of GOD</term> <note>original: "Ebenbild GOttes"; the theological concept of *Imago Dei*, suggesting that humans retain some divine intellectual qualities despite the Fall.</note>, a certain remnant <note>original: "Vor-Haut"; literally "foreskin," but used here mystically to describe a persisting layer or vestige of original divine wisdom left in the soul.</note> remained in his soul and understanding, through which he was able to recognize and name everything that existed in heaven and on earth. From this, through the impulse of the Spirit, he learned and discovered many glorious sciences and planted them also into his descendants, among which the noble <term>Art of the Stars</term> <note>original: "Stern-Kunst"; the combined study of astrology and astronomy.</note> may have been included. That many glorious arts and sciences perished and were obscured because of the world’s ingratitude and wickedness can be seen from the <term>Deluge</term> <note>original: "Sündfluth"; the Great Flood.</note> of the first world. In that destruction of all flesh, Noah with his household was nevertheless preserved, who afterwards rebuilt the world with his sons and brought forth all sciences anew.
