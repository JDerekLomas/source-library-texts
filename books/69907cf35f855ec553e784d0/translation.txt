# The Jewel of Marriage

Salutations to the venerable Ganesha. <note>Lord Ganesha is the elephant-headed deity traditionally invoked at the start of Hindu manuscripts to remove obstacles.</note> Now begins the *Jewel of Marriage*. Having bowed to the son of the Goddess <note>Ganesha, the son of Parvati</note> and meditating upon the light of astrological wisdom, this condensed *Jewel of Marriage* was composed by Kshemankara for the sake of prosperity. <note>original: "Kṣemaṃkareṇa" — the author’s name, which literally means "the doer of welfare."</note> (1) When Jupiter is in Leo <note>original: "siṃhe gurau"</note>, or at the end of the cycles of Venus and Jupiter, or during the birth month or the month of decay, one should not perform a marriage ceremony. (2) For the groom, one must ensure the strength of the Sun.

The Sun is auspicious in these positions: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11.

For the bride, one must ensure the strength of Jupiter. For both, the strength of the Moon—the king of the twice-born <note>original: "dvija rāja" — an epithet for the Moon</note>—must be present; then the marriage shall be a giver of happiness. (3) This concludes the rule for the Threefold Strength. The Moon is auspicious for men in the 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 11th positions; in the 4th, 10th, 11th, and 2nd, it is otherwise beneficial for mankind. (4) This concludes the rule for
