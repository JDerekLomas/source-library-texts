## C. VITRINGA

# SACRED OBSERVATIONS.

### CHAPTER I.

->*In which the opinion concerning the confusion of languages from Genesis 11 is examined:*<-

<image-desc>A decorative woodcut drop cap letter 'V' featuring intricate floral scrollwork and a bird figure within the letter's frame.</image-desc>

Just as it is fitting that we follow with due reverence those things found to have been handed down to us by our ancestors, so also the servitude of those minds is deservedly blamed which seek arguments from those ancestors for matters in which they neither achieved, nor could have achieved, any greater certainty than our own. Scholars constantly seek the origin of multiple languages in this passage, yet not a single word regarding that is contained in the entire Mosaic narrative. From this, it appears the matter must be dug up from its foundations, so that we may finally understand what *Babel* truly signifies.
