## Of the Quantity and Quality of Wares.

The quantity of all wares is known by weight or measure. The cause of gravity is not understood, nor is it relevant to this purpose; whether it proceeds from the elasticity of the air, or the weight of the outermost sphere, or from whatever other causes, it is sufficient that the ways of testing the weights of bodies are perfectly discovered by the balance. There are two sorts of weights in common use: *Troy* and *Avoirdupois*.

The first are used to weigh goods of most value, such as *gold*, *silver*, and *silk*, etc. The latter are used for coarser and more bulky goods, such as *lead*, *iron*, etc.
