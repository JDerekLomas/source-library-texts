# General Flat Map of the Five Continents of the Earth

<insert>
General Flat Map
of the Five Continents
of the Earth
Summer of the fifth year
of the Guangxu reign <note>1879</note>
In the fourth month, Mr. Yang
of Shidai <note>Shidai is a county in Anhui Province, China</note>
engraved this in the capital city
of Paris, France.
</insert>

<note>This map, titled 'General Flat Map of the Five Continents of the Earth', was engraved in Paris in 1879 (5th year of the Guangxu reign) by an individual named Yang from Shidai, China. It is a rare example of a Chinese scholar learning Western engraving techniques abroad.</note>

### Oceans
* **Arctic Ocean**
* **North Atlantic Ocean**
* **South Atlantic Ocean**
* **Southern Ocean**
* **Indian Ocean**
* **Great Eastern Ocean / Pacific Ocean** <note>The map uses both names, reflecting the transition in Chinese terminology for the Pacific.</note>

### Continents, Countries, and Regions
* **Asia**
    * China
    * Russia
    * Arabia
    * India
    * Japan
* **Europe**
    * France
    * England
    * Prussia <note>A prominent German state at the time of the map's creation.</note>
    * Italy
    * Spain
    * Portugal
* **Africa**
    * Egypt
    * Great Sahara Desert
* **North America**

    * Alaska
    * Mexico

* **South America**
    * Brazil
    * Chile
    * Peru
* **British Australia**

<term>Earth (地球 - Diqiu)</term>: Literally "the terrestrial sphere," a term popularized by Jesuit missionaries in China to describe the planet.
<term>Guangxu (光緒)</term>: The reign name of the eleventh Emperor of the Qing dynasty, who ruled from 1875 to 1908.

<summary>A late 19th-century world map printed in Paris, showcasing the collaboration between Chinese engraver Mr. Yang and the French cartographic house Erhard. It reflects the global exchange of geographic knowledge and printing technology during the Qing Dynasty.</summary>
