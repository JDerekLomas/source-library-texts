# The Wondrous Life-Course, etc. (Chapter 1)

### Chapter 1.

<margin>Verse 1.</margin>
God, the eternal Love, has at all times looked for true worshippers in spirit and in truth, as for such pillars of Christendom who would stand in the breach <note>A biblical metaphor from Ezekiel 22:30, referring to those who intercede to protect others from judgment</note>, so that they might halt the wrath of God’s justice—which is revealed and ignited by the sins of mankind and seeks to devour everything—extinguishing that same wrath through the blood and death of Christ within the creature <note>The "creature" refers to the individual human being as a created soul</note>, and might not cease, with the faith of Jacob or the Canaanite woman <note>References to persistent faith: Jacob wrestling the angel (Genesis 32) and the woman who would not take "no" for an answer from Jesus (Matthew 15)</note>, until God’s mercy declared itself in blessing for the reconciliation of the natural wrath, for the benefit of humanity.

v. 2. It is, therefore, that same fundamentally good God who has at all times raised up brave warriors in the world, who like Moses persisted so that He might spare the world: since they, with their ignition of love in prayer, pressed against the wrath, moved God to compassion, and through Jesus, the only true High Priest, opened the path for their fellow men to the inner life that is from God.

<summary>This introductory chapter describes the necessity of spiritual "pillars" or intercessors who, through persistent faith and the love of Christ, turn away divine wrath and lead others toward an inner life with God.</summary>

# (Chapter 1) The Wondrous Course of Life

...who also, like Paul <note>original: "Paulo"; referring to the Apostle Paul's metaphor of spiritual birth-pains in Galatians 4:19</note>, have given birth to children into the Kingdom of God through great suffering.

v. 3. In particular, however, this great grace of our Heavenly Father broke through in our present times, so that He might let us see how He not only infinitely loves the human race in Christ Jesus, but also wishes to create a powerful rescue in the Spirit for them in their distress. He intends to let the long-prophesied <term>Lily-Time</term> <note>A term used by mystics like Jakob Böhme to describe a coming age of spiritual purity, peace, and the blossoming of divine revelation on earth</note> of salvation dawn in the hearts of men; and thus, for those who venture for His sake and with Him in faith, He can help them far overcome all the power of the enemy.

v. 4. And for this purpose, God has chosen for Himself, above others, two men after His own heart. These He equipped with uncommon divine power and wisdom within the goal of His holy purpose of grace, in order to encounter in the Spirit the violence of Satan—as well as the countless magical deceptions of the old serpent—and to fulfill God's will, as it is in heaven, so also here on earth.

v. 5. Since one of the two aforementioned <term>Instruments of God</term> <note>A Pietist term for individuals chosen by God to enact His will or bring forth new revelation</note> passed from this temporal world to God some years ago, through a blessed departure into eternal joy, we have ventured, while calling upon God, to draft the following account of his holily-conducted course of life, with the assistance of divine grace.
