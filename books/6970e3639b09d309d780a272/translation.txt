<margin>Solution by heat.</margin>
Put the Medicine into a glass vessel and bury it in our **moist fire** <note>original: "igne nostro humido"; likely a *balneum Mariae* or water bath, providing gentle, indirect heat.</note> for seven days or more, until the Medicine is dissolved into water without any cloudiness.

<margin>Solution by vapor.</margin>
Solution by vapor <note>original: "raritatis"; referring to the thinning of matter into steam or vapor.</note>: Take a glass vessel containing the Medicine and suspend it in a new brass pot with a narrow opening. Boil water in the pot and keep the opening closed so that the rising vapor of the boiling water dissolves the Medicine.

But note that the boiling water should not touch the glass vessel containing the Medicine by a distance of three fingers; this solution may take one, two, or three days to complete.

After the Medicine has been dissolved (*the same author continues*), remove it from the heat to let it cool, fix, congeal, harden, or dry. It should be dissolved many times, for the more the Medicine is dissolved, the more perfect it will become. <margin>1</margin> **"Now you dismiss upon the foundations."** <note>original: "Nunc dimittis super fundamenta."
