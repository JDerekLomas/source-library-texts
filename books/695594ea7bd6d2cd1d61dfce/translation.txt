### On Charity and Faith

Charity exists without faith, as it does in infants and among the Gentiles <note>original: "gentiles"; in this context, non-Christians or those outside the specific religious tradition</note>. This is easily accepted, as is clear from what has been said regarding infants and the Gentiles. Faith, however, does not exist without charity. <note>original Latin: "Charitas datur absque fide, ut apud infantes et gentiles, id facile se ipsum patitur, ut patet ab illis quæ de infantibus et gentilibus dicta sunt: fides vero absque charitate non datur."</note>

->(Facsimile from one of his notebooks.)<-
