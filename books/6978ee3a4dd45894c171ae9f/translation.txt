# Preface

## To the Art-Desiring
## Reader.

I must indeed myself confess that already a considerable number of chemical <note>The original "Chymischer" refers to the early modern period when chemistry and alchemy were not yet distinct sciences.</note> books are available, for which reason I should rightly hesitate to fill the world with even more of them.

However, because these excellent writings of **MICHAEL SENDIVOGIUS** <note>original: "MICHAELIS SENDIVOGII." Sendivogius was a famous Polish alchemist and physician (1566–1636), renowned for his theories on the "food of life" in the air.</note> have been lacking for a considerable time, yet have nevertheless been so eagerly sought by many, I have wished to remedy this lack through this new edition.

As far as I am aware, this jewel in the German language has not been printed more than three times, namely in the year 1613, 1628, and the year 1681, in octavo <term>octavo (8vo): a printing format where a large sheet of paper is folded three times to create eight leaves, resulting in a portable, medium-sized book.</term> in Strasbourg.
