He who has killed a cow, a drinker of intoxicants, one who eats forbidden food, or one who accepts improper gifts— || 84 ||

—it burns away all such major sins; of this there is no doubt. Whoever hears this daily or chants it with a concentrated mind— || 85 ||

—becoming a soul purified of all sins, he is glorified in the world of the Sun. The childless shall obtain children, and the poor shall obtain wealth. || 86 ||

Even one suffering from a severe disease is freed from that ailment if he reads this always with devotion. He who, on the Day of the Sun <note>Sunday.</note>, O Partha, stands in water reaching up to his navel— || 87 ||

—and facing the Sun <term>Bhaskara</term> <note>Literally "The Light-Maker".</note> as he has risen over the eastern mountain <note>original: "Udayācala" — the mythical mountain from behind which the sun rises.</note>, remains in a posture of prostration, and if that person chants with devotion or even hears it with faith— || 88 ||

—he goes to the supreme abode where the Lord Day-Maker <term>Divakara</term> <note>Another name for the Sun God.</note> resides. When one begins the subduing of enemies, O Partha— || 89 ||

—then, having made an image of the enemy using the dust from their footprints, and stepping upon it with the left foot, one should chant the *Aditya Hridayam*. || 90 ||
