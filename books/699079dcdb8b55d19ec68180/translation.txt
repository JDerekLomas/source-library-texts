<term>At the Back of the Black Man's Mind</term> <note>original: "At the Back of the Black Man's Mind". Published in 1906 by Richard Edward Dennett, this work is an early ethnographic study that attempts to explain the complex religious, legal, and social systems of West African cultures, specifically the Bavili of the Loango Coast and the Bini of Nigeria, to a Western audience.</note>

<summary>This is the title page for R.E. Dennett's ethnographic work exploring the underlying philosophy and spiritual beliefs of West African societies during the colonial period.</summary>
