# APHORISMS
## MAGICAL
# OF EUGENIUS.

->*This is the First Truth:*<-
->*This is also the Last.*<-

<margin>I.</margin>
Before all things, the Point existed: not the indivisible atom <note>original: "τὸ ἄτομὸν" (to atomon)</note>, or a mathematical point, but a Diffusive one. It was a Monad <term>Monad: A single, indivisible unit representing the divine source or the absolute "One" from which all existence originates.</term> explicitly; implicitly, it was a Myriad <term>Myriad: Traditionally meaning ten thousand, but used here to represent an infinite multitude or the "Many" contained within the "One."</term>. It was Light, and it was also Night: the Beginning, and the End of the Beginning: Everything, and Nothing: It Is, and It is Not.

# Magical Aphorisms

<margin>2.</margin> The Monad <term>Monad: The primary unit or "the One," representing absolute unity and the source of all being.</term> moved itself in the Dyad <term>Dyad: The principle of duality or "the Two," representing the first step from unity toward division and plurality.</term>: and through the Triad <term>Triad: The number three, often representing the first manifestation of a complete form or the "Faces" of divine light.</term> the Faces of the second Light came forth.

<margin>3.</margin> A simple, uncreated Fire went out: and beneath the Waters it clothed itself in a Garment of multiple, Created Fire. <note>Philalethes distinguishes here between the "uncreated" divine fire of God and the "created" fire that powers the physical world.</note>

<margin>4.</margin> It looked back toward the superior Source: and having drawn down the Pattern <note>original: "Typo"</note>, it sealed the Inferior world with a Triple face.

<margin>5.</margin> Unity created one: and the Trinity distinguished it into Three. There is also the Quaternary <term>Quaternary: The number four, which in Hermetic thought acts as the "Nexus" or bridge that links the complex physical world back to its simple divine origin.</term>, the Bond and the Medium of Restoration.

<margin>6.</margin> From visible things, Water first shone forth: the Female of the brooding Fire, and the pregnant Mother of all things that can be shaped.

<margin>7.</margin> It was porous within, and varied in its layers <note>original: "Corticibus," literally "barks" or "rinds," suggesting a layered or nested structure like an onion.</note>: its womb held the heavens rolled up, and the stars undistinguished.

<margin>8.</margin> The Artificer <term>Artificer: The "Separator Artifex" or divine craftsman who organizes the chaotic "prime matter" into the ordered regions of the universe.</term> divided this into wide Regions: and as the Offspring appeared, the Mother vanished.
