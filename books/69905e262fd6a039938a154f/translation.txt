# On Occult Philosophy

I offer to you the documents of undefiled truth and the impregnable discipline of Magic, along with the most pleasant experiences of the holy Divinities <note>original: "Numinum"</note>. Just as you eagerly desire to know these things by reading books on Occult Philosophy, so too, by reading this book, you may truly triumph over them. Therefore, keep this work hidden within the secret inner chambers of your religious heart, and conceal it with constant silence.

You should truly know this: that the names of the presiding Intelligences <note>In this context, "Intelligences" refers to the high-ranking celestial spirits or angels believed to govern the planets.</note> for each of the Planets are established according to this method. This is done by projecting from each degree aspected by the Planet itself, starting from the degree of the Ascendant.

By a similar reasoning, the names of the Princes of the evil Spirits presiding under each of the Planets are obtained in retrograde order. # Book IV.

The method by which the sacred names of the Spirits <note>original: "Spirituum nomina"</note> are correctly and solemnly drawn out consists in the arrangement of vowels, which form the name of the spirit and by which a proper and ritually sounding name is established. This craft is performed in the following way: first, in placing the vowels of those letters which are found through the calculation of the Celestial figure <note>a horoscope or astrological chart</note>, one must pay attention to drawing out the names of the spirits of the second order, both the good and the evil presiding governors. 

And this, among the good spirits, is performed thus: having considered the stars which constitute the letters and having placed them in order, first subtract the degree of the eleventh house from the degree of the star which is first in the order; and that which remains from there, project it from the degree of the Ascendant <note>the degree of the zodiac rising on the eastern horizon</note>. And where that number ends, there is the location of the vowel for the first letter. Begin therefore from that point to project the vowels <gloss>A, E, I, O, U</gloss> of those letters according to their number and order; and whichever vowel falls upon the place of the star which is first in the order, that vowel is attributed to the first letter.
