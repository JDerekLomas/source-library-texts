A complex allegorical and cosmological engraving. The entire scene is framed by heavy, billowing dark clouds. 

At the top right, a winged celestial figure (possibly Time or a divine messenger) with a crown and cross atop its head walks upon the clouds, pulling a rope that is attached to the outer rim of a large circular diagram below.

The central diagram consists of numerous concentric circles representing the Macrocosm. Within the center of these circles is a "Microcosmus" represented by a nude male figure in a Vitruvian-like pose, with arms and legs outstretched. The man stands within a smaller series of circles labeled with the four humors: "Pituita", "Sanguis", "Cholera", and "Melancholia" (partially visible at the base). 

The larger circles surrounding the man are labeled with "Macrocosmus" and "Micro-cosmus" and contain astrological and zodiacal symbols (Aries, Taurus, etc.) alongside planetary symbols (Sun, Moon, etc.). The innermost circles of the Macrocosm contain representations of the sun and moon.
