<meta>This page serves as the preface to a larger work on cultural history, specifically introducing a manuscript related to the Faust legend.</meta>

# Preface.

---

That I am able to open my larger undertaking, described more specifically on the title page, with a contribution so very important for <term>cultural history</term> <note>German: Kulturgeschichte; the study of the development of a society's customs, arts, and intellectual life</note>: I owe this advantage solely to the kindness of the Chief Librarian, Professor Dr. Eberhard in Coburg, who so graciously permitted me a copy of the famous manuscript of the *Faustian Compulsion of Hell* <note>German: Faust'scher Höllenzwang; a type of "grimoire" or book of magic spells attributed to the legendary Doctor Faustus</note> located in the Ducal Library there.

This manuscript, which evidently dates only from the previous century, consists of 303 leaves in <term>folio</term> <note>a large book format made by folding sheets of paper only once</note>, which are interwoven with a multitude of illuminated illustrations. The diligent painter Mr. Karl Kohl in Coburg copied the text as well as the pictures most faithfully, and I am reproducing all of the latter in a somewhat reduced scale.

<summary>The author introduces a significant manuscript on the Faustian "Compulsion of Hell" magic, thanking Professor Eberhard for access to the 303-page illustrated volume in Coburg.</summary>
<keywords>Preface, Faust, Compulsion of Hell, Grimoire, Coburg Ducal Library, Karl Kohl, Book Illustration, Cultural History</keywords>
