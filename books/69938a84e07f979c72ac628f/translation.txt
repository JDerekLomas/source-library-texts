If a straight line is bisected, and a straight line is added to it in a straight line <note>meaning the line is extended</note>, the **rectangle** contained by the whole line with the added segment and the added segment itself, together with the **square** on the half, is equal to the **square** on the line made up of the half and the added segment. For let a certain line **AB** be bisected at **C**, and let a certain straight line **BD** be added to it in a straight line; I say that the rectangle contained by **AD** and **DB** together with the square on **CB** is equal to the square on **CD**. For let the square **CEZD** be described on **CD**, and let **DE** be joined; through the point **B** let **BH** be drawn parallel to either **CE** or **DZ**, and through the point **Θ** let **KL** be drawn parallel to either **AD** or **EZ**, and again through **A** let **AK** be drawn parallel to either **CL** or **BH**. Since then **AC** is equal to **CB**, the rectangle **AL** is also equal to **CΘ**. But **CΘ** is equal to **ΘZ**; therefore **AL** is also equal to **ΘZ**. Let **CH** be added to each; therefore the whole **AH** is equal to the gnomon **NXO**. But **AH** is the rectangle contained by **AD** and **DB**, for **BH** is equal to **BD**; and therefore the gnomon **NXO** is equal to the rectangle contained by **AD** and **DB**. Let **MΘ** be added to each, which is equal to the square on **CB**; therefore the rectangle contained by **AD** and **DB** together with the square on **CB** is equal to the gnomon **NXO** and the area **MΘ**.
