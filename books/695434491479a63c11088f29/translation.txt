<meta>This page continues the discussion of the evolution of Jewish theological concepts through contact with Greek thought.</meta>

Greek philosophy was in its turn cross-fertilized by Hellenism <note>The culture and ideals of ancient Greece</note>. Jehovah was gradually depersonalized until the world was rendered independent of Him. Meanwhile, under the influence of mysticism, He becomes an immanent principle <note>A divine presence existing within and throughout the universe</note> of the universe, similar to the *Substance* <note>original: "Substantia"</note> of Spinoza. In this way, the God of Israel—originally viewed as a Deity acting from the outside <note>original: "ab-extra"</note>—had been transformed through cross-fertilization into a continuous, energetic Principle.
