# Om! Salutations to the Illustrious Ganesha. 
# Om! Now begins the writing on the twelve houses of the horoscope.

Om! When the **Sun**, **Mars**, **Rahu**, and **Saturn** <note>original: *Surya-Bhauma-Rahu-Shani*. These are considered "malefic" or harsh planets in Vedic astrology.</note> are located in the **House of Death** <note>original: *Mrityu-bhava*, the eighth house of a horoscope.</note>, there is great distress and ailments related to the blood. However, if all the planets present there are **benefics** <note>original: *Saumya*, literally "gentle" or "lunar" planets like Jupiter or Venus.</note>, the person remains free from disease. ॥ 1 ॥

If all **malefic planets** occupy the **House of Wealth** <note>original: *Dhana-sthana*, the second house.</note>, it results in financial loss. In contrast, if auspicious planets are located here, everything is favorable, leading to prosperity, growth, and the arrival of wealth. ॥ 2 ॥

When **malefic planets** are situated in the **third house**, one will be deprived of siblings and relatives. If **benefics** are there, the house provides full gains, and the person becomes beloved, known for their fame and renown. ॥ 3 ॥

**Malefic planets** located in the **fourth house** <note>the house of the home and the mother.</note> bring great suffering to the mother during one's childhood. But if **benefics** are positioned there, they act as the givers of royal honors.

All **auspicious planets** in the **fifth house** are the cause of obtaining children and sons. **Malefic planets** in this house cause the death of one's offspring. 
<margin>Rama</margin> <note>This marginal note "Rama" is a common sacred invocation written by scribes for protection or as a blessing on the page.</note>
<insert>Venus, sons, and relatives in the third...</insert> <note>original: *Sukra putra bandhavastrite*. This appears to be a later addition or a correction clarifying the influence of Venus on family members.</note> ॥ 5 ॥

**Malefics** in the **sixth house** make a man deprived of his wealth and property. **Benefics** there bring constant trouble and disease; furthermore, the **Moon** in the sixth house is a giver of death. ॥ 6 ॥

<summary>This page outlines the astrological consequences of planets occupying the first six houses of a horoscope, contrasting the negative effects of "cruel" planets (malefics) with the positive results of "gentle" planets (benefics).</summary>

--- Page 5 ---
<meta>This page continues the instructional text on the "Twelve Houses" (Bhavas) of a horoscope, picking up from the description of the fifth house and concluding the manuscript with the twelfth house and a final benediction.</meta>

In the **seventh house** <note>original: *Bharya-saptame*, literally the "spouse" house, which governs marriage and partnerships.</note>, **malefic planets** <note>original: *krura*, meaning "cruel" or "harsh" planets like Mars or Saturn.</note> cause strife, whereas **benefic planets** <note>original: *saumya*, meaning "gentle" planets like Mercury or the Moon.</note> make one beloved by all people. If **Jupiter** and **Venus** <note>original: *Guru* and *Shukra*.</note> are present there, the spouse is endowed with great beauty and charm. ॥ 7 ॥

If all planets are situated in a position of weapons or great wickedness <note>referring to the eighth house, which deals with accidents and longevity.</note>, it is highly dangerous. The **Moon** <note>original: *Shashin*.</note> in particular, if present there at the time of birth, is a giver of death. When **malefic planets** are situated in the house of the Lord of Death <note>original: *Yame*, another name for the eighth house, ruled by Yama.</note>, they make a person a sinner.

Those established in a position of good character and conduct <note>referring to the ninth house of Dharma or religion.</note> perform meritorious and auspicious deeds. ॥ 9 ॥

In the **house of action** <note>original: *Karma-sthana*, the tenth house, governing career and social status.</note>, **malefic planets** bring about poverty and leave one deprived of the father's fortune. In contrast, **benefic planets** in that same house make a man well-established in his profession and very humble. ॥ 10 ॥

All planets in the **house of gains** <note>original: *Labha-sthana*, the eleventh house.</note> provide the result of gains from the ruler. **Benefic planets** here constantly provide a man with elephants and horses <note>historical symbols of high status, power, and transport.</note>. ॥ 11 ॥

Planets located in the **house of expenditure** <note>original: *Vyaya-sthana*, the twelfth house, often associated with loss and the end of life.</note> make a person ill-behaved, sinful, penniless, cruel, and very wicked.

३ Thus ends the "Twelve Houses of the Illustrious Birth-Horoscope." May there be auspiciousness! ॥
