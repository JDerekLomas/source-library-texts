# TRANSLATOR.

JUST as the prefaces of translated works usually serve to impress upon the readers the idea that the work, upon which one has bestowed the effort of translating, is both useful and rare, and that the genius of the Author well deserved to be revealed to others beyond his own countrymen; so I see no necessity why I should dwell long on saying much of either. The utility of chronology <note>The translator uses the Dutch term *tydkunde*, literally "time-knowledge," referring to the science of arranging historical events in their proper order.</note>, for the proper understanding of ancient and modern histories, is so well known to all book-lovers that it would be like lighting a candle at high noon if I wished to demonstrate it with words.
