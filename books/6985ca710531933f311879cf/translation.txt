That God added the revelation of His essence and will, which we have in the Holy Scripture, to the work of Christ’s redemption, is an invaluable benefit. For it does not merely place the fundamental truths—which we already recognize in part from the light of nature <note>The "light of nature" (Latin: *lumen naturae*) is a theological and philosophical term referring to knowledge of God and morality that can be gained through human reason and observation of the natural world, independent of the Bible.</note> concerning the immortality of the human soul and concerning God according to His essence and will, as well as His works of creation, governance, and the general Day of Judgment—into the greatest light with proper certainty; but it also places before our eyes, with the clearest conviction, the foundation and order of our spiritual and eternal salvation. And since, in the divine Word, everything depends on the truth of history and the doctrines connected with it—and thus on the credibility of the Christian religion—everything rests upon a firm and immovable foundation, and hangs together like a chain, so firmly that one truth gives birth to, confirms, and explains the next.

For as far as the biblical histories are concerned, in their extreme antiquity, they surpass all other histories of the entire human race in their credibility. When we see the Jews <note>original: "Jüden" — an older German spelling. The author is using the continued existence of the Jewish people as a "living proof" of the historical accuracy of the Old Testament, a common argument in 18th-century apologetics.</note> before our eyes even today, and are certain that they descend from Jewish ancestors, and these from their own; when we observe their customary celebration of the Sabbath, and consider how this has continued in an uninterrupted order back through all the hundreds and thousands of years; then we have such a historical chain, whose links are most firmly joined together [in...] <note>The text ends mid-sentence as the author prepares to continue the metaphor of the historical chain on the following page.</note>

<summary>This opening of the preface argues that the Bible is a necessary gift from God that clarifies natural reason and provides a firm, chain-like historical foundation for the Christian faith. The author cites the continuity of the Jewish people and the Sabbath as physical evidence of the Bible's historical reliability.</summary>

reach in a straight line from our times back to the creation. Thus, one should not view superficially that which happened during and after the destruction of the city of Jerusalem and its excellent Temple, according to the just and wise providence of GOD. Specifically, the great Roman general and later emperor, **Titus Vespasian**, had the holy vessels, the golden table of showbread, the seven-branched golden lampstand, as well as the book of the law with two trumpets, taken away from the Temple while it was already in flames. And when he made his entry into Rome with a most prestigious triumph alongside his father, **Titus Flavius Vespasian**—who before him had led the war against the Jewish people as commanding general—he had these items carried for public display, according to the credible testimony of **Josephus** in *The Jewish War*, Book 7, chapter 24 <note>original: "Josephi vom Jüd. Kr. L. VII. c. 24." refers to Flavius Josephus's *De Bello Judaico*.</note>, after they had been figuratively engraved onto a magnificent triumphal arch erected for that purpose <note>The author is referring to the Arch of Titus in Rome, which still exists today and features reliefs showing Roman soldiers carrying the Menorah and other Temple treasures.</note>. Since this arch still stands among other antiquities in Rome to this very day, the famous **Hadrian Reland** <note>original: "Adrianus Relandus" (1676–1718), a Dutch scholar and specialist in Middle Eastern studies.</note> had sketches of it engraved in copper and explained with learned annotations in his book *On the Spoils of the Temple of Jerusalem* <note>original Latin title: "de spoliis templi Hierosolymitani"</note>, published in **Utrecht** in the year 1716.

Since we are led in this manner to the second Temple <note>The Second Temple was the Jewish holy house rebuilt after the Babylonian exile and later expanded by Herod the Great; it was destroyed by the Romans in 70 AD.</note>, and this points us back to the first, and the first to the Mosaic **Tabernacle** <term>Tabernacle: (from the German "Stiftshütte") the portable tent-sanctuary used by the Israelites during the Exodus from Egypt.</term>, and this to the times of the Patriarchs, the truth of biblical history is placed beyond all doubt in its clearest light. And what is most important here is that, at the same time, the credibility of the Messianic and Christian religion is also most clearly proven in a historical manner. For the Mosaic Tabernacle and the Temple in Jerusalem were the center of that religion; within them and the **Levitical service** <note>The religious duties and rituals performed by the tribe of Levi as prescribed in the Old Testament.</note>, everything was directed toward Christ in a symbolic way, just as we have long since possessed the fulfillment <note>original: "Gegenbild" — literally "counter-image," a theological term for the reality that fulfills a symbolic prophecy or "type."</note> of these things in Christ and Christianity according to the New Covenant. Therefore, biblical history—in regard to the true religion contained within it—is like a precious garment in which gold is so richly woven that it constitutes the most distinguished part of the whole.
