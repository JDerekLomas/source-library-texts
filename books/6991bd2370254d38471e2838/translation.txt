When we remember that many English attempts to interpret Hindu philosophies have been made by men who lived in India as Christian missionaries, we can see how likely these attempts are to be inaccurate. A paid attorney, zealous for his client’s interest and filled with a one-sided view of the case, is not very likely to display a purely objective and impartial mind, no matter how honest he may be.
