BY express privilege of the King our Lord, dated the eighth day of August in the year one thousand five hundred fifty-six, which has been published and registered in the court of the Seneschalty <note>A royal administrative and judicial district in Renaissance France.</note> of Lyon, permission has been granted to Macé Bonhomme, printer of Lyon, to print and cause to be printed with such characters <note>In this context, "characters" refers to the specific typefaces or fonts used by the printer.</note> as he shall see fit, to offer for sale, and to distribute the present book, titled, *the mirror of alchemy by Roger Bacon, most excellent philosopher*. Wherefore, all booksellers, printers, and others are forbidden from printing or causing to be printed the said book in whatever form or manner it may be, or from importing or exposing for sale in the lands and territories of this Kingdom those [copies] that may have been counterfeited or printed elsewhere during the time and term of ten years, beginning from the day that the printing of the present book shall be completed, under heavy penalties against those who contravene directly or indirectly the said privilege.
