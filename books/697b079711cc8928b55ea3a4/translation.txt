# Greetings to the Kind Reader <note>original: "B. L. S." for *Benevolo Lectori Salutem*; a standard Latin epistolary greeting</note>

It was my intention to address you at greater length here, *Friend Reader*. But a certain affliction of health has interrupted my purpose: hence I have been forced to make a brief summary of my words. If you are fair-minded, I shall have no need for an apology; if not, even an apology will achieve nothing. I present to you certain scholarly reflections <term>lucubrations; scholarly work produced by intense study, traditionally by lamplight at night</term> on several Biblical passages, not all of which were developed with the same method or (for why should I deny it?) the same diligence. 

For although for sixteen years now I have embraced a more sacred Philology <term>Philology; the study of language and literature; here specifically the linguistic study of the Bible's original languages</term> with an earnest affection, because I hoped for no negligible assistance toward sacred Theology, to which I have been consecrated from a boy: yet, as the current state of my affairs and fate stands, where my first and last care is almost entirely about earning a living, it is not permitted to always live as I wish. 

But if **GOD** grants a kinder fortune and allows me to struggle out of these miseries, you shall one day expect more solid works, more strictly thought out and in every respect more complete.
