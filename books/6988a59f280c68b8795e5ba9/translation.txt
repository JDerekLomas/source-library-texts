# THE PRINTER

Friend READER,

My plan being to show you the decoration of a **CHEMICAL THEATER**, upon which appear as Actors only persons of irreproachable Authority in the knowledge of the Wonders of **TYPOGRAPHY**—or the printing of signatures <note>In 17th-century printing, "signatures" were letters or numbers at the bottom of pages used to guide the binder in folding the sheets correctly.</note>—and of the *HARMONY* or universal balanced temperament of all the most curious things that are in Nature, especially concerning the preparation of the great **ELIXIR** or universal **MEDICINE** of the **HERMETIC Philosophers** <note>Followers of Hermes Trismegistus, referring here to alchemists seeking the "Philosopher's Stone" or a universal cure.</note>.

I intend to find the arrangement for this undertaking within my own Mark or harmonic and Typographic **ESCUTCHEON** <note>A printer's "mark" or device was a decorative emblem used as a trademark on the title page.</note>, from which I have drawn the design of a small figure that I placed on the frontispiece of this **BOOK** in favor of the **SEVEN** Authors noted therein, and particularly of **BASIL VALENTINE** and **HERMES Trismegistus**, one of whom is from the West and the other from the East, and who I hope shall be the two principal Guardians of this aforementioned Mark or **ESCUTCHEON**.

The decoration of this **THEATER** will consist firstly of **SEVEN** varied Columns, each with the Color and particular circumstances of the harmonic Relations that can be observed in the Blazon <note>Heraldic description or symbolic coat of arms.</note> of each of the **SEVEN** Metals.
