# EPISTLE

->The greatest Glory of the HOUSE OF ANHALT<-

As I considered whether, in these most wretched Times of ours—in which almost nothing is usually safe from the most insolent slanders of an envious Tongue—I should release into the light this, my **CHEMICAL ROYALTY** <term>Basilica Chymica</term><note>The title translates to "Royal Chemical Pharmacy." This work was one of the most influential texts of iatrochemistry, a branch of medicine that sought chemical solutions to diseases, heavily influenced by the teachings of Paracelsus.</note>, which was gathered through various and lengthy travels, tireless labors and night-watches, at no small expense, and finally by long observation and meditative study, and written several years ago; and whether I should venture upon the perilous sea of public opinion: various obstacles met me as I hesitated at the crossroads of this uncertain judgment. For those who, with right reason, devote themselves entirely in this most unhappy age of ours to the studies of true Wisdom...

<summary>In this opening of his dedicatory epistle, Oswald Croll expresses his hesitation to publish his long-researched medical work due to the harsh criticism and envy prevalent in his time. He emphasizes the immense personal effort, travel, and expense he invested in compiling the "Basilica Chymica."</summary>
