<term>Half-binding</term> <note>A method of bookbinding where the spine and corners are covered in leather for durability, while the rest of the cover is finished with paper or cloth.</note>

<term>Marbled paper</term> <note>Decorative paper produced by floating pigments on a liquid surface and transferring the pattern to paper; the "snail" or "shell" pattern seen here was popular from the 17th century onward.</note>

<term>Gold-tooling</term> <note>The process of pressing heated metal tools into leather through gold leaf to create decorative designs or titles.</note>

<term>Spine label</term> <note>A thin piece of contrasting leather glued to the spine to display the book's title and author.</note>
