## TO THE READER.

<image-desc>Historiated ornamental initial 'N' depicting two figures in a landscape with trees.</image-desc>In our age—which is as unjust as it is polluted by unspeakable crimes—it is no wonder that all those heresies, which were long ago laid to rest and condemned by the Catholic Church (in which alone the writings of the New and Old Testaments are held in high esteem), are now rightfully springing up again. For almost everyone is now permitted to propose to others whatever they have dreamed of by night or pondered by day, led by Satanic suggestions. And what is amazing is that these heresies always find followers and supporters who are so bold, so frivolous, and so shameless that they dare to thrust whatever comes to their lips as if it were a Divine oracle upon the simple-minded, who are easily led astray.

The cautious are deceived and beautifully persuaded by the charm of words, so that they think everything these men babble was brought forth as if from the <term>tripod of Apollo<gloss>An ancient Greek reference to the Pythia, the priestess at Delphi who sat on a tripod to deliver oracles; here used to mean a supposedly infallible pronouncement.</gloss></term>, or spoken by **CHRIST** himself, the Son of God. Therefore, we are not warned in vain by **CHRIST** himself not to believe all those who say: "Behold, here is **CHRIST**, or behold, there he is." They are seduced, and they seduce others, so that they do not seem to wander in error alone. 

Yet this is the consolation of the Church: that God always raises up some from among the elect who may oppose themselves to these impious seducers, refute the false dogmas being scattered about, and bring forth the Truth from the <note>original: "panoplia"</note> full armor of the divine Scriptures, so that no escape remains for the false prophets to either respond or defend their own views. Professors of Theology ought to diligently drag this troop of impious men out of their hiding places through sound doctrine. 

Did not the Satanic offspring of <note>Arius (c. 256–336) was a priest whose teachings—denying that Jesus was of the same substance as God the Father—led to the first major Christian council at Nicaea.</note> **Arius** cause much trouble for the godly, only to be finally destroyed by the word of **CHRIST**? Did not <note>Paul of Samosata was a 3rd-century bishop condemned for his views on the nature of Christ.</note> **Samosatenus** perish by his own knife? And were not also the <term>Anabaptists<gloss>A radical wing of the Reformation; the author groups them here with ancient heretics.</gloss></term>, **Storch**, <note>Niklaus Storch (d. 1525), a leader of the "Zwickau Prophets."</note> **Servetus**, <note>Michael Servetus (1511–1553), a Spanish scholar executed for his anti-Trinitarian views.</note> and countless others exposed to the mockery of the whole world because of the rages they devised without the authority of God’s word? The same fate befalls all heretics. 

However, when **PIETRO GALATINO** noticed the arrogance, pride, and insolence of the Jews toward the Christian religion, he considered it his duty—having made no small progress in <term>Hebrew literature<gloss>The original Latin "Hebraicis literis" refers generally to the study of the Hebrew language and Jewish texts.</gloss></term>—to finally bring something forward into the public (and indeed he set his hand to it successfully). He wrote these **Twelve Books**, filled with every kind of erudition and learning, for the benefit of the Church of **CHRIST** as it wavered amid various opinions. He wrote against that treacherous crowd which not only denies both the divinity and humanity of **CHRIST** (even though they are convicted by the Scriptures), but also overturns all the Scriptures, especially those of **Moses** and the **Prophets**. They devise false interpretations against the genuine sense of Scripture and even against the **Rabbis**, whom they hold in the highest esteem. For those ancient Rabbis described the times of **CHRIST** so clearly that, among men of sound mind, the testimonies which the **Talmudists** recorded regarding the kingdom of **CHRIST**, his passion, and Re— <note>The text cuts off mid-word, likely "Resurrection" (Resurrectione).</note>

[re]surrection. **GALATINUS**, certainly a wise man, perfectly learned in Hebrew, <term>Chaldaic<gloss>An older term for Aramaic, the language of the Babylonian Talmud and various biblical passages.</gloss></term>, Greek, and Latin letters, directs his writings toward a single goal (leaving the life of the Jews—devoted to greed, deceitful, and contaminated by various crimes—to God the judge). Nor could anyone more clearly deliver the promises made to the Fathers concerning **CHRIST**, namely to Adam, Noah, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, Moses, the Prophets, David, and countless other faithful servants of God. 

In addition, he describes the times in which the scepter was taken away from the Jews, their Judges were either overthrown or killed (whom they called the <term>Sanhedrin<gloss>The supreme council and tribunal of the Jews in ancient Israel.</gloss></term>), and they lost the use of Sacrifices. They are now without a Priesthood, without the multitude of Levites, and without a King, after the Temple was destroyed and all things were reduced to desolation. The miracles that once happened both inside the Temple and outside have vanished; and the wretched people have been cast out into every corner of the earth. These are certain signs that **CHRIST**, whom the holy Fathers expected, has come and has purged the world of sins. 

Furthermore, their own Rabbis—whom they dare not deny—described those final times with beautiful words; so that they could easily grasp the Truth, if they were not insane in their opposition to the religion of **CHRIST**. Yet this blinded nation continues with an obstinate spirit to defend things against their own conscience, to devise false interpretations, and to imagine an outward Kingdom of **CHRIST** and a most splendid procession in which their own Messiah is to enter. 

I tell you, honest reader, our Author handles all these things so skillfully in a <term>dialogue style<gloss>A literary format where information is presented through a conversation between characters; Galatino's work is written as a debate between himself, Reuchlin, and a Jewish interlocutor.</gloss></term> that you can desire nothing more in this matter. And whoever reads this book with an attentive mind will feel that great benefit has accrued to them when they wish to study the Bible; especially in obscure and little-known passages, the Author has focused all his effort so that the reader can go through the **Old Testament** without hesitation. 

Everything is divided into Chapters. Scriptural passages are noted throughout, so that the reader's mind does not waver while searching for the quoted locations. Furthermore, he brings forward whatever has been said anywhere about **CHRIST**, with interpretations not gathered from <term>the crossroads<gloss>original: "ex trivio"; meaning common or vulgar sources.</gloss></term>, but from the Jewish Rabbis who wrote about the Messiah's kingdom before **CHRIST** was born, as well as from Christian Doctors. You have here (to summarize much in a few words) a Commentary on all the difficult passages of the entire **Old Testament**. Does this not offer itself as a great treasure, which a student of Theology cannot do without except to his own greatest loss? 

Moreover, the honest reader will easily perceive, if he compares the old edition with this new one, how much diligence Master **Francis Stancarus**, most highly skilled in Hebrew literature, applied in correcting these books. <note>Francesco Stancaro (1501–1574) was an Italian theologian and Hebraist who edited this version of Galatino's work.</note> No one believes how easily even well-written copies can become corrupted. 

There has also been added the <term>Cabalistic works of Capnio<gloss>Johann Reuchlin (1455–1522), whose Greek-style pen name was "Capnio," was a pioneer of Christian Kabbalah. His "De Arte Cabalistica" is included in this volume.</gloss></term>, a fitting subject for this work, in which much mention is made of the ancient Fathers who awaited **CHRIST** the redeemer with the greatest longing, and knew best that the lost salvation was to be recovered upon the <term>wood<gloss>A traditional reference to the wood of the Cross.</gloss></term>. 

Truly, <note>original: "vino vendibili non opus est suspensa hedera"</note> "good wine needs no ivy-bush." <note>A Latin proverb meaning that a good product does not need an advertisement; an "ivy-bush" was a traditional sign for a tavern.</note> All things, however, look toward this goal: that the Church founded by the Apostles may be rebuilt, setting aside the sects and opinions which have never failed to bring in a sea of evils, and have either changed or utterly overthrown all well-established Republics. The Jews are a daily example to us, that we should not fall into the wrath of God through impenitence after the Truth has been recognized.
