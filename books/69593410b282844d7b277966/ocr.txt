The title and author information are contained within a central rectangular panel, which is surrounded by a highly detailed engraved border featuring mythological figures and Egyptian-themed symbols. 

At the top center is the figure of TYPHON, depicted as a winged, multi-breasted monster with legs that terminate in serpents; he holds a battle-axe and a torch. To his left stands OSIRIS, crowned and holding a scepter. To his right stands ISIS, holding a situla (pot) and a plant.

On the middle left, labeled HERCVLES, the hero is shown holding a club near a tree (possibly the Apple tree of the Hesperides) with a stag in the background. On the middle right, DIONYSVS reclines naked beneath a heavy cluster of grapes on a vine.

Flanking the central panel are two tall, slender obelisks resting on plinths. Both obelisks are inscribed with various pseudo-hieroglyphic symbols.

At the bottom center are three animals: a long-beaked IBIS on the left, the APIS bull in the center (bearing a crescent moon on its flank and a solar disk between its horns), and a baboon-headed CYNOCEPHALVS on the right with its hands raised in a gesture of adoration.
