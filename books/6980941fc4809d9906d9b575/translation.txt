## *To the Kind Reader*

# P R E F A C E.

I had intended never to make these three <term>Dialogues<gloss>a literary form where philosophical or theological ideas are debated between characters</gloss></term> public property, but rather to share them only here and there with good friends and those who have treated me well. For this reason, I granted several people the opportunity to copy them. However, against my wishes and expectations, they abused the very kindness they received by allowing their own friends to have copies as well. Consequently, the texts have leaked out to the general public. Through repeated copying, as is usually the case, many errors have crept into the text, and the meaning of my words has been distorted <note>original: "in sequiorem partem abierit," literally "departed into a worse part"</note>. When I realized this was causing me more trouble than benefit—especially since the work was being circulated under my name regardless—I decided it was best to serve the common good and officially publish it myself. In this way, I have chosen the lesser of two evils to protect the reputation of my good name. I did not do this, however, with the goal of gaining any personal fame for myself...
