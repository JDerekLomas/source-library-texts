### The Human-Figure Chart (Nara-chakra)

Having drawn the figure of a man, one should identify where the Sun is positioned. Starting from that constellation <note>Nakshatra</note>, one should assign three constellations to the head. || 96 ||

Assign three to the face, one to each of the two shoulders, one to each of the two arms, and one to each of the two hands. || 97 ||

Five constellations are placed in the heart, and exactly one in the navel. One constellation shall be in the private parts, and one in each of the two knees. || 98 ||

The wise should place the remaining six constellations in the feet. One counts from the constellation occupied by the Sun at birth up to the birth constellation <note>Janma Nakshatra</note> of the individual. || 99 ||

If the birth constellation falls in the head, the man becomes a wearer of the royal turban <note>a sign of high office or royalty</note>. In the face, he will be an enjoyer of sweet foods. On the shoulder, he possesses fortune and vehicles. || 100 ||
