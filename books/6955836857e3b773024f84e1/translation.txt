# GLOSSARY.

**a. I.** *possessive pronoun*, his, her, (its), their.
**A.** Regarding the changes produced by the pronoun on the initial letter following it, it is to be observed that: 1°, *a*, ‘his,’ aspirates a *tenuis* <note>original: "tenuis"; a voiceless consonant like p, t, or c</note>, *s* or *f*; 2°, *a*, ‘her,’ prefixes *h* to a vowel; and 3°, *a*, ‘their,’ prefixes *n* to a vowel, and an assimilated nasal to a media <note>original: "media"; a voiced consonant like b, d, or g</note> [m(b), ṅ(d), n(g)]; it sometimes affects *f* [192. 386. 993], and doubles *l* [1036. 4628. 29. 4670. 5103, 36. 6658], rarely *r* [393. 7003]. [There are, however, many cases of merely inadvertent omission of the mark of aspiration; *for example* after *a*, ‘his,’ *f* 70. 80. 418. 494. 576. 2628; *s* 122; *c* 85. 87. 943. 1023. 1323. 1680. 2275. 2301. 2440. 2648. 3115. 3309; *p* 302. 977. 1386. 1595; *t* 2283, 85, 92, 93. 3118]. Sometimes, in the case of *a* ‘their,’ it is thinned off into *i* before the nasal: *compare* 6685 *i ṅdochum* <note>towards them</note>. Occasionally the pronoun is omitted after prepositions ending in a vowel: *compare* 2896 *ó ’mullach* <note>from his summit</note>; 6637 *o ’meor* (but 6834 *o n-a meor* <note>from his finger</note>); 2801 *o ’chind co a chois* <note>from his head to his foot</note>; 4902 *o’athair* <note>from his father</note>; 7716 *le ’cheli* <note>with his companion/each other</note>; 2659. 7776 *re’cheli* <note>with each other</note>. — The pronoun is frequently met in compound prepositions or adverbial phrases: *compare* *i n-a agaid* <note>against him</note>, *(as) a aithle* <note>after it</note>, *im-a-cuairt* <note>around it</note>, *a dochumm* <note>towards him</note>, *dí-a deoin* <note>of his own will</note>, *im-a-sech* <note>in turn</note>, *i n-a thimcell* <note>around him</note>; see under the last element in each case.

**B.** Besides its familiar use as a possessive pronoun, the following peculiarities may be noticed:—1°. It is used pleonastically <note>redundantly, where the pronoun and the noun it refers to both appear</note>; 134 *im a chend in crochda* <note>around the head of the crucified one</note>; 857 *for a gualaind na mná* <note>on the shoulder of the woman</note>; 1037 *a n-anmunda in morfesir út* <note>the names of those seven men</note>; 1945. 2177. 6299 *a ainm in hídail* <note>the name of the idol</note>; 5116 *a remess in tíí Iúdais* <note>the time of that Judas</note>; 6928 *a fhot in inntig-sin* <note>the length of that house</note>; 6964 *a cenel na ṅdruad* <note>the race of the druids</note>;—2°. Idiomatically in expressions of quantity, number, resemblance, etc.: *for example*, *a oenur*, ‘alone,’ ‘he (she) only,’ 871. 875. 1599. 1746. 3356 *léic he a oenur*, ‘leave him alone’; 4725 *co mbá dó a aenur fhogna*, ‘him only shalt thou serve’; 5753. 6645. 7087;—*a hoenur* 66. 4881 *in fhírinde a hoenur*, ‘Truth itself’ <note>original Latin: "ipsa Veritas"</note>;—*a ṅdís*, ‘the two of them,’ 862. 4968;—*a triúr*, ‘the three of them,’ ‘all three,’ 2004;—*a bec*, ‘a little of it,’ with a negative ‘any whit of it,’ 124 *na facbam-ne a bec do neoch do-ronsat*, ‘we will not omit any of what they did’; 256 *ni dernsat a bec dib* <note>they did not do any of them</note>; 266 *tucad a bec do’n fhuil* <note>a little of the blood was given</note>; 2622 *ni ro-pheccaig a becc*, ‘he sinned not at all’; 2908 *ni mirbuile itir a bec*, ‘it is no miracle at all, not a bit!’—[? *a chách*, ‘at all,’ 1970, 71];—119 *denum-ne a choibes 7 a macsamla* <note>let us make its equal and its like</note>;—497 *tuc a leth dam-sa* <note>give half of it to me</note>;—3091 *tria tharmcruthugud a letheti*, ‘through the transformation of its like’;—61 *a lor adartha*, ‘their fill of adoration’; 410 *cen a lor bíd* <note>without his fill of food</note>;—*a met*, ‘to such an extent,’ 83. 1121. 1590. 1906. 6192. 6589. 8342;—*a shamail*, ‘its equal,’ 7328.

**Ísu a beth 'n-a mac mna pósta**, 'certified that Jesus was a legitimate child' <note>literally: "Jesus, his being in his [status as] a son of a married woman"</note>: *compare* 1317 **atchi ní ba machtad le .i. in gabul tirimm a beth fo blath**, 'she saw a thing she deemed wonderful, namely, the dry beam being in blossom.'

**a. II.** *relative pronoun: who, which; that, that which.*—**A.** There is no independent, simple relative pronoun in the *nominative* or [verbal] *accusative* cases; the construction being in either case paratactic <note>original: "paratactic"; this refers to placing clauses side by side without a connecting relative word like "who" or "which"</note>: *compare* 40 **na nechi follsighim-sea**, 'the things which I show'; 196 **na ferta do-roine**, 'the miracles he did'; 241 **in mac ro-chroch-sat**, 'the son whom they killed'; 124 **in lucht ro-chrochsat**, 'the people who crucified'; 92 **in fer tanic**, 'the man who came'; 239 **in uóit dlegar**, 'the vow that is due,' etc. (The possible cases of an infixed relative pronoun will be considered elsewhere.)—This paratactic construction is sometimes used even when the relative pronoun is oblique <note>referring to grammatical cases other than the nominative, such as the dative or genitive</note>: *compare* 83 **a met tuc na hathise**, 'the extent to which he launched abuse'; 1121 **a mét ro-s-lín oman**, 'the extent to which terror filled them'; 4472 **is-na ferannaib-se ro-po rí foraind t'athair**, 'in the lands in which your father was king over us': *compare* 5071.—When the verb is in the 3rd *person present* or *future* of the active voice, a special form of the verb is employed, the so-called relative form ending in *-es, -as, -us, -ius; -fes, -fas, -fus*: *compare* 238² **in spirit bethaiges 7 inorchaides**, 'the Spirit that vivifies and illumines'; 462 **cech duine shirfess ni fort**, 'every man who shall ask anything of you'; 717 **cindus tócbas in génntligecht a cend**, 'how paganism lifts its head'; 799 **co nach mesa labras mo thenga**, 'so that my tongue speaks no worse'; 1041 **finnta latt cid thacras Déicc di-ar ṅdáil**, 'find out what Decius pleads regarding our meeting,' etc.—In negative relative clauses, the relative is shown by the form of the negative [**na, nach**, not **ni**]: *compare* 474 **cach oen na cretfed**, 'everyone who would not believe'; 666 **in lucht na r-dhelig marttra**, 'the people whom martyrdom did not sever'; 757 **baile na hadarthar Crist**, 'place where Christ is not honored.'

**B.** In *oblique* cases governed by prepositions, the relative pronoun is **a(n)** [for older **san**, the original **s** being preserved in connection with the prepositions **co, for, fri, iar, la, re, tre**, in the forms **cos-a, fris-a, iars-a, las-a, res-a, trias-a** (especially with this last); and in the case of **as** (= *from/out of* <note>original Latin: "ex"</note>), with the **s** intact].
