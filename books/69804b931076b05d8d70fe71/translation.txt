# Preface.

The noble, distinguished, and highly learned **Dr. Heinricus Screta of Zavorziz**, the eminent and famous city physician of Schaffhausen in Switzerland, has informed us that he possesses a manuscript from his late father, **Dr. Johannes Screta of Schotnau and Zavorziz**. This manuscript contains the **annotations of Hartmann** <note>Johannes Hartmann (1568–1631), the first professor of chemistry in Europe, who provided influential commentary on Croll’s work.</note> regarding **Crollius** <note>Oswald Croll (c. 1563–1609), a prominent alchemist and physician whose "Basilica Chymica" became a standard textbook of iatrochemistry (chemistry-based medicine).</note>, exactly as Hartmann composed them. His father had transcribed them before they were ever sent to print, and within them, the **mineral laudanum** <note>original: "Laudanum minerale"; unlike the common opium-based laudanum, this was an alchemical preparation, often containing gold or antimony, intended as a universal cure.</note>—which is corrupted and mutilated in all printed copies—appears complete and unadulterated.

He most laudably sent us the entire manuscript, in which we discovered that not only the aforementioned *Laudanum* but also several other **arcana** <note>secret, highly potent medicinal remedies, typically alchemical in nature.</note> had been partly corrupted or entirely omitted in the printed editions.
