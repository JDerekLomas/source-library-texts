When the **Lord of the Ascendant** is situated in the **Ascendant** (First House), he makes the person born free from disease, long-lived, and very powerful—perhaps even a ruler of the earth—endowed with the acquisition of land. **1**
<term>Lord of the Ascendant: In Sanskrit, *Lagnapati*. This is the planet that rules the zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of birth. It represents the self, health, and general vitality.</term>

The **Lord of the Ascendant** in the **House of Wealth** (Second House) makes a man wealthy, of vast lifespan, granted a modest position of status, honored, and devoted to performing good deeds. **2**

When the **Lord of the Ascendant** has gone to the **Third House** (House of Siblings), he makes a man surrounded by excellent kinsmen and good friends, devoted to righteousness, a charitable giver, and blessed with sons. **3**

In the **Fourth House**, the **Lord of the Ascendant** makes the person beloved by people, an excellent survivor, a devoted child to his mother and father, and one who experiences many enjoyments. **4**

The **Lord of the Ascendant** gone to the **Fifth House** makes a man with sons, a follower of true renunciation, long-lived, well-versed in beautiful songs/music, and constantly engaged in good works. **5**

In the **House of Enemies** (Sixth House), the **Lord of the Ascendant** makes one free from disease, a gainer of land, strong, handsome, wealthy, a destroyer of enemies, and established in a position of merit. **6**

When the **First Lord** is in the **Seventh House**, that man becomes virtuous in his conduct toward women; his wife also is endowed with the arrival of happiness and is of good character. **7**

When the **Lord of the Ascendant** is in the **Eighth House**, the man becomes compassionate, a servant of wealth, and long-lived. If influenced by cruel planets, he is troubled; if by gentle planets, the man becomes prosperous and bright. **8**

Gone to the **Sixth House**, the **Lord of Wealth** is intent on accumulating money; he gains land through his enemies. If the planet is in a high or auspicious state, this is true; if it is a **malefic**, the man will be devoid of wealth. **६**

When the **Lord of Wealth** has gone to the **Seventh House**, if it is a **benefic**, the man becomes famous for his charity and fortunate. His wife will be one who accumulates wealth. When the **Lord of Wealth** is situated in the **Ninth House** <note>the House of Dharma/Religion</note>, if it is a **benefic**, he attains fame through his charity. If it is a **malefic**, he follows a lowly path, is broken, and possesses a mind full of many anxieties. **९**

### The Lord of the Third House (House of Siblings)

When the **Lord of the Third House** <note>original: *sahajapati*, the lord of the house governing brothers, courage, and communication</note> is situated in the **Ascendant** <note>the First House</note>, the man becomes an eloquent speaker, yet he is lecherous, creates divisions among his own kinsmen, is devoted to service, greedy, and performs cruel deeds. **1**

When the **Lord of the Third House** goes to the **House of Wealth** <note>the Second House</note>, the man becomes a beggar, lacks wealth, is small in stature, and opposes his relatives. If a **malefic planet** is involved, these hardships occur; however, if a **benefic planet** is involved, the man becomes a lord or a person of means. **2**

When the **Lord of the Third House** is situated in the **Third House** itself, it creates a person of balanced character. If the planet is **auspicious** <note>original: *śubhe*</note>, he is devoted to good people, gods, and his gurus, and remains happy among his kinsmen. This placement brings profit to the man. **3**

When the **Lord of Brothers** <note>original: *bhrātṛpatau*</note> goes to the **Fourth House**, the man is nourished by his father, relatives, and siblings. However, he creates enmity with his mother and consumes his father's wealth. **4**

When the **Lord of the Third House** <note>original: *sahajapatau*</note> goes to the **Fifth House** <note>the House of Children</note>, the person is nourished by his siblings and becomes long-lived. His mind is solely intent on helping others. **5**

When the **Lord of the Third House** goes to the **Sixth House**, he opposes his relatives and suffers from eye diseases. While he may gain much land, he is occasionally troubled by clusters of diseases. **6**

When the **Lord of the Third House** goes to the **Seventh House**, he appears in the assemblies of men. His wife will be of excellent form, fortunate, and chaste. If a **malefic planet** is present, she may go to the house of her brother-in-law <note>original: *devaragṛhaṃ*, potentially implying remarriage or dependency</note>. **7**

The **Lord of Brothers** going to the **Eighth House** causes the death of siblings. If a **malefic planet** is involved, the man lives with a deformity or weakness in his arms. **8**

When the **Lord of the Third House** goes to the **Ninth House** <note>the House of Dharma or Fortune</note>, if a **malefic planet** is present, the man is conquered by his relatives. If a **benefic planet** is present, he is highly honored, virtuous, and possesses many siblings. **9**

In the **Tenth House**, the **Lord of the Third** makes one devoted to or supported by servants. He has excellent relatives and becomes a powerful lord or leader.

### The Lord of the Ninth House (House of Fortune and Father)

Now, when the **Lord of the Ninth House** <note>original: *navapati*, governing fortune, dharma, and the father</note> is situated in the **Ascendant** <note>the First House</note>, the person is a true son to his father, acts as a "plow" <note>original: *halo*, likely a metaphor for preparing the ground for family prosperity</note>, and makes friends. If afflicted by enemies, he quarrels with the father’s side, yet he remains famous by his father's name.

When situated in the **House of Wealth** <note>the Second House</note>, if joined by a **malefic planet** <note>original: *krūra*, "cruel" planets like Mars or Saturn</note>, he creates opposition with the father. If joined by a **benefic planet** <note>original: *shubhe*, "gentle" planets like Jupiter or Venus</note>, he is a protector of the father, is famous, and enjoys the wealth accumulated by the father. **2**

When the **Lord of the Ninth House** is among his own kinsmen <note>situated in the Third House</note>, the man becomes a "maraka" <note>a bringer of death or end</note> to his father. He is a man who quarrels with his father, though he acts as a protector of the father’s wisdom. **3**

When the **Lord of the Ninth House** goes to the **Fourth House** <note>original: *hibuka*</note>, he is devoted to his father and possesses personal pride. He is destined to receive gains from his father, performs good deeds, is melodious in speech, and is a master of treasures. **4**

When the **Lord of the Ninth House** is in the **House of Children** <note>the Fifth House</note>, he obtains good gains from the father and lives a long life. This is well-known. He is a protector of his children and his own body. **5**

### The Lord of the Fifth House (House of Progeny and Intellect)

He is an enemy to his father and brings faults upon him. If a **malefic planet** is present, these traits appear; if a **benefic planet** is present, the son is a servant who gains wealth. 

When the **Lord of the Fifth House** <note>original: *panchamapaḥ*</note> is in the **Ascendant** <note>the First House</note>, he is famous in the world and is blessed with children. This makes the man knowledgeable in songs <note>arts</note> and intent on performing good deeds.
