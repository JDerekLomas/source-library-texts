# *Hermippus Redivivus:* <note>original: "Hermippus Redivivus"; Latin for "Hermippus Restored to Life." The title refers to a legendary Roman figure who supposedly lived to age 115.</note>

->OR, THE<-

# SAGE’S TRIUMPH

->OVER<-

## OLD AGE and the GRAVE.

->WHEREIN, A<-

# METHOD

->IS<-

->Laid down for PROLONGING<-

->THE<-

## Life and Vigor of MAN.

->INCLUDING,<-

->A Commentary upon an Ancient INSCRIPTION,<-
->in which this great SECRET is revealed;<-
->supported by numerous Authorities.<-

->The Whole Interspersed<-

->With a great Variety of remarkable, and well-attested<-
->ACCOUNTS.<- <note>original: "RELATIONS"; in this context, the term refers to narrated stories or historical reports.</note>
