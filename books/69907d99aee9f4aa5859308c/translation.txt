# GEM

**Om.** In this regard, the physician <note>original: वैद्य: (vaidyaḥ). In this context, it refers to a learned scholar or a traditional doctor who understands the connection between the mind, body, and omens.</note> explains the state of perceiving dreams: when all the senses are at rest, but the mind is not at rest and remains active among objects of thought, it enters into dreams of various forms. These are of two kinds: those with **auspicious results** <note>original: इष्टफल (iṣṭaphala)</note> and those with **inauspicious results** <note>original: अनिष्टफल (aniṣṭaphala)</note>.

In general, an auspicious dream includes: crossing a river or the ocean; traveling through the sky; the rising of the sun; a blazing fire; and the sight of planets, lunar mansions, stars, or the orb of the moon. Likewise, climbing to the top of a mansion or a palace roof—seeing such things, a person may attain success. In a dream, drinking liquor, eating fat or meat, being smeared with worms or feces, being bathed in blood, or eating yogurt while dining—even if these seem unpleasant, seeing oneself adorned with clothes, ointments, jewels, and ornaments in a dream leads to success.

One also attains a gain of wealth in a dream by seeing: deities, <term>Brahmins<note>original: विप्र (vipra); members of the priestly or scholarly class.</note></term>, twice-born men, umbrellas, <term>tapa<note>unclear: possibly referring to a sun-shade or a specific ascetic</note></term>, lotuses, or kings; women dressed in white clothes and garlands and adorned with ornaments; climbing a bull, a mountain, or a fruit-bearing tree; or seeing a mirror, a fish, a garland, or a vast heap of cooked rice. By these, one is also liberated from two [unspecified] troubles.

In general, an inauspicious dream is one such as: climbing a Kimshuka tree, an anthill, or a Paribhadra tree; seeing oil, cotton, <gloss>oil-cake</gloss> <note>original: पिण्याक (piṇyāka)</note>, or iron; performing a marriage; wearing red garlands or red clothes; being carried away by a current; or being lost in the mud or eating [raw] meat.

Furthermore, these two kinds of dreams are divided by the time they are seen. In the *Treatise on Dreams*, the dreams seen in the first <term>watch<note>original: याम (yāma); a three-hour period of the night.</note></term> ripen into results within a year. Those in the second watch ripen in eight months; those in the third watch in three months; and a dream in the fourth watch is known to give results within a month. If seen at the hour of **dawn** <note>original: अरुणोदय (aruṇodaya)</note>, the result will occur soon. If seen at the time when cows are released for grazing, the result will be immediate.

Specifically, regarding auspicious dreams: he who sees in a dream a king, an elephant, a horse, gold, a bull, or a cow, his prosperity increases. Climbing upon cows, bulls, or elephants; reaching the top of a palace, a mountain, or a tree; being smeared with feces; weeping; or even dying in a dream—these are auspicious. Similarly, "going and coming" <note>likely a euphemism for sexual union</note> in a dream is fortunate. If a person dreams they are standing on a fruit-bearing tree that is sprouting and then they wake up, they will quickly obtain wealth.

He who is bitten by a snake on the right hand in a dream will gain a thousand [pieces of gold] that very day. If a man sees a snake, a ray of light, or is swallowed by water, he will be granted victory, wealth, and a son. A man who climbs a palace or mountain and crosses the sea, even if he is born into a lowly family, will become a king. He who, in the middle of a beautiful pond, eats **ghee** <note>clarified butter</note> and rice pudding on an unbroken lotus leaf, know him to be a lord of the earth. He who wakes up hearing the voices of the Ruddy Goose, the Osprey, or the Crane, will obtain a virtuous daughter or a sweet-speaking wife. He who is bound by chains or a rope will either have a son born to him or will quickly attain wealth. If, during the first watch, the body is surrounded by water... <note>The text breaks off regarding the specific result of water in the first watch.</note>

If a person sees the all-encompassing disc of the sun <note>original: आदित्यमंडलं (ādityamaṇḍalaṃ). Specifically referring to the solar orb or mandala.</note> or the moon in a dream, one who is afflicted by disease is released from illness, and a healthy person shall obtain prosperity. If a man drinks blood or spirits in a dream, a Brahmin <note>The priestly or scholarly class in the traditional social structure.</note> will gain knowledge, while others will gain wealth. If one sees a woman dressed in white garments, adorned with pearls and scented unguents, and she embraces him in a dream, one should declare that prosperity is coming to him. 

If one wakes after dreaming of sandals, vehicles, or an umbrella, or of leaping over something... <unclear>unfavorable</unclear>... he should declare that food will be obtained. 2. He who climbs alone into a carriage yoked with oxen shall quickly obtain wealth accompanied by the brilliance of lightning. Gaining yogurt brings fame; drinking clarified butter <term>ghṛta<note>original: घृत. Ghee, used in rituals and considered a substance of purity and health.</note></term> brings the lord of prosperity and fame; and eating yogurt also brings fame. 

If a man is wrapped in intestines, whether in a city or in a house—if in a house, he becomes a local ruler <term>maulika<note>original: मौलिक. A headman or local chieftain.</note></term>, and if in a city, he becomes a sovereign king. He who sees human flesh in his dreams, or dreams of eating it while asleep, the result is the gain of a thousand [pieces of gold]; by eating a horse, one gains a kingdom, or perhaps a hundred thousand by eating a head.

All white objects seen in dreams are beautiful and auspicious, except for cotton, ashes, cooked rice, and buttermilk. Conversely, all black objects are highly condemned and ominous. If one drinks foamy milk directly while the cow is being milked—or milk from an elephant, a god, or a Brahmin—he shall see the arrival of a woman as beautiful as a wheel. The gains of yogurt and gems are three-fold: the arrival of cattle, lotus-like wealth, and Vedic study. One should know that seeing barley signifies the arrival of a person, as does the gain of mustard seeds. If one receives a betel leaf, a certain mountain-born fruit, or pale sandalwood in a dream, his prosperity will be all-encompassing.

### Now, Specifically Regarding Fruitless or Inauspicious Dreams

In this regard, the sage Shaunaka <note>A legendary scholar and sage to whom many ancient Indian texts on ritual and observation are attributed.</note> says: Now I shall speak of the omens heard regarding dreams. If a human sees the sun or the moon distorted, 1. or deformed, or falling, or sees the sky without constellations or stars, he shall encounter death and sorrow. In the *Svapnadhyaya* <note>original: स्वप्नाध्याये. Literally "The Chapter on Dreams," likely referring to a specific section of a larger work like the Agni Purana or a Brihat Samhita variant.</note> it is said: if a man sees a blossoming Ashoka tree, a Karavira <note>Oleander.</note>, or a Palasha tree <note>Butea monosperma, known for its bright orange "flame of the forest" flowers.</note> at the end of a dream, he shall encounter sorrow. 

He who climbs a boat to cross a river or the ocean—if the water is clear—one should declare the swift arrival of a person. 2. If a man dreams of a woman wearing [red] garments and red scented unguents who embraces him, one should declare his death. If the body is anointed with oil, or bathed in milk, or with fat, or any other oily substance, it indicates illness for the dreamer. If the hair of the head falls out or is scattered, or if the teeth fall out, it signifies the loss of wealth or the loss of a son. 

He who climbs upon a donkey, a camel, or a buffalo while alone—his death is being urged; he shall meet his end quickly. The loss of nails or the nose, the falling of teeth or hair, the eating of cooked meat, and riding on a donkey, camel, or buffalo—all these states signify death in a dream. 

Thus ends the Determination of the Fruits of Dreams. <note>original: इति स्वप्नफल निर्णयः (iti svapnaphala nirṇayaḥ).</note>
