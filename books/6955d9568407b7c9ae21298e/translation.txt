The portrait is housed within an ornate, occult-themed architectural frame. The top of the frame features a central medallion depicting a profile of an Egyptian figure in a striped headdress <note>This is a "Khat" or "Nemes" headdress, typical of ancient Egyptian royalty</note>, flanked by two large outspread wings. The vertical pillars of the frame are entwined with serpents <note>specifically cobras, or uraei, symbols of sovereignty and divine authority</note> and topped with bearded heads with flames rising from their crowns. The bottom corners of the frame feature two heads wearing turbans and large hoop earrings. In the center of the base pedestal is a stylized facsimile signature.</image-desc>
