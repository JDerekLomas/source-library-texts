# Transformation of Metals.

It is a reasonable question to ask whether metals can be transformed. Since such a transformation necessarily requires the destruction of one metal and the creation of another, it is first and foremost necessary to investigate how Nature assembles such metals within the bowels <note>original: "Eingeweiden der Erde"; a common early modern metaphor suggesting that minerals and metals "grew" inside the earth like organic life.</note> of the earth. Yet who would not confess their ignorance here? Or who can construct a clear system of knowledge <term>Lehrgebäude: a structured, comprehensive theoretical framework</term> regarding this matter, given the darkness in which it remains hidden? For many centuries, no one has yet emerged who could reliably demonstrate or reveal the fundamental causes of metals, even though we investigate other natural things so precisely that we reach the very "parts of particles" <note>This refers to the 17th-century interest in corpuscular theory or early atomism, where scientists attempted to understand matter by its smallest physical components.</note>.
