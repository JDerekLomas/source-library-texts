For because they do not wish to come to Christ with genuine humility in their searching of the Scriptures, they also do not attain life or the inner, secret sense of the Spirit. At most, they might grasp something of the spiritual in a **moral sense**, insofar as reason may draw lessons from Scripture aimed at life and conduct; however, as is well known, they prefer to dwell mostly in the **literal sense**. This literal sense is indeed good in itself, but it also easily gives rise to all sorts of differing opinions and interpretations, and thus to all manner of strife, unnecessary questions, and inquisitive knowledge. Poor human beings, who do not possess the spirit of faith, are so fallen that they speak of tangible, physical, and visible bodies as if they were a real and essential matter, while they imagine invisible spiritual things to be merely a figurative concept. Yet, in truth, invisible spiritual things are far more essential to **GOD** than physical, precious things can ever be. So it often goes with the interpretation of Scripture by those who wish to be masters of the Scripture: but they thereby reveal that they speak from their own self.

Furthermore, one well knows how to be modest in acknowledging that the Holy Scripture does not have a double or triple sense, but actually only one sense. This is what these people claim just as strongly—and often with great zeal, though not according to knowledge. However, it is believed that just as Wisdom is one and yet manifold <note>Wisdom of Solomon 7:22</note>, so too does the Holy Scripture—as an outflow from Wisdom and a great prophetic word—speak many things at once and with a single word. These things, in their entire summary, remain one sense, yet in various openings. Everything good that an enlightened person may draw and bring forth from the Scripture is actually intended by the Holy Spirit Himself. For from where else should a person have it, if not from this source? Is not all good from above? And if a person can find more than one thing in one place, why should **GOD** not be able or willing to place more than one thing there? The Jews recognized this well, saying in former times: there was not a single letter in the Scripture upon which entire mountains of knowledge did not hang. **GOD** often speaks something in which the literal, the spiritual, and also the prophetic understanding are all expressed together at once. Because of this, they are not three contradictory meanings, nor a double or triple understanding; rather, one always includes the other, and all together remain only one divine understanding. This is recognized as divine precisely because it knows how to express many things at once. And how should He not be able to do this, since **GOD** is equally near to all times and sees what will happen in each? He then knows how to summarize everything in one utterance in such a way that every age finds its measure of fulfillment therein, until finally everything is fulfilled, and the spoken Word of **God** is recognized in its entire greatness, breadth, height, and depth. And whoever spiritually fulfills and allows the recognized letter to be fulfilled within themselves will also easily and soon be able to look into the prophetic sense, as it shall be fulfilled in the fullness of time in the whole body of the perfect man. If, for example, the letter says one should flee from **Babylon** <note>original: "Babel"</note>: and the person, in obedience, goes out from Babylon and has no fellowship with the works of Babylon, then they will also understand the proclamation of the last times—how **GOD** will one day lead His entire Church out of Babylon and bring them to Zion. Just as in the creeds one represents **GOD** Himself as three persons, who yet in another way and in essence are not three but one: even so, three things are often expressed by **GOD** in one saying, of which He has made one the image of the other, and yet they are not three but one sense; just as a human being, although consisting of body, soul, and spirit, is not three humans, but is and remains only one human.
