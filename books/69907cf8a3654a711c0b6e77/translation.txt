### The Auspicious Time for Marriage
The stars <term>Revati</term> <note>original: "रेवत्युत्तर" (Revatyuttara) — referring to Revati and the three 'Uttara' constellations: Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha, and Uttara Bhadrapada.</note>, Rohini, Mrigashirsha, Hasta, Anuradha, and Swati are providers of good fortune. When the <term>Lagna</term> <note>The zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at the time of an event.</note> is in Gemini, a **marriage** is auspicious. The recommended months are Phalguna, Magha, Margashirsha, Jyeshtha, and <term>Madhava</term> <note>original: "माधवाः" (mādhavāḥ) — another name for the month of Vaishakha (April–May).</note>. Monday is a favored day, provided the lunar date is not an "Empty" date or the New Moon.
**1. Thus concludes the Auspicious Time for Marriage.**

### The Second Entry of the Bride
For the <term>Dviragamana</term> <note>original: "द्विरागमनं" (dvirāgamanaṃ) — The ceremonial "second entry" or return of a bride to her husband's home after the initial wedding.</note>, the stars Pushya, Punarvasu, Mrigashirsha, Anuradha, Ashwini, Hasta, Rohini, Shravana, Mula, and Dhanishta are suitable. The favorable signs are Aquarius, Aries, Scorpio, Libra, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces, and Capricorn.
**2. Thus concludes the Second Entry.**
The months of Margashirsha, Phalguna, and Vaishakha, during the <term>Shukla Paksha</term> <note>The bright, waxing half of the lunar month.</note> on an auspicious day when Jupiter and the Sun are pure, are favored for the wife’s second entry. **3.**

### Rituals for Pregnancy: Pumsavana and Simantakarana
For the <term>Pumsavana</term> <note>original: "पुंसवने" (pumsavane) — A ritual performed during pregnancy to ensure the health of the fetus (traditionally associated with the desire for a male child).</note> and the <term>Simantakarana</term> <note>original: "सीमंत कर्म" (sīmanta karma) — The "parting of the hair" ritual, performed for the well-being and mental health of the mother and unborn child.</note> in the eighth month, the stars Mula, Ashwini, Hasta, Shravana, Punarvasu, Revati, and Mrigashirsha are chosen. One should avoid "Empty" <note>original: "रिक्ता" (riktā) — certain lunar days considered unsuitable for new beginnings.</note> lunar days and ensure the moon is free of defects. Stable signs like Sagittarius, Scorpio, and Pisces are preferred for these rituals.
**4. Thus concludes the Ritual of Hair-parting and the Rite for the Fetus.**

### The Naming Ceremony
The naming of a child <note>original: "नामकरणं" (nāmakaraṇaṃ)</note> is auspicious during the constellations of Pushya, Anuradha, Mula, Magha, Jyeshtha, Dhanishta, and the three Uttaras. It should be performed on a favorable lunar day, ideally on the twelfth day after birth, or another auspicious day when the Sun, Mercury, Jupiter, and the Moon are well-positioned in signs such as Leo, Scorpio, or Aquarius.
**5. Thus concludes the Naming Ceremony.**

### Taking the Child Out for the First Time
For the <term>Nishkramana</term> <note>original: "निष्कासने" (niṣkāsane) — The first time a newborn is taken out of the house to see the sun or the world.</note>, the stars Hasta, Pushya, Punarvasu, Shravana, Anuradha, Revati, and Uttara Phalguni are used. This should occur in the third or fourth month. Signs like Virgo, Leo, and Aquarius are considered auspicious for this outing.
**6. Thus concludes the First Outing.**

### The First Feeding of Grains
The stars Revati, Hasta, Ashwini, Pushya, Punarvasu, and Rohini are used for the <term>Navanna</term> <note>original: "नवान्न" (navānna) — Also known as Annaprashana, the ritual of feeding a baby solid food (grains) for the first time.</note>. The ritual should be performed in the presence of women and elder relatives when the Moon and Sun are favorably placed. <unclear>..</unclear>
**7. Thus concludes the First Feeding of Grains.**

### The First Feeding of Grain
For the <term>Annaprashana</term> <note>The ritual where a child is first fed solid food, usually rice.</note>, the auspicious stars are those associated with the directions or specific deities: Purva-Ashadha, Purva-Phalguni, Purva-Bhadrapada, Bharani, <term>Bhujanga</term> <note>original: "भुजंग" (bhujaṃga) — The "Serpent" star, Ashlesha.</note>, and <term>Varuna</term> <note>original: "वरुणे" (varuṇe) — The star Shatabhisha, ruled by the god Varuna.</note>. Regarding the planets, the influence of Mars and Saturn should be considered. One should avoid the <term>Parva</term> <note>Full moon or new moon days.</note> days, the seventh lunar date, and the "Empty" <note>original: "रिक्ता" (riktā) — The 4th, 9th, and 14th lunar days, traditionally considered unfavorable for new ventures.</note> dates, as well as the twelfth. This ritual should be performed in the sixth month for boys and in the fifth month for girls. Favorable zodiac signs include Taurus, Virgo, and Gemini, provided Mercury is strong in the lunar half and the timing is auspicious.
**8. Thus concludes the First Feeding.**

### The Tonsure Ceremony
Regarding <term>Chudakarma</term> <note>The ritual of the child's first haircut or tonsure.</note>, the favorable stars are Revati, Ashwini, Hasta, Chitra, Swati, Jyeshtha, and the three stars of Vishnu <note>original: "विष्णत्रये" (viṣṇatraye) — Shravana, Dhanishta, and Shatabhisha.</note>, as well as Pushya and the three "Uttara" stars. The Sun, Jupiter, Venus, and Mercury should be in favorable positions during the bright half of the month. The signs of Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn, and Aquarius are beneficial, but one must avoid the "Empty" lunar dates. It is highly auspicious if performed during the child's birth month or under a favorable lunar alignment.
**9. Thus concludes the Tonsure Ceremony.**

### The Commencement of Education
For <term>Vidyarambha</term> <note>The formal beginning of a child's education, usually involving the writing of the first alphabet.</note>, the auspicious stars are Revati, the five stars of the Mrigashirsha group, Shravana, the "Purva" stars, the three stars of Hasta, and Mula. This should be done when Jupiter, Venus, and the Sun are in favorable signs such as Sagittarius or Pisces. One should avoid the fifth and sixth lunar positions. When the Sun and Jupiter are pure and the "Empty" dates are avoided, the acquisition of knowledge is always successful.
**10. Thus concludes the Beginning of Education.**

### The Sacred Thread Ceremony
For the <term>Vratopanayana</term> <note>The initiation ceremony where a boy receives the sacred thread and begins his formal Vedic studies.</note>, the following stars are recommended: Purva-Ashadha, the three stars of Hasta, Mrigashirsha, Revati, Jyeshtha, and Pushya. The Sun should be in its northern course <note>original: "उत्तर गते भानौ" (uttara gate bhānau) — Uttarayana, the period between the winter and summer solstices.</note> during the bright half of the lunar month. Favorable signs are Taurus, Pisces, Virgo, and Sagittarius. Venus, the Sun, and Jupiter should be well-placed. This vow is prescribed for the second, fifth, eighth, and tenth lunar dates.
**11. Thus concludes the Sacred Thread Ceremony.**

### The Piercing of the Ears
For <term>Karnavedha</term> <note>The ritual of piercing the ears.</note>, the favorable stars are Rohini, the Uttara stars, Mula, Anuradha, Mrigashirsha, the three stars of Vishnu, Ashwini, Revati, and the two stars of Punarvasu. It is auspicious when the zodiac signs of Pisces, Virgo, or Aquarius are rising and Mercury is strong. This ritual should avoid days that suggest "widowhood yoga" <note>Special astrological alignments thought to bring misfortune to the household.</note>. The Moon and Venus should be in favorable positions <unclear>Vishnu</unclear> during the proper months.
**12. Thus concludes the Ear Piercing.**

### The Piercing of the Ears
Regarding <term>Karnavedha</term> <note>The ritual of piercing a child's ears, usually performed for health and decorative purposes.</note>, the auspicious lunar dates are the second and the sixth. The favorable <term>Nakshatras</term> <note>Lunar mansions or star clusters used to track the moon's position.</note> are Rohini, the three stars of <term>Shravana</term> <note>original: "श्रवण त्रये" (śravaṇa traye) — Shravana, Dhanishta, and Shatabhisha.</note>, Punarvasu, the three stars of <term>Hasta</term> <note>original: "हस्त त्रये" (hasta traye) — Hasta, Chitra, and Swati.</note>, Mula, and the three "Uttara" stars <note>Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha, and Uttara Bhadrapada.</note>. One should also include Anuradha and Mrigashirsha. These rites are favorable on all days except for Tuesday and Sunday.
**15. Thus concludes the Piercing of the Ears.**

### Architecture and Construction
For <term>Vastu-karma</term> <note>Rituals related to the construction of a building or dwelling.</note>, the favorable zodiac signs are Leo, Taurus, Virgo, and Gemini. These works should be undertaken during the months of Shravana, Kartika, Margashirsha, or Phalguni.
**15. Thus concludes the Rituals of Construction.**

### Entering the New Home
For <term>Griha-pravesha</term> <note>The house-warming ceremony performed when first occupying a new home.</note>, one should avoid the day of Mars (Tuesday). The entry is auspicious when the Sun is in a favorable position. The best zodiac signs for this are Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, and Aquarius. It is highly recommended that Venus be well-placed. For merchants, the months previously mentioned for construction are also suitable for entering the home.
**15. Thus concludes the Entry into the Home.**

### Consecration of Reservoirs and Orchards
When the Sun has moved into the northern transit <note>The Uttarayana period, when the sun moves northward in the sky, considered spiritually auspicious.</note>, and under the influence of Mercury, Jupiter, or Venus, one should perform the <term>Pratishtha</term> <note>The formal consecration or "establishment" of a site.</note> for <term>Tataga</term> (ponds/reservoirs) and <term>Arāma</term> (groves or gardens). The constellations of Rohini, the three Uttaras, and Revati are best for this purpose. 

### Consecration of a Deity
The ritual for <term>Deva-Pratishtha</term> <note>The ritual of "bringing life" to a divine image or temple.</note> should avoid the <term>Kuhu</term> <note>The New Moon day.</note>, the "Empty" dates (4th, 9th, 14th), and the 8th, 9th, and 12th lunar dates.
**16. Thus concludes the Consecration of the Deity.**

### Buying and Selling
For <term>Kriya-Vikriya</term> <note>Commercial transactions, buying and selling.</note>, the favorable stars are Mula, Ashwini, the three Hastas, the three Uttaras, and Shravana. Also, the stars of Pushya, Swati, and Anuradha are beneficial. When Mercury and Venus are in an auspicious alignment, commerce yields good results.
**17. Thus concludes the Rites of Buying and Selling.**

### Undertaking a Journey
For travel (<term>Gamana</term>), the stars Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, the three Uttaras, Krittika, Bharani, and Magha are generally avoided. Favorable stars include Pushya, Hasta, Punarvasu, the three Uttaras, Anuradha, and Revati. One should avoid traveling on the 6th and 12th lunar dates, as well as the "Empty" dates and the <term>Parva</term> <note>Full and New moon days.</note>. For the signs of Libra and Gemini, the timing is particularly beautiful. 
**18. Thus concludes the Rules for Travel.**

### Forbidden Directions and Times
One should not travel toward the East on Saturdays or Mondays. Travel to the South is forbidden on certain days <note>The text refers to "Yamyam" or the direction of Yama, the god of death.</note>. Avoid the direction of the West on the days associated with Mercury.
**19.**

### Forbidden Planetary Combinations
The following <term>Yogas</term> <note>Specific angular relationships between the Sun and Moon that create a particular "flavor" or quality of time.</note> are considered malefic and should be avoided for all auspicious ceremonies and "mangala" (blessed) works:

*   **Vishkumbha** (The Poison Jar)
*   **Vaidhriti** (Poor Support)
*   **Vajra** (The Thunderbolt)
One must also avoid the time of <term>Bhadrā</term> <note>A specific period during certain lunar dates considered unlucky for new ventures.</note>.
**22. Thus concludes the section on the Vajra Yoga and the Four Mandalas for Travel.**
