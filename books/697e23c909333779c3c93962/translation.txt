->OF<-
# The Evangelical Churches
### Of the VALLEYS of
# PIEDMONT <note>original: "Piemont"</note>.

->With a most unvarnished <note>original: "naked"</note> and precise <note>original: "punctual"</note> Relation of the late<-
->**BLOODY MASSACRE**, 1655. And a Narrative of<-

->1658.<-

->All which are validated <note>original: "justified"</note>, partly by various *Ancient Manuscripts*<-
