## Usage Guidelines

Google is proud to work in partnership with libraries to digitize public domain material and make it accessible to a broad audience. Public domain books belong to the public, and we are merely their custodians. Nevertheless, this work is costly. To continue providing this resource, we have taken steps to prevent abuse by commercial parties. This includes technical restrictions on automated queries.

We ask that you comply with the following guidelines:

+ *Use of the files for non-commercial purposes* We designed Google Book Search for end users and want you to use these files only for personal, non-commercial purposes.

+ *No automated queries* Do not send automated queries of any kind to the Google system. If you are conducting research on machine translation, optical character recognition <note>OCR</note>, or other areas where access to text in large quantities is useful, please contact us. We encourage the use of public domain material for these purposes and may be able to help you.

+ *Retention of Google branding elements* The Google "watermark" that you find in every file is important for providing information about this project and helps users find further material via Google Book Search. Please do not remove the watermark.

+ *Stay within the law* Regardless of your intended use, you must be aware of your responsibility to ensure that your use is legal. Do not assume that a book that we believe to be in the public domain for users in the USA is also in the public domain for users in other countries. Whether a book is still subject to copyright varies from country to country. We cannot provide advice on whether a specific use of a specific book is legally permitted. Do not assume that the appearance of a book in Google Book Search means it can be used in any form and anywhere in the world. A copyright violation can have serious consequences.
