# The index of all questions is to be printed hereafter.

1. **W**hy God has punished the Bohemians so, and what the cause may be?
2. Whether the Bohemians also committed *high treason* <note>original Latin: *Crimen læsæ Majestatis*</note>, and whether they had the power to make use of their liberties.
3. Whether it was a matter of *territory* <note>original: *Region*</note> or rather a matter of *religion* for the Bohemians.
4. Whether the Bohemians initially recruited their soldiers against the Emperor, whether they are so bloodthirsty, and with what sort of conscience they took up *defense* <note>original: *Defension*</note>.
5. Whether the Bohemians, with a good conscience in *political matters* <note>original: *Politice*</note>, rejected **FERDINAND** <note>Ferdinand II, Holy Roman Emperor</note> and elected **FREDERICK** the *Elector* <note>Frederick V of the Palatinate, the "Winter King"</note> as King.
6. Whether Elector Frederick, Count Palatine of the Rhine, is still King in Bohemia and is to be considered as such by those of understanding?
7. Whether the *Union* <note>The Protestant Union, a coalition of Protestant German states</note> is from God, where its foundation lies in Scripture, and what is to be thought of it altogether?
8. Whether, according to an outward political conscience, one should *respect* <note>original: *respicieren*</note> authority and help persecute fellow believers against one's inner conscience.
9. Whether the Bohemians have then become *Calvinist*?
10. Whether the Bohemians alone have sinned, and what reward those shall receive who sided with the *Papists* <note>A derogatory term for Roman Catholics</note>?
11. Whether and how the Bohemians and the Kingdom can be compared with *Jerusalem*, and how they are likewise compared through divine wisdom?
12. How things have fared and will fare for *Jerusalem*, and how they will later fare for other nations?
13. How it will be with *religion* in the future.
14. How it stands with the Roman Eagle <note>The heraldic symbol of the Holy Roman Empire</note> that is now with the House of Austria <note>The Habsburgs</note>, and otherwise with the House of Austria according to the secret wisdom of God?
15. Who then has triumphed in the Bohemian matter: the Emperor or the Bohemians, according to a secret understanding?
16. Whether—to speak of it *theologically* and *plainly* <note>original: *simpliciter*</note>—the Bohemians are in the wrong because they defended themselves against authority, and whether a Christian should also wage war?
17. Whether Doctor Hoe <note>Matthias Hoe von Hoenegg, a prominent Lutheran court preacher who controversially supported the Catholic Emperor against the Bohemian Protestants</note> did wrong in becoming a *Count Palatine* <note>original: *Comes Palatinus*; a title of high legal and social standing</note>?
