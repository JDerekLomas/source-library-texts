## TO THE
# R E A D E R.

Behold here a small work, which is particularly suited for such readers who, being unable to spare much time, are nevertheless filled with a noble desire to enrich their minds with useful knowledge. These people seek, with good reason, short guides to assist them in this aim. To occupy themselves in their spare hours in a pleasant as well as useful manner, they must have small works; their time is too precious to apply themselves to folios <term>Folios: Large-format books, often very thick and heavy, which were typically used for scholarly or legal reference rather than casual reading.</term>, the very sight of which daunts them.

Meanwhile, experience teaches that striking pieces from History, and especially from National History <note>original: "Vaderlandsche Historien" — refers specifically to the history of the Netherlands.</note>, are read and heard with eagerness by all people; and seldom or never does one find among respectable folk such an insensitive ignoramus whose sluggishness is not awakened by the recounting of some principal traits of our ancestors. The benefits yielded by contemplating extraordinary characters, whether good or evil, are great. One could enumerate them; but because this is a recognized truth to anyone who thinks with any degree of sense, we shall not dwell on that for now. — Our countrymen have always lacked a work such as this, namely one that taught them to know our ancestors without effort; and which
