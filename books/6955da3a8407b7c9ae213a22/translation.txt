# In this book are contained
## these following heads.

1. The nature, condition, and property of mine-works <note>original: "Myne-works"; referring to the natural environment where minerals are found</note> in which minerals and metals grow; of their first seed, birth, quality, and property, and of their exhalation and inhalation <note>The theory that minerals "breathe" or release vapors within the earth</note>, and how they are supported in their growth and augmentation.

2. The origin of ores <note>original: "Oars"</note>, stones, rocks, and marbles; of their passages and clefts, their liquids, juices, energies, and operations—namely of gold, silver, copper, iron, tin, lead, quicksilver <note>mercury</note>, and of minerals.

3. A plain, literal explanation of that Universal Elixir <note>The Philosopher's Stone</note>, whereby a healthful life is prolonged, all diseases expelled, and abundance of riches obtained; with a perfect declaration of the Twelve Keys <note>A famous alchemical text attributed to Basilius Valentinus</note>.

4. A particular description of metals, whereby their several endowments are declared, and how they serve to keep man in perfect health. This includes many manuals for working with them to help man live more comfortably in outward means, and how metals and minerals in particular are brought to their highest preparation, by which it is ensured that men may live in this world without lacking necessary things.

# PREFACE

*riches*, which have a *fullness* of *everlasting joys*; I thought it my duty in *conscience* to be reconciled in a *Christian way* to my fellow members <note>meaning: fellow Christians or members of the human race</note> who are alive at present, and with those who are to *succeed* in *future ages*. I do this so that my *soul* may be at *rest*, patiently *waiting* for the *Lord's call*, and to become an *inhabitant* of that *heavenly Paradise*, and to be *matriculated* <note>original: "matriculated"; meaning: formally enrolled or registered</note> into the *book* of *life*, and *stand* in *readiness day and night* to *look for my Lord's coming*. 

At the *consideration* of this, I call to *mind* my *writings*, which I *set down* on *paper* just as other *ancient philosophers* have *done* before me. I am *publishing all such mysteries* of *nature* so that *artists* <note>original: "Artists"; in this context, practitioners of alchemy or the "Hermetic art"</note> and those who bear an *affection* and *love* for *such mystical truths* may be *benefited*. I *lovingly* and *readily leave* these truths to them, as *much* as the *highest* <term>Spagyric</term> <note>Spagyric: a term coined by Paracelsus referring to the production of herbal medicines or alchemical preparations through the process of separation and recombination (from the Greek words for "to draw out" and "to bind")</note> and *heavenly Physician* has *granted* and *revealed unto me*. 

*My conscience further* has *pressed me*, in the *pursuit* of a *Christian love* and the *performance* of my *promise dictated* by *nature*, to make a *larger account*. This is *because* it is *fitting* and *necessary* to *set forth* the *manuals* which *belong to this work*. These *ought* to be *described in detail* to *fully inform* the *judgment* of *men who are devoted to this study*. *Namely*, I will show *how nature's bolts*—which she has *pushed forward* in *various ways* to *strongly lock* her *secrets*—are to be *pushed back*. In this way, the *doors* of *worldly treasures* might be *unlocked*, the *knowledge* of *transcendent mysteries* may be *attained*, and—upon *serious, continued prayers* *unto* the *Creator*—*men's judgment* and *understanding* might be the *more* *encouraged* <note>original: "egg'd on"</note>.
