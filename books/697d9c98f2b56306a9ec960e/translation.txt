# N o t i c e .

---

The price for one volume of this journal, which consists of four parts like the current one, is four Saxon Thalers <note>A silver coin used in the Electorate of Saxony</note> or 7 Florins and 12 Kreutzers in Imperial Currency <note>Reichswährung; the standard currency system of the Holy Roman Empire</note>. Since individual parts cannot be sold separately, the full amount must be paid immediately upon receipt of the first issue.

In this regard, one should apply either directly to the editor via postage-paid letters, to any reputable bookstore in Germany, or to the honorable <note>original: "Löbl."/Löblich; a formal, respectful address for institutions</note> post offices and newspaper distribution offices, to whom the customary commissions are granted.

Macklot’s Court Bookstore in Karlsruhe also accepts orders for this publication.
