## TO THE READER.

In these final times, most honest reader, matters have reached such a point that mortals have almost entirely abandoned the light and dwell in darkness. If I say that this happens for various reasons, no one experienced in the world’s affairs (unless my judgment is entirely mistaken) will protest against me.
