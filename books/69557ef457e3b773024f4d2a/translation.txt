it sends; where else should this little work turn, if not to **YOU, AUGUST CAESAR**? <note>original: "Auguste Caesar" – a title used here for the Holy Roman Emperor Charles VI to evoke the prestige of the Roman Empire.</note> For it is yours by right of ownership, being the offspring of your servant. It is yours by origin, because under the auspices of your illustrious ancestors, my music took its beginning and drew its growth. You, exalted Monarch, have given me courage; you have supplied the expenses; and by the most merciful nod of your kindness, it now comes forth into the light. Therefore, whatever benefit the studious youth shall take from it, the whole must be credited to **YOUR MAJESTY**. For this reason, you will not disdain to look with kind eyes upon that which is yours in every respect. You have approved the labors of my past life with such merciful assent—and how great a task it is to satisfy your exquisite and select taste—that I have no doubt you will embrace this, nearly my last work, like a swan song, with that same kindness. Relying on this hope, prostrate at your sacred feet, I remain until death
