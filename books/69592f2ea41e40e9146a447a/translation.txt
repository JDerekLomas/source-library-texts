# CHIROMANCY

It is clear how carefully Nature, the maker of all things, acted regarding the creation of the human body, for she has granted man the ability to recognize many judgments throughout the nobler parts of a human being. Regarding the other parts, it is not quite the same. For there are certain signs of the heart, the brain, and the liver in the hand of a man, because the greatest part of a human life resides in them. This is not so much the case for the eyes, ears, mouth, hands, and feet, although these seem to be made more for beauty than for necessity. For that reason, every hand always has these three lines <note>The three "natural" lines: the Life, Head, and Heart lines</note>, while many hands do not have the others. Peasants are an exception because of their continuous labor, by which the other lines are worn away. Hence, I must repeat quite often: these arts <note>Chiromancy and Astrology</note> are so joined together that they almost always require mutual support. To make this clear, let us return as if by right of recovery to Astrology, and specifically to the nature of the wandering signs—that is, the planets, as the Greeks call them. For it is they who shape not only the bodies of men, but even their characters. For Saturn makes men mature,

<margin>Distinctions of characters</margin>
provident, relaxed, greedy, men of few words, and self-pleasing. Jupiter makes them placid, kind, tranquil, sober, and elegant in speech. On the contrary, Mars makes them ferocious, cruel, and liars. The Sun makes them pious, candid, fortunate, and proud. Venus makes them incontinent, lustful, graceful, and shining with the splendor of charm. Mercury makes them clever, versatile, learned, scientific, and agile. The Moon makes them indeed sharp and bright, but unstable, idle, and lazy. This is said in general regarding the variety of characters. Now, so that you may see more clearly how far they differ among themselves and produce diverse effects, I shall add something regarding the voice. Saturn gives a slow and noisy voice. Mars gives a

<margin>Judgments of voices</margin>
shrill one. Jupiter a sonorous, resonant, and gentle one. Venus an enervated, soft, effeminate, and sweet one. The Sun and Mercury do the same. The signs <note>The Zodiac signs</note> also have their own voices. For those which emit good sounds are Virgo, Gemini, Libra, and Aquarius. Those which are mediocre are Aries, Taurus, Leo, Capricorn, and the last part of Sagittarius. The voiceless ones <note>original: "άφωνα" (aphona)</note> are Cancer, Scorpio, and Pisces. And indeed, this is concerning the voice alone. There are signs which make birth and offspring fertile, and are called "fecund."

<margin>Fecund signs. Sterile signs.</margin>
These are Cancer, Scorpio, and Pisces. Some are sterile, such as Gemini, Leo, and Capricorn. Thus, whatever human actions exist can be fitted to the signs—namely, to their promoters. Because this can hardly be denied, those who mock Astrology as a trifling rather than a divine art blaspheme all the more deeply; we shall paint them in their own proper colors in the appropriate places, but meanwhile, let us return to our purpose.

<image-desc>A woodcut illustration of a left hand, palm facing upwards, used for chiromancy. The drawing highlights specific palm lines and markings. There is a prominent life line (linea vitae) with a cross mark near its upper junction between the thumb and index finger. Along this life line are several dots. At the base of the palm, near the wrist, three short diverging lines are shown. Another line crosses the lower palm diagonally, marked with four small hash marks.</image-desc>

If a cross is found in this manner near the upper angle <note>original: "angulum supremum"; this refers to the space between the thumb and index finger where the primary lines meet</note>, proceeding from the **line of life** <note>original: "linea vitae"</note>, and there are three small lines at the lower part and two at the upper part, as shown here, it proves a woman to be lustful and immodest. If, however, these three lines are found at the end of the **line of life**, turned backward, it signifies a woman who is to be punished with the extreme penalty <note>that is, the death penalty</note> on account of a crime.
