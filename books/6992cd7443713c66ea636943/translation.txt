By striking at the gaps and pounding the emptiness <note>original: 批亢擣虛 (pī kàng dǎo xū). In this single move, we shall lift the siege of Zhao and reap the benefit of Wei’s exhaustion. 

The King followed this counsel. In the tenth month, <term>Handan</term><note>The capital city of the State of Zhao, which was under siege by Wei.</note> surrendered to Wei. <term>Marquis Cheng</term>, Zou Ji, said, "It is better not to rescue them." 

<term>Tian Ji</term><note>The high commander of the Qi armies and a patron of Sun Bin.</note> said, "If we do not rescue them, Han will be broken and forced to submit to Wei. It is better to rescue them early."
