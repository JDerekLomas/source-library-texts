## Count de GABALIS:

### CONTAINING,

An Account of the **ROSICRUCIAN** <term>Rosicrucian: A member of a secret society (the Order of the Rosy Cross) that claimed to possess secret wisdom about nature and the spiritual world.</term> Doctrine of Spirits, namely *Sylphs, Salamanders, Gnomes*, and *Demons*; showing their various influence upon *human bodies*. <note>In this system, these spirits represented the four elements: Air (Sylphs), Fire (Salamanders), Earth (Gnomes), and Water (Demons/Undines).</note>

II. The nature and advantages of studying the *occult sciences*.

III. Why the *carnal knowledge* <note>meaning sexual relations</note> of *women* must be renounced.

IV. **ADAM**'s Fall was not caused by eating the *apple*, but by his carnal knowledge of **EVE**.

V. The rise, progress, and decay of *oracles*.

VI. A parallel between *ancient* and *modern witchcraft*.
