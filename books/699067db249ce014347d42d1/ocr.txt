# Notes of Experiments

Dec 10. 1678 to Jan 15 - subliming Sb with NH$^4$Cl - alloying Sb with Pb & other metals - (no definite result of value).

Jan 16 $\frac{79}{80}$ subliming antimonial sublimate with lead antimoniate &c

Jan 22. action of HNO$^3$ & NH$^4$Cl on Sb$^2$S$^3$ &c & further sublimations (most of these experiments are roughly quantitative)

Feb 16 $\frac{79}{80}$. fusing Sb$^2$S$^3$ with vitriol & other things - sublimation of various metals by help of Sb & NH$^4$Cl &c - action of H$^2$SO$^4$ on PbS - of HNO$^3$ on sublimate of Sb. - & other such like - (no results of value)

Aug 1682. Similar expts - some on lead ore - others on an alloy of tin & bismuth (which he seems to call Diana)

July 10 - no year - vidi <unclear>*</unclear> Philosophorum. Sublimations of calx albus with NH$^4$Cl - what calx albus is is not apparent - it seems a carbonate as it effervesces with HNO$^3$, but not CaCO$^3$ as the quantity volatilized in expt. 5 - on July 27 - is too much.

Ap. 28. 1686 - On a volatile salt of Zinc (ZnCl$^2$ apparently.) & on an <del>alloy</del> alloy derived from y$^e$ ores of Fe, Sb, Sn, Pb & Bi.

May 16. - on ve. vo. (whatever that may mean)

Mar 5. 169$\frac{0}{1}$ & Mar 16. on some bismuth comp$^d$ & action of aquafortis on alloys of Sn & Bi & Zn.

Experiments & observations Dec 1692 & Jan 169$\frac{2}{3}$ - working of barm - he says "in distilling new wine before fermentation y$^e$ flegm rises first & then y$^e$ spirit but after fermentation y$^e$ spirit rises before y$^e$ flegm."

Contains comparison of fusibility of alloys of Pb, Sn & Bi. - April 1693 & June 1693. further experiments

April 1695 - melting Sb$^2$S$^3$ with ores of Fe, Cu & Sn & Ap. 2 the resulting mass sublimed with NH$^4$Cl. Other sublimations.

Feb. 169$\frac{5}{6}$. sublimations of Sb with iron ores

No date. Action of aqua fortis on Sb$^2$S$^3$ &c.

sublimation of alloy of Sb & Pb with NH$^4$Cl & similar expts

Some of this <insert>caput mort.</insert> laid on a red hot iron flowed like
