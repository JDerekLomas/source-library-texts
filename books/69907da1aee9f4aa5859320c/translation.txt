When the **Sun** <note>original: "Ravi"</note> is situated in the **First House** <note>the house of the self or physical body; original: "Tanustha"</note>, he destroys half of one's learning. Such a person suffers from mental distress and experiences the loss of family members and successors. The physical body is constantly afflicted by imbalances of **wind and bile** <note>the Ayurvedic humors "Vata" and "Pitta"</note>. Furthermore, the individual tends to wander and experiences constant fluctuations in their prosperity. **1**

For one who has the **Sun** in the **Second House** <note>the house of wealth and family; original: "Dhana"</note>, there is an abundance of good fortune, including possession of four-legged animals and horses. However, such a person faces constant strife <note>original: "kali"</note> within the family and is often defeated by his wife in arguments. His duties toward his father or religious rites <note>original: "pitrikriyam"</note> become fruitless, even when performed for the sake of profit. **2**

When the **Jewel of the Day** <note>a poetic epithet for the Sun; original: "Aharmannir"</note> is in the **Third House** <note>the house of prowess and siblings; original: "Tritiya"</note> from the time of birth, he bestows great majesty and increases one’s valor. Such a person is always regarded with respect, travels to holy pilgrimage sites, and consistently destroys his enemies. In battle, he is seen as a leader among men. **3**

In the **Fourth House** <note>the house of home and emotions; original: "Turiya"</note>, the **Lord of the Day** <note>the Sun; original: "Dinesha"</note> grants great splendor and positions of authority. The person may acquire a house through relatives. However, he often lives as a traveler or exile <note>original: "pravasi"</note>. In times of misfortune or in the heat of battle, he may suffer a loss of honor, and his physical luster and mental peace may diminish. **4**

When the **Sun** enters the **Fifth House** <note>the house of children and intelligence; original: "Sutasthana"</note>, there is misfortune regarding the first-born child. The person’s mind tends toward wandering <note>original: "kushagramatir"</note>, and their knowledge remains limited. Though they may be eloquent or clever in speech, they are prone to negligence or error <note>original: "pramadi"</note>. There is also a risk of death or disease related to the stomach or internal organs. **5**

If the **Sun** is placed in the **Sixth House** <note>the house of enemies and debt; original: "Shastha" or "Ripustha"</note>, he causes constant expenditure due to both enemies and friends. The person's lineage or family may suffer, and there is danger from four-legged animals. During travels, such a person finds themselves lacking in resources and faces hardships. **6**

When the birth occurs with the Sun in the **Seventh House** <note>the house of marriage and partners; original: "Dyuna"</note>, the person’s beloved <note>spouse</note> suffers from distress. There will be constant worry regarding physical ailments or pain in the limbs.

# The Influence of the Moon

With a body prone to stoutness <note>original: "Medasva"</note> and extremely restless eyes, he is born to be deeply devoted to his family <note>original: "Kutumba"</note>. He is so focused on his domestic life that he is not distracted even by the sight of a celestial maiden. ->**2**<- 

When the **Moon** <note>original: "Vidhu"</note> is in the **Third House** <note>the house of valor and siblings</note>, he gains through the "sale" of his own courage, yet he may become a wanderer or an ascetic <note>original: "Tapasvi"</note>. Even if he does not seek fame, he possesses a certain inner light. The wise say the majesty of his righteousness <note>original: "Pratap"</note> will eventually lead him to victory. ->**3**<- 

In the house of **Happiness** <note>the 4th house</note>, there is a "union of kinsmen" <note>original: "Bandhu-yoga"</note>. The learned scholars declare such a person is born to be an authority over all <note>original: "Sarvadhikari"</note>. He finds joy through his circle of friends and slowly acquires wealth and a connection with many women. ->**4**<- 

When the Moon is in the **Fifth House** <note>the house of children and intellect</note>, a person finds great contentment through their children <note>original: "Santana"</note>. Their intellect remains pure and clear. They gain profit from the land and through the practice of money-lending <note>original: "Kusidena"</note>, finding success in various business ventures. ->**5**<- 

In the house of **Enemies** <note>the 6th house</note>, he shines even through conflict <note>original: "Vigrahena"</note>. He may conquer kings, yet enemies will continue to arise against him. He is often troubled by diseases of phlegm <note>original: "Kapha-vyadhayo"; one of the three humors in Ayurvedic medicine</note>. Despite his own struggles, he never wishes for his son to experience such conflict. ->**6**<- 

The **Cold-Rayed One** <note>the Moon; original: "Sheeta-rashmi"</note> in the **Seventh House** <note>the house of partnership and trade</note> makes a man wealthy through commerce and trade <note>original: "Vanijya"</note>. He finds great pleasure in the company of women and enjoys dining with friends. However, during the **dark fortnight** <note>original: "Krishna-pakshe"; the waning phase of the moon</note>, he suffers from a loss of vitality and a fear of impending misfortune. ->**7**<- 

Even if he is famous in public assemblies, if the Moon resides in the **Eighth House** <note>the house of longevity and obstacles</note>, his mind will not find peace or joy. He faces the threat of chronic diseases and has a particular fear of dangers arising from water. The Moon <note>original: "Shashi"</note> in this position provides very little benefit and often causes distress. ->**8**<-

# The Influence of Mars

If the planets are favorably inclined through the grace of a youthful spirit, the individual experiences the joy of being seen by friends. However, without such grace, one may not receive gifts of land or fine garments from the ruler <note>original: "bhumi-pattat"; referring to a king or landowner</note>. ->**4**<- 

When **Mars** <note>original: "Kuja"; the planet associated with energy and conflict</note> resides in the **Fifth House** <note>the house of progeny and intelligence; original: "Panchamo"</note>, it strengthens the digestive fire <note>original: "jatharagnim"; the internal fire responsible for digestion according to Ayurvedic tradition</note>. Yet, I know not why—indeed, I know not why—it may also act to destroy. At such a time, the intellect <note>original: "mati"</note> becomes diminished or unstable; it appears to us as milk that is constantly being consumed or changed. ->**5**<- 

If the planetary alignment is not pure, even with the strength of sacred chants <note>original: "mantra"</note> and combined power, one does not find happiness. In this state, the maternal uncle <note>original: "matulo"</note> provides no benefit to the heart’s desires. Without the proper alignment, even with the support of friends or the hand of a powerful protector, one finds no success in commerce. Peace remains elusive when **Mars** <note>original: "Medinija"; literally 'born of the earth'</note> is afflicted in the **Seventh House** <note>the house of partnership and marriage; original: "Saptame"</note>, often leading to strife between spouses <note>original: "dampati"</note>. ->**7**<- 

What use are other auspicious planets <note>original: "khacharah"</note> if the **Son of the Earth** <note>Mars; original: "Bhumisunu"</note> is placed in the **Eighth House** <note>the house of longevity and obstacles; original: "Ashtamam"</note>? Even if a friend makes a virtuous effort, or if one seeks the protection of the King’s own bow in the heavenly realm, the results may be grim. ->**8**<- 

In the forest of misfortune, a fierce Mars <note>original: "Mahogra"</note> does not bring auspiciousness <note>original: "mangalam"</note> to the house it occupies. Whether it be the first-born or a brother <note>original: "sodaro"</note>, when the results of past actions ripen in a state of turmoil, can there be any true welfare? Peace is not found among the people or through the King's middle-path if Mars has already determined a different end through its own power.

### Results of Jupiter

If the Priest of the Gods <note>Jupiter; original: "Deva-puje"</note> is in the **Ascendant** <note>original: "Lagnage"; the 1st house of self and body</note>, the person is well-dressed, happy, and possesses a divine-looking body, yet may have little physical vigor <note>original: "alpa-viryah"</note>. ->**1**<-

In the **Second House**, the mind is inclined toward poetry. Through the power of speech, one may face difficulties or a lack of enjoyment. ->**2**<-
