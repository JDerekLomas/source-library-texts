<margin>*Aristotle*</margin>
Both the ancient Greeks and Romans who preceded our age held Plato to be much more excellent than Aristotle. However, the people of our time—and especially the greater part of those who live in the West, <note>In the 15th century, "the West" refers to Catholic Latin Europe (Italy, France, etc.) as opposed to the Greek-speaking Byzantine East.</note> who are considered wiser than the rest—admire Aristotle more than Plato. And certainly, Aristotle may have been a great and incomparable man in other matters—upon which I would not dare to pass judgment so easily—but as for his opinion regarding the soul, he was of such a perverse mind that he did not fear to make it mortal and corruptible. 

<margin>Many great men prove all things universally by the authority of Aristotle.</margin>

## CHAPTER
