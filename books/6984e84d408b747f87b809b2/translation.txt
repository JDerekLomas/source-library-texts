John 1. **COME, SEE**, (Dear reader, before you judge it, come first and see it for yourself—according to the Weimar Glossary.) Therefore, dear reader, do not be quick to believe what the evil-academic <note>original: *Caco-Academischen*, a play on "cacophony" and "academic," implying scholars who produce harsh or discordant ideas</note> artists and faculty members say; come, read it yourself, though without preconceived delusions. etc.

> *Tertullian, from the book On the Veiling of Virgins, p. 490.*
> *Truth cannot be dictated to by any person: not by the passage of time, nor by the patronage of individuals, nor by the privileges of regions.* <note>original Latin: *Veritati nemo potest præscribere, non spacium temporum, non Patrocinia personarum, non privilegia regionum.*</note>

It does not make Jacob Böhme a heretic simply because such a teaching has not been heard for a long time, nor because many distinguished, perverse-learned men—**learned in word and letter, but not truly so named and not spiritually taught** <note>original Latin: *verb: & liter: non nomin. & spiritualiter docti*</note>—are against it. Nor does it matter that many provinces give, take, and use privileges, power, law, and freedom to slander Böhme, ordering people to accept common, sworn confession-formulas and statutes (as the Mark of the Beast, **Revelation 13:17**). Or that whoever does not carry this "mark" has no privilege to come to the fairs <note>referring to the great trade fairs of the era</note>, to buy or to sell, but must instead be cast under the ban as a dreamer, fool, and visionary <note>original: *Schwermer*, a derogatory term for religious enthusiasts or fanatics</note>—along with his books—by the superlative "Teacher-Fools" <note>original: *Docen-Thoren*, a pun on *Dozenten* (lecturers) and *Thoren* (fools)</note> at the "Un-versity" <note>original: *Unverstät*, a pun on *Universität* (university) and *Unverstand* (lack of understanding)</note>. These people reveal themselves as heresy-makers who seek to maintain their worldly kingdom not with truth, but with bans and force. As enemies of Christ and followers of the Antichrist-like Pharisees, they reveal themselves to all the world against their own will and show whose spirit’s children they truly are.
