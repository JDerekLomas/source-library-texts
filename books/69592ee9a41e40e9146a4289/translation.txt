# TO THE READERS.

...stition or any kind of Idolatry <note>original: "Idololatria"</note>, and that they may not be unbecoming of a wise man, a good Christian, and a faithful one; For I also am a Christian, baptized in the name of the Father, etc. which faith, with the help of God, I shall hold firmly inviolate as long as I live; Far be it from me, therefore, to learn or to write anything contrary to the Christian faith and purity, harmful to holy morals, or in any way adverse to them. I fear God and have sworn myself to His service, from whom neither living nor (as I trust) dead shall I be separated: *This small treatise I therefore commend to all the lovers of art and learning, in which I hope they will attain their desires, as much as shall be granted by God <note>original: "quantum a Deo concessi erit"</note>; so that I hope I have not cast a Pearl before the swine, but set a glass before the grateful doves.*

->12 March, 1656.<-
