CATTAN (Christofe de) La Geomance. Liure non moins plaisant & recreatif, que d'ingenieuse inuention, pour scauoir toutes choses, presentes, passées, & à aduenir, Auec la Roüe de Pythagoras. Le tout corrigé, augmenté, & mis en lumiere par Gabriel du Preau, . . . *Paris, Par Gilles Gilles, 1558*.

First edition. With a fine woodcut historiated border around the title, many woodcut diagrams in the text (including one of the 'Wheel of Pythagoras,') tables and decorative initials. Small 4to. Contemporary calf, spine and corners neatly repaired £60

A good copy of the original edition of a comprehensive treatise on astrological geomancy and prediction. A rare book, it treats of the history and practice of the art, the twelve houses of the constellations and their significance, and the manner of elucidating the figures. Caillet, I, no. 2093; Graesse, *Bib. magica*, 104; Gardner, no. 216 (where the date is incorrectly given as 1552); *B.M. Cat. of French books*, p. 96.
