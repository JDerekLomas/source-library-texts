# The Holy Kularnava Tantra

## First Chapter

---

> Seated upon the peak of Mount Kailasa, Parvati questioned the Supreme Lord, the God of Gods, the Teacher of the World, the Lord of Supreme Bliss. (1)

<note>Mount Kailasa is traditionally considered the earthly abode of Lord Shiva and a symbol of the highest state of consciousness.</note>

->The Holy Goddess said:<-

> O Blessed One, Lord of the Gods, Ordainer of the five sacred acts <note>The five acts (panchakritya) are creation, preservation, destruction, concealment, and grace.</note>, All-Knowing One, easily reached through devotion, Lover of those who seek refuge! (2)
>
> Lord of the Kula <term>Kula: the divine family or the path of Shakti</term>, Supreme Sovereign, Ocean of the nectar of compassion! In this essence-less, terrible cycle of worldly existence <term>Samsara: the continuous cycle of birth, death, and rebirth</term>, which is stained by every kind of misery, (3)
>
> Infinite multitudes of living beings, inhabiting various types of bodies, are born and die, yet no liberation is found for them. (4)
>
> Always afflicted by sorrow, O God, nowhere is happiness found. By what means, O Lord of Gods, can they be released? Tell me, my Lord. (5)

<summary>The text opens with Goddess Parvati asking Lord Shiva how living beings, trapped in the painful and repetitive cycle of birth and death, can find ultimate spiritual liberation.</summary>
