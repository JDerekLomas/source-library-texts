# Exterior Cover

->[Front Cover]<-

This manuscript is protected by a traditional half-leather binding. The spine and the corners are bound in brown leather for durability, while the front and back boards are covered in decorative **marbled paper**.

The paper features a classic "combed" or "arch" pattern—a decorative style created by floating pigments on a liquid surface and drawing a comb through them to create rhythmic, wavy lines. The palette includes muted shades of pink, tan, and light blue.

<note>Hand-marbled paper was a standard feature of high-quality bookbinding from the 17th through the 19th centuries. The "combed" pattern seen here is one of the most recognizable historical designs in the decorative arts.</note>

*Do not, I pray, despise the advice I give to you,*

## XIIX.

*Should I go further in noting the most shameful deeds*
*Of the Brother, than whom the heavy world holds nothing worse?*
*Not at all: But I am silent. It must be thought that anyone*
*Has said enough of him,*
*Who denies that he can ever say enough.* <note>The author uses a paradox here: the Rosicrucians' crimes are so numerous that one can never finish describing them; therefore, admitting defeat in describing them is the only way to truly express how wicked they are.</note>
