### Salutation and Introduction

**Salutations to the auspicious Lord Ganesha.** <note>original: "Śrī Gaṇeśāya namaḥ"; it is traditional in Sanskrit manuscripts to begin with a prayer to Ganesha, the remover of obstacles.</note>

Having bowed my head before the **Sun**, Prithuyashas—the son of Varahamihira and a man of true fame—composes this work to illuminate the deep and hidden meanings of lost objects and matters. **(1)** <note>Prithuyashas was the son of the 6th-century polymath Varahamihira, one of the most famous astronomers in Indian history.</note>

Having understood the wisdom of the Vedas and the traditional laws, just as the day is defined by the clothing of the sun's setting, so too do the planets enter their respective "travels" or "dwellings" depending on the specific time the question is asked <term>Prashna-vilagna<gloss>The "Rising Sign" or Ascendant at the exact moment a question is posed to the astrologer.</gloss></term>. **(2)**

### General Rules of Interpretation

Whichever **house** <term>Bhava<gloss>One of the twelve sectors of the zodiac representing different areas of life, such as wealth, health, or loss.</gloss></term> is aspected by or joined by its own **Lord**, or by **benefic planets**, the prosperity of that house is assured. However, for those people asking questions, the loss or destruction of that house's matters must be predicted if it is influenced by **malefic planets**. **(3)** <note>Benefic planets (Saumya) include Jupiter, Venus, and a well-placed Moon; Malefics (Papa) usually include Mars and Saturn.</note>

If a benefic sign is rising, or if the rising sign is a "head-first" sign <note>original: "Śīrṣodaya"; certain signs of the zodiac, like Leo or Virgo, are considered more auspicious because they are said to rise head-first over the horizon.</note>, or if it is **Leo**, the task will be accomplished. If the opposite conditions exist, failure is predicted. If the influences are mixed, the result will be a combination of success and failure. **(4)**

### On the Recovery of Lost Items

If the **Moon** is in the rising sign, full in body, and is aspected by **Jupiter** or **Venus**, it brings about the quick recovery of a lost object. Likewise, a strong and benefic planet situated in the house of gains ensures success. **(5)**

### Determining the Subject of the Query

If the rising sign or its division is the planet’s own, the thought concerns **minerals/metals**. If it is in a "trine" position or in its own division, the astrologer should see a concern for **minerals**. If Jupiter is placed in a division belonging to another planet, it indicates a concern for **living beings**, and if in a different division, it relates to **plants/roots**. **(6)** <note>Sanskrit astrology categorizes all objects into three groups: Dhatu (minerals/inanimate), Mula (roots/plants), and Jiva (living beings/animals/humans).</note>

The experts say that Mineral, Plant, and Living Being can be known entirely through the pairs of zodiac signs <gloss>ti</gloss>. This is summarized here according to the rising sign and its divisions.

### Signs of Stability and Arrival

The expansion of these rules reveals further distinctions. **(7)**

If the **Fixed Signs**—**Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, or Aquarius**—are the rising sign at the time of the query, there is no arrival of the person expected. <note>original: "Vṛṣa-siṃha-vṛścika-ghaṭaiḥ"; these are the four signs of the zodiac considered "fixed" or "immobile" in Vedic astrology.</note> In such a configuration, there is no loss of what was destroyed, no onset of disease or sorrow, and no gain of wealth. **(8)**

If the Ascendant is occupied by two **retrograde planets** <term>Vakra<gloss>Planets that appear to be moving backward in the sky, often signifying a reversal or delay in astrological readings.</gloss></term> but is aspected by **benefic planets**, the mind of the seeker will be as beautiful and clear as the sight itself. **(9)**

When **malefic planets** <term>Papa-grahas<gloss>Harsh planets like Mars, Saturn, or Rahu.</gloss></term> are positioned in the fifth or sixth houses, the enemy's path is diverted. If these malefics are in the fourth house, they bring about the total breaking of the enemy's forces. <gloss>10</gloss>

### Success for the Traveler

If a **benefic planet** is well-placed in the rising sign at the moment of departure, it brings auspicious results for those setting out on a journey. Even if the sign is a **Fixed Sign**, which usually suggests staying still, the presence of a benefic makes the outcome favorable. **(11)**

When a Fixed Sign is rising, a traveler or enemy will not arrive. However, if the signs are reversed <note>meaning if a Moveable Sign is rising instead of a Fixed one</note>, the outcome is also reversed, and an arrival should be predicted. **(12)**

If a Fixed Sign <term>Sthira-rashi<gloss>Signs that represent stability: Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, and Aquarius.</gloss></term> is the Ascendant, it indicates a steady mind; the enemy will turn back even if they have already traveled a very long distance to get there. **(13)**

<column-break/>

If a **Moveable Sign** <term>Chara-rashi<gloss>Signs representing motion: Aries, Cancer, Libra, and Capricorn.</gloss></term> is in the Ascendant, or a **Dual-natured Sign** <term>Dvideha<gloss>Signs that share both fixed and moveable qualities: Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, and Pisces.</gloss></term>, the person returns after having covered only half the path. <unclear>17</unclear> In the opposite case, there is no arrival; if malefic influences are present, there may be defeat. **(14)**

If any one of the planets—the **Sun**, **Saturn**, **Mercury**, **Jupiter**, or **Venus**—is in the rising sign at the time of the query, predict a quick and successful journey. However, this should not be said if the planet is in retrograde. **(15)**

### Predictions on Enemy Movements

If a Fixed Sign is rising and **Jupiter**, **Saturn**, or **Venus** are present, one should not predict an arrival because of their unfavorable positions. Malefics in the third, fifth, or sixth houses indicate a meeting with the enemy for the purpose of battle, while in the fourth house, they indicate a retreat. **(16)**

If **Mercury** is in a reversed position <note>likely meaning retrograde</note> or if the **Moon** is situated in the fourth house and is aspected by **Jupiter, Mercury, or Venus**, the person will arrive very quickly. **(17)**

If the Ascendant is **Aries, Sagittarius, Leo, or Taurus**, or if Mercury is positioned there, the enemy will retreat, whether they are currently accompanied by their forces or alone. **(18)**

If a Fixed Sign is rising and **Saturn** or **Jupiter** is located there, the enemy stays put. But if Jupiter or the Ascendant is in a Moveable Sign, then an arrival will occur. **(19)**

The most powerful planet, positioned below the Ascendant and aspecting it, determines the timing. One should predict the traveler's return in a number of months equal to the number of the sign it occupies. **(20)**

When the Lord of the seventh house <note>the house of "the other" or the enemy</note> is in the Ascendant, and is extremely retrograde, it is said by the sages that the enemy is hindered by their own internal struggles. **(21)**

When the **Moon** is waning in the Ascendant, it is like a child among fires. If there are no planets in the middle houses, there will be no arrival. **(22)**

Benefic planets—**Mercury, Jupiter, and Venus**—in the tenth, first, or seventh houses bring victory to kings. **Mars, Saturn, Mercury, Jupiter, and Venus** can either cause a breakdown of forces or grant victory depending on their strength.

### The Influence of Planetary Houses

The positioning of planets within the twelve houses of the zodiac determines the favorability of an undertaking. **(27)**

Benefic planets <term>Shubhas<gloss>Benevolent planets like Jupiter, Venus, or a waxing Moon.</gloss></term> placed in the third house from the rising sign provide auspicious results for those arriving. Malefic planets <term>Papas<gloss>Harsh planets such as Saturn, Mars, or the Sun.</gloss></term> are not desired in the tenth or twelfth houses, but they bring gain if they reside in the eleventh house. <note>original: "shubhayaṃtu"; the eleventh house is the house of 'Aya' or income/gain, where even malefics are often considered productive.</note> **(28)**

Planets situated in the sixth or twelfth houses may become providers of shelter if they are well-aspected. In such cases, the benefics retain their virtuous form, while the strength of malefics is halved when they are under the gaze of others. **(29)**

Benefics that have reached the centers <term>Kendras<gloss>The 1st, 4th, 7th, and 10th houses, which represent the pillars of a chart.</gloss></term> or are aspecting the rising sign bring affection and joy. However, if the planets are positioned in the opposite manner, they bring destruction. **(30)**

If planets are in the second or third houses, they can either grant or take away the success of a mission. In such a configuration, an army on horseback might depart, or a traveler may return—of this there is no doubt. **(31)**

### Conditions for Total Success

When benefics occupy the centers or the trines <term>Trikonas<gloss>The 1st, 5th, and 9th houses, considered the most fortunate positions.</gloss></term> and the malefics are relegated to the twelfth house, total success in all matters <note>original: "sarvārtha-siddhi"</note> is granted to the person. If the positions are reversed, the results are likewise reversed. **(32)**

Profit and gain for a man are provided by benefics, whereas malefics fail to give such fruits. Auspicious results are specifically noted when planets are in the "human signs"—**Libra, Virgo, Gemini, and Aquarius**. <note>original: "narāśayeṣu"; these signs are depicted as human figures and are thought to be particularly influential in matters regarding human affairs and communication.</note> **(33)**

If benefics occupy the tenth, seventh, or the rising sign of the year, they grant a stable position. If malefics are joined with the twelfth house, they do not provide good results. Furthermore, if the Lord of the Rising Sign or the Moon is in the tenth house, it is considered an auspicious omen. **(34)**

When the rising sign is occupied by benefics, it is said that even a person afflicted by disease will find relief and auspiciousness within seven days. **(35)**

### The Return of the Traveler

<margin>If the rising sign is a Fixed Sign, or if the planet has reached its best division <term>Vargottama<gloss>A planet that occupies the same sign in both the main birth chart and the ninth divisional chart (Navamsha), giving it exceptional strength.</gloss></term>, the traveler will return in a time equal to the number of signs passed.</margin>

A traveler’s return happens very quickly when the benefics are retrograde. **(35)** <note>original: "vakra"; while usually indicating delay, in horary astrology for a return, retrogression implies the person is 'turning back' toward home.</note> If planets are in the sixth or eighth houses, or if malefics are in the trines, the person who has gone abroad may be delayed or obstructed. **(36)**

If malefics are aspecting the sixth house, or if they are in the third house or the centers, the traveler may face obstacles. However, if aspected by benefics, even those who were lost or captured by enemies will return safely to their kin. **(37)**

The number of days until an arrival can be known by the signs occupied by the planets and the Lord of the Seventh House. If planets are retrograde, they indicate a turning back or a reversal of the journey. **(38)**

### Lost Items and Marriage

At the beginning, middle, or end of a decanate <term>Drekkana<gloss>A 10-degree division of a zodiac sign; there are three in every sign.</gloss></term>, if a benefic is in the rising sign, one can predict arrival or the recovery of property at the door of the house. **(39)**

If the full Moon is in the rising sign, or if a benefic planet is aspected by another benefic, a lost item will be recovered. Success is swift if the planet is strong and auspicious. **(40)**

In the absence of planets in the centers during the day, one should determine the result through the rising sign. The distance of the journey can be calculated through the portions <gloss>navamshas</gloss> of the rising sign. **(41)**

When Jupiter, the Sun, or Mercury aspect the Moon in the houses of children (5th) or desire (7th) from the rising sign, a marriage will take place. Benefics in the trines and centers also facilitate marriage. **(42)**
