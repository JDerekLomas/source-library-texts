->Ancient Ensigns of the Valleys<- <note>original Latin: "Antiqua Convallium Insignia"</note>

->The light shines in the darkness<- <note>original Latin: "Lux lucet in tenebris"; a reference to the Gospel of John 1:5, often used by the Waldensians to signify their role as keepers of the light of the Gospel through the Middle Ages.</note>

->Though I am burned, I am not consumed<- <note>original Latin: "Quamvis uror non comburor"; a reference to the Burning Bush of Exodus, symbolizing the Church surviving the fires of persecution.</note>
->I struggle and emerge<- <note>original Latin: "Luctor et emergo"</note>

## MEMORIAL RECORD
->*Concerning the Events of the Evangelical Churches*<-
### OF THE
## VALLEYS
### OF
# PIEDMONT,
### OTHERWISE CALLED
# WALDENSIANS.
### VOLUME ONE

->AT LEIDEN.<-
->BY JAN LE CARPENTIER, 1671.<-
->*With Privilege and Approval*<- <note>This indicates the book was published with official legal permission and ecclesiastical sanction.</note>

->The hammers are worn out, the anvil remains<- <note>original Latin: "Trivantur mallei, remanet incus"; a famous Protestant metaphor. It suggests that while many "hammers" (persecutors) strike the "anvil" (the Church/the Truth), the hammers eventually break while the anvil remains stands firm.</note>
