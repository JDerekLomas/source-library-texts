# Treatise on Armament Technology, Volume 27
## Study of Strategy: Three Kingdoms III

<page-num>Twelve</page-num>

"If his defense could even delay us for a single day or night while he waits for those he relies upon to arrive, that would be one thing. But now, as I calculate my strength and weigh my plans to attack this place, the city will surely fall in less than a day. Once the city is breached, of what benefit will his death be to his cause? And to cause his hundred-year-old mother, with her white hair <note>original: "戴白" (daibai), a literary term for the elderly.</note>, to be executed—would that not be heart-wrenching? 

I reckon that because he is cut off from outside news, he believes reinforcements can be relied upon; that is the only reason he has persisted this far. You <note>Referring to Deng Yuanzhi.</note> should go see him and lay out the choice between disaster and fortune."

In a brief moment, **Hao Pu** emerged. **Lü Meng** greeted him, took him by the hand, and they went down to the boat together. After they finished speaking, **Meng** pulled out a dispatch and showed it to him. Then, **Meng** clapped his hands and laughed loudly. When **Pu** saw the letter, he realized that **Liu Bei** was already at **Public Peace** <note>original: "公安" (Gong'an), a strategic location in Jing Province.</note> and **Guan Yu** was at **Yiyang**. 

Filled with such shame and regret that he wished he could sink into the ground, **Hao Pu** surrendered. **Lü Meng** left **Sun He** in charge of the aftermath and immediately set off...
