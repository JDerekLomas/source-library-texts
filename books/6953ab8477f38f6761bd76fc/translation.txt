<note>The emblem consists of a heraldic shield. Inside the shield is a cross with the pans of a balance scale hanging from its horizontal bar, symbolizing justice or the precise weights required in alchemical work. The entire shield rests upon a coiled serpent biting its own tail, known as an **uroboros** <term>uroboros: an ancient symbol depicting a serpent or dragon eating its own tail, representing eternity and the cyclic nature of the universe</term>.</note>
