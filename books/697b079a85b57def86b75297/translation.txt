# THE ELIXIR OF THE PHILOSOPHERS,

->OTHERWISE,<-

->*The <term>Art of Transmutation</term>, very useful, attributed to Pope JOHN XXII of that name: not yet seen, nor printed before now.*<-
<note>Transmutation refers to the alchemical process of changing base metals, like lead or copper, into noble metals like gold or silver. While this work is attributed to Pope John XXII (reigned 1316–1334), historians generally consider such attributions to be "pseudo-epigraphical," meaning the name of a famous figure was used to give the book more authority. In reality, John XXII issued a famous decree against alchemical fraudsters.</note>
