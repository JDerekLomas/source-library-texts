### Explanation of the Strategy
In the context of the *Taiyi Shenshu* (The Grand One's Divine Calculations), the "Guest" represents the active, attacking party. Because the numerical calculation for the Guest (fifteen) results in an "Obstructed Gate" (Dumen), their movement is cosmically paralyzed. They have "no gate" through which to advance or retreat effectively.

For the practitioner (the "Main" side), the omens suggest:
1.  **Passive Resistance:** Do not seek out the enemy; let their own stagnant energy defeat them.
2.  **Solidify Defenses:** The term *Gushou* (firm defense) implies reinforcing walls, stockpiling supplies, and maintaining a high state of alert without overextending.
3.  **Directional Warning:** The text specifically highlights the **Southeast** and **East** as the sectors requiring the most preparation, likely as they correspond to the "Tiger" (Yin) position and the "Great Yang" mentioned on the previous page.

### Interpretation of Pattern Fifty
This chart provides a spatial representation of a specific moment in time. The "Main" (the defender) holds a numerical advantage of 16, which is described as a "Harmonious" number where all cosmic factors (Heaven, Earth, and Man) are in alignment. 

Conversely, the "Guest" (the attacker) is in a dire position. Their calculation of 15 lands on the **Obstructed Gate**, meaning their path is cosmically barred. Furthermore, the text notes that their commanders ("Great General" and "Assistant General") are unable to even leave the "Central Palace," suggesting that the enemy is paralyzed by internal indecision or logistical failure. For a general reading this, the advice is clear: the defender is secure, and the attacker is currently incapable of a successful strike.
