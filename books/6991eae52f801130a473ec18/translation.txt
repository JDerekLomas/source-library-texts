# STUDIES OF THE HUMAN FACE AND PROPORTIONS

The soul seems to reside in the judicial part, and the judicial part seems to be in that place where all the senses meet, which is called the **common sense** <note>original: "senso comune"; a Renaissance anatomical concept referring to the area of the brain where sensory inputs are integrated</note>. And it is not all through the body, as many have believed, but rather it is entirely in this part. Because if it were all through the body, it would not have been necessary for the instruments of the senses to converge in one single place.

<margin>The eye, the ear, and the smell are the servants of this common sense.</margin>

### ON THE MEASUREMENT OF THE HEAD

The distance from the chin to the nose is the same as that from the roots of the hair to the eyebrows, and also the same as the length of the ear. 

->**The Proportions of the Face**<-

| ANATOMICAL MARKER | PROPORTIONAL MEASURE |
|:---|:---|
| From the chin to the start of the nose | 1/3 of the face |
| The length of the nose | 1/3 of the face |
| From the nose to the hair line | 1/3 of the face |
