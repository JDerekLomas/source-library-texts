...may happen to, encounter, or unpleasantly affect our just cause and the achievement of our **restitution** <note>restitution; here referring to the restoration of his lands and titles lost during the Thirty Years' War</note>.

We also testify, in conclusion, and swear by God, the Searcher of Hearts, that with this, our compelled and lawful **protest** <note>original: *Protestation*</note>, we do not intend to diminish or offend either the Highness and **preeminence** <note>original: *præminentz*</note> of the Imperial Majesty—whom we are at all times and steadfastly inclined to **honor** <note>original: *veneriren*</note> with due and most humble **respect**—nor the honor and justice of any other. Rather, our **intent** <note>original: *Intent*</note> and purpose are directed solely toward this: that through such lawful means, our innocence, just cause, indubitable **right** <note>original: *jus*</note>, traditional ancestral dignity, and rightful **possession** might be increasingly preserved, proven, and made known; and that these might not be neglected, endangered, or disadvantaged through silence or the omission of a necessary, legally required protest.

And we have caused this, our protest, which we performed **before a notary and witnesses** <note>original: *coram Notario vnd testibus*</note>, to be put in writing and issued in public print, so that it may come to everyone’s knowledge and no one concerned may **claim** <note>original: *prætendiren*</note> or pretend any ignorance of it. We have also intended to send and deliver **authentic copies** <note>original: *authentica exemplaria*</note> thereof, confirmed by our own signature and pressed seal, both to the Imperial Majesty and to the Prince-Electors. **Done** <note>original: *Actum*</note> at **Hampton Court** in England, on the 27th of January, in the year 1637.

Indeed, it did not stop there. This military harassment <note>original: *vexatio militaris*</note> prevailed so far that many cities had to provide written commitments <note>term: *Reverse* — formal legal declarations, in this case renouncing Protestantism</note> under the seals of their councils, communities, guilds, and corporations. Furthermore, statutes were made—and later confirmed by Imperial Authority <note>original: *Imperatoriâ Autoritate*</note>—decreeing that for all eternity, no one should be admitted to the rights of a citizen or a guild member who had not previously become Catholic. Whether this was truly a voluntary and spontaneous acceptance <note>original: *ultronea ac spontanea acceptatio*</note> and an act of free will is left to the judgment of any impartial person who is somewhat familiar with the history of such wretched "salvation."

Each of them, whether they were rich or poor, had to pay a certain **ransom** <note>term: *Ranzion* — a fee paid to secure release from custody</note>, of which one half went to the executioner and the jailer, and the other half was to go to the Catholic priest.

And although it might be further argued against the **Hereditary Principalities** <note>Silesian territories directly ruled by the Emperor as King of Bohemia, unlike the "Mediatized" principalities ruled by local dukes</note> that they had perhaps sinned against His Imperial and Royal Majesty at that time—since it is not known that many of them failed to contribute to the Elector-Saxon, as well as the Swedish and Brandenburg armies, provided for them, and offered various assistance—a response is quite easy to provide. Specifically, it was not within the power of the Hereditary Principalities to resist such a great force, which even the Imperial army found itself unable to withstand and was forced to retreat. Thus, it naturally followed in several places that the people—completely abandoned by the Imperial soldiery and stripped of all help and protection—could not prevent themselves from contributing to the **Evangelical** <note>Protestant</note> army, nor from taking in some troops in one place or another. However, this happened **with Imperial devotion preserved and unimpaired** <note>original: *salvâ & illibatâ devotione Cæſareâ*</note>. In this devotion, the Hereditary Principalities (which were excluded in the Peace of Prague) always remained constant. They knowingly undertook nothing against honor, loyalty, duty, and sincerity, nor did they take part in any dangerous counsels or alliances.
