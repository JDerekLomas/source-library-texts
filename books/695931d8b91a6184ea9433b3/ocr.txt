This most singular work is the production of Messahalah
an Arabian Astrologer. It was translated for the Dauphin
who was brought into England a Prisoner with his
father John, King of France, by our Edward the black prince.
In all probability, the Dauphin brought the book with him
the translation having undoubtedly been made for him
as appears by the red ink matter at the top of
the opposite page. The Translators name is on the
last leaf. The date of the MS. which appears on
the Title to the second book is 1349.

Amongst the abundantly curious matter of this MS.
is the assertion that the earth (as since proved by Sir Isaac Newton)
is flattened at the poles—a most remarkable fact to have
been stated at a period when every Astrologer affirmed that
the poles were elongated.

The Arms opposite are the Dauphins to whom
the Manuscript belonged.

He was afterwards King of France.
</insert>
