Six of them are masculine and six are feminine. Aries is a masculine sign and is among the signs of the day; Taurus is feminine and among the signs of the night. In like manner, the twelve signs follow one another, one masculine and the next feminine, until the end of the twelve signs. 

And of these twelve signs, there are six that mount and rise straight up over the horizon—that is to say, their ascendant or rising is quite straight—and six that rise obliquely, meaning they have an oblique and crooked ascent. Those that rise straight are from the beginning of the sign named Cancer until the end of the sign named Sagittarius. The reason for this is because the latitude of each is greater than its longitude, and it takes more than two equal hours <note>An "equal hour" is a fixed 60-minute hour, as opposed to "unequal hours" which varied by season</note>—meaning that each of these signs takes more than two equal hours to rise—and for this reason they are named "straight-ascending." 

Those that rise obliquely are from the beginning of Capricorn until the end of Gemini. This is because the latitude of each of them is less than the longitude, and it rises in less than two equal hours—meaning that each of these six takes less than two equal hours—and for this reason they are named "obliquely-ascending."

### The First House

The first house is the entire beginning place where one begins the quest, in the place of the interrogation or of the birth or of the beginning of any work. And it signifies life and death, for it is the <term>ascendant<note>The degree of the zodiac rising over the eastern horizon at a specific moment.</note></term> of the life of man. And it goes before life when he issues from the womb of the mother. (And this line is ascending from the lowest part of the earth to the highest; it goes from darkness to light. And likewise, the rising from the darkness of the womb to the light of this world, and from the narrowness of the womb to the largeness of the air. And he who makes the request shall put his interrogation from the secret of his heart <insert>into light</insert> and it shall be the manifestation of what was previously hidden.) Therefore, it signifies the body and life and every beginning and movement of the body, and whatever thing shall be held from any ascendant; and the beginning of every healthy thing or the outcome, and every movable or rational thing, and the beginnings of all things.

### The Second House

The second house follows the ascendant and does not <term>regard<note>In astrology, "regarding" or "beholding" refers to the geometric aspects or lines of sight between different parts of the zodiac.</note></term> the ascending line; and it signifies the gathering of substance <note>Refers to personal wealth, possessions, and material resources.</note> and begins from life, and the following state, and the acceptance of promises and proximities or friendships.

### The Third House

The third house is not entirely removed from the ascendant. And it signifies one's siblings and kindred, one's neighbors and relatives, their well-being, and such similar things. Also faith and religion, contention in letters, epistles, legacies, pilgrimages, and dreams.
