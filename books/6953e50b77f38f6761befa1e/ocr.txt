<image-desc>An elaborate allegorical engraved title page. At the top, two globes representing the "MUNDVS VISIBILIS" (Visible World) and "MUNDVS INTELLECTVALIS" (Intellectual World) are joined by two hands clasped in a handshake emerging from clouds. Above the hands is the motto "RATIONE ET EXPERIENTIA FOEDERANTVR" (By reason and experience they are joined). Below the handshake is the title of the larger work: "INSTAVR: MAG: P. I." (Instauratio Magna, Part I). The central title is presented on a hanging banner, flanked by two obelisks representing Oxford ("OXONIVM") and Cambridge ("CANTABRIGIA"). Between the obelisks, a ship is seen sailing across a sea, referencing discovery and the expansion of knowledge. Beneath the sea, stacks of books represent the parts of Bacon's projected "Great Instauration." At the bottom are two pedestals with owls (symbols of wisdom) and triangular diagrams of the sciences and philosophy.</image-desc>
