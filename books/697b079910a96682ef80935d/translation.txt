...vices; let us but examine the merits of this cause with the same impartiality with which we judge, or ought to judge, between one person and another. Then the controversy shall soon be ended, and the "Sun of Righteousness" will rise "with healing in his wings" <note>A reference to Malachi 4:2, traditionally interpreted by Christians as the arrival of Christ</note>, dispersing those false lights and flickering mists <note>original: "glimmering Exhalations"</note> rising from the fumes of a troubled <note>original: "distempered"</note> imagination. These are meant only to make the uneducated <note>original: "Rude"</note> and ignorant stare in wonder, to pull the traveler <note>original: "Pilgrim"</note> away from his path to heaven <note>original: "Bliss"</note>, and to indulge his passions in the vain pursuit of hollow pleasures.

For once the strength of Christianity and the beauty of pure <note>original: "undefiled"</note> religion have been diligently investigated and impartially surveyed, it is utterly impossible for a person who sincerely seeks the truth to be mistaken in his way to happiness. Impartial people <note>original: "The Disinterested"</note> of all nations have become converts <note>original: "Proselytes"</note> to this faith. Nothing but "*the god of this world*" <note>A biblical reference to Satan or worldly distractions that blind the mind (2 Corinthians 4:4)</note>—nothing but the passions and prejudices of mankind—has ever been able to hide or conceal the evidence which <term>God</term> has given of Himself from the beginning. Nothing else could cast a veil upon that religion—that unchanging rule—which must otherwise always have appeared with all the charms of persuasion and the inherent excellence that commands swift conviction.
