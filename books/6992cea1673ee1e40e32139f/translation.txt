## Records of Divination and Observation
### Divination Section 21
### Divination of the Five Elements

Master Mao <note>referring to the author, Mao Yuanyi</note> says: "In my studies of divination <note>original: 占 (zhan); the practice of interpreting omens and natural phenomena to predict future events</note>, I have already verified the details and analyzed the methods thoroughly. However, there is also the concept of the Five Elements—Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, and Earth—and their transformations. These manifest across the physical world: in the earth, the mountains, the waters, the plants and trees, the birds and beasts, and even in the four limbs of the human body. I have gathered these diverse portents here to ensure this work serves as a complete and exhaustive collection."

Regarding earthquakes: they occur when the *yang* <note>the active, light, or solar principle</note> is suppressed and cannot emerge, while the *yin* <note>the passive, dark, or terrestrial principle</note> is pressured and cannot enter. It is the result of these two primal forces striking against one another.

## Divination of Tremors, Part One
### Divination of the Earth

Therefore, tremors <note>referring to earthquakes</note> occur. It is also said that the Earth represents *yin* <note>the passive, receptive, and terrestrial principle</note>. According to the laws of nature, it should be peaceful and still. If it exceeds its duties as *yin* and usurps the governance that belongs to *yang* <note>the active, celestial principle, often associated with the Emperor</note>, then it responds with a tremor. 

It is also said: When *yin* overcomes *yang*, it is a sign of the weak overcoming the strong. 

It is also said: When *yin* turns its back on *yang*, the earth will crack and the Qiang and Yi <note>terms used historically to refer to non-Han ethnic groups on the western and eastern borders of China</note> will rebel. 

It is also said: When the earth moves, it indicates an excess of *yin* and that subordinates <note>ministers or officials</note> are becoming too powerful and prosperous.

When the earth splits and cracks, the gentry and the common people will become estranged and separated.

When the ground sinks, the royal household will fall into chaos.

If the earth cracks and emits a sound, soldiers will rise up in all four directions. 

It is also said: When the Sanmiao <note>an ancient ethnic group often cited in classical texts as rebels against early Chinese sage-kings</note> are on the verge of destruction, the earth will crack deep enough to reach the springs.

It is also said: If the earth splits, ministers will rebel. If an earthquake occurs at the Ancestral Temple <note>the ritual site where the ruling family worshipped their ancestors</note>, the local district will have rebellious officials.

If an earthquake startles oxen and horses, the Son of Heaven <note>the Emperor</note> will lose his position.

When mountains collapse, this is called *yin* overcoming *yang*, or the weak overcoming the strong.

**Obstructed with No Exit.** This is an omen of misfortune and a lack of passage. If a general does not understand this principle, dispatching troops will lead to defeat, and they shall not return. In this configuration, although the **Great One** <term>太乙 (Tàiyǐ): The supreme celestial agent in this system of divination, representing the governing cosmic force</term> assists the **Host** <note>The defending or home force</note>, the **Main and Assistant Generals** are **Compressed** <term>迫 (pò): A condition where a general’s position is pressured by the Great One, indicating internal friction or lack of tactical room</term>.
