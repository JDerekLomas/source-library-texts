<margin>2</margin> <margin>10</margin> After these events—for discussions were constantly ongoing between the Athenians and the Lacedaemonians <note>the Spartans</note> regarding the territories they still held of one another—the Lacedaemonians hoped that if the Athenians recovered Panactum <note>a strategic border fort</note> from the Boeotians, they themselves might get back Pylos <note>a Spartan stronghold captured by Athens</note>. Consequently, they went to the Boeotians as ambassadors and begged them to hand over both Panactum and the Athenian prisoners so that they could receive Pylos in exchange. <margin>15</margin> <margin>3</margin> The Boeotians, however, refused to give them up unless the Spartans made a private, separate alliance with them, just as they had done with the Athenians. The Lacedaemonians knew they would be acting unjustly toward the Athenians—as it had been agreed <note>in the Peace of Nicias</note> that neither side should make peace or war with any third party without the other's consent— <margin>20</margin> but they were desperate to receive Panactum so they could trade it for Pylos. At the same time, the faction in Sparta that was eager to disrupt the existing peace treaties was enthusiastically supporting the deal with the Boeotians. Thus, they concluded the alliance just as winter was ending and spring was approaching; and Panactum was immediately demolished. So ended the eleventh year of the war.

<margin>40</margin> At the very start of the spring of the following summer, the Argives became alarmed. The Boeotian ambassadors they expected had never arrived, <margin>25</margin> and they learned that Panactum was being demolished and that a private alliance had been formed between the Boeotians and the Lacedaemonians. They feared they would be left isolated and that the entire network of alliances would shift back to the Lacedaemonians; <margin>30</margin> for they assumed the Boeotians had been persuaded by the Lacedaemonians to demolish Panactum and to join the Athenian [side...] <note>The text here implies the Argives believed a grand realignment was happening that would leave them without allies.</note>
