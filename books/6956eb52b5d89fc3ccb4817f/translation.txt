# The Book of the King’s Face
<note>Hebrew: Sefer Pnei Melekh. This title likely refers to the Divine Presence or the countenance of a sovereign.</note>

And I, the humble Isaac, son of the house of those who preceded me, the least of the scribes in the life of the resurrection of the dead; and from there my name is written, and my hand succeeded with good fortune and suc<unclear>cess</unclear>. And good is His Name, blessed above all blessing and praise, He who guides; and He brought me by His hand to write. And the merit is mine and that of the holy and awesome congregation, to ascend to the City of the King, the chosen one which was of old, **Mount Gerizim**. <note>The sacred mountain of the Samaritans.</note> And I desired again with love, and with a heart and purity of grace and sweetness. I wrote by the grace of the Most High, and I sanctified the vessel and the throne for the spices of the sources of the fields, upon the planting where my portion lies. And may those who preceded the return of the passerby and the traveler be found worthy. And let no man’s dreams wear me out; let [this work] stand firm and not depart, for I trust in the strong ones. It is full and wide and fixed and supported by Him, to uphold all bonds and to leave over the remnants of the city of lips <note>A poetic reference to prayer or speech.</note>, simple so-and-so, and written completely, and in time, and pleasant and diligent. And may the Holy One arise—peace, peace in joy—and may fear and distress not rule in my dwelling. And may he who is of high degree hasten and write for him; and even so, let it not be a small thing in the eyes of the one who took pity, for he was my son, my very presence, a bond for a memorial of the miracle of grace and kindness. And at first I intended to say: the "Kindness of the King" <note>Hebrew: Hesed Melekh</note> is the Guide, the bestower of kindnesses; and He sent to him the horn in which the Holy One, Blessed be He, <note>A translation of the Aramaic/Hebrew "Kudsha Brich Hu," a common title for God.</note> His ancient ones and His hosts, and the one who makes His paths good, and the heights of the abundance of the fields of good intellect to bind up, and a vessel to fill the pious one in his soul. And may he rejoice in their building, and especially Solomon the King who preceded me; lean upon them and write for them, Amen.

# The Book of Life and Holiness

In this book of life, before the Holy One <note>original: "Kadosh"</note>, I have ascended to their heights.
A complete composition <term>Chibur: a formal rabbinic work or treatise</term> written in its day and time, leaning upon the high fortress by His permission and grace.
The oaths are presented with offerings and sweet saturation, a law <note>original: "nimus," often referring to social or natural law</note> from the mouth of a scribe written in a book.
Good is the reputation of the signs of the chapters and the established laws <note>original: "dinim"</note> which were extracted from the skin of Sodom <note>A metaphorical reference to harsh or wicked origins being transformed through justice</note>.
In righteousness, he joins Enoch <note>The biblical figure Enoch, often associated in mystical literature with the recording of heavenly secrets</note> and their commandments, which bound grace and His kindness.
And he planted the ancient root, while the wicked are trampled by those wearing the vision they have forged.
The desire of his vision was drawn to their garment, and they separated a portion of the strength of Joshua <note>original: "Yehoshua"</note>.
Lest their vision be hidden; for they sinned in transgression, yet the merit of the transgression plucks away the lack here.
They sinned for themselves, and in the thread of their deeds they abandoned, and they honored and sought a revealed vision.
The mouth of their vision was snared, and to add to the soul, he shall also strengthen the bond of grace over transgression.
In its source, merit is kindness and heart, and the princes; and he has knowledge of the transgression now.
They lived in transgression and lacked nothing, yet they have a lack; and the man of the cold shall support us.
Grace and sight, and when his refuge is established and they answered; and they turned their faces if they should be completed.
Pure and sparing of grace, his grace and clean; they inclined their mouths to precede, and he has salvation.
And upon the kindness of the source and the vision of his overseer, etc. The peace-offerings and the desire of the openings.
They returned to grace, and in kindness the wicked, the sons of Isaac <note>original: "Yitzhak"</note>, desired the wicked and the silence of the wicked.

His wrath ruled, and the silence and the multitude of voices—they tasted it—and the majority to investigate, to strengthen, and to turn away.
The living being desired and sought for the hope of its mouth, and it abandoned and was in the gate of grace.
He erased transgression; grace favored us in kindness; the lack preceded us, and the vision of the one who carries it.
And strengthen and show them favor; and the request found a solution, and the vision shall break, and the kindness of their heart is fear.
Open your face, and the groups of their thoughts and their attainment you have envisioned; and the vision of His kindness.
