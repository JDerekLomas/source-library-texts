The workmen no longer understood one another and were obliged to separate from each other; therefore, each took his part. *Phaleg*, who gave the impetus for this building <note>The Tower of Babel</note> and was the director of it, was consequently the most guilty: he thus condemned himself to the most severe penance and departed for the North of *Germany*, where he finally arrived after having endured many pains and difficulties in the wild lands, finding no other food than roots and wild fruits.

In this part of the world (which is called *Prussia*) he built some huts to protect himself from the bad weather and the discomforts of the air: he also built a Temple in the shape of a triangle, where he shut himself in to beseech GOD for mercy in atonement for his sins.

In the year 553, when the ruins in the salt mines of PRUSSIA had been excavated fifteen *cubits* <term>cubits: an ancient unit of length based on the forearm, roughly 18-22 inches</term> deep, a kind of triangular building was found, in which there was a white marble column. On the lower end of this column, the entire *History* was written in Hebrew letters. Beside this column, a gray hardstone tomb was found, in which some dust was noticed, and beneath it an *Agate Stone*, upon which was the following *Epitaph*.

> *Here rests the ashes of our great Architect of the Tower of Babel: the LORD had compassion on him because he showed himself humble.*

All these monuments are with the KING OF PRUSSIA. The epitaph does not say that *Phaleg* was the Architect of the Tower of Babel; but the history found at the bottom of this column shows us that *Phaleg* was the son of *Heber*, whose father was *Arphaxad*, son of *Shem*, eldest son of *Noah*. The password is *Phaleg*: the sacred word S. C. J. signifies *Shem*, *Ham*, and *Japheth* <note>The three sons of Noah</note>.
