# Consultation for the white menstrual flux descending from the womb, occurring due to a lapse of seed and a humid imbalance of the liver and stomach.

<margin>When the white flux occurs</margin>
I consider it not easy, but rather most difficult, to be able to fully understand the condition of this most honorable lady, which is specifically a white menstrual flux <note>original: "fluxum menstruum album"; likely leukorrhea or "the whites"</note> descending from the womb. And this same thing happens to them at the time or shortly before the period in which she is naturally accustomed to menstruate. It is not, however, concluded from this condition that this woman cannot become pregnant; it is common that as the birth approaches and increases, this aforementioned menstrual flux occurs with heaviness and other symptoms perceived specifically in the loins. And there precede symptoms in the body such as sleepiness, abdominal pain, and a lack of wakefulness; and finally, at the end of the week or shortly after, the woman gives birth to a very weak child, which shows the shortness of the preceding time, because it rarely extends beyond one, eight, or ten days, as I have observed. Unless a diligent observer and contemplator in such accidents attends carefully, it is indeed a course of white menses from the womb—a condition about which a very exquisite summary is rarely gathered by authors. And it is a wondrous thing indeed, which, as I have elsewhere declared in a certain very diffuse treatise, this sickness is judged in the most serious terms not only by friends and relatives, but even by many physicians who encounter it very often; so much so that I have certainly dared to say to the people that this sickness happens more to physicians than to others. And this is a certain condition either naturally found in the womb or existing by its essence in it; or both, coming from the womb by connection to the whole body, or from a determined part. And if it is from the whole, then it will be necessary to assign this condition as occurring due to an increase of a bad "complexion" <note>The "complexion" refers to the balance of the four humors: blood, phlegm, yellow bile, and black bile.</note>, dry and humid, prevailing in the whole body.

<margin>When a bad complexion is in the whole body</margin>
Because of which, by way of indigestion, these menstrual matters are dominated. And if the menses are generated in places other than the liver, then it will be necessary to note them as gases or vapors of the matter in great quantity. And these matters happen to the whole genus of women, as from a bad dry and humid complexion which is prepared for the generation of inanition with a cause; which matters, being somewhat digested by the stomach, happen to be expelled by the stomach, which increases and resumes them through the common pregnant veins to the lower parts until they are expelled to the womb, and from there flow out from it. And of these menses expelled for this reason, the determined expulsion of menses is recognized by these signs:

<margin>Blood when it comes from a bad complexion in the whole body</margin>
1. The first sign is the prolonged descent of them.
2. The second sign is whiteness in color.
3. The third sign is the viscosity of them in substance.
4. The fourth sign is a persistent foul odor.
5. The fifth sign is the manifestation of their actual coldness.
6. The sixth sign is the inspection of them at their exit.
7. The seventh sign is the continuous opening of the lower mouth of the womb.
