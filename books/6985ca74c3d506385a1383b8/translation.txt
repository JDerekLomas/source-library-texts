### *Beginning of the Work.*

The Most-Wise <note>the title given to the Master of the Lodge, representing King Solomon</note>, with the crown on his head, being seated, says to the King of Tyre <note>Hiram, the King of Tyre, who provided materials and craftsmen for the Temple</note>, who presents himself at the foot of the Throne: "Most-Powerful King of Tyre, what have you come here to do?" The King of Tyre responds: "Most-Wise, I come to ask of you vengeance for the death of the Architect of the Temple <note>This refers to Hiram Abiff, the master builder whose murder is the central mystery of Masonic legend</note>, which until this day has remained unpunished." The Most-Wise says: "Take your place, my Brother, and be a witness to the searches for the murderer that I am going to order to be made." The King of Tyre ascends <margin>a</margin> to the Throne and takes his place. Solomon names a Brother who, at that instant, comes to place one knee on the ground at the foot of the throne. Solomon takes his scepter and places it upon his head, saying to him: "Brother, I constitute you the Intimate of the Council, to watch over the safety of the Lodge: begin your duties to ensure the qualifications of the Brothers here present." The Brother rises, salutes the two Kings, then goes to take the sign, the grip <term>grip: a specific way of shaking hands used as a secret sign of recognition</term>, and the word <term>word: the secret password of the degree</term> from each Brother. Upon returning to the foot of the Throne, he says: "Most-Wise, the Council has only faithful subjects."
