## The Author’s own recommendation of the Art and the present Little Work.

It is known to the whole world that we have risen high in almost all professions and arts; just as <term>medical chemistry</term> <note>original: "Chymia medica," the application of chemical principles to the creation of medicine, often associated with Paracelsian thought.</note> has also reached such a height that almost all pharmacies are filled nearly entirely with chemical medicines. 

For what are all the distilled waters, spirits, essences, oils, extracts, tinctures, <term>magisteries</term> <note>original: "Magisteria," which were refined chemical precipitates or extracts believed to contain the essential virtues of a substance.</note>, <term>calcinated substances</term> <note>original: "Calcinata," materials reduced to a powder or "ash" through high heat.</note> and preparations (one need only look into their precious cabinets here and there) but pure chemical products?

People only say: “Oh! **Laboratory work** <note>original: "Laboriren," referring to chemical or alchemical experimentation.</note> wastes house and home; one hears enough stories of that, etc.” Yes, certainly, depending on how one goes about it. The shameful, deceitful, wandering alchemists who promise gold have indeed frightened and ruined great lords: but what does this have to do with **medical chemistry** <note>original: "Chymiam Medicam."</note>? Is health not the greatest treasure on earth? 

The great and powerful spend much on banquets, hunts, comedies, and balls—indeed, they often gamble away so much in one sitting that they could have secured for themselves and their neighbors the most precious medicines for health and a long life. Instead, through this wastefulness, they bring illnesses upon themselves and willfully shorten their own lives.
