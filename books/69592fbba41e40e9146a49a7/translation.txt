Those to whom God has given this gift do not wish to reveal themselves to Kings, Princes, great masters, and Lords, fearing the threats made to them by philosophers—both ancient and modern—of being moved from inestimable liberty to a servitude that is almost intolerable. I have experienced this myself and have seen it happen to others in many places. Notwithstanding, Sire, setting aside their words and threats, and not relying at all upon their admonitions—however charitable and worthy of note they may be—I know and recognize that Your Royal Majesty is not equal nor similar to those who use such extortions against God and nature toward their faithful servants, after having received from them a <term>Science</term> <note>In this context, "Science" refers to Alchemy or the "Sacred Art" of transformation.</note> producing inestimable treasures.
