# The Three Pulses and Natural Enmities

Even if there is a difference in the **Five Stars** <term>Panchatara: A method of calculating compatibility based on the distance between birth stars.</term> or the **Temperamental Groups** <note>original: "Gana," referring to the categorization of stars into Divine, Human, or Demonic temperaments.</note>, a marriage with a single **Pulse** <term>Nadi: The biological or energetic constitution; couples usually require different Nadis (Aadi, Madhya, or Antya) to be compatible.</term> is recorded as auspicious [in certain specific exceptions]. **24**॥ 

If the couple shares the same **Lunar Mansion** <term>Nakshatra: One of the 27 sectors of the sky the Moon passes through.</term> and the same **Quarter** <term>Pada: A sub-division of a Nakshatra; each mansion has four quarters.</term>, the marriage causes the loss of life. However, if they share the same Mansion but are in different Quarters, the marriage shall be auspicious. **25**॥ 

Regarding the mansions **Hasta** and **Chitra**, friendship is formed through their various sequences; however, the same Pulse is generally not desired for a couple, and is considered only middling in quality. **26**॥ 

If the **Temperamental Groups** are the same but the Mansions are different, or if the Mansions are different but the Groups are the same, and even if there is a difference in the Quarters, the "faults" of the Pulses and the Groups do not cause harm. **27**॥ 

A shared **First Pulse** <note>original: "Aadi Nadi," associated with the Vata or 'air' constitution.</note> causes separation; a shared **Middle Pulse** <note>original: "Madhya Nadi," associated with the Pitta or 'fire' constitution.</note> brings the certainty of death; and a shared **Final Pulse** <note>original: "Antya Nadi," associated with the Kapha or 'water' constitution.</note> brings widowhood and extreme sorrow. Therefore, these three [identical pairings] must be avoided. **28**॥ 

The **Snake** and the **Mongoose**, the **Cat** and the **Rat**, the **Dog** and the **Deer**, and the **Horse** and the **Buffalo**—these pairs are mutual enemies [and their representative Mansions should not be joined]. **29**॥
