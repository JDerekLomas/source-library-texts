# Alchemical Works,

->namely<-

3. Philosophical Summary <note>original Latin: "Summarium Philosophicum"</note>.
4. The Great Explanation of the Philosopher's Stone for the Transformation of all Metals. <term>Philosopher's Stone: A legendary substance in alchemy believed to be capable of turning base metals into gold and providing the "elixir of life" for rejuvenation.</term>
5. Treasure of Philosophy.
