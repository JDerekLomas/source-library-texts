# Om. Now the examination of the Ascendant in the Chapter on Childbirth is written.

**Aries:** The person has a body and face like their own limb <note>This implies a well-proportioned or distinctively shaped head</note>, is talkative, active, of a hot temperament, and faces East [at birth]. Under the influence of 9 planets <note>original: "grahe 9"</note>, there are five or three women present with soiled clothes. Such is the one born in the **Aries Ascendant**. 1

**Taurus:** Of a reddish complexion, fair-eyed, endowed with good fortune, but lazy and fond of other women. There are five or three [women present], and [the birth occurs] facing South. Such is the one born in the **Taurus Ascendant**. 2

**Gemini:** Of a darkish complexion, with visible veins, and five or three women [present]. The person faces West, has a self-controlled body, and a high forehead. Such is the one born in the **Gemini Ascendant**. 3

**Cancer:** With [the appearance of] six eyes <note>original: "netra ṣat"; likely an astrological configuration or a specific omen regarding the gaze of the newborn</note> or sparks, with large hands and feet, a white complexion, wise, and very radiant.
