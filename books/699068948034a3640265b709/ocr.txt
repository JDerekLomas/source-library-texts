The tower has several levels representing the Liberal Arts and Sciences:
- Ground floor: "Donat" (Donatus) and "Priscian" (Priscianus) teaching students.
- Second level: "Aristote Logica" (Aristotle, Logic), "Tullio Rethorica" (Cicero, Rhetoric), and "poesis" (Poetry).
- Third level: "Boetius Musica" (Boethius, Music), "Pithago Arithmetica" (Pythagoras, Arithmetic), "Euclides geometria" (Euclid, Geometry), and "Ptolome⁹ Astronomia" (Ptolemy, Astronomy).
- Fourth level: "phīa phisica" (Natural Philosophy) and "Seneca morali" (Seneca, Moral Philosophy).
- Top level: A figure labeled "Petrus Lombardus" representing "Theologia" (Theology) and "sen Methaphī" (Metaphysics).
