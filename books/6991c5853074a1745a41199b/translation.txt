This manuscript is protected by a heavy **leather binding**, a fine example of craftsmanship likely dating from the 16th or 17th century. The brown leather has been decorated using a technique known as **blind-tooling**, where heated metal stamps are pressed into the surface to create intricate designs without the use of gold or ink.

The decoration is arranged in a series of nested frames:
*   The central rectangular panel is filled with **arabesque** motifs <note>Flowing, intertwined patterns of leaves and scrolls.</note>.
*   Surrounding the center are decorative borders, including a **roll-stamp** <note>A continuous pattern applied with a cylindrical tool.</note> that features small heads or portraits set within circular medallions.

To ensure the longevity of the volume, the cover is fitted with heavy metal hardware:
*   Five circular brass **bosses** <note>Raised metal studs placed at the corners and the center to protect the leather from wear when the book is laid flat.</note> are fixed to the front.
*   A sturdy metal **clasp** remains on the right-hand edge to keep the book's pages compressed and protected from dust and light.

<note>This binding is characteristic of institutional or archival volumes designed for heavy use and long-term preservation.</note>

## The Mystery of the Second Birth

**D**is power torments me, this scolding woman, <note>original German: "hackendes weib." The mother who gave birth to me was herself born through me upon this earth.
