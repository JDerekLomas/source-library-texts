When you see that it has turned white and reached the nature of ash, return to it the water that you distilled from it **and bury it** for twenty-one days. Then take it out and distill it in a clay-luted cucurbit <note>Coating glass vessels with clay (luting) protected them from breaking in high heat.</note> over a calm, burning dung-fire. Finally, take it out and distill it using an ash-bath <note>A method of indirect heating where the vessel sits in hot ashes.</note>.
