Recently, following the rumored report of my death, some idle mind presented me in the "Kingdom of the Dead" <note>A reference to the literary genre "Dialogues of the Dead," popular in the 18th century, where deceased historical figures engage in conversation.</note> as a General, alongside an actual military General.
