# 一犁杏雨

<note>The phrase "一犁杏雨" (Yī lí xìng yǔ) translates to "a plough's depth of apricot-blossom rain." It refers to the spring rain falling during the blooming of apricot trees, signifying a rainfall sufficient to begin the agricultural season.</note>

# 三徑葉雲

<note>The main text is written in large ink characters and is read from right to left. "三徑" (Three Paths) is a classical allusion to a hermit's secluded garden. "葉雲" (Leafy Clouds) likely refers to lush, cloud-like foliage. The vertical inscription on the left dates the piece to the second month of spring in the 35th year of the Kangxi reign (1696).</note>
