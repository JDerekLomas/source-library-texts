## Narrative and Illustrated Description of Henan

Gui E <note>A prominent Ming Dynasty official and scholar-official known for his expertise in geography and administration</note> stated: Henan is the ancient territory of Yuzhou <note>one of the "Nine Provinces" of ancient China, traditionally considered the center of the world</note>. It is situated in the heart of the Middle Kingdom <note>original: "中夏" (Zhongxia), a historical term for China</note>, where the four directions converge. Prefectures such as Zhangde control the region north of the Yellow River <gloss>modern Beizhili</gloss>; Mount Song and the Luo River serve to shield the lands south of the mountains <gloss>specifically modern Shaanxi and the southern borders of Nanyang</gloss>. Nanyang and Runing lead directly to the outskirts of Xiangyang and Huangzhou <gloss>Xiangyang and Huangzhou both belong to Huguang Province</gloss>, while Kaifeng serves as its capital.

In Henan, there are eight prefectures: Kaifeng, Guide, Zhangde, Weihui, Huaiqing, Henan <note>referring specifically to Henan Prefecture, centered on the city of Luoyang</note>, Nanyang, and Runing. These oversee eleven departments <note>sub-prefectural administrative divisions known as *zhou*</note> and ninety-two counties. Additionally, there is one independent department named <insert>Ru</insert> <note>Ruzhou</note>, which oversees four counties.
