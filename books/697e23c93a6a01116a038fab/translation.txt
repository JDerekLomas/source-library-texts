# PREFACE

The following work is part of that project which the learned <term>Dr. Lardner</term> <note>original: "Dr. LARDNER"; Nathaniel Lardner (1684–1768) was an influential English theologian and author of "The Credibility of the Gospel History," a foundational work in the study of early Christian evidence.</note> had planned in order to give a full and comprehensive view of the *Credibility of the Gospel History*. The world has long possessed the majority of those treatises which were intended to complete it: including *The Testimonies to the Occasional Facts Mentioned in the New Testament*; *The Testimonies of Christian Writers to the Books of the New Testament*; and also *The Testimonies of Jews and Heathens to the Books and Principal Facts*. The remaining part, as the doctor himself informs us (a), was intended to contain *The Testimonies of Christian Writers to the Principal Facts of the New Testament* and also *The History of the <term>Heretics</term> of the Two First Centuries* <note>In the context of early church history, "heretics" refers to groups like the Gnostics or Marcionites whose beliefs diverged from what became the orthodox Christian canon.</note>.

This history is now presented to the public. It was primarily written by the doctor himself and remained with him for several (b) years. Some parts of it were completely prepared for the press, having received his final corrections.

***

(a) See Preface to *The Credibility, etc.* Part 2, Vol. 12, p. 5, 6.
(b) Ibid. p. 7. <note>original: "Ibid."; abbreviation of the Latin "ibidem," meaning "in the same place," referring to the previously cited book.</note>
