# On the Ascendant and Bodily Afflictions

From the **ascendant** <note>Sanskrit: *lagna*; the zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at birth</note>, or likewise from the sign occupied by the Moon at birth, one determines the livelihood of the individual. This is calculated according to the strength of the planets at the time of birth. ||

Where the Lord of the Moon's sign becomes the Lord of the Ascendant, the individual is guided by their own intellect and innate wisdom. || 12 ||

If a malefic planet <note>Sanskrit: *pāpa*; a planet with a challenging influence, such as Saturn or Mars</note> is in the ascendant at the time of sunrise, or if it falls on the day of the week ruled by that planet, the person's conduct is influenced accordingly. However, if the eighth house <note>Sanskrit: *nidhana*; the house associated with death and transformation</note> is occupied by or aspected by benefic planets, the individual shall not meet an untimely death. || 13 ||

If a malefic planet resides in the eighth house from the ascendant, the person may be prone to illness; if this occurs at the time of birth, one should observe the potential for physical decline or chronic disease. || 14 ||

<margin>89</margin> When a benefic planet <note>Sanskrit: *saumya*; a gentle or auspicious planet like Jupiter or Venus</note> is in the eighth house, or if the Sun is in the seventh house, and the Moon is in the ascendant while positioned in a watery sign, the individual may suffer from obesity or disorders of the phlegm. || 15 ||

If the Lord of the sign occupied by Saturn is in the ascendant, or if Saturn itself is positioned there, or if malefic planets occupy the twelfth and seventh houses, the person will likely be sickly and lack physical strength. || 16 ||

When Saturn aspects the Moon, or if the Moon is in an **increasing house** <note>Sanskrit: *upacaya*; the 3rd, 6th, 10th, or 11th houses where planets grow in strength</note>, or if the Sun is in a specific alignment with Mars, there are various effects. || 17 || If there is a flaw in the ascendant, as described by the Sages, and the sixth house <note>Sanskrit: *ṣaṣṭha*; the house of debt, enemies, and disease</note> is aspected, there is no doubt that the person will suffer from persistent illness.

### Technical Vocabulary
*   **Ascendant:** The rising sign at birth, representing the self and physical body.
*   **Eighth House:** The house of longevity and death.
*   **Benefic:** Auspicious planets that bring positive results.
*   **Saturn:** A planet often associated with chronic disease and delays.
*   **Illness:** Physical afflictions resulting from planetary imbalances.
*   **Malefic:** Challenging planets that cause hardship.
*   **Increasing House:** Houses where the influence of a planet improves over time.
*   **Seventh House:** The house of partnerships and the setting sun.
*   **Sixth House:** The house specifically associated with disease and obstacles.
