# ON THE THREEFOLD LIFE OF MAN,
->or<-
### High and Deep Foundations
# Of the Threefold Life of Man.

---

### Brief Summary of this Book.

In this work, the Eternal as well as the Mortal is clearly shown; and why God, the highest Good, has brought all things to light; also why one thing runs against another and perishes; and then what is Right or False, and how one is separated from the other.

2. Within this are primarily established the three **Principles** <note>original: "Principia"; Böhme uses this term to describe the three fundamental realms of existence: the dark fire-world, the light-world, and the outer visible world.</note>, which are the single ground or fountain-spring from which all things flow and are born. From this is clearly recognized the various opinions of faith, why and for what reasons there are so many different opinions among men concerning the Divine Being and Will; also what is useful for man to do so that he may attain the highest Eternal Good.

3. And then concerning the departure and end of all things; why all things have appeared in such torment and in such a state. For the comfort of the poor, wounded, and sick soul of man, and for the edification of the true Christian religion; wherein the Antichrist <note>original: "Wiederchrist"; Böhme often used this term to refer to institutionalized "stone churches" that lacked spiritual life.</note> stands completely naked and revealed.

Composed for our own remembrance and encouragement in this confused and sorrowful time, in the year 1620.
