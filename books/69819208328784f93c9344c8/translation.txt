...religion, which has made God love you and the world honor you. This refutes that common and trivial objection of our common adversary <note>original: "Aduerſarie" (a standard term for the Roman Catholic Church in Protestant polemics of this era)</note> that our religion began with Luther <note>Martin Luther (1483–1546).
