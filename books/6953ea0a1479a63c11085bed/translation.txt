# Preface

---

I hereby present to the friends of Mr. Euler’s writings this translation of the second part of his *Introduction to the Analysis of the Infinite* <note>Original: "Einleitung in die Analysis des Unendlichen"; this refers to Euler's foundational 1748 work *Introductio in analysin infinitorum*</note>, prepared according to the very same rules that guided me during the first part. Regarding the added annotations, I have incorporated them directly into the text wherever clarification seemed necessary, setting them apart in brackets for distinction. However, I have had to be more concise with these notes than in the first part, as the present volume would otherwise have become too bulky. For the same reason, I had to abandon my intention of providing an appendix that would partly present the contents in tabular form and partly expand upon several of the subjects discussed, which would have made this second part entirely similar in format to the first.

# Preface

As reluctantly as I have done this, I could not expect the publishers—especially since so many copperplate engravings <note>original: "Kupfer"; these were expensive, high-quality illustrations etched into copper</note> were necessary for this second part—to provide the previously prepared additions for the price already established. I hope, therefore, that no one will reproach me for this, especially since I intend to avoid such issues entirely by providing the mentioned additions, along with the treatises mentioned in the preface to the first part and the investigation mentioned on page 426 (which likewise became too extensive for the present location), in a third volume as soon as the publisher is willing. Furthermore, I offer my most sincere thanks to the gentlemen reviewers <note>original: "Recensenten"; the academic critics of the day</note> who altogether announced and judged the first part with so much kindness and indulgence, and I wish that the present second part may experience the same kindness and indulgence.
