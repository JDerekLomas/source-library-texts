# Preface to the Fifth Edition.

---

The arrangement of this volume of Kant also corresponds entirely to those previously edited by me (Volumes 39–41, 45, and 46 of the *Philosophical Library* <note>The "Philosophische Bibliothek" is a famous series of foundational philosophical texts published by Meiner</note>). For the text and the history of the work's origin, I was pleased to be able to use the proof sheets of the Academy Edition of the *Critique of Practical Reason* prepared by Paul Natorp, which my esteemed friend, Professor Natorp, gratefully made available to me; meanwhile, I had participated in the revision of the text for that very same Academy Edition. The page numbers of the latter have been indicated by us in square brackets in the margin; regarding the history of the work's origin, the relevant page numbers from Natorp’s introduction and factual explanations were not yet available at the conclusion of this printing.

Solingen, July 31, 1906.

**Prof. Dr. Karl Vorländer.**
