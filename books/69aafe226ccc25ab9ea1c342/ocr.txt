<notes>
- The transliteration is based on the large central column visible in the photograph, which corresponds to the end of Tablet VII of Enūma Eliš.
- Reverse lines 9'-15' correspond to the canonical lines 157-162 of the epic.
- Line 11': 'gi-szar-ru-ti-szu' (his might/strength) is a key phrase in the transition to the epilogue.
- Line 16'-17': Typical Library of Ashurbanipal colophon. 'm {d}aš-šur-DU-A' is the common abbreviation for Ashurbanipal (Ashur-bani-apli).
- The museum fragments visible are K.2421 (top), K.2511 (right edge), and K.16765 (bottom).
</notes>
<vocab>
- e-nu-ma e-lisz: "When on high" (title of the epic)
- {d}AMAR-UTU: Marduk (main deity of the text)
- szu-me-e-szu: his names (referring to the 50 names of Marduk)
- {d}TU-TU: Tutu (one of the names/aspects of Marduk)
- m {d}asz-szur-DU-A: King Ashurbanipal
- NU AL-TIL: "Not finished" (indicating a multi-tablet series)
</vocab>
