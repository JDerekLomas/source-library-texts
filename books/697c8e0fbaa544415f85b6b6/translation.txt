If heart and eye observe these biblical pictorial sayings,
They find within them the most pleasant traces
Of God's holiness and His soul's desire, <note>original: "Seel Begierd"; this refers to God's deep longing for the human soul.</note>
And how His precious word leads as a path to heaven.
Whoever lets this remain their guideline at all times
And writes it upon their soft heart as on a tablet,
Shall be refreshed and delighted in their soul,
And through the external senses, the spirit is set in joy.
Here, here the taste can have the heavenly manna,
Which will most lovely refresh the soul;
The eye beholds here the true sunlight,
Whose clarity no darkness interrupts.
When through the ear into the heart the words of life fall,
It resembles the sweetest tone and the ringing of music.
The fragrance of flowers provides pleasure to the nose,
Yet more does the word's life-force refresh the heart.
Should sensitivity count on gentle rest,
Here is what only pleases, to be felt in richest measure.
Bread is praised as strength, yet only for the body alone;
The word, the food of the soul, shall be eternally healing.
The outstretched hand serves those who travel;
To Christians, the word wants to show the road to heaven;
The fresh spring refreshes the weary body;
For the soul, the stream of comfort flows here ever bright.
If the body falls ill, it finds rescue through medicines;
The word in spiritual distress <note>original: "Seelen-Noth"; a state of existential or religious crisis.</note> brings refreshment and prosperity.

In physical danger, shield and sword protect;
The power of the enemy of the soul <note>A reference to the Devil or temptation.</note> is destroyed through the word.
Who should not seek or love such a treasure,
And practice reading it both day and night?
Who should so neglect their soul's salvation,
Despising that which makes them happy for eternity?
Since now, moreover, this noble heavenly food
Is set before us here in a new way,
And alongside the mind, delights the outer senses,
All weariness and disgust vanish entirely.
Intermingled with pictures, many a saying appears here,
Since this work is intended for the benefit of the youth,
That through the eye into the heart the core might penetrate in good time
And through the grace of the Most High, bring many blessed fruits.
A beautiful pastime, well-suited for children,
This Bible-work may rightly be called,
Since with amusement, the word's dear treasure
Most edifyingly finds a place in the still tender breast.
The healing success, the wonderful trials,
These will praise this work with much utility;
Through its service, it will become known more and more
(At which it solely aims) the praise and honor of the Most High.
Happy is he who delights in looking at these images
And seeks to edify his children through them as well;
The fruit of this looking shall still be known there [in heaven],
Where the joy of beholding God <note>original: "Anschau-Lust"; a theological term for the "Beatific Vision," or the ultimate joy of seeing God in the afterlife.</note> delights us eternally.

That it deserves this title, however, the following brief explanation will make credible. Specifically, as previously mentioned, there are 250 **Biblical Verses** <term>Biblische Sprüche: short passages or maxims from the Bible, often used for moral instruction.</term> contained in this first part, interspersed with elegantly engraved figures, where the verse in the center is always taken according to the order of the books of **Holy Scripture** <term>S. Schrifft: abbreviation for "Heilige Schrift," meaning the Bible.</term>.

Then, for the service of those who might not have a Bible at hand, and also to prevent false interpretation or incorrect pronunciation, all occurring verses are printed separately and word-for-word beforehand; therein, the figure is always expressed with **red letters**, which the inexperienced can use as a **key**. The frequently occurring **hearts** <term>Hertzen: a common symbol in 18th-century devotional art, representing the human soul or the seat of faith.</term> are enclosed in this first part with **wreaths** <term>Kräntzen: decorative circular arrangements.</term> of all kinds of plants, flowers, and herbs, according to their nature, and a special index regarding the **coloration** <term>Coloriten: the arrangement or choice of colors in a work of art.</term> is placed before them. Art-loving young people may well benefit from the neat handwriting, good drawings, and clean strokes, etc. 

The main objective, aiming for **God’s honor and the pleasant instruction of youth**, will recommend this work without further boasting, when the outcome shows, to the heartfelt delight of Christian parents, how through this God-pleasing pastime even small children learn the most beautiful verses by heart as if **playing**, how idle hours are usefully shortened, how they so gracefully exercise and demonstrate their **judgment** <term>Judicium: the faculty of discernment or critical thinking.</term> and at the same time, through the help of the beautiful figures, obtain an **idea** <term>Ideam oder Begriff: a conceptual image or mental representation.</term> or concept of many things that would not have come to their sight for a long time; the early recognition of these things will be highly beneficial to them for learning the language and for the precise understanding of what they hear and read. In the undoubted hope of desired success and favorable approval and reception of this work (which has also been **illuminated** <term>illuminirt: hand-colored, usually with watercolors, to enhance the detail of the engravings.</term> for even greater delight and a deeper impression on the power of the imagination), the author recommends this and his other publications to art-lovers with becoming respect.
