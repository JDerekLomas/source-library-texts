# Indication of the Title Figure concerning the
## GREAT MYSTERY.

*Microcosm* <note>The "little world," referring to Man as a reflection of the universe.</note> and *Macrocosm* <note>The "great world," or the universe at large.</note>, *Moses* and *Messiah* are the *Great Mystery* <note>original: "Mysterium Magnum"</note> and great secret / like fire and light / law and grace / image and essence / which are unequal things / and must nevertheless be together / and love one another / for they are born out of the Unity / which has no life nor joy / except from these two / and yet is the life of them both.

But where God’s light and Spirit hides within itself / and does not flow out of itself / there is no true life / light / joy / peace nor rest / as is to be seen in the *Macrocosm*. For although the sunlight shines beautifully and gladdens everything / and the stars shimmer lovely / it is nevertheless only a modeled power in the conflict of light and darkness / of water and fire / which pursue one another / therefore the life of the spirit of the *Greater World* <note>original: "Majoris Mundi"</note> stands in anxiety and unrest / so that it constantly rolls and turns / and can find no satisfaction in its own light. And as it is / so also are its creatures / which lead a conflicting life against one another / for they inherit its driving fire-spirit / which He lets be seen in dark clouds / mist / steam / thunder / lightning and impetuous winds as his trumpets / so that everything is afraid / and flees before his terrible face / until the gentle water is born / and the sunshine breaks through: and so it drives forth through fixed times and years until the Judgment / when everything shall go through the fire / the good be separated from the evil and come to rest.

So also is *Adam* the image of God which was swallowed up in him through *Imagination* <note>In this context, a creative or formative power that can lead to a fall into material desire.</note> and became animal-like / also burdened with its spirit / nature and property; he drives his wonders in hate / envy / wrath / murder / self-mastery / violence / tyranny / as well as vain lust and joys in arts / piping / fiddling / as also heavy toil with plowing / building / planting and cattle-breeding / until the figure is fulfilled with Pharaoh and Egypt <note>A metaphor for spiritual bondage and the material world.</note> / so that man has lost the childlike freedom and his Paradisiacal Fatherland / and fallen under a stupid and animal-like compulsion and power / where he must form dead things for Death / and make dead stones out of the earth.

<summary>This preface introduces the "Mysterium Magnum" as the interplay between dualities like fire and light or Moses and Christ, explaining how the physical world (Macrocosm) and humanity (Adam) exist in a state of restless conflict until spiritual restoration.</summary>
<keywords>Mysterium Magnum, Microcosm, Macrocosm, Adam, duality, spiritual alchemy, Fall of Man, nature of God</keywords>

Therefore, God awakens a figure of redemption in the zeal of justice through Moses. In an imitation of the same process as the creation of the spirit of this world, Moses was cast into the water after his birth out of terror and fear of death from Pharaoh. He was then called in the fire <note>referring to the Burning Bush</note> (as the root of the strong power of life). God gives him the office of a leader and a mighty savior according to the governance of the spirit of this world, so that he rules over Pharaoh and Egypt with wonders and deeds as a "little God" <note>original: "kleiner GOtt"; a reference to Exodus 7:1 where Moses is made as a god to Pharaoh</note> and creator. He thereafter drives and leads the <term>Mysterium Microcosmi</term> <note>The Mystery of the Microcosm: the inner world of man reflecting the universe</note>, as the separated and fallen qualities of human life (like the stars in the sky) in the figure of the children of Israel. And he forces them with power toward the obedience of God through the Law, with terror and the horrific sight of dark clouds, steam, thunder, lightning, and the sound of trumpets. In the office of his spirit of zeal, he goes forth to destroy the mystery of the causes of sin and abomination outside of himself in the world through war, sword, slaughter, and the extermination of the heathens and foreign peoples; as well as to reconcile their own actual sins with the slaughter and fire-offerings of cattle, through the time of Joshua, the Judges, Kings, and Prophets, until the time of the Messiah, when the gentle baptism of water begins and the Sun of Life rises in Christ.

There the prototype ceases, and the fiery zeal of the Law must die and rest; for it cannot lead the human mystery through the wrath of God. Therefore, He calls Joshua—as Jesus—a second time into the Mystery upon Mount Tabor, and hands over to Him the <term>Mysterium Macrocosmi and Microcosmi</term> <note>The Mystery of the Great World (Universe) and the Little World (Man)</note>, so that He may destroy the introduced malice of the Devil in the world with the fire and sword of judgment. However, He leads man in the spirit of His love through the sacrifice of His own Divine flesh and blood through the wrath of God, and divides the land of Paradise for eternal rest. This land is still in this world, but covered with the curse of vanity, just as Moses’ glorified face was covered with the veil, Christ is covered with the false hypocrisy of the Anti-christ, and the Scripture is covered with secret prototypes and figures. Yet these are now, with the opening of the 7th Seal by the Angel of the 7th Trumpet, unlocked, discovered, shown, and unwound.

->**As enlightened eyes see in this book and in all the others.**<-
