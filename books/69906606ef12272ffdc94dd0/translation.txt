### On the Nature of Spiritual Ascent

There is a possibility that spiritual ascent occurs in two ways:
First, the ascent may be viewed from the perspective of reception, based on its existence within the intellect. However, if the ascent is viewed from the perspective of the one perceiving it, then if the perceiver draws close and the light grows upon him, he may appear weak <note>original: "chaloosh" - perhaps referring to the overwhelming nature of the Divine light</note>; yet if he remains at a distance, it may grow great in his eyes and he will say **"Holy, Holy"** <note>Isaiah 6:3</note>, and this is among the ascents toward the Holy One, Blessed be He.
The perceiver says "Ascent" and "Attainment," and it seems that even if he attains it, the attained level will be relative to the perceiver; from the perspective of the object attained, it will be perfect and its nature exalted in his eyes. And it is said: "He who follows after thought," and this is the true ascent.
