**D**espite what has been claimed regarding the religious beliefs of the Druids—whose abilities were said to overcome even the most evil tendencies through wise instruction—and however much they were praised for their virtue and morality, they permitted widespread and incestuous concubinage <note>The practice of living together and having sexual relations without being married; the author implies this occurred between close relatives</note>. This was a fault that not only Julius Caesar but other authors have identified <note>original: "descried"</note> as a sign of their barbarism, and which every rational person must condemn as a horrible depravity.

Trade with the Phoenicians was extensive.
