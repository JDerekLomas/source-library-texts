This page features a hand-mounted circular emblem that serves as a symbolic introduction to the volume. The central imagery is framed by a border containing the title **Hermetic Philosophy**, a term used to describe a tradition of knowledge that combines spiritual wisdom, alchemy, and the study of nature’s hidden laws.

At the heart of the emblem is a blue field enclosed by a green wreath. At the top, a **golden sun** radiates light, representing divine intelligence and the goal of spiritual illumination. Beneath the sun, a **pelican** is depicted "in her piety." 

<note>The "Pelican in her Piety" is a traditional image of a mother bird feeding her chicks with blood from her own breast. The combination of the sun, the self-sacrificing bird, and the floral cross illustrates the path of the "Great Work"—the transformation of the soul and the discovery of hidden truths.
