# Heavenly Metal is in the positions of Shen and You; Earthly Metal is in the positions of Zi and Chou.
<note>original: 申酉 (Shen, You) and 子丑 (Zi, Chou). These are Earthly Branches. Shen and You are naturally associated with the element of Metal.
