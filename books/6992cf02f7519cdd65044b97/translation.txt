Equal parts metal and tin <note>A 50/50 ratio, creating a highly reflective but very brittle "white bronze"</note> is called the **alloy for mirrors and fire-mirrors** <term>Jiansui: Polished bronze mirrors and "burning-mirrors" used to focus sunlight to start fires</term>.
