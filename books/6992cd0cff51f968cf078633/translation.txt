The Horn <note>original: "角" (Jiao). This mansion consists of two primary stars (Alpha and Zeta Virginis) and serves as the lead constellation of the Azure Dragon of the East.</note> consists of two stars and spans twelve degrees. It acts as the primary governor of the creation and transformation of the myriad things, and spreads the Emperor's majesty and trust. It is called the Celestial Gatetower <note>original: "天闕" (Tianque). In ancient Chinese architecture, "que" were ceremonial gate towers; here, the stars are seen as the entrance to the heavens.</note>. Its gate is the Celestial Gate, and its interior is the Celestial Court. Therefore, the Ecliptic <term>Yellow Path: the apparent path of the sun across the celestial sphere</term> passes through its center; it is the path where the Seven Luminaries <term>Seven Luminaries: the Sun, the Moon, and the five visible planets (Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn)</term> travel.

If these stars are bright, there is great peace in the world. If they <gloss>flicker</gloss> or shift, the nation will be restless. If a solar eclipse occurs at the Right Horn, the nation will not be at peace. If a lunar eclipse occurs at the Left Horn, the roads of the world will be severed <note>This likely refers to a breakdown in communication, trade, or social order</note>. If Venus or Mars encroaches upon these stars, there will be war and enemies. If Venus lingers there, a Great General will take control of the government.

The Left Horn is near the Celestial Fields <note>original: "天田" (Tiantian). Stars representing the Emperor’s personal ritual farmland</note>; it governs legal principles and presides over punishments. To its south lies the Solar Path; if the Five Planets <note>Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn</note> encroach upon it, there will be drought.

The Right Horn represents the General and governs military affairs. To its north lies the Lunar Path; if the Five Planets encroach upon it, there will be floods.

Generally, the "Three Gates of Heaven" serve as markers for the sky much like the "Four Pillars" do for the Mansion <note>A reference to the "Mansion" (Fang) lunar mansion, used here as a comparison for celestial orientation</note>. In divining these stars, if they are bright and large, the world is at peace and virtuous people hold office. If they shake or shift position, it indicates the King is traveling.

Between the Left and Right Horns are two stars called the Level Road <note>original: "平道" (Pingdao)</note>. These represent the eight-way thoroughfares used by the Son of Heaven. If they are bright and correctly positioned, it is auspicious. If they shake or waver, there is danger for the Imperial carriage. The two stars of the Great Fields <note>original: "大田" (Datian); likely a variation of "Celestial Fields"</note> are located to the north of the Left and Right Horns; they govern the Imperial Domain <note>The administrative region directly surrounding the capital</note>.

Those stars gathered in three groups of three are **The Pillars** <note>original: "柱" (Zhu). These are nine stars in the constellation Centaurus, envisioned as the structural supports for the celestial armory.</note>. The four small stars in the center are **The Guards** <note>original: "衛" (Wei)</note>. They govern the deployment of troops.

If a **guest star** <term>Guest Star: a temporary celestial visitor, such as a comet, nova, or supernova</term> lingers near them, it signifies the arrival of invading troops.

It governs the Son of Heaven's inner court as well as the world's rituals and legal codes. <note>original: "亢" (Kang). This mansion is the second of the twenty-eight lunar mansions. Furthermore, it acts as an outer temple and governs epidemics. 

When these stars appear bright and large, the people of the four seas submit to the King, his ministers offer their loyalty, and the populace remains free from disease. However, if the stars shift from their positions, illnesses will multiply. If they become invisible, the world will fall into turmoil <note>literally "bubbling like a cauldron"</note> and droughts or floods will arise.

Two feet below the sixteenth degree of the **Root Mansion** <note>original: "氐" (Di); the third lunar mansion</note> lies the central path for the five planets, the sun, and the moon. This is the **Imperial Living Quarters** <note>original: "路寢" (luqin)</note> of the **Son of Heaven** <note>the Emperor</note>. If these stars are bright, the high officials and the imperial consorts will serve their ruler without failing in their duties. If they are not visible or if they shift and move, it signifies that officials will plot internal treachery, and chaos will arise. 

Solar and lunar eclipses here signify internal rebellion. If **Jupiter** <note>original: "木" (The Wood Star)</note> encroaches upon this area, it portends worry for the empress and consorts. If **Mars** <note>original: "火" (The Fire Star)</note> encroaches, it signifies rebellion from high-ranking officials. If **Venus** <note>original: "金" (The Metal Star)</note> encroaches, it suggests the appointment of generals. # The Tail Mansion

The **Tail Mansion** <term>Wei Mansion</term> <note>The sixth lunar mansion of the Azure Dragon, located in the tail of the constellation Scorpius.</note> spans nineteen degrees. It is the residence of the **Empress and Consorts** <term>Houfei</term> and the grounds of the **Imperial Harem** <note>the secluded living quarters for the Emperor's wives and concubines</note>. Located ten feet to the north is the **Central Path of Heaven** <term>Tian zhi Zhongdao</term> <note>A term referring to the Ecliptic, the sun's apparent path across the sky.</note>.

Within this mansion, the first star at the top represents the **Empress** <term>Hou</term>. The next three stars represent the **High Consorts** <term>Furen</term>, and those following represent the **Legal Concubines** <term>Diqie</term>. The **Tail** is also known as the **Nine Children Stars** <term>Jiuzi Xing</term>.

### Omens of the Harem

If the colors of these stars are uniformly bright and their sizes follow a consistent sequence, it is an omen that the Empress and Consorts will not be jealous of one another, the internal order of the harem will be maintained, and there will be many descendants. However:
*   If the stars appear tiny and dim, the Empress will suffer from worry or illness.
*   If the stars appear scattered or distant from one another, the Empress will lose her influence and status.
*   If the stars appear to tremble or shift, the ruler and his ministers will fall into discord, leading to chaos throughout the land.
*   If the stars cluster together, it foretells great flooding.

### Planetary Visitations

The movement of the planets through this mansion provides specific warnings:
*   **Jupiter** <note>original: "Wood star"</note> encroaching upon the mansion or a halo appearing around the **Moon** <note>original: "Yue"</note> indicates the death of the Empress or a high consort.
*   **Mars** <note>original: "Fire star"</note> encroaching upon the inner court of stars indicates internal rebellion.
*   **Saturn** <note>original: "Earth star"</note> encroaching is an auspicious sign of stability.
*   **Mercury** <note>original: "Water star"</note> encroaching signifies that there will be "troubles" or administrative affairs to settle within the inner court.
*   **Guest Stars** <term>Guest Star</term> <note>A sudden celestial event, such as a comet or a supernova.</note> encroaching indicate the execution of high-ranking ministers.
*   **Solar or Lunar Eclipses** occurring here signify that the people will suffer from famine.
*   If **Venus** <note>original: "Metal star"</note> and **Mars** stay within the mansion for a full day, an armed uprising will begin within the palace.

### Associated Asterisms

**The Tortoise** <term>Gui</term>
The five stars of the Tortoise are located south of the Tail, within the **Milky Way** <note>original: "Han"</note>. They are used to determine fortune and misfortune:
*   If they are bright, the ruler and his ministers are in harmony.

## The Ox Mansion <term>Niu</term>

The Ox mansion spans seven degrees. It serves as the gate and bridge of the Heavens and acts as the central path <note>original: 中道 (zhongdao); refers to the ecliptic or the path where planets are observed</note> for the Sun, the Moon, and the five planets. It primarily governs sacrificial animals <term>xisheng</term> <note>Animals such as oxen, sheep, or pigs prepared for state ritual offerings</note>. 

To its north are two stars: one is called the Path and the other is called the Gathering Fire. Alternatively, it is said that the single uppermost star governs roads, the next two stars govern gates and bridges, and the following three stars govern the Southern Yue region. 

Master Gan <note>Refers to Gan De, a famous 4th-century BCE Chinese astronomer</note> states: The uppermost star governs roads, the next two stars govern gates and bridges, and the next three stars govern the Southern Barbarians. 

### Astrological Omens

If the central star of the Ox moves, the cattle will suffer many calamities. If the stars are bright and large, the King’s Way shall flourish. If the stars appear curved or distorted, the price of buying grain <term>di</term> will become expensive. 

It is also said: 
*   If the stars are bright and large, the gates and bridges of the empire will be open and passable. 
*   If the Ox stars appear "angry" <note>An astronomical term for stars that appear unusually piercing or flickering</note>, horses will become expensive. 
*   If the stars are dim or lose their regular appearance, the grain harvest will not be successful. 
*   If the stars are faint or small, the price of cattle will fall. 
*   If the central star moves up or down, many cattle will die. 
*   If the smaller stars are affected, there will be many epidemics among the herds. 
*   A lunar halo appearing around these stars signifies the loss of calves.

**Planetary Encounters:**
If Venus <note>original: 金 (Gold Planet)</note> or Mars <note>original: 火 (Fire Planet)</note> trespasses into this mansion, there will be disasters of war. If Mercury <note>original: 水 (Water Planet)</note> or Saturn <note>original: 土 (Earth Planet)</note> trespasses, it is an auspicious sign.

**The Celestial Field** <term>Tiantian</term>
The Celestial Field consists of nine stars. It is located south of the Ox mansion and east of the Supreme Tenuity <term>Taiwei</term> enclosure. It governs the agricultural lands within the Emperor's private domain <term>jinei</term>. Its prognostications are the same as those for the Celestial Field located near the Horn mansion.

**The Nine Pits** <term>Jiukan</term>
The Nine Pits consist of nine black stars located to the south of the Celestial Field. They govern ditches and canals, which are used to guide and reach the headwaters of springs and to drain overflows. If these stars appear full, blocked, collapsed, or irregular, it signifies issues with water management.
