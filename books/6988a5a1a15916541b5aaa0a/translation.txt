## Warning against Lust.

---

To resist the lure of lust—
Youth, if you love happiness and life,
Let this be your daily wisdom.
Flee from flattering desire;
It robs you of your heart's ornament, <note>original: "des Herzens Zierde." In the moral language of the time, this refers to one's inner purity, honor, or virtue.</note>
And its joys turn into pain.

So as not to increase its nourishment,
Never let food and drink weigh down your heart
And be a friend to sobriety.
To conquer yourself,

And steer your sensuality. <note>The text suggests that "sensuality" (Sinnlichkeit) is like a ship or a horse that must be steered or controlled by the will.</note>

Flee the bold jokes of the shallow wit, <note>original: "Witzling." A derogatory term for someone who thinks they are clever but lacks true wisdom or moral depth.</note>
And seek example, true wit, and pastime
In the company of noble hearts.
