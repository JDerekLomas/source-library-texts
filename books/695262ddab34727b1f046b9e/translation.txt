Then you will have the earth, the water, and the air; and although the earth contains within itself the nature of fire, nevertheless it is not yet apparent in effect, as you will see when, by a greater decoction, you make it become red, so that then you will manifestly see the fire in appearance. And thus one must proceed to the **Fermentation** of the white earth.

# THE THIRD OPERATION

## REDDENING

Take as much of the white Medicine as you wish and place it with its glass vessel upon hot ashes, until it is as dry as they are. Afterward, give it the <term>Water of the Sun</term> <note>likely a solution involving gold or a highly refined solar essence</note>, which you shall have kept aside for this task, and continue the fire at the second degree <note>a moderate heat</note> until it becomes dry. Then give it the aforementioned water again, and thus successively soak and dry it, until the matter turns red and liquefies like wax, and runs upon the red-hot plate as was said; then the matter will be perfect at the red stage. But note that each time you must not add more of the Solar Water than what is needed to cover the body, and no more, and this is done so that the <term>Elixir</term> is not submerged and drowned. Thus must the fire be continued until it is dry, and then the second soaking must be performed, proceeding in order until the perfection of the Medicine—that is, until the power of the fire's digestion converts it into a very red powder, which is the true Oil of the Philosophers, the Bloodstone, the Purple Red Coral, the Precious Ruby, the Red Mercury, and the Red Tincture.

The more you dissolve and coagulate, the more its power will multiply unto infinity. But note, that the <term>Medicine</term><note>In alchemy, the "Medicine" is another name for the Philosopher's Stone, believed to "heal" base metals into gold or the human body into perfect health.</note> multiplies more slowly by solution than by <term>Fermentation</term>. ## EPILOGUE FOLLOWING HERMES.

<image-desc>Large ornamental woodcut initial 'A' decorated with floral motifs.</image-desc>
Thus you shall separate the earth from the fire, the gross from the subtle, gently and with great ingenuity—that is to say, you shall separate the parts joined in the furnace by the dissolution and the separation of the parts, such as the earth from the fire, the subtle from the thick, and so on. This means the purest substance of the <term>Stone</term> until it remains clean for you, without any stain or filth. And when it says, *It ascends from the earth to the Heaven, and then once again returns to earth*<note>A direct reference to the "Emerald Tablet," a foundational cryptic text of alchemy attributed to Hermes Trismegistus.</note>, one must understand the <term>sublimation</term> of bodies. Again, to properly explain <term>distillation</term>, it says, *The wind carries it in its belly*—namely, when the water distills through the <term>Alembic</term><note>A classic distillation apparatus consisting of two vessels connected by a tube.</note>, where it first rises by the smoky and vaporous wind, and afterwards returns to the bottom of the vessel as water again. Wanting further to show the <term>congelation</term> of the matter, it says: *Its strength is entire if it returns to earth*—that is to say, if it is converted by <term>decoction</term><note>Prolonged heating or boiling to extract or concentrate the essence of a substance.</note>. And to generally demonstrate all the aforementioned things, it says, *And it shall receive the inferior and superior strength*—that is, of the <term>Elements</term>, for if the Medicine receives the strength of the light parts (namely air and fire), it will also receive the more grave and heavy parts, changing into water and earth. This is so that the matters thus perpetually conjoined may have permanence, endurance, firmness, and stability. Praised be GOD.
