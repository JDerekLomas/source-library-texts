# GOD IS LOVE

## THE PUREST.
<note>The title refers to the theological concept of "Pure Love" (French: *Amour Pur*), a prominent theme in 17th and 18th-century spirituality which describes loving God entirely for His own sake, without regard for personal reward or salvation.</note>
