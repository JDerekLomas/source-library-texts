->**A Treatise on the Preservation of Knowledge**<-

# On the Nature of the Elements

It is necessary, before we proceed to the operations of the <term>Great Work<note>The 'Magnum Opus' or the process of creating the Philosopher's Stone.</note></term>, to understand the fourfold division of the world. All things that are seen and unseen are composed of the primary qualities: Heat, Cold, Moisture, and Dryness.

<margin>The four roots of all things.</margin>

As the ancient masters taught, <note>original: "ignis, aer, aqua, terra"</note> Fire, Air, Water, and Earth are not merely the substances we touch, but the spiritual principles that govern change.
