**The Life and Doctrine of Louis Claude de Saint-Martin,** the Unknown Philosopher, and the Substance of his Transcendental Doctrine. Demy 8vo, 7s. 6d.

**Azoth, or, The Star in the East.** Imp. 8vo, Cloth. Cheaper editions. 5s. 6d. net.

> "The conclusions reached in this work are the result of considerable research into the significance of the Hermetic and Mystical Symbolisms contained in alchemical literature, and, as regards fundamental doctrine, they offer a reasonable harmony between the transcendental teachings of Eastern and Western Theosophy."

> "The lessons these stories are intended to convey are unmistakeable, but not so obtrusive as to weary even those of tender years, and so charming is the language in which the stories are told, so picturesque the scenes presented, that many children will turn to these pages again and again with delight."

# SEPHIROTIC SCHEME.
## AIN SOPH
### LATENT STATE OF DEITY.

<image-desc>A geometric diagram representing the "Sephirotic Scheme" or Tree of Life. It consists of ten primary nodes (Sephirot) and one semi-transparent node (Daath), connected by straight lines. The nodes are arranged in three vertical columns, labeled as pillars.

**The Pillars:**
- **The Left Pillar:** Labeled vertically as "PILLAR OF SEVERITY." and "LEFT HAND". It contains the nodes Binah, Geburah, and Hod.
- **The Central Pillar:** Labeled vertically as "CENTRAL PILLAR OF BENIGNITY." It contains the nodes Kether, Daath, Tiphereth, Jesod, and Malkuth.
- **The Right Pillar:** Labeled vertically as "RIGHT HAND" and "PILLAR OF MERCY." It contains the nodes Chokmah, Chesed, and Netzach.

**The Sephirot (Nodes) from top to bottom:**
- **KETHER**: Subtitled "The Crown". Positioned at the very top center.
- **BINAH** (Left): Subtitled "Understanding."
- **DAATH** (Center): Subtitled "Knowledge."
- **CHOKMAH** (Right): Subtitled "Wisdom,"
- **GEBURAH** (Left): Subtitled "Severity."
- **TIPHERETH** (Center): Subtitled "Beauty."
- **CHESED** (Right): Subtitled "Mercy."
- **HOD** (Left): Subtitled "Glory."
- **JESOD** (Center): Subtitled "Foundation."
- **NETZACH** (Right): Subtitled "Victory."
- **MALKUTH**: Subtitled "The Kingdom." Positioned at the very bottom center.

Connecting lines form a complex web: Kether connects to Binah, Daath, and Chokmah. Horizontal lines connect Binah-Daath-Chokmah and Geburah-Tiphereth-Chesed and Hod-Jesod-Netzach. Diagonal lines cross between the levels, forming various triangles and a central hexagram-like structure around Tiphereth. Lines also extend from Hod, Jesod, and Netzach down to Malkuth at the base.</image-desc>
