# **RAYMOND LULL OF MAJORCA**,
## most acute philosopher and most celebrated physician,
### *Preface to the first book on the secrets of nature, or the Fifth Essence.*

The Book of the SECRETS OF NATURE, or of the fifth essence <note>Latin: *quinta essentia*. In medieval science, this was a "fifth element"—distinct from earth, water, air, and fire—thought to be the pure substance of the stars and the key to universal medicine.</note>, which teaches its extraction and application to human bodies, for the performing of wonderful and almost divine works. Through this, the philosophical path of the entire medical art is to be completed; while this path is hidden, the true medicine is also hidden, and even the transformation <term>transmutation: the alchemical process of changing one substance, usually a base metal, into another, like gold</term> of metals is blocked. But when these secrets are unlocked, whatever belongs to them is revealed. This book is indeed the image <note>original: *imago*, here used in the sense of a summary or a representative model.</note> of all books treating these matters, which glorious God has shown to us, so that He might preserve our body from corruption as much as is naturally possible, until the end ordained for us by God; and also so that even imperfect metals themselves may be perfected and transformed into one another. For truly it is my desire that the practitioners <note>original: *artistae*, meaning those who practice the "Great Art" of alchemy or medicine.</note> of this art, contemplating God, might rather understand, remember, and love God through good works—by helping the poor, widows, and orphans in distress without demanding a high price, and by doing divine works of this kind. Let them not be like the one who hid the lord’s talent and did not put it to the use committed to him, as Matthew writes in chapter 25 <note>A reference to the Biblical Parable of the Talents (Matthew 25:14-30), where a servant is punished for burying his master's money rather than using it fruitfully.</note>. Trusting, moreover, in Him who said through Moses: "I will be in your mouth, and I will teach you what you shall speak," <note>Exodus 4:12</note> we have decided that this most secret of secrets, this mystery of mysteries <note>original: *archanorum archanum*. An "arcanum" refers to a deep, hidden secret of nature known only to initiates.</note>, must be declared to you, with divine power inspiring us and His infinite goodness helping us, while making a brief mention of the principles and rules, and of the course of nature itself.

# ON THE FIFTH ESSENCE

Furthermore, you must not be ignorant of the **power** <term>uirtus: the inherent active strength or spiritual essence of a substance</term> that is produced by the joining of the three stated principles and their derivation. The knowledge of this power is gained through its primary effect, which arises from distinct quantities and qualities, and even from the movement of the heavens and stars, and from things that can be generated and destroyed—those things which are moved and **given form** <gloss>informed</gloss> by it.

Truly, we do not say that it is the task of the practitioner to work with astrological figures or images of the sky through a technical knowledge of their movements, as many philosophers claim. Instead, it is enough to have knowledge of the influence of **celestial heat**, which is shaped by the configuration of the sky and stars. By reason of these configurations, powers are poured into suitably prepared matter.

The matter receives these powers through the natural industry of the practitioner, along with the **dissolution** <term>resolutione: the process of breaking a substance down into its primary components or "resolving" them into a liquid state</term> which is likewise performed by the art of nature. This process changes hard matter back to its natural beginning so that, in the end, it is fit for generation—ready to receive the powers infused into the waters of strong mineral virtues, just as the matter of an embryo takes and receives its formative power.
