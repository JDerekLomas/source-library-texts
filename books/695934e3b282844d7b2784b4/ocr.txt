A detailed mystical and cosmological engraving. The upper third of the page contains the title text. The lower two-thirds features a complex diagram centered on a large circle.

In the center of the diagram is a vertical axis topped with a fleur-de-lis. A banner wrapped around the upper part of the staff reads "Allen Völckern". Below this is a radiant eye set within a heart-shaped burst of light. Underneath the eye, a series of banners are stacked horizontally, reading from top to bottom: "Christen, Juden", "Türcken u Heijden", and "Allen Völckern". At the bottom of this central axis is a trumpet pointing down toward a globe of the Earth; a final banner next to the trumpet's bell reads "Zur Letzten".

The main circle contains two concentric rings: the outermost ring features the letters of the alphabet (A-Z, with 'I' used for 'J' and 'V' for 'U'), and the inner ring features the numbers 1 through 12. 

Above the main circle, a crown contains another eye, surmounted by seven stars and one central larger star. A cross and a palm or olive branch are crossed behind the crown. To the right of the central circle, a disembodied hand emerges from a cloud holding a sword. The background is composed of various intersecting arcs and circles, and a globe showing landmasses and seas is visible at the very bottom.
