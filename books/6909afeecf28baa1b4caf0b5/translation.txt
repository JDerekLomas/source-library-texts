The ways of the Lord were always beyond human understanding, and his footsteps were hidden in the great deep. So let not men find fault with their Maker because they cannot understand his words or his wisdom. Who is the man that can direct the Almighty? Or who can find him out to perfection? For those who judged my writings as nonsense, I must be plain and tell them that they do not understand good sense. 

If they came from myself, I would not say one word to justify them; I would blush to praise my own works. But knowing every truth in them—that they are from the Spirit of the Living God—I will take it upon myself to say that they are spoken with a wisdom greater than has ever been in man. They are brought about in such a manner as to fulfill the Bible; it is impossible for any spirit to align them so closely to the Bible if that spirit did not inspire men to write it in the first place. He who was the author of the first is now the finisher of both, whose unerring wisdom men have presumed to find fault with in every age of the world. 

But I am ready to stand trial and meet my judges as they are appointed for me. My trust is in the God of my salvation: he has promised to be with me in the fire, and the waters shall not drown me. Had I learned the foolish nonsense of this world, then the people of the world would have understood it; but as the natural man does not know the things of God, for they are spiritually discerned <note>A reference to 1 Corinthians 2:14: "The natural person does not accept the things of the Spirit of God... because they are spiritually discerned."</note>, then let not men judge things they know nothing about, nor involve themselves in matters too high for them. I have already told you my writings must be judged by learned theologians <term>original: "divines"</term>; and the sheep must be led by the voice of the shepherds.

And now I will call every man to bear witness from his own conscience: has perfect peace and true happiness ever remained in your home for a long time? ## FIFTH PART.

Having published such wondrous prophecies to the world—prophecies that many find difficult to believe and others judge to be the result of a disordered and confused mind—I will inform my readers that my head has been so "disordered" since my youth, as were the heads of my ancestors. Therefore, if the world judges that it is madness to have a firm belief in the Lord, to rely on all the truths of the Bible written by men inspired by God, and to believe the words left on record by our blessed Lord and Savior, then I was born mad. My mother was also "mad" before me, as readers will see in this volume from the brief account I will give of my life.
