## The 46th Figure.

->The Resurrection of the King.<- <note>original: "Resuscitatio Regis"</note>

Now the highest power has been given to our King, and he carries such great gifts from the paternal present. Although he overcomes everything, he is nonetheless overcome by a servant: this servant dissolves the King's limbs through his sprouting forth; however, the Mother gathers the dissolved limbs, and so both the Mother and the servant receive great honor from it.

## Interpretation.

Our multiplied red Tincture <note>the final medicine or "Stone" in its red, solar form</note>, as the Son of the Sun, now possesses the greatest power in the metallic kingdom; he can place golden crowns upon all. If he wishes to multiply himself further, however, he must allow himself to be killed by a servant. Because no one is his equal, his former oils and spirits are very lowly compared to his current highness and nobility; yet, since they sprang from his previous blood—from his grandparents, who were only poor peasants and subjects—they dissolve his limbs. But because the King possesses such great virtue and is in his previous bath of blood and sweat, the lowly blood is improved by the Royal blood. The fixed Jupiter <term>♃; tin, representing a stable, metallic state</term> maintains the volatile one, and the volatile one dissolves [the King]; and once again, through this struggle, the King becomes high and glorious, expanding his power and dominion. His land—the earth—is increased, and he tinges <note>original: "tingiret"; the alchemical act of transforming the nature and color of a base metal into gold</note> even much further than before.

## The 47. Figure.
### Three Fountains of Everlasting Water. <note>original Latin: "Tres Fontes perennis Aquae"</note>

We three watery fountains are seen within a cavern of a hidden heavenly rock, and are adorned with heavenly waters. To us, the Sun <term>☉; representing the solar, masculine principle or gold</term> and Moon <term>☽; representing the lunar, feminine principle or silver</term> give their powers, and all the stars are favorable to us through their rays. Whoever drinks from us shall live according to a fittingly renewed youth, and continue his desired whiteness <note>the "Albedo" or whitening stage of the alchemical process, signifying purification</note>.

## Interpretation.

The three watery fountains in the hidden caverns of the heavenly rock are: the first is the white water, the Virgin’s Milk; the second is the Blood; and the third is the Oil. All three are produced by the hard rock. These three fountains can increase the weights of the philosophical gold and silver. Also, the Sun and Moon will grow from this and yield their fruits within it once they have become the waters of health. Whoever drinks of it will live in constant youth—that is, in constant health.

## The 48. Figure.
### The Nymph of our Sea.

I, who was once conceived and born of a fluid father who so often flows around the entire earth, I pour out for you from our breasts milk with blood, which two give cooked, pleasant weights of gold.
