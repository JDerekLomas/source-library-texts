# Front Cover of the Manuscript

->[Exterior Binding]<-

The manuscript is encased in a traditional brown leather binding, a testament to the craftsmanship of its time. The surface is decorated using a technique known as **blind-tooling** <note>Blind-tooling involves pressing heated metal tools into the leather to create decorative patterns without the use of gold leaf or ink, resulting in a subtle, recessed texture.</note>. 

The design features several concentric rectangular borders that frame the entire cover. In the center, three decorative **medallions** are arranged in a vertical line. These medallions are shaped like **lozenges** <note>A diamond-shaped or rhombus-like geometric figure.</note> and contain intricate patterns pressed into the leather. 

<note>This style of binding, using leather over wooden boards with central geometric motifs, is highly characteristic of historical Armenian and Near Eastern bookbinding traditions. It was designed both for durability and to signify the importance of the text held within.</note>
