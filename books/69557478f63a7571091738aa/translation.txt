## To the Divine Spirits <note>original: "D M" or "Dis Manibus," a traditional Roman funerary invocation</note>

# OF HERMANN DIELS

->I call to you, the one beneath the earth, once a corpse,<-
->But now dwelling among the gods, a wise man among the wise.<-
<note>original: "ΣΕ ΔΗ ΛΙΕ ΑΥΔΩ ΤΟΝ ΚΑΤΑ ΧΘΟΝΟΣ ΝΕΚΥΝ / ΝΥΝ ΔΕ ΕΝ ΘΕΟΙΣ ΝΑΙΟΝΤΑ ΜΕΤΑ ΣΟΦΩΝ ΣΟΦΟΝ." This is a Greek funerary epigram composed in dactylic hexameter, honoring Diels' status as a preeminent scholar of ancient wisdom.</note>

<summary>A formal dedication page honoring the renowned classical philologist Hermann Diels, featuring a traditional Roman invocation and a Greek funerary poem. It signifies Diels' transition from the mortal realm to a place of honor among the great thinkers of antiquity.</summary>
<keywords>Hermann Diels, dedication, funerary epigram, Dis Manibus, classical scholarship, Greek verse, memorial</keywords>
