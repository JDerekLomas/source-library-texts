# A Very High and Secret Art / which is the most excellent and noble part of Solomon.

Take <note>original: "R." for the Latin "Recipe," meaning "Take"</note> very well-beaten pure gold, or well-purified unminted lead, or <term>virgin parchment</term> <note>parchment made from the skin of a stillborn or young animal, considered "pure" for ritual use</note>; write upon it this following sign with turtle-dove or <term>dragon's blood</term> <note>a bright red resin obtained from certain plants, used as a pigment and in magic</note>, using a pen cut with a new knife, on a Friday before sunrise, when the moon is new, in the sign of Gemini, Leo, or Virgo. For three days prior, you shall live and fast moderately, without consuming warm food; only out of the highest necessity shall you eat a little bread and drink one draught of beer each day. Also, the workman or artist shall live purely and chastely for one month prior, wearing white and clean clothes. Before he begins to write, he must first fumigate the metal plate or parchment well with white frankincense, myrrh, aloes, and <term>mastic</term> <note>a resin from the mastic tree used as incense</note>. Then write this following sign.

Keep this tablet afterward enclosed in a clean little book, and preserve this secret chastely, God-fearingly, faithfully, and honestly; use it or carry it with you daily, for it has unimaginably many powers and virtues. For:

1. It is an excellent secret art, of which it is not necessary to speak much; if someone knows it, they should not bring it to light carelessly, for it is good to use for the protection of one's body and life in all need and accidents, without violating one's Christian conscience.

2. If you touch a young woman on her bare skin, she will immediately gain a constant love for you.

3. If you touch a pregnant woman with it, she will soon give birth without pain and bring a healthy <term>fruit</term> <note>original: Frucht; referring to the child</note> into the world, without any harm to the mother.

4. If you give it to one who is condemned to death, so that he may carry it on him, he will certainly be released from death.

5. When you go before a court, no matter how much you have to contend with, this sign will weaken your adversary, so that no one will be able to do anything against you.

6. If you carry it in your right hand—provided that you have first washed and purified yourself—you will suppress and overcome all judicial sentences or false witnesses through the power of God and this sign. etc.
