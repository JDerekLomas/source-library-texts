<term>Coat of Arms</term>: A unique heraldic design on an escutcheon (shield), used to identify a person, family, or organization.

<term>Heraldry</term>: The profession, study, or art of devising, granting, and blazoning arms and ruling on questions of rank or protocol.

<term>Crest</term>: An ornament worn on top of the helmet in a coat of arms.

<term>Quarterly</term>: A method of dividing a shield into four or more sections to incorporate the arms of different families joined by marriage.

<term>Leopard's Faces</term>: In heraldry, a "leopard" often refers to a lion shown with its face turned toward the viewer.

<term>Scallop Shells</term>: A common heraldic symbol, often associated with pilgrimage (specifically the Shrine of St. James) or naval command.
