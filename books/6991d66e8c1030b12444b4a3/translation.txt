But it may be said that this kind of faith has rarely sprouted in anyone yet.

Well, even if that is true for now, it is still a fact that such a faith once existed and will certainly be revived in those who are willing to "die" to everything that has choked, stifled, and even bound down this "lily of faith."

Now, let us move on to the second rule of direction for spiritual souls who desire to become well-acquainted with a life in God and find rest within it.

Just as the first rule was to cast off all unnecessary external burdens and weights, this second rule is more internal. For he was composed of both good and evil essences, and so he became more easily corrupted and defiled.

Thus we give the inquirer an *answer* to the question: *How sin first entered the world.* Now it remains to let you know how this poison of sin (which has infected all of Adam’s descendants) shall be diluted and expelled. The original cure for this is so well known that I do not need to explain it, as it is generally believed that Christ came in the flesh to be a <term>propitiation</term> <note>original: "Propitiation"; an offering or action meant to regain favor or make atonement for sin.</note> for the sins committed in the flesh.

But now I have a deeper thing than this to declare and make known. We know all outward births have been conceived in sin; and yet, some element of good (from the original root of nature) has sprung forth from within everyone. Now, understand and consider that there is no other way to achieve a perfect cure except by removing the sting of sin. This happens through another birth conceived within the soul—which is Christ the <term>quickening spirit</term> <note>original: "Quickning-Spirit"; a spirit that gives life or restores vitality to the soul.</note>—a birth that is sinless. As that birth grows up and becomes strong in spirit, the sinful part withers and dies away. Thus, Christ, the God-man, works out sin within the properties of fallen nature and so victoriously completes redemption. *For whatever is born of God overcomes the world.* (1st Epistle of John, Chapter 2).

But here I am met with a question, namely: *Is Christ to be conceived and born so intrinsically within everyone for salvation?* Yes, surely. This is and must be the <term>foundation seal</term> <note>A spiritual mark of ownership or a primary guarantee of salvation.</note> by which God the Father will claim and rescue us, as we become the direct image of His beloved Son.

# A POSTSCRIPT.

But again, it may be objected: "I have asserted that God's love is so great and expansive that all fallen angels and spirits shall be redeemed. But the question is, how can that be when so many die without any appearance of this 'New Birth' <note>The "New Birth" refers to a spiritual rebirth or internal transformation into a Christ-like state.</note> you speak of?" It is true that millions of souls pass away out of this world in such a state, and it will lead to great loss and suffering for them. Yet know this:

> The root of the eternal God is in every soul, no matter how vile and evil they were in the body they lived and died in. For Christ is the light that has enlightened everyone who comes into the world (John 1:9). Even though this light and life of Christ has been shut up, and sin has gotten the upper hand, yet Christ (the eternal spark of life) will save Himself in all those who have apostatized <note>original: "Apostatiz'd"; abandoned their spiritual standing or rebelled against God.</note> from Him. Nothing shall suffer loss except the fuel of evil works, which cannot pass through the judgment and the burning. And if this life is passed over and little or none of this work is done, there must remain a fearful expectation of going through this terrible refining work after this life, in regions appointed for that purpose. Oh, that all careless, desperate soul-adventurers knew the hard struggles they must go through to clear and get rid of what they contracted of vile matter and evil deeds in this life!

Therefore, let everyone be warned to mortify <note>To "mortify" means to subdue or "put to death" earthly desires through spiritual discipline.</note> and renounce the earthly birth, so that they may escape the fiery indignation that will come upon the works of iniquity.

Oh! Now then, while you have day with the everlasting sunshine of love in your souls, turn into it. It will clarify and brighten you so that, being saved in this way, you may be free from all future punishments and find entrance into the joy of our Lord. This may be known even now, in this present time, in the "heavens" of a pure heart—the happy felicity of which I have already set forth in this treatise.

So, hoping this may give satisfaction to the impartial and sober-minded, and provide information into the mysteries that have been under a veil (which is now being torn away), let us earnestly pray for the break of day.

## A Visionary Appearance, or Prophecy.

->July 15, 1694.<-

I had a sudden representation come before me of a round globe, like an oval, that had four circles. Within the first circle was a pure white glass of light; in it appeared the personal Prince of Glory, with a countless number of bright bodily figures, with whom the Elders were. These were they who had gained victory over this world and departed in the faith of what they now enjoy. The circle that surrounded them was of a bright flame-color.

Next to this was an azure blue circle. Here appeared the faces only of numerous persons who are still living in bodily form in this world (some are known to me, and others unknown). The motto written on their foreheads was: *These are those who are waiting to break through into the Mount Zion principle, where Christ the Lord is, along with all those who are redeemed out of and from the earth.*

Then, in the third circle, which appeared like pale lightning, there opened Seraphim and Cherubim <note>High-ranking orders of angels in celestial hierarchy.</note>—bright, very numerous angels who ascended. They were appointed as a guard, always ministering to those in the second circle. I saw them break their circles and come among those who were pressing into the Light-World.

The fourth circle was of a darker, dusky color. Here were those who were yet to be gathered in and born again; some among them were known to me by face.

# A POSTSCRIPT.

They were still moving within the "Dark Principle" <note>In Jane Lead’s theology, the "Dark Principle" refers to the state of separation from God's light, often associated with hell or the fallen world.</note> (as if shut out from the Light). All of this was clearly revealed to me.

And as I was waiting further upon the vision (as it closed and then opened again the next night), I saw one mighty and powerful angel break through the circle where the glorified person and their elders were. There was a great shout, crying, "*The Principle of Salvation has broken through!*" Now the Beast’s kingdom <note>A reference to the worldly and corrupt systems described in the Book of Revelation.</note> will quickly fall. This mighty conqueror is worthy to receive (along with the Lamb of God) power, riches, wisdom, strength, honor, glory, and blessing. For the way has been cleared for those to follow who are in that same spirit of faith, letting go of their purely human reasoning.

Then I saw many striving to enter, but they could not until they had let go of the name and mark of the Beast (which represents this worldly principle), along with all its polluted associations <note>original: "Immercements"; likely referring to being immersed in or burdened by worldly things.</note> and "Babylonian trades" <note>original: "Babylonian Trafficks"; spiritual or worldly commerce that is corrupt or deceptive.</note>. By the sight of this all-conquering angel, they were persuaded to do so.

Then a free passage was made for many (known by their form and name) to enter and to receive mutual power to multiply and spread the glory of Mount Zion over the whole face of this earth.

Thus, all nations will be drawn in at the rising of this bright, eastern <note>original: "Orient"; suggesting the rising sun or the dawn of a new spiritual era.</note> spirit; for great powers are truly moving from heaven.

Oh, mighty Jesus! You are this "Circle-Breaker" through and within those whom you will choose and take up to open the fountain of great wonders. By this, our lost purity shall be restored, and the heavens shall be spread like a curtain over the whole world.

This is a prophetic vision, and its fulfillment is drawing near; therefore, it seemed good to me to add it to the previous section.

The author has much more of this kind of writing in manuscript form that may yet be published as God opens an effective door for more to be revealed. This will help prepare the "New Jerusalem Bride" <note>A mystical term for the purified church or the collective soul prepared for union with God.</note> to be married to the spotless Lamb of God. To this, I say to everyone: "Come, oh come away with speed."

The standard of Christ is raised, and the trumpet of his Spirit now sounds so that those dead in sin may live again in a new state. This comes from the resurrection life of Christ, who descends in a fiery, baptizing cloud. By this, he will enter into the souls of those who are willing to have their sin-stained garments taken away.

The oil of the Spirit is still flowing, but it must stop for now until vessels are found and prepared to receive it. For this purpose, the incense of pure prayers shall continue to ascend to heaven.
