### Giving a brief Account of,
**I.** The Fall of Angels, the ſeduction of Human Race, and their Impieties before the Flood.

**V.** Propoſitions concerning Witches and Witchcraft, the Character of a Witch; ſome conſiderations of the Original of their Power.
