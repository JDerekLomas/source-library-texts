# Om, Salutations to the Auspicious Samaveda <note>original: "ॐ नमः श्री सामवेदाय." The Samaveda is one of the four Vedas, the oldest scriptures of Hinduism, known for its musical chants.</note>

Even the <term>Danavas</term> <note>Demonic titans</note>, <term>Gandharvas</term> <note>Celestial musicians</note>, <term>Pisachas</term> <note>Flesh-eating spirits</note>, <term>Uragas</term> <note>Serpent deities</note>, and <term>Rakshasas</term> <note>Demons</note> are tormented by the afflictions of the planets; what then can be said for mere human beings? || 1 || 

By the influence of the Sun <note>original: "Aditya"</note> in the twelfth house, Ravana <note>The primary antagonist of the Ramayana</note> was killed along with his entire host. By the influence of the Moon <note>original: "Rashanka"</note> in the eighth house, the demon-king Hiranyakashipu was slain. || 2 || 

By the influence of Mars <note>original: "Angaraka"</note> in a "crooked" or retrograde motion, Rama was exiled from his kingdom. During an affliction of Mercury <note>original: "Budha"</note>, the Pandavas <note>The five hero-brothers of the Mahabharata</note> were forced into menial labor and improper deeds. || 3 || 

With Jupiter <note>original: "Guru"</note> stationed in the house of birth, King Suyodhana <note>Another name for Duryodhana, the antagonist of the Mahabharata</note> was killed. When these gods <note>The planets are personified as deities</note> are given offerings through sacrifice, they protect the earth. Therefore, the wise, upon rising in the morning, should consult an astrologer <note>original: "Daivajña," literally 'one who knows the divine/fate'.</note>. || 7 ||
