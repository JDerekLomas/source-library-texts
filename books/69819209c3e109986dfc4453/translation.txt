If those who—not without labor, sleepless nights, and sweat—demonstrated the path to **Christian philosophy** <note>original: *Christianam philosophiam*. Furthermore, I do not see how the most effective comfort of our inner connection could reach us if the writings were not seen, which allow us to see "face to face" those things which absence would otherwise hide. Let it be that we write humble things that do not "throw dust in the eyes" <note>A classical idiom meaning to deceive or confuse someone with trivialities.</note>; let them be small, or even judged to be the very least. But let there be forgiveness, for we are starting from the smallest things, yet things that lead to salvation.

# CHRISTIAN <note>original: *CHRISTIANA*. This header completes the title "CHRISTIAN STRUGGLE" begun on the previous page.</note>

...so that I might present to you a literary testimony of my respect. But a detractor will say: "What has a physician to do with Theology?" My good friend, even those in the profession of Medicine are Christians. Again, they may say: "Such things are not suitable for a physician." Yet in war, the wounded have need of a physician, and in this **Struggle**, the soul is wounded unless a remedy is at hand. Therefore, the physician, while treating the patient, should also advise the sick person to plead with God for the health of both "men" <note>Referring to the biblical concept of the "outer man" (the body) and the "inner man" (the spirit).</note>, especially the inner one. <header>C H R I S T I A N [S T R U G G L E]</header>

...grow, and mortals easily rush into the increase of crimes whenever there is certain impunity for sinners. But we face a **Struggle**, according to the witness of **Paul**, against spiritual deceits in the heavenly places; <note>Refers to Ephesians 6:12: "For our struggle is not against flesh and blood... but against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms."</note> therefore, it is necessary that those of us who have given our name to Christ respond to the day that has now dawned. The night has passed, as the same **Paul** writes to the **Romans**, and the day has approached. Let us therefore cast off the works of darkness and put on the **arms of light**, <term>arms of light: spiritual virtues or "armor" (from Romans 13:12) used to defend the soul against vice</term> so that we may walk orderly as in the day, not in revelries and drunkenness, not in the bedroom and wantonness, not in contention and jealousy. We profess Christ; let us express by our deeds him whom we profess with our words, lest it be said to us through **Isaiah**: *The name of God is blasphemed among the nations because of you.* <note>original: *Nomen Dei p[er] uos blasphematur inter gentes*. A citation of Isaiah 52:5, also quoted by Paul in Romans 2:24, criticizing those who claim to follow God but act wickedly.</note> **Christian doctrine** teaches sobriety; but how does one express Christ while soaked in wine, serving **Venus** <note>original: *Veneri parentans*. We can carry nothing with us except a life lived well and innocently. Meanwhile, forgetting our heavenly fatherland, some turn away from the **Kingdom of Christ** which is not of this world, and with their whole mind...
