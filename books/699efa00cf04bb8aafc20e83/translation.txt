Thus you may now easily notice and understand what this comet means, and what will come to pass and occur at the indicated time. As you shall be further informed about this in the other treatise. Look at how wonderfully and monstrously Daniel presents to us the King of Babylon, Nebuchadnezzar: how he will lose his human heart, be cast out by people, and must wander in the field among the wild beasts, in iron and bronze chains, eating grass like an ox; lying under the dew of heaven and becoming wet, so that his hair grows like eagle's feathers and his nails like bird's claws, until seven times have passed over him, etc. Indeed, what is this wonderful, monstrous form of the King of Babylon? And what will each detail mean? This, however, is apparent to the understanding person <note>The author is referencing Daniel 4, where Nebuchadnezzar is struck with madness. In 17th-century prophecy, this was often interpreted as a "type" or shadow of the pride and eventual fall of modern worldly rulers.</note>.

<margin>☞</margin> If you wish to know when these things will happen among us, take the number of the twelve moons <note>A lunar year</note> after which this befell the King; carry them through the Book of Revelation <note>original: *Apocalypsin*</note> as a "time, two times, and half a time" <note>A common prophetic period in Daniel and Revelation, usually interpreted as 1,260 days or 3.5 years.</note>, and you will have the Number of the Beast, 42 <note>Referring to the 42 months of the Beast’s authority in Revelation 13:5.</note>, as considered and proven before. Or take the 12 moons, convert them into 360 days; add these to the 1,260 days in Revelation <note>original: *Apocalypsi*</note>, and they become 1620. Thus you will see how our King Nebuchadnezzar will have lost his human heart, among what kind of wild animals he will wander in the field in iron and bronze chains—and what those chains are—and there you will see how he will eat grass like an ox. And there you will see that his hair is like eagle's wings, how he will come flying in, and you will see what terrible bird's claws he has. O, do penance, all you children of men on earth! Yet it is for the best, for Behemoth will be the beginning of the ways of God, etc. <note>A reference to Job 40:19, implying that these monstrous trials are part of God's divine plan.</note> After that will follow the Leviathan, Gog and Magog, the Turkish host with its Tartars and Saracens; the second part of this work shall also describe this and its downfall.

Thus you now see that the scripture: *numbered, numbered, weighed, and divided* <note>original: *mene, mene Tekel upharsin*; the "writing on the wall" from Daniel 5:25 foretelling the fall of Babylon.</note> is correctly interpreted and read. For everything is numbered, numbered, and considered; it is also placed in a scale, and outside of Christ, everything is found too light. Therefore, it follows that the Roman Empire <note>original: *R. Reich* (Römisches Reich), referring here to the Holy Roman Empire.</note> will be divided and given to the people of God the Most High (Daniel 7). These things shall be dealt with more extensively in the future, provided that you receive this work with gratitude. See, the understanding will hold it in high regard, but the godless will deny it and lead a godless life, traveling into destruction, and be plunged into the fiery pit with the beast and the false prophet. I have spoken <note>original: *dixi*</note>.
