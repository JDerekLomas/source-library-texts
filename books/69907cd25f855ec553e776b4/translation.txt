This document is a digital copy of a book that was kept for many generations on library shelves before it was carefully scanned by Google. This work is part of a larger project intended to make the world’s books searchable and discoverable online.

This book has survived long enough for its <term>copyright|The legal right that grants the creator of a work exclusive control over its use and distribution</term> to expire, allowing it to enter the **public domain**. A public domain book is one that was never protected by copyright or whose legal term of protection has ended. Whether a book is in the public domain can vary from one country to another. These books act as our gateways to the past, representing a vast collection of history, culture, and knowledge that is often difficult to find otherwise.

Any marks, handwritten notes, or other <term>marginalia|Notes, marks, or comments written in the margins of a book</term> present in the original physical volume will appear in this file. These are reminders of the book’s long journey from its original publisher to a library, and finally, to you.

## Usage guidelines

Google is proud to partner with libraries to digitize public domain materials and make them widely accessible to everyone. These books belong to the public, and we act merely as their custodians. However, this undertaking is expensive. To continue providing this resource, we have taken steps to prevent commercial misuse, including technical restrictions on automated searching.

We also ask that you:

+ *Make non-commercial use of the files* We designed Google Book Search for use by individuals. We request that you use these files only for your personal, non-commercial research and education.

+ *Refrain from automated querying* Please do not send automated queries of any kind to Google’s system. If you are conducting research involving <term>machine translation|The use of software to translate text from one language to another</term>, <term>optical character recognition|The electronic conversion of images of typed, handwritten, or printed text into machine-encoded text</term> (OCR), or other fields where access to large amounts of text is necessary, please contact us. We encourage the use of public domain materials for these purposes and may be able to assist you.

+ *Maintain attribution* The Google “watermark” you see on each file is essential for informing people about this project and helping them find additional materials through Google Book Search. Please do not remove it.

+ *Keep it legal* Whatever your use, remember that you are responsible for ensuring that your actions are legal. Do not assume that just because we believe a book is in the public domain for users in the United States, the work is also in the public domain for users in other countries. Copyright laws vary by country, and we cannot offer legal guidance on whether a specific use of a specific book is permitted. Please do not assume that a book’s appearance in Google Book Search means it can be used in any manner anywhere in the world. The legal consequences for <term>copyright infringement|The use of works protected by copyright law without permission, infringing upon certain exclusive rights granted to the copyright holder</term> can be very severe.
