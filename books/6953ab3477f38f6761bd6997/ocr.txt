Vaticana - Manoscritti

<image-desc>
A highly ornate 17th-century red morocco leather binding with extensive gold-tooling in the "fan" (éventail) style. The decoration consists of multiple concentric rectangular borders filled with repeating lace-like and foliate patterns. The central field is divided into compartments; the corners contain large quarter-circle fan designs composed of delicate pointillé (dotted) gold lines. 

At the center of the design is an octagonal frame housing a coat of arms. The shield features a sheaf of wheat (garb) with two bands across it. The shield is surmounted by a crown held aloft by two winged putti (cherubs). A decorative floral element sits directly below the shield. On the far left edge, a small white library label is attached with the text "Vaticana - Manoscritti".
</image-desc>
