## God-seeking / Dear Reader!

**I**T is a very important word, and an utterance worthy of all attention, from our dearest Savior when He says: "If your eye is simple <note>original: *einfältig*. # Preface.

Just as this is now the loveliest, straightest, and most precious way; so it often happens in the meantime that even many souls called by **God**, well-intentioned and well-meaning—especially in these our times—for lack of a pure, simple light and instruction or for other reasons, do not keep the right, actual purpose and final goal of their heavenly calling and drawing sufficiently in view. Instead, they easily stumble into side-issues and circumstances, or remain caught up in the first ways and means. For some like to remain in the first beginnings of repentance <note>Refers to Hebrews 6:1: "Therefore let us leave the elementary doctrine of Christ and go on to maturity, not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works..."</note>. # Conclusion.

->Much reading may well make one weary; he who has read one book aright /<-
->(I mean **Jesus Christ**) is eternally healed.<- <note>The author uses a play on words: "genesen" means both recovered/healed and saved. The "one book" is Christ himself, a common mystical trope suggesting that knowing the Person is superior to studying text.</note>

->❀<-

->The highest wisdom is to be godly on earth, /<-
->Conformed to **God’s** Son in morals and manner.<-

->❀<-

->Therefore, soul, do not wonder if I can look at nothing else /<-
->I must always turn myself toward Him, the Sun.<-

->❀<-

->For he who has come into **God**, his End and his Sabbath, /<-
->He is taken up into sweet rest and peace.<- <term>Sabbath</term> <note>In this context, "Sabbath" refers to the "eternal rest" (Sabbath-Ruhe) of the soul within God, a state of complete spiritual tranquility.</note>

->❀<-

->Friend, this is enough! In case you wish to read more, /<-
->So go and become the Scripture and the Essence yourself.<- <note>A final exhortation to the reader: do not just read these words, but embody them until your own life becomes a living "scripture."</note>
