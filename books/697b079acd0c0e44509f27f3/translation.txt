## LEGE.

Permission is granted to Nicolas Chrestien, merchant Bookseller, & Printer in Paris, to print, or have printed, & sell, the book of the *Elements & astronomical principles* with the *universal judgments* of the same Astronomy. Likewise, a treatise on <term>elections</term> <note>The astrological practice of choosing the most auspicious time to begin a task or event.</note> of things to be done, or desired to be done, following the same, with several chapters serving the same, among others for <term>nativities</term> <note>The calculation and interpretation of a birth chart to determine an individual's destiny.</note> and for drawing up celestial figures. The whole composed by master Richard Roussat. And forbidden to all other Printers & Booksellers of this Kingdom, to neither Print nor sell the said book without the consent of the said Chrestien, for up to four years after the first printing of the same. And this under penalty of confiscation of the books & an arbitrary fine as appears more fully by letters from the King, given on this matter at Paris, the last day of September. In the year of grace 1551, signed by the council, Turpin, and sealed on a <term>simple tail</term> <note>A legal term (original: "simple queue") for a seal attached to a strip of parchment or paper hanging from the document.</note> of yellow wax.
