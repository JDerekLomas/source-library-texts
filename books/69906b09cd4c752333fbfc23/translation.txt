# THE EVIL EYE
# AND RELATED TOPICS.

## A Contribution to the History
## of Superstition
## of all Times and Peoples.

> The higher the mind ascends in the sciences,
> The lower superstition sinks toward its fall.
<note>This rhyming couplet reflects the Enlightenment-era hope that scientific progress would naturally eliminate folk beliefs.</note>

->By<-

### **Dr. S. Seligmann**

->Ophthalmologist in Hamburg.<-
<note>Dr. Siegfried Seligmann (1870–1926) was a practicing eye doctor whose professional interest in the organ of sight led him to compile the most exhaustive global history of the "Evil Eye" ever written.</note>
