# Buckthorn
<note>Chinese: *Shuli* (鼠李). Literally "Rat Plum," this deciduous shrub is known in modern botany as *Rhamnus davurica*. In traditional medicine, its bark and berries were often used for their laxative properties and to treat skin conditions.</note>

### Ox Plum, Black Plum
<note>Chinese: *Niuli* (牛李) and *Zaoli* (皂李). These are synonyms for the buckthorn. "Black Plum" refers to the dark, ink-like color of the ripened fruit, which was historically used to create dyes.</note>
