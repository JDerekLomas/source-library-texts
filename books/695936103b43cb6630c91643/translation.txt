### Figure 153: The Magic Circle of Solomon
<note>This is the protective boundary used by the magician during ritual evocations. It is inscribed with various names of God to ensure the practitioner's safety.</note>

The circle is oriented to the cardinal directions: **SOUTH**, **NORTH**, and **WEST**. Within the concentric rings are inscribed sacred names and phrases, including:
*   **Alpha and Omega** <note>original: "AL-PHA" and "OME-GA"; Greek for "The Beginning and the End," a title for God.</note>
*   **Adonai** <note>Hebrew: "Lord."</note>
*   **Agla** <note>A Hebrew acronym for "Thou art mighty forever, O Lord."</note>
*   **Tetragrammaton** <note>The four-letter name of God, YHWH.</note>

### Figure 154: The Triangle of Solomon
<note>This is the space outside the circle where the spirit is commanded to appear.</note>

The triangle is oriented to the **EAST**. It is inscribed with the following names:
*   **Tetragrammaton**
*   **Anaphaxeton** <note>A divine name often used in conjurations.</note>
*   **Primeumaton** <note>A divine name meaning "The First and the Last."</note>
*   **Michael** <note>The name of the Archangel Michael, split across the three points of the triangle to bind the spirit within.</note>

### THE INITIATED INTERPRETATION OF CEREMONIAL MAGIC.

It is deeply amusing to the student of *Magical* literature who is not a complete fool—and such a combination is rare!—to observe the criticism directed by the unlearned <note>original: "Philistine"</note> against the stronghold of his science. Truly, since our childhood has ingrained in us not only a literal belief in the Bible, but also a substantial belief in the *Thousand and One Nights* <note>original: "Alf Laylah wa Laylah"</note>, and only reaching adulthood can cure us of this, we are all too likely, in the rush and energy of early manhood, to roughly and rashly reject both these classics. # PRELIMINARY DEFINITION OF MAGIC.

## LEMEGETON OR THE LITTLE KEY OF KING SOLOMON.
<note>original: "Lemegeton vel Clavicula Salomonis Regis"</note>

**MAGIC** is the highest, most absolute, and most divine knowledge of Natural Philosophy <note>The study of nature and the physical universe, which was the precursor to modern science.</note>. It is advanced in its works and wonderful operations by a right understanding of the inward and secret <note>original: "occult"</note> virtues of things; so that when true active agents are applied to the proper passive subjects, strange and admirable effects are produced. Because of this, magicians are profound and diligent searchers into Nature. Due to their skill, they know how to produce an effect <note>original: "anticipate an effort"</note> which, to the common people, shall seem to be a miracle.

Origen <note>An early Christian scholar and theologian (c. 184–253 AD).</note> says that the Magical Art does not contain anything substantial in itself; but even if it did, it must not be considered evil or subject to contempt or scorn. He distinguishes *Natural Magic* from that which is *Diabolical*.

Apollonius of Tyana <note>A Greek Neopythagorean philosopher and reputed miracle-worker from the 1st century AD.</note> only practiced *Natural Magic*, through which he performed wonderful things.

Philo the Jew <note>original: "Philo Hebraeus"; a Hellenistic Jewish philosopher from Alexandria.</note> says that true Magic—by which we arrive at an understanding of the secret works of Nature—is so far from being contemptible that the greatest monarchs and kings have studied it. Indeed, among the Persians, no one was allowed to reign unless he was skillful in this **GREAT ART**.

*Obey the mighty sounds! Obey the living breath of the voice of the Lord!*

---

->*The following is the charge.*<-

## THE LICENSE TO THE SPIRIT THAT HE MAY DEPART.

> *O you Spirit [Name], because you are a servant of loyalty and obedience, and because you are one who obeys my power and the laws of your creation; therefore I say: Descend unto your dwelling place. Obey the law which I have made, without causing terror to the sons of men, to creatures, or to any things upon the surface of the earth.*
>
> *Descend, therefore, I say, and be like a steward of Time; come forth in a moment, even as servants who listen to the voice of the Lord, in the very moment in which I invoke you, stir you up, and move you within the mysteries of the secret wisdom of the Creator!*
>
> *Descend unto your dwelling place in pleasure: let the mercies of God be upon you. Be friendly in your continued existence; may your long endurance be a comfort to all creatures. Amen.*
