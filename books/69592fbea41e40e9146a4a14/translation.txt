# The History of how the Mastery of Hermes was attained

They avoided the world through the perfect way of simplicity; in order to find, through communion and effect, the things you seek. These same things are valid for the uninitiated, improving their natural talents and bringing others into a stronger light. In the following sections, a divine explanation will be provided where the operation of the two <note>likely referring to the two "stones" or principles of alchemy</note> will be fully noted. But we are held by these two things: lest we be wretched and worn down by small matters, and so that such a great work may be sufficient for both in the long run. It is sufficient for the hand. Whence, concerning each point, the sum of the methods has been brought together for us by that God for the two who then desired it; we have seen it with living eyes. Before my name is placed at the start of the prologue, it pleased me that no living person who is familiar with these labors should lack praise or the means of living. I ask and beseech that my mind be further humbled, lest anyone ever swell with pride regarding my name, as often happens when good things are done. For God gives to whomsoever He has purposed His grace, and the highest wisdom proceeds through the Word, by which He preserves the merits of joy for those who fail, being the creator and restorer of all things, who helps their riches with a small word. Let that not be hidden from us entirely.

# On the Hidden Spirit

The hidden spirit is found everywhere and is named by no one unless it is revealed by a seal <note>original: "sigillum"</note>; it is adapted only for the wise, whose genius seeks it in time and finds it within, just as a lover of truth adorns it. For many indeed descend and open those first things which are considered as nothing by the ignorant, but which are called by the wise in their books "living water" or "spirit," because it overcomes something in the blood and changes it. And then it is congealed <note>original: "coangulatur"</note> so that it becomes like a gummy piece of flesh, and in such a manner it becomes like a grape or a berry, which by the succeeding gift of God becomes wine.

The spirit is also known by another name, such as "marsh water," yet it has the color of red apples; and it changes color, and the ancients feared to speak of it by its own name. It has other names too, such as "the tree of evil fruits," "the bird of the earth," "milk," and many other names which are found in the grape.
