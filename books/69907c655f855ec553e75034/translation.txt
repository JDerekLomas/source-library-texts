### On the Ten Great Faults

Now, the sage **Lalla** describes the **Ten Faults** <note>original: 'Dasha Dosha'</note>: 
In whatever constellation the Sun resides, and in whatever constellation the Moon is situated—when these are calculated and certain remainders are found, the results are the Ten Faults. These remainders correspond to the following numbers: 8, 4, 6, 8, 1, 9, 11, 15, 18, 19, and 20 <note>original: 'mayu-veda-ritu-rupa...' expressed in traditional word-numerals</note>. These Ten Faults are considered exceptionally powerful and destructive. They result in: 
- Storms and fire
- Trouble from rulers or kings 
- Theft and death
- Disease and conflict <note>original: 'kali'</note>
- Loss of property or land

The wise should always avoid these times for any sacred undertaking.

### On the Fault of Planetary Conjunctions

The sage **Narada** describes the fault of **Conjunction** <note>term: Yuti|When two or more celestial bodies appear close together in the same sign</note>:
When the **Moon** <note>original: 'Shashanka'</note> is joined with other planets, various effects occur:
- Joined with the **Sun**: It brings certain poverty.
- Joined with **Mars**: It leads to death or chronic disease.
- Joined with **Mercury**: It results in childlessness.
- Joined with **Jupiter**: It brings good fortune.
- Joined with **Venus**: It leads to rivalry among co-wives or specific domestic challenges, though some consider it fortunate.
- Joined with **Saturn** <note>original: 'Surya-putra' or Son of the Sun</note>: It leads to renunciation or becoming a wandering ascetic <note>note: This is considered a "fault" in a marriage context as it ends the domestic life</note>.
- Joined with **Rahu**: It causes constant quarreling.
- Joined with **Ketu**: It results in daily suffering and poverty.

### On the Seventh House from the Moon

From the text **Bhujabala**, the "Fault of the Friend" <note>or "Mitra Dosha"</note> is explained:
If certain planets occupy the seventh zodiac sign from the Moon, the bride suffers:
- If the **Sun** is there: She is abandoned and loses her wealth.
- If **Mars** is there: The woman meets her end or faces destruction.
- If **Saturn** is there: She suffers from widowhood or disease.
- However, if **Jupiter**, the **Moon**, or **Mercury** are in this position, they are considered "givers of auspiciousness" by some authorities.

### On the Minor Planets and Omens

The sage **Narada** further describes the faults of **Upagrahas** <note>term: Upagraha|Calculated sensitive points or "shadow planets" in the sky</note> and natural omens:
Events such as **earthquakes** <note>original: 'bhukampa'</note> occurring in the 7th constellation from the Sun, **lightning** in the 5th, or specific placements of Saturn in the 11th, 8th, or 9th are all indicators of significant astrological interference that must be accounted for.
