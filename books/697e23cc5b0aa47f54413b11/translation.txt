# EPISTLE

...from where I have taken the beginning of my writing; you will understand. I, therefore, am entirely unknown to you, and you are indeed unknown to me in face; but in spirit—which is the true and inner man—you have recently become most well-known to me. For indeed, when three days ago <note>original: "nudius-tertius," a Latin idiom for "the day before yesterday" or "three days ago"</note> it happened that I was crossing over the bridge of the Rialto <term>Rivi alti: literally "Deep Stream," the Latin name for the Rialto district and bridge in Venice</term>, a certain small book was offered to me by a friend who is also known to you. It was a new publication <note>original: "nunc primo excusus," literally "now first struck," referring to the printing press</note>, marked on the back with the coat of arms of the current Pope, and containing your elegant letter addressed to that same Pope.
