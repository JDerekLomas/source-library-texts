->Written by **Eyreneus Philoctetes**<-

> If you desire to exalt a thing beyond what nature has done, consider well in what, and by what it is improved, and let it always be done within its own kind. *Sendivogius, Treatise 1.*
<note>original: "Sendivo. Trea. 1." Michael Sendivogius (1566–1636) was a famous Polish alchemist and philosopher.</note>
