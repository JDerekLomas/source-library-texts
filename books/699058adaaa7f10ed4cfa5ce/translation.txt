## INTRODUCTION.

We take great pleasure in presenting this small work, based upon the world-old Hermetic Teachings <note>The philosophical and religious system based on the purported writings of Hermes Trismegistus</note>, to the attention of students and investigators of the Secret Doctrines. There has been so little written upon this subject—notwithstanding the countless references to these Teachings in many works upon occultism <note>The study of hidden or supernatural powers and laws</note>—that the many earnest searchers after the Arcane <term>Arcane: mysterious or understood by few</term> Truths will doubtless welcome the appearance of the present volume.

The purpose of this work is not the announcement of any special philosophy or doctrine. Rather, it is intended to give students a statement of the Truth that will serve to reconcile the many bits of occult knowledge they may have acquired, but which are apparently opposed to each other and often serve to discourage the seeker.

# Introduction

The lineage of the human race may be traced back to Hermes. Even the most ancient teachings of India undoubtedly have their roots in the original Hermetic Teachings.

From the land of the Ganges, many advanced occultists <note>original: "occultists"; here referring to students of hidden or esoteric spiritual knowledge</note> wandered to the land of Egypt and sat at the feet of the Master. From him, they obtained the Master-Key which explained and reconciled their divergent views, and thus the Secret Doctrine was firmly established. From other lands also came the learned ones, all of whom regarded Hermes as the "Master of Masters." 

His influence was so great that, despite the many departures from the original path by teachers over centuries in these different lands, one may still find a certain basic resemblance and correspondence. This underlying unity exists beneath the many—and often quite divergent—theories held and taught by the occultists of these different lands today. The student of Comparative Religions will be able to perceive the influence of the Hermetic Teachings in every religion worthy of the name.

# *The Divine Paradox*
