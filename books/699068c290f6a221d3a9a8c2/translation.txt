## Also known as the Wish-Fulfilling Tree of Inquiry <note>original: "Prashna Kalpa Vriksha"; a 'Kalpa Vriksha' is a divine, wish-granting tree in Indian mythology.</note>

1. O Inquirer <note>original: "Prachchak"; the person asking the astrological question</note>, regarding this question you have posed:
Whatever has happened in the past is done. Now, joyful news is about to arrive. The bad days have passed and the best are coming. There will be relief and your task is already successful. Whatever you desire, you shall receive. However, this work you have conceived in your mind will take some time to manifest. For many days now, your circumstances have been mediocre; you have not seen profits according to your wishes. In this diminished state, there is grief, distress, pain, hidden worries, and enmity. Because there are two or three unfavorable <term>Nakish (unlucky or deficient)</term> planets <note>original: "Graha"</note> currently affecting your zodiac sign, you must perform acts of charity <note>original: "Dan"</note> and the chanting of sacred prayers <note>original: "Mantra Japa"</note>. Only then will there be happiness and celebratory rituals in your home, the fulfillment of your soul's desires, profit from travels, and success in your endeavors. You will receive hidden gains. On your body, there is a mark of a wound—that is, a boil or a pimple. You are prone to laziness and find yourself constantly contemplating new and different matters.
