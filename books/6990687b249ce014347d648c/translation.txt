## 111—The 111th Verse <term>Viruttam</term>

If you joyfully touch the number one hundred and eleven
With a flower <note>In this practice, the seeker often uses a flower to point at the number grid while their eyes are closed.</note>,

Will succeed without any obstacles, and you shall conquer your enemies.
For one fruitful year, there shall be no deficiencies;
O wise one, your domestic affairs
Will be fulfilled. As proof to recognize this sign,
Look, there will be a mole on your right shoulder.

This zodiacal sign <note>The term "rasi" is used here to mean the specific divinatory result or "sign" obtained.</note> remains favorable to you for a period of one year. As proof of this, see that there is a mole upon your right shoulder. **Excellent.** (1)
