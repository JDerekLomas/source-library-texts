<term>Tinctura (Tincture)</term>: A potent, concentrated medicinal or chemical liquid produced at the end of the Great Work, believed to have the power to "stain" or transform other substances into its own perfected nature.
<term>Oleum (Oil)</term>: Often called the "Oil of the Sun" or "Incombustible Oil," this refers to the refined, non-volatile essence of the matter that remains stable under heat.
<term>Sudatio (Sweating)</term>: A laboratory stage where heat is applied to the "fixed" matter to drive off its moisture or "soul," which then collects as droplets (sweat) on the interior of the glass.
