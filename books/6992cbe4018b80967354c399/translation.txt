The original work consists of twenty-six volumes. Among the volumes that were not originally supplemented, there are ten or more that now contain additions. However, when we use the current text to examine and verify these additions, many do not align with the correct categories. For example, the ten items regarding <note>original: 故事 (Gushi). This refers to the "Precedents" category, dealing with court rituals and institutional history.</note> **"Precedents: Not Attending the Front Palace"** and so forth were added to Volume 2. But Volume 2 is specifically for "Precedents"; how could these [lexical and botanical] definitions be placed there?
