### <term>廩</term> (Lǐn) and <term>倉</term> (Cāng) — Granaries

This manuscript page discusses the terminology and historical architecture of granaries. It cites classical sources:

*   **Classic of Poetry (詩經):** References the poem *Fengnian* (豐年 - Abundant Year), mentioning the storage of grains like <term>黍</term> (millet) and <term>稌</term> (glutinous rice) in high granaries (<term>高廩</term>).
*   **Shuowen Jiezi (說文解字):** Explains that <term>倉</term> (Cāng) is defined by its function of storing grain to be taken out as needed.
*   **Tangyun (唐韻):** Distinguishes between different types of storage, noting that <term>廩</term> specifically refers to a roofed structure, whereas <term>庾</term> (Yǔ) might refer to storage without a roof (or a distinction in walls).
*   **The Didactic Poem:** Includes a mnemonic poem linking the earthly granary to the <term>天廩</term> (Celestial Granary constellation), emphasizing its dual role in ritual (<term>粢盛</term> - grain for offerings) and survival (<term>備凶饑</term> - preparing for famine).
