# THE PREFACE.

THIS Life is Written that the Reader may Live it, and to preserve, in the Records of time, à Light that influenc'd all the Schools of PHILOSOPHY and Divinity of the time he Liv'd in. Example Operating with more Strength upon the intellectual Man than Precept, I hope it may be of some use to Mankind that I revive the Memory of a Person who was as Ornamental to the Schools, to Christianity, and Morality, as any one upon Record since the sixth Daies Worke of the Creation excepting our Original, in his purer State, and such as have been extraordinarily distinguish'd from above.
