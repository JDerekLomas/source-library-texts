<meta>Title page of the 1659 edition of John Dee's 'A True & Faithful Relation' edited by Meric Casaubon.</meta>

->A **TRUE** & **FAITHFUL**<-

# RELATION
->**OF**<-
->What passed for many Years Between<-

## DR. JOHN DEE
->(A Mathematician of Great Fame in the Reigns of **QUEEN ELIZABETH** and King **JAMES**) and<-

## SOME SPIRITS:
->**TENDING** (had it Succeeded)<-
->*To a General Alteration of most **STATES** and **KINGDOMS** in the World.*<-
->*His Private Conferences with **RODOLPHE** <note>Rudolf II</note> Emperor of Germany, **STEPHEN** <note>Stephen Báthory</note> King of Poland, and various other **PRINCES** about it.*<-
->The Particulars of his Cause, as it was debated in the Emperor’s Court by the **POPE’S** Intervention: His Banishment, and partial Restoration.<-

->**AS ALSO**<-

## The Letters of Sundry Great Men
->*and **PRINCES** (some of whom were present at some of these Conferences and Apparitions of **SPIRITS**) to the said **DR. DEE.***<-

---
->**OUT OF**<-
->The Original Copy, written with **DR. DEE’S** own Hand: Kept in the **LIBRARY** of<-
## Sir THOMAS COTTON, Knight Baronet.
---
->**WITH A**<-

## PREFACE
->Confirming the **Reality** (regarding the matter of **SPIRITS**) of This **RELATION**: and showing the several good **USES** that a **Sober Christian** may make of **All.**<-

->**BY**<-

## MERIC CASAUBON, Doctor of Divinity.

---
->**LONDON,**<-
->Printed by *D. Maxwell*, for *T. GARTHWAIT*, and sold at the *Little North door of St. Paul’s*, and by other Stationers. **1659.**<-

<summary>This is the title page of the 1659 publication of John Dee's private journals, documenting his purported conversations with spirits and his diplomatic dealings with European monarchs. Edited by Meric Casaubon, the work seeks to prove the reality of the spirit world to a Christian audience using Dee's original manuscripts.</summary>
