# PUBLISHER'S NOTE.

Astrology is a favorite subject liked and appreciated by all. But, it is a pity that such an important and very rare work as **The Path of Inquiry** <note>original: "Prasna-marga"</note> — which is, true to its name, a foot path to the <term>Prasna (horary astrology, which interprets the moment a question is asked)</term> and <term>Jataka (natal astrology, which interprets the moment of birth)</term> parts of astrology — is not yet printed in Sanskrit. This is the best book of its kind that exists at present and contains 32 <term>Adhyayas (chapters)</term> in all.

Now, we have great pleasure in publishing the first part of this excellent work containing 16 chapters with the explanatory footnotes by the **Great Scholar** <note>original: "Maha Vidwan"</note> Punnasseri Nambi Neelakandha Sarma Avl. <note>The abbreviation "Avl." stands for "Avergal," a South Indian honorific suffix used to show high respect to a person of standing.</note>, Principal of the Central Sanskrit College, Pattambi, and one of the greatest living astrologers of Kerala. His vast capabilities and rare abilities in astrology are so well known to the Sanskrit-reading public that it would be presumptuous on our part to expand upon them. His selfless labors in the field of Sanskrit literature and eagerness to bring to light such rare works have contributed significantly to the publication of this book. We are greatly indebted to this **distinguished gentleman** <note>original: "Mahasaya"</note> for his abiding interest in all that concerns the welfare of our Book Depot, and especially for the encouragement he has given in publishing this book.

The second part of this book will be published shortly, and we hope that the book will be well received by all those who are interested in the study of astrology.

->*The Publisher.*<-
