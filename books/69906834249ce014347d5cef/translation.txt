## With Hindi Commentary

Having bowed to the Great Lord <note>Sanskrit: Maheshvara (Shiva)</note>, the Daughter of the Mountain <note>Sanskrit: Shailaja (Parvati)</note>, the Leader of the Ganas <note>Sanskrit: Gananayaka (Ganesha)</note>, the Guru, and the Supreme Soul, I worship Him who carries us across the ocean of worldly existence. || 1 ||

I worship—that is, I serve through prostration and other acts—the Great Lord, Parvati, Ganesha, the Guru, and the Supreme Soul who delivers us from the cycle of rebirth. || 1 ||

### The Goddess Spoke

O God of Gods, Great Lord! Having shown mercy upon me, O Lord, tell me that knowledge which grants all perfections <note>Sanskrit: Siddhis</note>. || 2 ||

Parvati says: O God of the Gods! O Lord! By showing grace to me, speak to me of that knowledge which accomplishes all perfections. || 2 ||

How was the universe <note>Sanskrit: Brahmanda, literally "the Egg of Brahma"</note> created? How does it change and persist? How, O Lord, does it dissolve? Tell me the definitive truth of the universe. || 3 ||

Lord! How is this universe born, in what manner is it maintained, and how does its dissolution occur? Tell me the final judgment regarding this universe. || 3 ||

### The Lord Spoke

From the Elements <note>Sanskrit: Tattvas</note> the universe was born; by the Elements it is sustained; into the Elements it dissolves, O Goddess. Such is the definitive truth of the universe. || 4 ||

Mahadeva <note>The Great God, Shiva</note> said: O Goddess! ### The Goddess Spoke
<note>original: "Devyuvacha"</note>

> The elements alone are the supreme root, as determined by those who know the truth.
> O Lord, what is the true nature of these elements? Please reveal the nature of the elements to me. || 5 ||

Parvati said: Since the seekers of truth have determined that the elements are the very root of the universe, O Lord, what is the nature of these elements? Please reveal this to me. || 5 ||

### The Lord Spoke
<note>original: "Ishvara Uvacha"</note>

> From the one stainless, formless God, the Great Lord,
> Space was produced; from Space, Air was born. || 6 ||

Mahadeva said: From the one Supreme Lord, who is formless and free from the illusions of Maya, Space <note>Akasha; the ether or void</note> was born, and from Space, Air was produced. || 6 ||

> From Air came Fire, from Fire came Water, and from Water the Earth was born.
> These are the five elements, expanded fivefold. || 7 ||

From Air came Fire, from Fire came Water, and from Water the Earth was produced. These five elements each expand in five ways; that is to say, through the process of <term>Panchikaran; the quintuplication or mixing of elements</term>, they become twenty-five variations. || 7 ||

> From these the universe is born, by them it is sustained.
> It dissolves back into them, and dwells within them again in subtle form. || 8 ||

The universe is born from these, it is nourished by these, and having dissolved into these, it remains again in a subtle state. || 8 ||

> By the power of the breath, one travels to other lands; by the power of the breath, one takes food. By the power of the breath, one should perform the passing of urine and stool. || 24 ||
