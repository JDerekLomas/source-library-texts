# SYNOPSIS
## OF THE UNIVERSAL MAKING OF MUSIC
### ARRANGED INTO TEN BOOKS.

->Of which the first seven are contained in Volume 1. The remaining three in Volume 2.<-

**Book I.** *Physiological*, demonstrates the genesis, nature, and properties of natural sound, as well as its effects.
**Book II.** *Philological*, investigates the first institution and propagation of artificial sound, or Music.
**Book III.** *Arithmetical*, teaches the science of harmonic motions through numbers and a new Algebraic Music.
**Book IV.** *Geometrical*, shows the origin of consonant and dissonant intervals through the Geometrical, Algebraic, and Mechanical division of the monochord <note>A single-stringed instrument used to measure musical intervals</note> in manifold variety.
**Book V.** *Organical*, reveals the structure of musical instruments of every kind through new experiments.
**Book VI.** *Melothetic* <note>The art of composing melody</note>, produces a new and demonstrative method for composing songs of every kind; and contains whatever curious, rare, and secret thing can be desired regarding this business.
**Book VII.** *Diacritical* <note>Analytical or evaluative</note>, establishes a comparison of ancient music with the modern, detects abuses, commends the dignity of Ecclesiastical song, and reveals the method by which the perfection of pathetic <note>In the sense of "pathos"—music intended to stir deep emotion</note> music may finally be reached.
**Book VIII.** *Miraculous*, exhibits a new "Musarithmic" art <note>Kircher's system for automated composition</note>, by which anyone, even one unskilled in music, can reach a perfect knowledge of composing in a short time; it contains Combinatorial, Poetic, and Rhetorical Music. It adapts the Musarithm of Pangeoglossia <note>original: "Planglofsiani," likely referring to his universal language system</note> to all languages by a new artifice.
**Book IX.** *Magical*, produces the more recondite secrets of all Music; it contains the Physiology of consonance and dissonance; furthermore, Musico-medical magic, Phonocamptics (or the perfect doctrine of the Echo, how it is to be measured and how established); the construction of new hearing tubes or ear-trumpets; likewise, it teaches the structure of Statues and other "Autophonic" Musical Instruments (sounding by themselves), as well as Sympathetic instruments, through curious and new experiments. To these is annexed Musical Cryptology, by which the hidden concepts of the mind are manifested at a distance through sounds.
**Book X.** *Analogical*, exhibits the ten-stringed lute <note>original: "decachordon"</note> of nature, by which it is demonstrated through 10 steps—as if through 10 Registers of Nature—that God, in the construction of the 3 Worlds (the Elemental, Celestial, and Archetypal), looked toward musical proportions.

<image-desc>A large decorative right-hand curly brace encompasses the following list of ten Registers, pointing to the word "exhibet." centered vertically on the right.</image-desc>

Register 1. *The Symphonies of the Elements, or Elemental Music.*
Register 2. *The admirable Symphony of the Heavens in their motions, influences, and effects.*
Register 3. *The Symphony of Stones, Plants, and Animals, in Physical, Medical, and Chemical matters.*
Register 4. *The Music of the Microcosm with the Megacosm, that is, of the lesser world [man] with the greater world [the universe].*
Register 5. *Sphygmic Music, or the music of the pulse manifesting itself in the veins and arteries.*
Register 6. *Ethical Music, shining forth in the sensitive and rational appetite.*
Register 7. *Political, Monarchical, Aristocratic, Democratic, and Economic Music.*
Register 8. *Metaphysical Music, or the comparison of the interior Powers to the Angels and God.*
Register 9. *Hierarchical Music, or that of the Angels distributed into 9 choirs.*
Register 10. *Archetypal Music, or the harmony of God with all of nature.*

->Upon a ten-stringed psaltery I will sing praises to Thee.<- <note>A reference to Psalm 144:9</note>
