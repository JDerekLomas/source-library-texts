### The Post-Rider
Elster, Pleiße, and Parthe, <note>The three rivers that meet in Leipzig.</note> you three beloveds
Of love and unity, of wise and high customs,
Of Divine Scripture, and ancient German
Loyalty:
How does the song of lamentation come to you from the Elbe-stream?
You who flowed so full of wisdom and rich in goods,
Poured out from your narrow lap into all the world
The gifts of nature and God's sanctuary,
For the welfare, honor, and glory of all Christendom.
What have you done? What was your crime?
What is the misdeed for which you are being punished?
With such cruelty! With robbery, murder, and fire!
Reaching to the Mulde and Saale, and to the Elbe-strand!
Surely you did not burden yourselves with such great guilt
That you should endure this harassment for so many years:

Much shame and hardship you suffered and suppressed,
Yet neither hand nor finger ever twitched for revenge.
Indeed, you even placed three crowns upon the Eagle, <note>Referring to the Hapsburg Imperial Eagle.</note>
And whetted your heroes' swords for his protection,
Whereby all Silesia was brought to peace and rest,
And your gold and money were spent on it as well, etc.
But why do I lament so much? You have overcome:
On Saint Regina’s day, injustice was found out;
And our strong God, through the hand of our Swede,
Has turned the fury back upon the head of the Crocodile-Tilly. <note>original: "CrocoTillens"; a wordplay on "crocodile" and the name of the Imperial General, Count Tilly.</note>
Therefore, write now, and carve into the bark,
Into your palm trees and your veil-white linden trees,
What God has done for you, and spread abroad
At all your trade fairs this Memorial of Vengeance.

### Monument of Vengeance and Grave

With all his cunning and all his loot,

### RELIGION.

How wonderfully faithful you are in your stern judgments!
You watch for a long time, yet you do not forget at all
The insults committed against you and your Word, you faithful God of Truth, <note>original: "Amens-Gott." Refers to God as the "Amen," the final and true word.</note>

Praised be the faithfulness and goodness shown to us;

Grant further luck and victory: and let us also enjoy the spoils,
