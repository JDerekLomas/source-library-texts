A highly ornate engraved title page rich in symbolic imagery. 
- **Top:** The Hebrew Tetragrammaton (יהוה) shines within a radiant sunburst, encircled by the Latin text "EXALTABITUR SOLUS". Two winged angels kneel on clouds in adoration.
- **Center-Top:** A portrait of a bearded man with a ruff (King Christian IV of Denmark) in an oval frame, crowned and flanked by two cherubs.
- **Main Body:** The title text is inscribed on a large draped sheet. To the left stands a legendary Danish king or warrior in classical/medieval armor holding a scepter. To the right stands a female personification of Denmark or History, crowned with a wreath and holding a harp.
- **Base of Figures:** At their feet lie various runestones (monumenta) from the Danish landscape. The runes on the stones are legible: on the left "HULMKIR" (ᚼᚢᛚᛘᚴᛁᚱ) and on the right "THURUI : KUNU" (ᚦᚢᚱᚢᛁ ᛬ ᚴᚢᚾᚢ), referring to the Jelling Stones. The pedestals are inscribed "CONTERENTUR" (left) and "PENITUS" (right).
- **Bottom:** A central coat of arms (Danish royal arms) supported by cherubs sits above a cartouche containing the publication details. A frieze at the bottom depicts soldiers and battle scenes.
- **Artist Signature:** Along the bottom edge: "Sereniß: Daniæ, Norvegiæ &c: Regis sculptor Simon de Pas sculpsit."
