...[they] neither speak, nor work, nor study, nor do anything for the duration of their lives except for the sake of the earth and for time <note>original: "la terre & le temps" — referring to material possessions and the temporary nature of mortal life</note>, or to try to make themselves comfortable and happy in this miserable life, as if man were created only for it; this they do even though all men have come here only to perform **penance** <term>penance: an act of devotion or self-discipline to show sorrow for sin</term> and to work toward recovering the **Love of God** which all men lost in **Adam** through his sin.

<margin>14. *This is a life of penance, and not our primary purpose.*</margin>
Consequently, men should do nothing in this world that does not aim at this single purpose, since they are sent here for this reason alone. Human judgment can understand this by seeing that no men do anything here but eat and drink, sleep, and work for the maintenance of their **mortal life**; they are born in suffering and die in distress. Since all these things are bad, they cannot have been created by God, and even less can they have been the end or the goal for which God created man. It is a necessity that God must have had another purpose in creating man than these **temporal** and fleeting things, which cause man so many evils and miseries—nearly unbearable for many.

<margin>15. *Miseries of this life and of nature. They do not come from God.*</margin>
Such things cannot come from a God who is all-good and all-perfect, who can do nothing but good; for all His works are without any mixture of evil, being all good and all perfect, just as their Author is, who can never do wrong. All the **evils** we see in **nature**, both general and specific, have emerged from sin. This sin, having forced its way into the works of God, has corrupted and spoiled all the things that God had made very good. For after having created fire with a sweet and temperate heat to pleasantly warm all of nature, sin brought to it the burning heat and the smoke, which are two bad things that God did not create.
