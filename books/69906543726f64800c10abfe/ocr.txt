<header>About the Translator</header>

MICHAEL E. MARMURA was born in Jerusalem on November 11, 1929. He received his B.A. in 1953 from the University of Wisconsin and went on to obtain both an M.A. and a Ph.D. from the University of Michigan. He is professor emeritus at the University of Toronto, where he taught from 1959 until 1995 and where he twice chaired the Department of Middle East and Islamic Studies.

Dr. Marmura has published medieval Arabic philosophical texts and translations, as well as numerous articles on Islamic philosophy, many of them devoted to the thought of Avicenna and al-Ghazālī. His publications include an edition of Ibn Sīnā’s *Fī ithbāt al-nubuwwāt* (*Proof of prophecy*); *Refutation by Alexander of Aphrodisias of Galen’s Treatise on the Theory of Motion*, with N. Rescher; and *Islamic Theology and Philosophy: Studies in Honor of George F. Hourani*, of which he was the editor. He has also published a history of Islamic philosophy incorporated in *Der Islam II: Politische Entwicklungen und theologische Konzepte*, coauthored with W. Montgomery Watt.
