# About the Translator

MICHAEL E. MARMURA was born in Jerusalem on November 11, 1929. He received his Bachelor of Arts in 1953 from the University of Wisconsin and went on to obtain both a Master of Arts and a Doctor of Philosophy from the University of Michigan. He is professor emeritus <note>A title granted to retired professors to honor their distinguished service</note> at the University of Toronto, where he taught from 1959 until 1995 and where he twice chaired the Department of Middle East and Islamic Studies.

Dr. Marmura has published medieval Arabic philosophical texts and translations, as well as numerous articles on Islamic philosophy, many of them devoted to the thought of Avicenna <note>The Latinized name of Ibn Sīnā (980–1037), a Persian physician and philosopher</note> and al-Ghazālī <note>A major 11th-century Persian theologian and jurist</note>. His publications include an edition of Ibn Sīnā’s *On the Proof of Prophecies* <note>original: "Fī ithbāt al-nubuwwāt"</note>; *Refutation by Alexander of Aphrodisias of Galen’s Treatise on the Theory of Motion*, with N. Rescher; and *Islamic Theology and Philosophy: Studies in Honor of George F. Hourani*, of which he was the editor. He has also published a history of Islamic philosophy incorporated in *Islam II: Political Developments and Theological Concepts* <note>original: "Der Islam II: Politische Entwicklungen und theologische Konzepte"</note>, coauthored with W. Montgomery Watt.
