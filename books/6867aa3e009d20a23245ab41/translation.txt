As the Light rises and sets, it also receives four different names; for when the Moon is joined with the Sun and begins to shine again, it is called the **New Moon** <note>literally "New Light"</note>. When it has filled a quarter of its body, it is called the **First Quarter**; when it is entirely complete and bright, the **Full Moon**; and when it has decreased again to a quarter of its body, it is called the Second or Last Quarter. The year begins in **January** with the letter **a**. Whenever this is the **Sunday Letter**, it is printed large and in red, as occurred in the year 1637.
