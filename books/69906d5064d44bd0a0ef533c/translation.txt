### Now, the fragments within the Bhadrika Dasha

Great prowess, victory over enemy factions, the favor of the king, high prestige, a cheerful countenance, and great wealth are produced within the sub-period of **Bhadrika** <note>The Gentle or Auspicious; the fifth major Yogini period lasting five years.</note> occurring within the **Bhadrika** major cycle itself. 49

Affliction by skin diseases such as sores and itching, facial ailments, sorrow over the loss of horses or vehicles, and harassment from fire, thieves, the king, or enemies occur when [**Ulka**] is joined with **Bhadrika**. 50

The performance of sacrifices and rituals, the company of holy men, great joy in devotion to priests and gods, and the attainment of knowledge, righteousness, wealth, and power occur when **Siddha** falls within the **Bhadrika** sub-period. 51

Fever, vomiting, and obstructions in one's work; calamity, fear of fire, sorrow, loss of wealth, and destruction caused by the wicked; and distress, pain, and wounds for one's own family occur if **Sankata** moves within **Bhadrika**. 52

Gains of grain, happiness for one's own group, nourishment, the acquisition of a wife, and luxury in towns and cities are achieved; wealth and the power of counsel are made great when **Mangala**—and not just any luck—moves into the **Bhadrika** cycle. 53

Stable and infinite wealth, pure intelligence, luxury, and happiness; combined with stainless fame, these are granted by **Pingala** when it moves within **Bhadrika**, acting like the teacher of the gods <note>original: "Suraguru"; a reference to Brihaspati/Jupiter, the planet of wisdom and expansion.</note>. 54

## The Chapter on Bhadra
<note>original: "Bhadra-adhyaya"; the five-year period known as 'The Blessed' or 'The Gentle' associated with Mercury.</note>

In the **Bhadra** period, one gains brilliance and light. However, if the beginning of that period is unfavorable, even a man of great strength <note>original: "saddantashcha," literally meaning "one with good teeth," a metaphor for health and vitality</note> is surely destroyed. 81

In the **Mangala** period <note>original: "Mangala dasha"; the one-year period of 'The Auspicious' associated with the Moon</note>, there is auspiciousness. If the start of that period is also favorable, the fulfillment of your heart's desires is achieved as if one is bathed in success. 82

If the two periods <note>referring to the combination of the major cycle and the sub-cycle</note> are both favorable, the outcome is excellent. If one of them is otherwise, one should understand the result to be mixed. If both are unfavorable, the cycle grants an unmixed <note>entirely negative</note> result.

Thus ends the **Yogini Dasha** <note>The 'Cycle of the Yoginis,' a specific system of eight planetary periods in Indian astrology used to predict the timing of life events based on the lunar mansion at birth.</note>.
