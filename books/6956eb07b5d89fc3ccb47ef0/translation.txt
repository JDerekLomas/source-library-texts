# Description of the Three Principles of Divine Essence.

->That is<-

Concerning the without-origin <note>The German "ohn Uhrsprung" refers to that which is eternal and uncaused.</note> eternal birth of the Holy Trinity of God / and how through and out of the same were created the Angels / as well as the Heavens / also the Stars and Elements, together with all Creaturely Being / and everything that lives and moves therein.

->Primarily<-

Concerning Man / for what purpose he was created / and toward what kind of end : And then how He fell from his first Paradisiacal Glory into the angry Fierceness <note>original: "Grimmigkeit." In Böhme's thought, this refers to the harsh, consuming quality of the "dark fire" or the wrathful side of nature.</note> / and in his first beginning died away unto Death / and how he was helped again.

->And then also<-

What the Wrath of God (Sin / Death / Devil and Hell) may be : How the same once stood in eternal Rest / and in great Joy : Also how everything in this time took its beginning / and how it operates now / and what it shall finally become again.

### By Jacob Böhme, of A. S. <note>A. S. stands for "Alt Seidenburg," Böhme's birthplace.</note>

## Necessary Warning from the Author to the Reader of this Book / from Chapter 4, Section 43 of this Treatise.

But Reader / I want to have you faithfully warned / if you are not on the path of the lost son who is returning to the Father <note>A reference to the Parable of the Prodigal Son from Luke 15</note>, then leave this book of mine unread / otherwise it will go badly for you. For the Grand Prince Lucifer will not rest from troubling you. For in this book he stands quite naked and exposed before the children of God. He is ashamed of this, just like a man who is put to public shame before all people because of his misdeeds. Therefore be warned / if your tender flesh is ever dear to you / then leave my book unread. If you do not follow this / and disaster arises for you / then I shall be innocent / blame only yourself. For what I now recognize / I write for myself as a <term>Memorial</term> <note>original: "Memorial" — a private record or memorandum intended to preserve spiritual insights for the author's own use</note> / but God well knows what He intends to do / which is still hidden from me to some extent.

<image-desc>A decorative scrollwork flourish centered below the main text block.</image-desc>

->J. B.<- <note>Initials for Jacob Böhme (1575–1624), the German Christian mystic and theosopher.</note>
