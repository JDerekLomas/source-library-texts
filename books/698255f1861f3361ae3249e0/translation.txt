*...that philosophical blessing of God was not denied to certain men. For certain reasons, it did not seem advisable to publish my name, as I do not seek praise for myself, but rather strive to be useful to the lovers of Philosophy. Therefore, I leave the vain desire for glory to those who prefer to **SEEM** rather than to **BE** <note>original: "VIDERI quàm ESSE." A common philosophical maxim emphasizing that true character and substance are more important than public reputation.</note>.
