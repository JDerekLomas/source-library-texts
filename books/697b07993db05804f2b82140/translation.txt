->HERMETIC PHILOSOPHY<-
<note>original: "PHILOSOPHIA HERMETICA." Hermeticism is a philosophical and esoteric tradition based on writings attributed to the mythical figure Hermes Trismegistus, often associated with alchemy and ancient wisdom.</note>

<image-desc>A circular emblem or bookplate is pasted onto the center of the textured page. The central oval depicts a deep blue sky with a golden sun at the top emitting rays of light. Below the sun, a white bird—specifically a "pelican in its piety," a symbol of self-sacrifice and the alchemical process—stands upon a silver crescent moon. Beneath the moon is a heraldic shield featuring four red roses on a white field, separated by a dark saltire (an X-shaped cross). The text "HERMETIC PHILOSOPHY" is printed in a circular fashion around this central heraldic device.</image-desc>
