For Fechner, God is the totalized <gloss>underlined in original:</gloss> consciousness of the entire universe. The Earth's consciousness forms one part of this, just as your human consciousness and mine form parts of the Earth's total consciousness. As I understand Fechner (though I am not certain), the entire Universe—and therefore God as well—evolves over time; that is to say, God has a genuine history. Using us as its human organs of experience, the Earth enriches its own inner life until it also "perishes" <note>original: "geht zu grunde"; in this context, it refers to the transition from physical existence to a higher spiritual state through "going under" or dying</note> and becomes immortal. It achieves this immortality in the form of those even broader elements of inner experience which its history is even now building.
