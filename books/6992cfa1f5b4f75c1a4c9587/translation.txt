Following the defeat of **Napoleon**, the ministers of various nations convened a congress in **Vienna**. Because they were interspersed among the other territories, **Geneva**, **Neuchâtel** <gloss>formerly belonging to Prussia</gloss>, and **Valais** <gloss>formerly belonging to Austria</gloss>—these three cantons were joined with the others, making twenty-two cantons in total to form the nation of **Switzerland**. 

Adhering to their ancient customs, the people collectively elect local officials to manage their affairs. Based on the local terrain and the census of households, they conscript able-bodied men to defend against foreign aggression. The Great Powers are unable to exert control over them.
