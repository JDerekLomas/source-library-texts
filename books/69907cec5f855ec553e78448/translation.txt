### The Results of the <term>Aries Ascendant</term>
<note>original: "Meṣa Lagna Phalam." The "ascendant" is the zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at the exact moment of birth.</note>

One born when <term>Aries</term> <note>original: "Meṣa." The first sign of the zodiac, symbolized by the Ram.</note> is rising will have a dark-complexioned body and face. They are virtuous, possess glass-like <note>original: "kācara" (usually referring to grey, blue, or light-colored eyes)</note> eyes, and are talkative, yet can be harsh or rough in temperament. They are short in stature and have a physical constitution dominated by <term>wind and phlegm</term> <note>original: "Vāta-Śleṣma." Two of the three 'doshas' or humors in Ayurvedic medicine that govern health.</note>. At the time of birth, the infant's head faces East in an old, permanent house. The child may be wrapped in the umbilical cord across the right shoulder or the left knee, and may be found in soiled cloths.

**Life Events and Risks:**
*   **First Year:** The child will experience some form of physical pain or suffering.
*   **Fifth Year:** A period of thief-like cunning may emerge; there is a risk of danger from fire, dust, or water.
*   **Tenth Year:** Risk of fear from disease.
*   **Twenty-seventh Year:** Risk of danger from poison.
*   **Thirty-second Year:** Risk of <term>untimely death</term> <note>original: "apamṛtyu," a death that occurs before one's destined lifespan is complete.</note>.

If the birth chart is viewed by auspicious planets, the person will live for eighty-five years. Death will occur in the month of <term>Kartika</term> <note>approximately October–November</note> on the day of the <term>New Moon</term> <note>original: "Amāvasyā"</note> under the <term>Mrigashira</term> star <note>original: "Nakṣatra"</note> due to illness. 
**Thus ends the results for the Aries Ascendant.**

### The Results of the <term>Taurus Ascendant</term>
<note>original: "Vṛṣa Lagna Phalam." The second sign of the zodiac, symbolized by the Bull.</note>

One born under <term>Taurus</term> has a white or very fair complexion and fair limbs. They are fortunate and possess slightly tawny or reddish hair. This sign is generally auspicious; if the infant is a girl, she will possess these same qualities. At birth, the child is wrapped in the umbilical cord. They are born in the middle of a beautiful house with the head facing West. They possess a handsome nose. 

The father of such a person is of a stable character. The house of birth has a door facing East. The individual is a person of few words but is prone to headaches. They are energetic and likely to be a writer or scribe.

**Life Events and Risks:**
*   **Fourteenth Year:** Danger from water.
*   **Twenty-seventh Year:** Danger from poison.
*   **Eighty-fifth Year:** Risk of <term>epilepsy</term> <note>original: "apasmāra," which can also refer to fainting fits or loss of consciousness.</note>.

If viewed by auspicious planets, the person lives for eighty-five years. Death occurs in the month of <term>Magha</term> <note>approximately January–February</note> on the thirteenth day of the bright fortnight, a Wednesday, under the <term>Ardra</term> star, caused by a <term>female spirit</term>...
<note>original: "ḍākinī." In medieval astrology, this often refers to a specific type of supernatural affliction or a sudden, unexplained illness attributed to "consuming" spirits.</note>

### The Results of the <term>Gemini Ascendant</term>
<note>original: "Mithuna-phalam." Gemini is the third sign of the zodiac, symbolized by the Twins.</note>

One born when **Gemini** is rising will have a rounded stature, <term>weak digestion</term> <note>original: "mandāgni." A slow metabolic rate according to Ayurveda.</note>, and a dark complexion specifically on the upper thighs. They are wise, appear mature or elderly, and are extremely restless or fickle. They possess a long nose, a fair-complexioned body, and a physical constitution dominated by <term>wind and phlegm</term>. 

They have thick hair and may be prone to skin eruptions or boils. Their speech is harsh. At the time of birth, the infant's head faces West and the house is West-facing. In terms of family, their situation regarding wives is "uneven"—perhaps having three, five, seven, or nine. Their father may be in a foreign land.

**Physical and Personal Traits:**
The individual is tawny or reddish-brown in color, may have dark teeth, is virtuous, and is fond of physical intimacy. 

**Life Events and Risks:**
*   **Third Year:** Danger or fear from fire.
*   **Fifth and Seventh Years:** Periods of physical pain or suffering.
*   **Longevity:** If the birth chart is aspected by <term>benefic planets</term> <note>original: "śubhagrahā," referring to auspicious planets like Jupiter or Venus.</note>, the individual will live for seventy years.
*   **End of Life:** Death occurs on the tenth lunar day of the bright fortnight of the month of **Pausha** <note>December/January</note>, on a Wednesday, under the **Mrigashira** star, following a period of illness.

Thus ends the results for **Gemini**. ||3||

### The Results of the <term>Cancer Ascendant</term>
<note>original: "Karka-phalam." Cancer is the fourth sign of the zodiac, symbolized by the Crab.</note>

One born under **Cancer** is sharp-eyed with sparkling or fiery eyes, sometimes of a light or glass-like color. They may have weaknesses or defects in the hands and feet. They are devoted to their mother and father, wise, and skilled in business affairs. Their constitution is dominated by <term>wind</term> <note>original: "Vāta."</note> and they have a preference for white clothing. 

They often experience periods of suffering and have tawny hair. Their relationships are balanced, and they have a high or prominent forehead. At birth, the head is positioned to the left and the feet toward the West. They may be prone to laziness but are pleasant to look at and dedicated to the service of others.

**Life Events and Risks:**
*   **Eighth Month:** A period of suffering in infancy.
*   **First Year:** Danger from fever.
*   **Longevity:** If aspected by benefic planets, the individual will live for ninety years.
*   **End of Life:** Death occurs on the seventh lunar day of the bright fortnight of the month of **Magha** <note>January/February</note>.

Thus ends the results for **Cancer**. ||4||

### The Results for the Sign of Virgo
<note>original: "Kanyā-phale." Virgo is the sixth sign of the zodiac, symbolized by a Maiden.</note>

**Character and Social Standing:**
They are devoted to their teachers, the gods, and the sacred fire. They may have two marriages. At the time of their birth, the mother experienced great hardship. They possess a broad head and distinct lines on their feet. They are fond of royal courts or assemblies, enjoy dairy foods, are fortunate, and act as a "lamp" to their family—bringing them great honor.

**Life Events and Risks:**
*   **Sixteenth Year:** A period of danger or "small death."
*   **Longevity:** If the birth chart receives the auspicious gaze of benefic planets, the individual will live for eighty years.
*   **End of Life:** Death occurs on the eighth day of the bright half of the month of <term>Ashadha</term> <note>June–July</note> on a Saturday, when the Moon is in a specific position, due to <term>abdominal wind-tumor</term> <note>original: "vāyu-gulma-roga." # Scorpio (Vrishchika)
One born under **Scorpio** has tawny hair, is proud, and possesses a flickering or restless nature. They are a hearty eater and often wear soiled clothing. There is a distinct birthmark on the genitals. They have well-bound or curly hair and a handsome appearance. They are destined to have two wives <note>original: "dvivaravan," which can also imply receiving two boons or choices</note>, and they are skilled as a craftsman or artisan. They are virtuous, and their relationships with women are balanced.

**Birth Omens and Risks:**
At the time of birth, a shadow is cast over the right arm; the hand is harsh or cruel in appearance. The infant is fair-complexioned and the head faces North. At the moment of delivery, a lamp is lit and the child is wrapped in red cloth. 
*   **Twelfth Year:** Danger of a premature death.
*   **Thirty-second Year:** Death occurs due to a disease.

# Sagittarius (Dhanu)
