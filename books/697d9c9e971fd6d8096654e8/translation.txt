## Preface.

The article concerning heavenly medicine is rich and precious, well-grounded in Holy Scripture, and extensive, encompassing also the conversion of the sinner. It is most lovely, useful, and comforting to all poor sinners who desire to be rid of their sin, even though few concern themselves with it. It shows, therefore, how the merciful, kind God deals with his elect, whom he receives into grace, whom he transfers out of Satan’s kingdom into his heavenly kingdom; how he converts them, treats the soul-sick with medicine <note>The original German uses the verb *arzeneyet*, a now-archaic term meaning to apply medical treatment or to act as a physician.</note>, heals them, and makes them healthy—all through Jesus Christ <note>original: *Jesum Christum*</note> his Son, our Lord. Thus, four main points will primarily be dealt with here in a summary <note>original: *Summa*, a Latin term for a compendium or comprehensive summary of a subject.</note>, so that what follows may be more easily understood.

## Preface.

First: All these sick ones are natural, "old-Adamic" humans <note>The term "old-Adamic" refers to the theological concept of the "Old Adam," representing human nature in its fallen, sinful state inherited from the biblical first man, Adam.</note>, none excepted, who descend from the blood and flesh of *Adam* and *Eve*.

Second: Spiritual sickness is nothing other than the evil, sinful nature which brings eternal death, under which all people are confined, so that *God* <note>original: *GOtt*</note> may have mercy on all. *Romans 11:32.*

Third: Jesus Christ <note>original: *Jesus Christus*</note> with His grace is the only physician of the sick soul—indeed, of the entire sinful human being who was corrupted through Adam’s disobedience. God, the kind and merciful Father, has appointed Him to us poor, sick people as the Restorer, Physician, and Savior, after having given Him all power and a name that is above every name, so that all who believe in Him shall not die the eternal death, but shall instead become healed, joyful, and healthy.
