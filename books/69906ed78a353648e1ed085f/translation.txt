# In the name of the Lord, here begins the book containing all the faculties, which is called the **NOTARY ART.**

The work of all sacred Eloquence and certain knowledge begins here, in the holy Lebanon <note>original: "sanctiū libanũ"; likely a metaphorical reference to a place of purity or divine wisdom</note>; however, it is forbidden to those who are unworthy, since all things depend upon it and it exceeds human faculty. And because a gift so excellent is deserved by name, devout words cannot find their effect without it. But because the mystery of this great work is in this natural method, the failure is not in the work itself but in the operator. It is my duty to instruct properly in the form of the instruction. This Notary Art was revealed to Apollonius <note>original: "apendrosia"; likely a corruption of Apollonius of Tyana, to whom this art was often attributed</note> through certain vows, that is, prayers, and it works marvelously. Through it, the operation is purified and made effective. Those who wish to have all things in this work must first observe faith and reason, so that nothing which was visible remains hidden, and the darkness is compelled to reveal itself. And pray to Him who gave the command that he should persist in this, according to the devotion described herein: that whoever applies himself to learning this Art, whether he gives or receives it industriously, let him know he stands in no small danger of body or soul if he acts with a lying mind.

### The prologue ends. The precepts of this art begin.

Thus, coming to the prologues and the orders of this art, on the first day you must fast (unless it be a Sunday). There is an expiation of alms and rituals, that is, the days which are to be remembered with the highest devotion in prayer. On that very day, and every day, once in the early morning, and three times at the third, sixth, and ninth hours, and at vespers. <note>These refer to the canonical hours: Terce (9am), Sext (12pm), None (3pm), and Vespers (sunset).</note> In the evening twilight, what is revealed to you that night, you must never presume to reveal to anyone.

### This is the order and time, as was said before, that must be done.

**M**ost High God, Father of Mercy, Christ Jesus most sweet, Holy Spirit most worthy, through whose merits man is made pure; most holy and most high Unity. King of Kings most powerful, Lord Almighty most glorious, Creator and Disposer most merciful, Moderator and Ruler of all creatures, visible and invisible. Before whose terrible and ineffable majesty the smallest things are to be feared, O Almighty. Heaven, earth, sea, and the depths, and all that they contain, the triple machinery of the world <note>The celestial, terrestrial, and infernal realms</note> dominates, venerates, trembles, and obeys. God of Sabaoth <note>Lord of Hosts</note>, ineffable and most strong God, insuperable, great and immense God, whom no sense can grasp. Above all things lovable, admirable instructor, most just teacher of works, most wise teacher of teachers, most merciful instructor of all prelates, most benign lover of the humble. God of gods and Lord of the wise, in whom are all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge; who alone without error or end, gives wisdom and science to whomsoever you wish to teach, and you are able if you will, you who are the expert of experts. Spirit of spirits, most pious inspector, present in constant prayer, through whom we are, we live, and we move; who sits above the Cherubim, beholding the abyss and the depths of hearts and the joints of the fingers. I confirm today before your holy and tremendous, and ineffably glorious majesty, and before all the celestial virtues and powers, Adoration.

[I stand before] your glorious majesty, praising and blessing you, my Lord and my God, and invoking your great name above all names, which is God above all. And I pray to you, most high Lord, to be adored, worshipped, and feared, great God Adonay <note>Hebrew: "Lord"</note>, and the wonderful dispenser of all blessed dignities and of all the best gifts, and the most generous giver to whom you will: that you may mercifully and abundantly pour out today the hand of your power and the spirit of grace upon me. And you, most merciful Lord, who deigned to create the first man in your image, deign to look upon and strengthen the temple of my body. And may it please you today to dwell in my whole heart, descending from the most splendid temple of the heavens in the flow of grace and the work of ineffable majesty. Send forth today, most high and benign Lord, the spirit of the glory of your name and the holy seat of your glorious majesty, and bless me with the everlasting blessing of your grace: the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and fortitude, the spirit of knowledge and piety, the spirit of the fear and love of you, to know your will and to perform it freely in all your incomprehensible ways. To enjoy, understand, remember, and wonder at the holy power of your mysteries, the secrets of your wisdom which it becomes you to manifest, which lie hidden and are believed. Grant your faithful to know, so that I may be able to understand and comprehend the profound benignity and ineffable sweetness of your mercy in the mind of the piety of your divinity. You yourself, most merciful Lord, who once breathed the face and hand of man into being, deign today to pour into my memory, mercifully and permanently, a full, true, subtle, uplifting, swift, right, and perfect intelligence. Give me a modest, provident, tenacious, and effective memory. May the grace of the Holy Spirit be a honeyed, ready, and overflowing genius, so that from the multiplication of many fruits, and from the most generous bounty of many gifts and the multitude of your graces, I may remain copiously enriched. May I never be rejected, and may I ardently love, glorify, praise, adore, bless, and exalt you alone, the living Lord, who are the only true, supreme, and ineffable good above all things, King of kings and Lord of all lords, at all times. And in this, the sweetness of your magnificence, may the most sweet and blessed ardor of your love be vehemently and permanently in my heart, and may my soul flame and flourish entirely in you. With whom, O Almighty God, is the highest peace and true wisdom, ineffable sweetness, unquenchable suavity, ineffable joy, the desire of all good and blessed things. A blessed society and likewise glorious has been yours from eternity, and is, and shall be. To you be unchangeable health, bread of life, inestimable splendor, blessing, and clarity, honor, praise, and ineffable glory, and power and strength, for all ages of ages. Amen.

# On Abstinence

**Concerning the work of abstinence, truly from the twentieth day and beyond**, you shall apply yourself and observe carefully. On the twenty-first day, from morning until midday, perform eight [recitations] with their mental additions <note>original: "mentitialibus," referring to specific internal meditations or visualizations that accompany the spoken prayers</note>. From midday until twilight, perform seven. On the twenty-second day, likewise, you shall perform eight until midday. On the twenty-third day, from morning until midday, eight times; from midday through the whole of twilight, eight times; and after twilight, seven. On the twenty-fourth day, likewise, after twilight you shall perform eight. On the twenty-fifth day, from morning until the ninth hour <note>approximately 3:00 PM</note>, fourteen times; from the ninth hour until twilight with its mental additions, twelve. On the twenty-sixth day, from morning until the ninth hour, twice seven <note>14</note>; from the ninth hour until twilight, eight, yet this time without the mental additions. On the twenty-seventh day, likewise, from the ninth hour until after twilight, you shall perform twice seven. On the twenty-eighth day, from morning until midday, twice ten <note>20</note>; from midday until twilight, nine from the start. On the twenty-ninth day, counting from the moon <note>likely referring to the lunar calendar cycle</note>, let it be known that on the twenty-ninth day there is no prayer after twilight; instead, perform it ten times. After these days, you shall spend two holy days mixing the books, holding the Arts in memory, not merely treating this art but entreating God assiduously, so that through Him you may obtain the result of such difficult labor. For the rest, it is placed for you in the hand of the Omnipotent, who is everywhere present, from whom and through whom He made all things and the world. Amen. Up to this point in this Art, perform the prayers without interruption. All things are possible through this altar; no part of it is evil. Happy is the disciple who is firmly taught.

"Let there be light, and there was light." <note>Genesis 1:3</note> Light <term>lux: the source of light</term> is made by a single radiating thing, like the sun in the sky or fire on the earth. But "illumination" <term>lumen: light in transit</term> is that which proceeds from the source-light and is spread through a medium, just as the air is said to be illuminated by the sun or by a candle. And "color" is light incorporated or fixed in an opaque body, such as in wood or stone, or in another dense body. Whence source-light is in the fountain-head, illumination is in the middle, and color is at the extremity. And note that source-light is said to be in the angels through grace; illumination is in them through glory; color is through their union with an assumed body <note>when angels take on human form to appear to men</note>. Source-light is in them according to how they are in God; illumination according to how they are in themselves; color according to how they appear to men. As it is said in Genesis: "God saw the light, that it was good." <margin>Genesis 1</margin>

<margin>What light is</margin>
Source-light <note>original: "lux"</note> is the primary active quality existing by itself in a shining body, and it is the fountain and origin of illumination. Illumination <note>original: "lumen"</note> is truly the appearance of light received in a transparent medium. A Ray is illumination extended through a straight line. Splendor is illumination reflected from a polished and solid body. <unclear>Whence</unclear> source-light is the <unclear>initial</unclear> act of the shining thing, while illumination is the <unclear>progressive</unclear> act of the medium.
