### TO THE
## READER

It has been many years now, Dear Reader, that one hears of nothing but quarrels and disputes throughout all of <term>Christendom</term><note>The collective community of Christians or Christian nations.</note>, concerning the articles of faith: for this reason, so many <term>Colleges</term> and <term>Universities</term> have been established in the world, and such a great number of weighty books have been written regarding the various opinions in matters of <term>Theology</term><note>Original: "Godts-geleertheit," literally meaning "God-learnedness."</note>; so many <term>Councils</term><note>Formal assemblies of church leaders meant to settle matters of doctrine.</note> have been assembled; so many high Teachers have been brought together in our times, and with such great care, diligence, effort, and expense: and yet, until now, one has not been able to prevent discord and division. The schisms and disputes within Christendom are greater today than ever before. How many contradictory opinions are there among the theologians? How many persons have separated themselves from the <term>Roman Church</term> in order to uphold their own opinions? How many...

<summary>In this preface, the author laments the persistent and growing religious divisions within the Christian world, noting that despite the proliferation of universities, theological texts, and church councils, unity remains elusive.</summary>
