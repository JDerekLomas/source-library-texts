# Om, salutations to Lord Shiva || Now, the Wheel of the Nine Planets ||

This is the written human-figure wheel <term>Nru-chakra; a diagram where stars are mapped onto a human body</term> for you, according to where the Sun is situated. 1

Taking that star <term>Nakshatra; one of the 27 lunar mansions of Hindu astrology</term> as the starting point, one should assign three stars to the head and three to the face. One star should be given to each of the two shoulders, one to each of the two arms, and one to each of the two hands. Five stars are placed in the heart, one in the navel, one in the private parts, and one in each of the two knees. Finally, assign six stars to the feet. 

A star situated on the feet indicates a person who is fearless in their own city. If one is born under a star in the feet while traveling abroad, they may become a householder with another's wife <note>original: "paradaraka"; a traditional astrological warning of social or moral transgression during travel</note>. In the navel, one is easily satisfied. In the heart, one is well-settled and stable. If the stars fall on the pair of hands, one is a thief. On the arms, one will be displaced from their position. On the shoulders, one attains the status of one who rides an elephant <note>original: "gajaskandhagami"; a metaphor for high social status or royalty</note>. In the face, one enjoys eating sweet foods. If the star is on the head, the person becomes a leader or person of high rank. The calculation is made from the Sun's current star to the individual's birth star. 

**Thus the Sun Wheel**

One should count from the birth sign <term>Rashi</term> to the current star. ### The Results of the Crow’s Landing

By the command of Lord Shiva <note>original: "shiva-ajñaya"; implying the following omens are divine laws</note>, if a crow <note>original: "vayasah"; a common Sanskrit term for a crow or raven</note> stays or lands [upon a person], that human faces death and the loss of wealth; of this, there is no doubt.

### The Results of Crow Droppings

**The Great Garuda said:**
"O Blessed One, Supreme Lord <note>original: "parameshvarah"; referring to Shiva</note>, I wish to hear what you have spoken. A doubt has arisen in me: what are the effects of <term>Kaka-vishta; crow droppings</term> falling upon the head, the neck, the arms, the bladder, the belly, the middle of the body, or the abdomen? What are these signs called?"

**The Lord replied:**
"Death is to be understood if it falls on the head. If on the neck, it signifies the destruction of wealth. Death is also predicted if it falls on the heart. If in the region of the belly, it signifies ruin; if on the back, it indicates harm to the father <note>original: "pitri-ghatane"; can also refer to ancestors or the lineage</note>. If on the abdomen, one attains disease; it indicates the end of life and the destruction of sons. If on the feet..."

### The Results of Crow Droppings

Now begins the section on the results of crow droppings <note>original: "kāka-viṣṭā"; considered a significant omen in Indian augury (Śakuna Śāstra)</note> and the ritual remedy. One should prepare an equal mixture [of cleansing pastes] and have a full-body massage performed.
