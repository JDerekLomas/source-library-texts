# PREFACE.

*For those persons who consider these introductory pieces to be essential to the structure of a work, the author has seen fit, on behalf of his honest printer, to provide these lines under the title of* **A PREFACE**; *and to declare:*

" *That (according to his best judgment and authority) these presents <note>original: "these presents"; a legal term referring to the document currently being read</note> ought to pass, and be received, interpreted, and taken as a full and satisfactory substitute for all preliminary writing, dedications, or any direct or indirect requests for favor from the public, or from any private patron or party whatsoever.*

" *Nothing to the contrary appearing to him from the perspective of truth or reason.*" *Witness his hand, this fifth day of December, 1710.*
