*Persons who esteem these Introductory Pieces as very essential in the Constitution of a Work; he has thought fit, in behalf of his honest Printer, to substitute these Lines under the Title of* A PREFACE; *and to declare,*
" *That (according to his best Judgment and Authority) these Presents ought to pass, and be receiv'd,*
" *constru'd, and taken, as satisfactory in full, for all Preliminary*
" *Composition, Dedication, direct or indirect Application for Favour to*
" *the Publick, or to any private Patron, or Party whatsoever:*
" *Nothing to the contrary appearing to him, from the side of Truth,*
" *or Reason.*" *Witness his Hand, this Fifth Day of December, 1710.*

A. A. C. A. N. A. Æ.
C. M. D. C. L. X. X. J.
