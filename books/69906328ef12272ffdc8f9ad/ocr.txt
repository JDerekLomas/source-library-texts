<header>The DEDICATION.</header>
<meta>catchword: The</meta>

As the very Nature of the Subject carries both Horror and Difficulty, polite Men, and great Lovers of Ease, will turn away their Thoughts from it with Disdain ; and tho' it be of as much Importance as a sanguinary Law, and the Lives of Men, and the Honour of our Nation's Justice can make it, they will rather let the several Schemes take their own Course, than fully and disturb their own Minds, or engage their Reputations about them.

Dark superstitious Tempers, that usually form their Religion suitable to their own Complexions, will always have a Bias towards the Belief of them; and upon every sudden Surprise, will be frighted into Actions that will disturb the Places where they shall be, and greatly hurt the suspected Persons.
