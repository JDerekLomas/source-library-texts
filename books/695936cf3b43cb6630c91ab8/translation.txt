### HOME USE RULES

All books are subject to recall <note>The library may request the return of a book before its due date if another reader needs it.</note>.

All borrowers must register in the library to borrow books for home use.

All books must be returned at the end of the college year for inspection and repairs.

Limited books <note>Books with restricted circulation periods.</note> must be returned within the four-week limit and cannot be renewed.

Students must return all books before leaving town. Faculty officers should arrange for the return of books requested during their absence from town.

Volumes of periodicals and pamphlets are kept in the library as much as possible. For special purposes, they may be lent out for a limited time.

Borrowers should not use their library privileges for the benefit of other persons.

Books of special value and gift books—when the donor requests it—are not allowed to circulate.

Readers are asked to report all cases of books being marked or damaged.
