### On the
## *Reverend and Most Excellent*
## *Man*
# Lord THEODORIC Hackspan, <note>Theodoric (Dietrich) Hackspan (1607–1659) was a noted Lutheran theologian and Orientalist at the University of Altdorf.</note>
### MOST RENOWNED THEOLOGIAN AND PHILOSOPHER,
### DEATH.

Those whom an equality of studies had joined for many years;
These a single day of sad fate separates.
Heavenly rest is won for you, HACKSPAN! your body
The earth covers: your fame flies through the mouths of men. <note>original: "volitat fama per ora virum" — a phrase famously used by the Roman poet Virgil, suggesting that the deceased’s reputation will live on through the speech of the living.</note>
But the anxiety of my labors exhausts me:
Hence I long for the quiet dwellings of my Father's heaven.
One day shall join us again in the citadel above.
O, may that day come more swiftly to us!

->JOHANN MICHAEL DILHERR,<- <note>Dilherr (1604–1669) was a significant German academic, clergyman, and hymn-writer in Nuremberg.</note>
->Pastor and Public Professor<-
->in Nuremberg.<-

# TO THE MEN
## *Most Reverend, Most*
## *Renowned as well as Most Excellent,*

**LORD JOHANNES MICHAEL DILHERR,**
Chief Pastor of the Church of St. Sebald, Public Professor of Holy Theology and Philosophy, Director of the Gymnasium and most distinguished Librarian. <note>Dilherr was a central figure in 17th-century Nuremberg, managing both the city's spiritual life at St. Sebald's and its educational standards at the Egidien-Gymnasium.</note>
**LORD DANIEL WÜLFFER,** Most vigilant Pastor of the Church of St. Lawrence, Public Professor of Logic and Metaphysics. <note>The Church of St. Lawrence (St. Lorenz) was the other primary parish church in Nuremberg; Wülffer was also a famed hymn-writer.</note>
**LORD LEONHARD FRISCH,**
most faithful Preacher at St. Giles. <note>St. Giles (St. Aegidien) was a prominent church associated with the city's high school.</note>
**LORD JOHANNES FABRICIUS,** most pious Preacher at the Church of the Blessed Virgin. <note>The Frauenkirche, located on the central market square of Nuremberg.</note>
**LORD MARTIN BEER,** most deserving Preacher at the Holy Spirit, Public Professor of History. <note>The Holy Spirit Church (Heilig-Geist-Spital) was historically significant as the repository of the Imperial Regalia of the Holy Roman Empire.</note>
**LORD JUSTUS JACOBO LEIBNITZ,**
most religious Preacher at St. James. <note>St. James (St. Jakob) was the church of the Teutonic Knights in the city.</note>
