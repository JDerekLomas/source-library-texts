### EPITAPH OF A DRUNKEN <gloss>little</gloss>
### little jester, in Scazon meter.
<note>Scazon, also known as "limping iambics," is a poetic meter where the final rhythm is intentionally "broken" or reversed. It was traditionally used for satire or mockery, perfectly suiting this poem's subject.</note>

Peace be with you, traveler; read these verses in silence,

lest the noise steal my sweet sleep away from me,

the end of my drinking and the end of my living
were one and the same. But someone might believe me drunk even now,
or dreaming, since I say these things while sleeping.
Farewell, traveler; now depart silently.
