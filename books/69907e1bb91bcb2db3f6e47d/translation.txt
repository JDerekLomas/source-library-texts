# The Effects of the Sun in the Twelve Houses

**Om! Salutations to the Auspicious Ganesha.** <note>original: "Om Shri Ganeshaya Namah." It is traditional in Sanskrit manuscripts to begin with an invocation to Ganesha, the remover of obstacles.</note>

If the **Sun** <note>original: "Ravi"</note> is situated in the **Ascendant** <note>original: "Lagna," the first house of the zodiac which represents the self and physical body</note>, he takes away the happiness provided by the husband and cattle. There is pain in the skull as well as the upper limbs. She will constantly suffer from disorders of the wind and flatulence, and her body will always be lean. Such is the nature of the Sun in the first house. 1

When the **Sun** is in the **Second House** <note>original: "Dhana-stha," the house of wealth and speech</note>, she is prone to bilious temperaments. Her wealth is diminished, though she remains a woman worthy of respect and honor. However, she may become a speaker of harsh words and her mind often dwells on the past. Despite having many assets, her heart finds little true happiness. 2

In the **Third House**, the Sun makes a woman brave and industrious, yet she may exist in a state of constant restlessness. She is fond of disputes and quarrels. She frequently suffers from ailments affecting the throat and the ears. 3

If the **Sun** is in the **Fourth House** at the time of birth, he takes away the happiness of the heart and is harmful to the mother <note>The text suggests this placement may cause early loss of or conflict with the mother</note>. She is fond of grains and circular ornaments, yet she suffers from pain in the breasts and is prone to "neck-garland" tumors <note>original: "kanthamala," likely referring to goiter or scrofula, an inflammation of the lymph nodes</note>. 4

With the **Sun** in the **Fifth House**, the woman is respected, yet she may have no daughters, or her children may not bring her joy. She may lack intelligence and possess a very lean frame. She suffers from chronic enlargement of the spleen or stomach ailments, though she remains devoted to her family.

## Mars
When **Mars** <term>Kuja|In Vedic astrology, Mars is the planet of energy, fire, and aggression, often associated with a "cruel" or "malefic" nature in traditional interpretations.</term> is in the **Ascendant** <term>Vilagna|The First House, which represents the physical body, temperament, and self-identity.</term>, he acts as a thief of wealth and happiness. She suffers from ailments regarding her husband and is afflicted by diseases of the head. She eats poor-quality grains <note>original: "kudhānya"</note>, possesses excessive body hair, and has a rounded physique. She is constantly prone to deceit, deviousness, and base behavior. **1**

When he is situated in the **House of Wealth** <note>original: "Dhanastha," the Second House</note>, there is no happiness for her. She suffers from pain caused by a major disease, specifically from hemorrhoids <note>original: "arśa-rogāt"</note>. There is no joy to be found within the family <note>original: "kutumbe"</note>. She is highly argumentative <note>original: "mahāvādinī"</note> and suffers from dental diseases and bile-related issues <note>original: "pitta"</note> as she ages. **2**

In the **Third House**, Mars acts as the destroyer of wisdom <note>original: "jñāna-hantā"</note>. 
<margin>ch</margin> 
Even while wandering to auspicious pilgrimage sites, she lacks the intellect to engage in righteous duties. She is preoccupied with the thoughts of many people. Mars in this position grants a rounded body, a tall stature, and a beautiful, golden complexion <note>original: "suvarṇī"</note>. **3**

When Mars is in the **Fourth House**, there is distress from disease and an excess of bile, as well as a burning sensation in the heart <note>original: "hrdo-dahaṃ," which could refer to physical heartburn or emotional anguish</note>. This placement destroys honor and peace of mind. However, she faces no loss in her activities or the work she performs, and she remains endowed with wealth.
