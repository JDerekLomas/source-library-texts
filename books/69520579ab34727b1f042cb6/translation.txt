...of GIORDANO BRUNO <note>The text continues from the previous page, completing the author’s name in the genitive case, "IORDANI BRVNI."</note> genera, many of the lowest <term>species<note>In the logic of this period, "species" refers to specific sub-categories within a broader "genus."</note></term> have been enumerated for convenience. Let those be multiplied into three within their special <term>subjects<note>A "subject" in Bruno’s art of memory is a mental location or "seat" where an image is placed.</note></term>. Let these, in turn, be derived equally within their own subjects.

   Fourthly, under these and those species of the three terms, you will have individuals pertaining to each single species; you will situate these and those equally within individual subjects.

   Therefore, let the most general form follow the most general subject: the general, the general; the specific, the specific; and the individual, the individual.

   Thus, one subject includes and contains another, and one form includes and contains another. By this method, you will achieve an easy memory not only for the *Incantation of Circe* <note>original: "cantus Circæi"; referring to Bruno's system of using mythological transformations as memory triggers.</note>, but for all things that are presented to you to be remembered.

**ALB.** I shall try it.

->THE END.<-
