# ROYAL SOCIETY.
### IN WHICH

I. The present deficiency of Natural Philosophy <note>The study of nature and the physical universe; the precursor to modern science.</note> is discussed, along with methods for making it more certain and beneficial.
II. The nature, motion, and effects of LIGHT are treated, particularly that of the *Sun* and *Comets*.
III. A hypothetical explanation of MEMORY; how the organs used by the mind in its operations may be mechanically understood.
IV. A hypothesis and explanation of the cause of GRAVITY, or GRAVITATION, MAGNETISM, etc.
V. Discourses on EARTHQUAKES, their causes and *effects*, and histories of several; to which are annexed *physical explanations* of several of the fables in *Ovid's Metamorphoses*, very different from other mythological interpreters.
VI. Lectures for improving NAVIGATION and ASTRONOMY, with descriptions of several new and useful *instruments* and *inventions*; the whole full of curious inquiries and experiments.

## ROYAL SOCIETY.

->IN WHICH<-

I. The present deficiency of **NATURAL PHILOSOPHY** <note>original: "Natural Philosophy"; the historical term for the study of nature and the physical universe, now called science.</note> is discussed, along with methods for making it more certain and beneficial.
II. The nature, motion, and effects of **LIGHT** are treated, particularly that of the sun and comets.
III. A hypothetical explanation of **MEMORY**; how the organs used by the mind in its operations may be understood mechanically.
IV. A hypothesis and explanation of the cause of **GRAVITY**, or **GRAVITATION**, **MAGNETISM**, etc.
V. Discourses on **EARTHQUAKES**, their causes and effects, and histories of several; to which are added explanations of several of the least known and most difficult parts of Scripture, very different from other mythological interpretations.
VI. Lectures for improving **NAVIGATION** and **ASTRONOMY**, with descriptions of several new and useful instruments and inventions; the whole work is full of curious inquiries and experiments.
