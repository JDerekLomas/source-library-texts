<meta>Traditional Chinese cosmological diagram titled "五辰之圖" (Diagram of the Five Chronos/Periods). The text details the calendrical division of the year (365.25 days) among the Five Elements (Wu Xing). Each of the four outer quadrants (representing Wood, Fire, Metal, and Water) is assigned 91 days and 25/80 parts (91.3125 days). The central element, Earth (土), is described as drawing from these seasons to create five equal periods of 73 days and 5 parts each.</meta>
