The seven articles concerning the Humanity <note>Referring to the human life and nature of Jesus Christ.</note> are well-known and recited daily: the first, conceived by the Holy Spirit; the second, born of the Virgin Mary; the third, suffered, was crucified, died, and was buried; the fourth, descended into hell (but according to his soul); the fifth, rose again; the sixth, ascended and sits [at the right hand of the Father]; the seventh, to judge the living and the dead.

It is not necessary for any simple member of the faithful to know how to distinguish, recognize, or affirm these twelve articles in such a way.
