# PREFACE

---

The *Theosophical Glossary* suffers from the disadvantage of being an almost entirely posthumous work <note>published after the death of the author</note>, of which the author only saw the first thirty-two pages in proof. This is all the more regrettable because H.P.B. <note>Helena Petrovna Blavatsky, 1831–1891, co-founder of the Theosophical Society</note>, as was her habit, was adding considerably to her original manuscript. She would no doubt have increased the volume far beyond its present limits, and so would have shed light on many obscure terms that are not included in the present Glossary. More importantly, she would have provided us with a sketch of the lives and teachings of the most famous Adepts <note>highly advanced practitioners or masters of esoteric wisdom</note> of the East and West.

The *Theosophical Glossary* intends to provide information on the principal Sanskrit, Pahlavi, Tibetan, Pali, Chaldean, Persian, Scandinavian, Hebrew, Greek, Latin, Kabbalistic, and Gnostic words, as well as Occult terms generally used in Theosophical literature. These are primarily found in *Isis Unveiled*, *Esoteric Buddhism*, *The Secret Doctrine*, *The Key to Theosophy*, and so on; as well as in the monthly magazines *The Theosophist*, *Lucifer*, and *The Path*, and other publications of the Theosophical Society. The articles marked [w.w.w.], which explain words found in the *Kabbalah* or which illustrate Rosicrucian or Hermetic doctrines, were contributed at the special request of H.P.B. by Brother W. W. Westcott, M.B., P.M., and P.Z., who is the Secretary General of the Rosicrucian Society and the Instructor <note>original: "Præmonstrator"</note> of the Kabbalah to the Hermetic Order of the G.D. <note>The Golden Dawn, a secret society devoted to the study and practice of the occult, metaphysics, and paranormal activities</note>.

H.P.B. also desired to express her special gratitude—regarding the organization of facts—to the *Sanskrit-Chinese Dictionary* by Eitel, *The Hindu Classical Dictionary* by Dowson, *The Vishnu Purana* by Wilson, and the *Royal Masonic Cyclopedia* by Kenneth Mackenzie.

As the undersigned can make no claim to the elaborate and extraordinary scholarship required for editing the diverse and multilingual contents of H.P.B.’s last contribution to Theosophical literature, there will necessarily be mistakes in transliteration and other details which specialists in scholarship will immediately notice. Meanwhile, however, as nearly every Orientalist has his own system, varying transliterations may be excused in the present work and should not be blamed entirely on the "Karma" <note>the spiritual principle of cause and effect</note> of the editor.

->G. R. S. MEAD.<-

London, *January*, 1892.

# THEOSOPHICAL GLOSSARY

## A.

**A.**—The first letter in all the world-alphabets except for a few, such as the Mongolian, Japanese, Tibetan, and Ethiopian, among others. It is a letter of great mystic power and “magic virtue” to those who have adopted it, and for whom its numerical value is *one*. It is the *Aleph* of the Hebrews, symbolized by the Ox or Bull; the *Alpha* of the Greeks, the one and the first; and the *Az* of the Slavonians, signifying the pronoun “I” (referring to the divine concept “I am that I am”). Even in Astrology, Taurus (the Ox, Bull, or *Aleph*) is the first of the Zodiacal signs <note>In some ancient systems, the zodiac began with Taurus rather than Aries</note>, its color being white and yellow. The sacred *Aleph* acquires an even more marked sanctity with the Christian Kabbalists <note>Mystics who applied Jewish Kabbalistic principles to Christian theology</note> when they learn that this letter typifies the Trinity in Unity, as it is composed of two *Yods* <note>The smallest letter of the Hebrew alphabet</note>, one upright and the other reversed, joined by a slanting bar or connection, depicted as—א. Kenneth R. H. Mackenzie states that “the St. Andrew’s cross is occultly connected to it.” The divine name, the first in the series corresponding with *Aleph*, is *AêHêIêH* or *Ahih* when written without vowels, and this is a Sanskrit root.

**Aahla** (*Egyptian*). One of the divisions of the *Kerneter* <note>The dwelling place of the gods or the underworld</note> or infernal regions, also known as *Amenti*; the word means the “Field of Peace.”

**Aanroo** (*Egyptian*). The second division of *Amenti*. The celestial field of Aanroo is encircled by an iron wall. The field is covered with wheat, and the “Defunct” <note>The deceased soul</note> are represented gleaning it for the “Master of Eternity.” Some stalks are three, others five, and the highest are seven cubits high. Those who reached the last two numbers entered the state of bliss (which is called in Theosophy *Devachan* <note>A peaceful, heavenly state between incarnations</note>); the disembodied spirits whose harvest was only three cubits high went into lower regions (*Kâmaloka* <note>A semi-material plane where desires and passions are purged; similar to purgatory</note>). To the Egyptians, wheat was the symbol of the *Law of Retribution* or *Karma*. The cubits referred to the seven, five, and three human “principles” <note>The different layers of the human constitution, such as body, soul, and spirit</note>.

**Ab** (*Hebrew*). The eleventh month of the Hebrew civil year; the fifth of the sacred year beginning in July.

**Abaddon** (*Hebrew*). An angel of hell, corresponding to the Greek Apollyon <note>meaning "The Destroyer"</note>.

**Abatur** (*Gnostic*). In the Nazarene system, the "Ancient of Days"—the *High Ancient* <note>original Latin: Antiquus Altus</note> and the Father of the *Demiurge* <note>term for the artisan or creator of the physical world</note> of the Universe—is called the *Third Life* or "Abatur." He corresponds to the Third "Logos" <note>the "Word" or manifested deity</note> in the *Secret Doctrine*. (See *Codex Nazaraeus* <note>The sacred scripture of the Mandaeans</note>).

**Abba Amona** (*Hebrew*). Literally, "Father-Mother"; the occult names of the two higher *Sephiroth* <note>emanations of God in the Kabbalah</note>, *Chokmah* <note>Wisdom</note> and *Binah* <note>Understanding</note>, of the upper triad, the apex of which is *Sephira* or *Kether* <note>The Crown</note>. From this triad issues the lower seven levels of the Sephirothal Tree.

**Abhamsi** (*Sanskrit*). A mystic name of the "four orders of beings," which are: Gods, Demons, *Pitris* <note>ancestral spirits</note>, and Men. Orientalists somehow connect the name with "waters," but esoteric philosophy connects its symbolism with *Akasa*—the ethereal "waters of space." This is because the "four orders of (lower) beings" and the three higher Orders of Spiritual Beings are born upon the bosom and the seven planes of "space." (See *Secret Doctrine* Vol. I, p. 458, and "Ambhamsi.")

**Abhasvaras** (*Sanskrit*). The *Devas* <note>shining beings or deities</note> or "Gods" of Light and Sound; the highest of the upper three celestial regions (planes) of the second *Dhyana* <note>state of profound meditation</note>. They are a class of gods sixty-four in number, representing a certain cycle and an occult number.

**Abhava** (*Sanskrit*). Negation, or the non-being of individual objects; the *noumenal* <note>the thing-in-itself, as opposed to how it appears to the senses</note> substance, or abstract objectivity.

**Abhaya** (*Sanskrit*). "Fearlessness"—a son of *Dharma* <note>Duty or Law</note>; and also a religious life of duty. As an adjective meaning "Fearless," Abhaya is a title given to every Buddha.

**Abhayagiri** (*Sanskrit*). Literally, "Mount Fearless" in Ceylon <note>modern-day Sri Lanka</note>. It has an ancient *Vihara* or Monastery in which the well-known Chinese traveler Fa-hien found 5,000 Buddhist priests and ascetics in the year 400 of our era. It also housed a School called *Abhayagiri Vasinah*, the "School of the Secret Forest." This philosophical school was regarded as heretical because the ascetics studied the doctrines of both the "greater" and the "smaller" vehicles—the *Mahayana* and the *Hinayana* systems—as well as *Triyana*, or the three successive degrees of Yoga. This is much like how a certain Brotherhood functions now beyond the Himalayas. This was the most mystical of all the schools and was renowned for the number of *Arhats* <note>enlightened saints</note> it produced.
