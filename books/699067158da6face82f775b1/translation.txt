If these are seen in the middle of the day, rain will follow. If the entrance of the month of *Chaitra* <note>the first month of the Hindu lunar calendar</note> is void, and if portents are seen during this season, the people will attain prosperity. On the first day of the month of *Pushya*, prices will be moderate. A girl who reaches menarche <margin>Woman</margin> under the star *Ashwini* will not enjoy the vow of having sons <note>implying potential difficulty in bearing male heirs or specific ritual restrictions</note>.

However, this star is excellent for auspicious activities such as: sexual union, the first feeding of solid food <term>Annaprashana</term>, naming ceremonies, the first haircut <term>Chaulopanyana</term>, beginning the study of Vedas and Sciences, oil baths, wearing new clothes and ornaments, royal coronations, riding vehicles like palanquins or chariots, architecture and construction, painting, agriculture, sowing seeds, planting coconut trees, starting journeys, ancestral rites <term>Shraddha</term>, and medical treatments.

### Qualities of one born under Ashwini:
Long-lived, stout body, long hands, delicate nose, large eyes, handsome appearance, beloved by all people, capable, intelligent, honored by kings, fond of ornaments, but also prone to the company of wicked people, adventurous, and of a restless mind. He will have a fish-shaped mark on his waist. These are the internal qualities.

*   **First Quarter (Pada):** Ruled by Mars. Part of the body: the head. He will possess earned wealth, be a seeker of pleasure, a warrior, a backbiter, and a thief who lusts after others' wives.
*   **Second Quarter:** Ruled by Venus. End of enjoyment. He will be wealthy, radiant, and see an increase in money and grain.
*   **Third Quarter:** Ruled by Mercury. Part of humility. He will possess earned wealth, be a seeker of pleasure, and victorious in battle.
*   **Fourth Quarter:** Ruled by the Moon. Part of the vision. He will be devoted to the worship of gods and always engaged in righteous deeds.

The major planetary period <term>Mahadasha</term> for this birth is Ketu, lasting seven years. The first two minutes of birth are considered a "knot-end" <term>Gandanta</term>, an inauspicious transition. This is the fault of the "unconsumed" <note>Abhukta Dosha, a specific astrological affliction</note>. In the first year of the native's life, there is fear of fire; in the third year, stomach ailments; in the fifth year, eye diseases; in the middle of the sixth year, loss of wealth. In the tenth or twentieth year, a great illness. In the twenty-first year, danger of untimely death. In the twenty-fourth year, fear of wild animals or thieves. In the eighty-fourth year, the disease of untimely death. If these are remedied, the maximum lifespan is ninety years.

# The Jewel of the Star-Crest
<note>original: "Nakshatra Chudamani" — a classical Sanskrit title for a treatise on stellar astrology</note>

Born under this sign. The syllable is "Ta." After twenty-four *ghatikas* <note>a unit of time equal to 24 minutes</note> the "poisonous moments" <term>Visha Nadis: specific intervals during a star's duration considered inauspicious</term> begin. Travel should be avoided. If one eats rice after the first seven *ghatikas*, travel toward the East or North will be auspicious. If a fever begins now, there is a danger to life within seven days. Alternatively, twenty-one days are specified as critical. 

### Remedial Rites
To pacify these effects <term>Shanti: a propitiatory rite to avert evil</term>: One should invoke an image of Yama <note>the God of Death, who presides over the star Bharani</note> who is dark-complexioned, two-armed, holding a noose and a staff, and riding a buffalo. Offer blue garments, black agarwood paste, and blue water-lilies. Use bdellium incense and an offering of rice cooked with jaggery. Perform the sixteen-fold worship <term>Shodashopachara: the standard 16 steps of honoring a deity</term> with the mantra "To Yama, the Soma..." <note>original: "Yamaya Somam..." from the Rig Veda</note>. After performing one hundred and eight oblations of honey and clarified butter while reciting the Gayatri mantra, one should place an offering of fragrance, garlands, and jaggery-rice in the Northern direction. After eleven days, the person will become happy.

### Planetary Combinations
Now, the results of the combinations of lunar days and weekdays:
*   **Sunday:** Fire combination <note>danger of fire</note>
*   **Monday:** Obstacle combination
*   **Tuesday:** "Staff of Death" combination <note>Yama Kantaka: a highly inauspicious time</note>
*   **Wednesday (on the 7th lunar day):** Destruction combination
*   **Thursday:** Cruel combination
*   **Friday:** Spear combination <note>Shula Yoga: sharp or piercing troubles</note>
*   **Saturday:** Death combination and "Lifeless" combination.

In the second quarter of this star, there is a "Poison" combination. If a cruel planet is situated in Magha <note>the 10th star</note>, it is the "Great Spear" combination. In the month of Phalguna <note>February/March</note>, this star is considered "void." If a meteor is seen falling in this star, it results in cold fevers and famine. If red clouds are seen at midday, it brings rain. During the Sun's transit, prices will be middling. 

### Social and Practical Effects
A girl who reaches menarche <note>original: "Rajasvala"</note> under the star Bharani will be poor and lose her husband. 

**Auspicious Activities:** This star is favorable for starting wars with enemies, administering poison, entering water, burying or collecting treasure, applying medicinal collyrium, entering caves, sorcery, deceptive acts, gambling, thievery, seizing property, giving "cruel" gifts, planting thorny trees, hiring servants, and going to Gaya <note>a holy city for ancestral rites</note>.

### Characteristics of the Bharani Native
One born under Bharani is:
Fickle, has many enemies, yet is happy, long-lived, truthful, fond of good food, a heavy sleeper, has reddish eyes, a broad forehead, curly hair, and a body built for enjoyment. The navel area is prominent and well-defined.

### Quarter-Specific Traits
*   **First Quarter (Sun):** Royal portion. Respected by kings, endowed with all good qualities.
*   **Second Quarter (Mercury):** Eunuch portion. Greedy for wealth, impotent.
*   **Third Quarter (Venus):** "Fearless" portion. Restful soul, very enthusiastic, and brave.
*   **Fourth Quarter (Mars):** Sinful portion. Ungrateful, envious, cruel-natured, experiencing distress, but having many sons. 

### Life Path and Longevity
The major planetary period <term>Mahadasha: a long period of influence in a person's life</term> for this birth is Venus, lasting twenty years. 
*   **3rd day from birth:** Danger from fire at midday.
*   **5th year:** Danger from wind-related fever.
*   **30th year:** Loss of wealth, danger from four-legged animals, and danger from weapons.
*   **40th year:** A "near-death" crisis <term>Apamrityu: an untimely or accidental death that can be averted through ritual</term>. 

If this crisis is overcome, the maximum lifespan is eighty-five years.

## Detailed Description of the Qualities of the Krithika Mansion <note>Krithika is the third lunar mansion, associated with the Pleiades star cluster and the element of fire.</note>

Conception, wearing new clothes, initiation into the first rites, worship of the sacred fire, wearing ornaments prescribed by the Shaiva traditions and others, the preparation of mantras, medicines, elixirs, and alchemical tonics—all these should be performed. The digging of wells and works involving fire are also favored in this mansion of the Fire-God.

**Attributes of Krithika:**
The syllable is "E" <note>original: "Ekara-aksharah"</note>. The color is red. The shape is like a razor. The deity is the Lord of Fire. The location is the void. The element is the Sphere of Fire. The sacred tree is the Cluster Fig <note>original: "Audumbara"</note>. The bird is the western peacock. The gender is male. The temperament is of the Demon class <note>original: "Rakshasa Gana"</note>. The animal type is the goat. The caste is Brahmin. The gaze is downward-facing. The eyes are beautiful. The duration of the poison-period <note>term: vishanadi - an inauspicious time-segment within a star's duration</note> is thirty ghatikas <note>term: ghatika - a traditional unit of time equal to 24 minutes</note>.

The Lord of Fire was born in this mansion. Travel should be avoided. However, if one eats curd-rice after twenty-eight ghatikas, travel toward the North-East is auspicious.

If a fever begins during this time, there is a fear of death; or otherwise, there will be great suffering for nine days. To pacify this, one should invoke an image of the Fire-God <note>Agni</note>, who is red-colored, four-armed, holding the gesture of granting boons, and riding a ram. One should perform the sixteen-fold worship <note>Shodashopachara Puja</note> with red cloth, red sandalwood paste, oleander flowers, incense of ghee and guggul resin, and an offering of ghee-soaked rice, using the mantra: "May the Krithikas and the Fire-God protect us."

By performing a fire sacrifice <note>Homa</note> one hundred and eight times with ghee and honey while reciting the Gayatri mantra, and by offering a sacrifice of sandalwood, garlands, and curd-rice in the South-East direction, one becomes healthy after nine days.

**Results of the combinations of Lunar Days and Weekdays:**
*   **Sunday:** The Yoga of the Void <note>Randhra Yoga - generally inauspicious</note>

*   **Thursday:** The Yoga of the Spear
*   **Friday:** The Auspicious Yoga
*   **Saturday:** The Yoga of Friendship

On the seventh lunar day <note>Saptami</note>, it results in the Yoga of Death. If cruel planets are situated in Shravana, it results in the Yoga of the Hundred-Headed Spear. The month of Kartika is considered "void" or empty. If omens of calamity appear during this time, there will be fear of famine and fire.

When the Sun enters this mansion, prices will be moderate. A girl who reaches puberty for the first time under the Krithika mansion will be poor. However, if it occurs in the first quarter <note>Pada</note>, it is auspicious. In the second quarter, it becomes auspicious only after feeding twenty-five Brahmins. In the latter half, one must perform pacification rituals <note>Shanti</note>.

Shaving should be avoided. This mansion is excellent for "cruel" or sharp activities: the use of poisons, alchemy, working with gold, wood, or metal, seizing the property of enemies, clearing debts, gathering grain, selling cattle, collecting collyrium (eye-salve), and planting thorny trees.

**Qualities of a person born under Krithika:**
Radiant, intelligent, marked with a fish-sign on the throat <note>original: "matsyankitah"</note>, but prone to sinful thoughts.
*   **First Quarter (Jupiter/Wealth division):** Skillful, majestic, and wealthy.
*   **Second Quarter (Saturn/Sinful division):** Inclined toward sinful deeds, frequents the houses of prostitutes.
*   **Third Quarter (Saturn/Fierce division):** Dull-witted, of bad character, and speaks with wicked friends.
*   **Fourth Quarter (Jupiter/Excellent division):** Endowed with learning and humility...

->The Five-Lath Mutual Obstructions<-
<note>Pancha-Shalaka Vedha: A method of checking astrological compatibility or obstacles by drawing five parallel and five perpendicular lines.</note>

Krittika and Vishakha; Rohini and Abhijit; Mrigashira and Uttarashadha; Arudra and Purvashadha; Punarvasu and Mula; Pushyami and Jyeshtha; Ashlesha and Dhanishta; Magha and Shravanam; Pubba and Ashwini; Uttara and Revati; Hasta and Uttarabhadra; Chitra and Purvabhadra; Swati and Shatabhishak; Anuradha and Bharani—these are mutual obstructions <term>Vedhas</term> to one another.
