# Binding Description

The volume is bound in a traditional style featuring a brown leather spine and corners. The front and back boards are covered in a tan-colored <term>marbled paper<note>A decorative paper created by floating pigments on a liquid surface and then transferring the pattern to paper.</note></term>, displaying a dense, dark-brown speckled pattern. 

The spine is constructed with five <term>raised bands<note>Horizontal ridges on the spine that indicate where the internal cords were sewn to hold the pages together.</note></term>, which divide the spine into several decorative compartments. In the second compartment from the top, there are visible remnants of a <term>gold-tooled<note>A decorative technique where gold leaf is pressed into the leather using heated brass tools.</note></term> title label. However, the gilding and leather in this area are significantly worn, making the original title illegible. 

The physical condition shows notable signs of age:
*   The top and bottom right corners of the boards are heavily worn.
*   The marbled paper has peeled away in these areas, exposing the underlying <term>pasteboard<note>A sturdy material made by gluing multiple sheets of paper together, used as the core of the book cover.</note></term>, which has a blue-grey color.
*   The text on the spine is severely faded and cannot be read.
