# Preface to this First Part.

->Beloved Reader in GOD and His eternal Truth!<-

It is now a quite general saying and lament among all people that the times and also the people nowadays are very evil, which in truth is indeed the case; and if I am to reveal my thoughts on such matters quite sincerely <note>original: "ungeheuchelt"; meaning without hypocrisy or pretense. Schütz is signaling the Radical Pietist value of "heart-honesty."</note>, I do not believe that a worse and more wicked generation of men has ever lived in the world than this present generation. And whoever has received even a little enlightenment from GOD, and has learned what a righteous character is like in Christ <note>original: "Christo"; the author uses the Latin dative form of Christ's name.</note>, will very gladly believe me when I say that we are living right now in the dreadful times which the Holy Spirit prophesied and pointed to long ago—especially through his chosen vessel <note>original: "Rüstzeuge"; literally "tool" or "equipment," a common biblical metaphor (Acts 9:15) for a person used by God.</note>, Paul <note>original: "Paulum"; the Latin accusative form of Paul.</note>—stating that they would come in the last days. This is because the distressing evidence, alas! testifies more than enough to everyone; namely, that all cities and lands are quite full of such people as the aforementioned dear Apostle described in his second <note>Schütz is referring here to the "Second" Epistle to Timothy, specifically chapter 3, which describes the perils of the last days.</note>
