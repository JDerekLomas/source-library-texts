# MAGNETISM

## ANIMAL.

<term>Animal Magnetism: A theory popularized by the German physician Franz Mesmer in the 1770s. It posited that an invisible "universal fluid" flowed through the human body and that illnesses occurred when this flow was blocked. Practitioners believed they could heal patients by using their hands to redirect this magnetic energy.</term>
