23. *Dryandra cordata <note>Now known as Vernicia fordii, the Tung oil tree</note> Thunberg, Flora Japonica page 267.*

    *Dryandra oleifera. Lamarck, Encyclopedie Methodique volume 2, page 329.*

    Observation: Lamarck, based on specimens from Commerson, describes the flower stalks in the female plants as simple and single-flowered, whereas they are shown as composite in *Kaempfer's* illustration. Is the tree cultivated in the Royal Garden of the Isle of Mauritius <note>Modern-day Mauritius</note> therefore different from the Japanese one?

24. *Taxus macrophylla <note>The Yew-plum pine</note> Thunberg, Flora Japonica 276.*

25. *Dolichos ensiformis <note>The Sword bean</note> Thunberg, Flora Japonica 279.* but it is different from the *Dolichos ensiformis* of *Linnaeus*, which he described in the *Hortus Cliffortianus* <note>A famous 1737 work by Linnaeus describing George Clifford's garden</note> as having a pod a foot long. It more closely approaches *Dolichos Lablab <note>The Hyacinth bean</note> of Linnaeus.*

    The name used by *Kaempfer*, assigned to this figure, *Reedsu*, is cited by Thunberg under his *Dolichos lineatum*.

    Thunberg describes a tree among his "obscure plants" on page 354, adding this name from *Kaempfer*; however, the description does not correspond to this figure.

38. *Ssi no ki <note>Japanese: Shiinoki; the Japanese Chinquapin</note> Kaempfer, Amoenitates 816.*

    This name from *Kaempfer* is cited by Thunberg under *Quercus cuspidata*.

39. *Natta mame <note>Japanese: Nata-mame; the Sword bean</note> Kaempfer, Amoenitates 836.*

    It is referred by Thunberg to his *Dolichos incurvus*, but the clustered flowers stand in the way of this identification; it is more likely *Dolichos gladiatus <note>Jacquin, Collectanea volume 2, page 276, illustration volume 2</note>.*

40. *Atsuki <note>Japanese: Azuki; the Azuki bean</note> Kaempfer, Amoenitates 837.*

41. *Kádsune <note>Japanese: Kudzu</note> Kaempfer, Amoenitates 840.*

42. *Kobus <note>The Kobus Magnolia</note> Kaempfer, Amoenitates 845.*

43. *Mokkwurèn <note>Japanese: Mokuren; the Lily Magnolia</note> Kaempfer, Amoenitates 845.*

    Thunberg assigns both of *Kaempfer's* names to *Magnolia glauca*. They are certainly different from the American *Magnolia glauca*.

44. *Mokkwurèn* with a white flower *Kaempfer, Amoenitates 845.*

45. *Korèì Utsúgi <note>Japanese: Korei-utsugi</note> Kaempfer, Amoenitates 855.*

    Entirely different from *Weigela japonica*, to which Thunberg adds this synonym.
