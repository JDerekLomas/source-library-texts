However, because David's organs had only ten strings, and the *nablum* <term>nablum: an ancient Hebrew stringed instrument, often equated with a psaltery or harp</term> or cymbals had twelve strings and were plucked with the fingers or struck with hammers (as Polydore [Vergil] testifies in book 1, chapter 15), the organs of this present time are far different from the ancient ones and cannot be compared to them, as the forms of both types clearly demonstrate.
