<note>Half-binding became a standard practice in the late 18th and 19th centuries. <term>Half-binding: A binding style where the spine and corners are covered in one material (usually leather) and the sides are covered in another (usually paper or cloth).</term>

<term>Raised bands: The horizontal ridges across the spine of a book. Traditionally, these were formed by the thick cords used to sew the sections of the book together, though in later periods, they were sometimes added purely for aesthetic reasons.</term>
