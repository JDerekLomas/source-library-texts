*<term>Alembicus</term> or <term>capitellum</term>, a **helmet** <note>original: "ein Helm"</note>; which is the vessel placed upon the lower vessels to receive vapors. One type of alembic is <term>rostrate</term> <note>beaked or spouted</note>, and another is <term>blind</term> <note>without a spout</note>. The beaked version is that which sends the dissolved humors through a channel or "beak" into a receiving vessel. The other is blind—that is, without a beak—and is used for sublimations <note>the process of heating a solid until it turns into vapor and then solidifies again on a cool surface</note>. In sublimations, this blind alembic is sometimes slightly perforated at the very top to allow the rising humors to escape.*
