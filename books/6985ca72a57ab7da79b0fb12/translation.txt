# JEAN RIVIÈRE OF BORDEAUX
### Controller of the Royal Treasury, Upon the Trismegistus 
### of Lord François de Foix de Candale.

->*<-

Does the Thrice-Greatest Hermes <note>original: "ter Maximus Hermes," referring to Hermes Trismegistus</note> breathe and live even now,
Sacred in religion, distinguished in learning and art,
The just governor and arbiter of the Nile's shores?
He does, while you live, O Prince—the greatest glory of our age—
Descendant of Foix? Whether you trace this lineage from the first
Origin of heroes, whose illustrious part as Kings
Restrained conquered nations with both law and strength;
Or whether you look upon the goods which prosperous Fortune pours
From her generous horn, mingled with eternal blessings and the body:
Nothing is lacking to you; three "greatest" things remain joined in you.
To these, join the new arts in which you shine so brightly,
And a character worthy of so great a high priest of sacred things.
Pious Religion accompanies these and goes before them, like
A glowing flame; while with the eyes of a Lynx, 
You traverse the heavenly paths and celestial movements. 
But whoever else—ministers of sacred things—attempted to handle
Secrets previously granted to no one to be moved, 
They were all struck dumb; and like Argus of the hundred eyes, 
They crawled into trackless places like blind moles. 
You are the new interpreter of the gods: you are the Thrice-Greatest Hermes 
(If only the teachings of a reborn Pythagoras are true).
Mercury was submerged in the dark waves of the Phlegethon <note>In mythology, a river of fire in the underworld.</note>, 
Leaving behind the Egyptian rites of his ancestral Canopus; 
By your effort, you send him back, recalled into the upper air, 
And restore him, shining and without covering, to the clear sky. 
And you are a God to him, and a God snatches you from swift Death <note>original: "Orco," referring to Orcus, the underworld.</note>, 
Scattering your bright name upon the earth and fixing it in Heaven, 
As monuments of your genius. While you are safe, never 
Shall the Aquitanian Muse <note>The "Aquitanian Muse" refers to the poetry and scholarship of the Bordeaux region.</note> yield to the Roman Muses. 
Whether you join French to Latin, or exchange Greek 
For Latin: the page shines revived in its true color, 
Which imitates and increases its very first origins.

---

### EXTRACT FROM THE PRIVILEGE.

The King, by letters patent, has permitted Simon Millanges, printer of Bordeaux, to print, or cause to be printed and offered for sale, a book titled *The Pimander of Hermes Trismegistus, restored in both languages by the industry of Lord François de Foix de Candale.* And prohibitions and warnings are made to all printers and others not to print it, or cause it to be printed, sold, or distributed, without the consent of the said Millanges, during the time and term of six years, upon pain of confiscation of the books and an arbitrary fine, as is more fully contained in the privilege given for this purpose at Paris, the fifth day of August, 1574.

->By the King, [space] Signed DE PUYBERAL.<-
