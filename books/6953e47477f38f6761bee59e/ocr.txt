At the very top of the frame, a small circular crest supported by two cherubs contains the text: **MEDICEA SIDERA**. Directly beneath this is a sunburst design with concentric rings inscribed with: **SOBOLES MAGNVM IOVIS INCREM.** and **CLARA DEVM NATVM**.
