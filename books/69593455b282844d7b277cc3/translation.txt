## The First Letter.
### To Mr. A. M.

->Amsterdam, in the year 1696.<-

->Jesus Immanuel! <note>original: "JEsus JmmanuEl!" — Hebrew for "God with us," a common Pietist greeting.</note><-

1. Much beloved Mr. M., worthy friend. Concerning the <term>inward hidden man</term> <note>A concept from 1 Peter 3:4 referring to the spiritual soul as opposed to the physical body.</note>, it is indeed an unspeakably great grace of God when such a being is alive within us, and desires and longs for Paradise as a place of rest from all earthly craving and desire.

2. Which, in our evil time and dark night, is almost a rare thing; therefore, it should rightly be observed that we—with prayer, sighing, and supplication—must always blow upon such an inward <term>soul-fire</term> <note>A mystical term for the spark of divine life or zeal within the heart.</note> as a gift of God, and indeed never let it be extinguished.

3. I have also observed this myself, and have avoided every hindrance and <term>diabolical dampness</term> <note>original: "teufelische Nässe" — likely referring to spiritual lethargy or worldly influences that "dampen" the fire of faith.</note> which reveals itself in flesh and blood, partly from without and partly from within; and I have constantly kept and exercised myself in the love of God, because I knew that without love, it is impossible to break through the <term>Cherub's sword</term> <note>A reference to the flaming sword of the Cherubim guarding the entrance to Eden in Genesis 3:24.</note>.

4. For this reason, I can recommend nothing higher than the love of God, which alone can extinguish and temper the <term>wrath-fire</term> <note>original: "Zorn-Feuer" — the divine judgment or the heat of human passions.</note> of God in body and soul.

# Concerning the Holy Faith etc. (2nd Letter)

12. In the year 1664 at R. <note>likely Regensburg, where the Imperial Diet met</note>, with the consent of all Lutheran professors, seniors, and superintendents <term>Superintendent: a high-ranking Lutheran cleric overseeing a district</term>, I made a proposal to the ambassadors on how to assist our crumbling Christianity. In connection with this, I also presented a capital sum of 30,000 Thalers <note>a significant fortune in the 17th century</note> held in banks at Nuremberg and Frankfurt. From the interest of this fund, Christian people who wished to sacrifice themselves for this work could be paid a salary. All the politicians unanimously consented to this and encouraged me.

13. However, when it was time to put the plan into action, the theologians withdrew and pulled their heads out of the noose, because they realized that the reformation would have to begin with them first.

14. Since then, I have turned to GOD and tirelessly pleaded for the outpouring of His Holy Spirit, that He might open the eyes of the laity <note>original: "Laicis," referring to non-clergy members of the church</note> and reveal to them the blindness of their own teachers. I also have a childlike confidence in Him, who is Love itself, that He will bless and carry forward the work of our hands.

15. The word "Faith" has been stretched <note>original: "extendiret"</note> so far that it is now a non-entity <note>original: "Non-Ens," a philosophical term for something that does not exist</note>. The bond of unity has been completely torn apart by crucifixions <note>likely referring to sectarian infighting</note>, so that people hate one another for the sake of a mere opinion.

# The III. Letter.
## To the Same.

->Amsterdam, Year 1696.<-

1. I have recently received **both** of your letters, but have not yet seen your dear **son**, and shall await his **arrival** and **visit**. **In the meantime**, I pray that **God** may be his **Father**, clothe him in **Jesus**, and graciously impart His **Holy Spirit** as the true **Teacher** into his **heart** and **mind**, so that being thus inwardly anointed, he may outwardly preach **God’s Word, Jesus**, according to the **letter**! **Amen.**

2. My **copies** regarding **Böhme’s figures** have been delivered to **Monsieur N.**, who intends to send them along with others to **Your Lordship** at the first opportunity, so that you may do with them as with your own **property**, however you please; they can be of no use to me anyway. **Mr. N.’s prophecies** originate from the fiery **constellation** <note>A reference to astrological or "astral" inspiration, which the author views skeptically</note> and do not come to pass; they also have their determined **judgment** from the **Lord** in **Deuteronomy 18** <note>A biblical reference to the test of a true prophet: if the word does not come to pass, the prophet has spoken presumptuously</note>.
