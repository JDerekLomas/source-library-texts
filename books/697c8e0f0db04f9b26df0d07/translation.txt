# G O D

## is the purest Love.

<note>The title "God is the purest Love" (original: "God is de reinste Liefde") was famously associated with a prayer book by Karl von Eckartshausen, which was widely translated and used across Europe during the Catholic Enlightenment. It reflects a shift toward a more emotional and personal relationship with the divine.</note>
