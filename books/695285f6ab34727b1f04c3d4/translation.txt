# ON THE SEVEN SECONDARY SPIRITS, BOOK II.

From 1508, there remain 17 years until the end of the government of Samael <note>The planetary spirit of Mars, whose reign Trithemius characterizes by war and upheaval</note>. In these years, figures will be given signifying the beginning of evils. <margin>The beginning of evils.</margin> For in the Christian year 1525, the crosses seen on people's clothing ten years in the past will show their effect <note>original: "cruces in vestimentis"; this refers to a famous omen from 1501–1503 where cross-shaped marks supposedly appeared on garments across Germany, interpreted by Trithemius as a warning of the coming Reformation or social unrest</note>. But 13 years from now, summoned by right, you will give place to one who does not know; after your death you shall rise greater for me in the third [book] unless you hide behind a cloud <note>This cryptic passage appears to be a personal address to the spirit of the age or perhaps a reference to a planned third volume of the work</note>.

**I**n the twentieth order, Gabriel, the angel of the Moon, will receive the rule of the world in the year of the world 6732, in the 4th month, on the 4th day of June, in the year of Christ 1525. He will govern the world for 354 years and 4 months, until the year of the world 7086 and the 8th month; which corresponds to the year of our Lord’s birth 1879 and 11 months. The sequence of this future revolution requires a prophecy <note>Gabriel is the final angel in Trithemius’s cycle of seven; his reign (1525–1879) is associated with the Moon, representing growth, moisture, and perhaps the final gathering before a new cycle or the end of days</note>.

I have not written these things as absolute assertions, most sacred Emperor <term>Cæsar</term> <note>Referring to Maximilian I</note>, for they are by no means to be believed if they cause injury to the Orthodox <note>meaning the "correct" or established Catholic</note> faith. There are indeed those who have calculated lunar months in these matters; if you consider that they should be agreed with, then the things I have written otherwise must be changed. I also protest with my own hand and confess with my mouth that in all these things I believe nothing and admit nothing except what the Catholic Church believes; the rest I reject and despise as vain, fabricated, and superstitious.

->Praise to God Almighty.<-
