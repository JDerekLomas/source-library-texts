->A.<-

**A**barbenel. Look for Isaac Abarbenet.

אבקת רוכל *Afkáth róchel* <note>original: "Afkáth róchel"; likely *Avkat Rochel* (The Merchant’s Powder), a work on eschatology and the afterlife.</note>, was written by Rabbi Makkir and printed in Augsburg in quarto format in the year 300 according to the Jews' small count / that is / in the year of Christ 1540. It deals with the pains to be endured at the time of the Messiah / as well as death / hell / eternal life / and the resurrection. <note>The "small count" refers to the Hebrew calendar year omitting the thousands digit; for example, the year 5300 is written as 300.</note>

*Ahabbáth ólam* <note>The Love of the World</note> look for *Sepher Ahabbáth ólam*.

עקדת יצחק *Akedáth Jizchak* <note>The Binding of Isaac</note>, was written by R. Isaac Arâma and printed in folio in Venice / in the year 307 i.e. 1547. It is a commentary on the 5 Books of Moses.

Old *Nizzáchon*, look for *Sepher Nizzáchon*, etc.

עמודי גולה *Ammúde gólah* <note>Pillars of the Exile</note>, was written by R. Isaac, the son of R. Joseph of Corbeil / and printed in Cremona in quarto in the year 317, which is / 1557. It deals with the 613 commandments of the Jews.

עמודה שבעה *Ammudeha schibba* or *schifa* <note>Its Seven Pillars</note>, was written by R. Bezáleel and printed in Dyhernfurth in quarto in the year 453 i.e. 1693. It is a figurative interpretation of many verses of the Old Testament.

ארבע טורים *Arba turim* <note>The Four Rows</note> was written by R. Jacob bar Asher and printed in Krakow in folio in the year 375 i.e. 1615. It deals with Jewish customs and laws.

ערוך של רבי נתן *Aruch* or Lexicon of R. Nathan / was printed in Venice in folio in the year 295, which is / 1535.

עשרה מאמרות *Afaráh maamaróth* <note>Ten Utterances</note>, was written by R. Asárja Mippâno <note>Azariah da Fano</note> and printed in quarto with the commentary of Joel Moshe in the year 304, which is / 1544. These are Kabbalistic matters.

עבודת הקודש *Avodáth hakkodesch* <note>Service of the Holy</note>, was written by R. Meir ben Gábbai and printed in Krakow in folio in the year 337 i.e. 1577. It deals with the unity of God / and His service / as well as the purpose of man / and his creation / and the secrets of the Law.

*Bammidbar rábba*. look for *Rabbóth*.
