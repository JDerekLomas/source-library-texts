# Plastering and Masonry Works

## Per square area of one Zhang <note>Approximately 100 square feet (10 feet by 10 feet).</note>

<gloss>For palaces, pavilions, and similar structures where there are corners requiring the use of trowels for specialized finishing, add 0.5 units of labor to the standard allotment for each task. For heights of 20 feet (2 zhang) or more, for every additional 10 feet (1 zhang), add 0.012 units of labor to each task, capping the addition at a total height of 40 feet (4 zhang); beyond this height, no further labor is added.
