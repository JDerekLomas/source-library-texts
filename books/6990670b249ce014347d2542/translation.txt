For your senses being exercised in the knowledge of God’s truth, and your minds enlightened by the continual practice of the Church, you would easily have recognized the seal of divine truth <note>original: "stampe of the Sanctuarie"; a metaphor for the mark of official religious authority or biblical truth</note>. 

Your judicial determination would (no doubt) either have prevented or at least stayed much of the violent proceedings, many of the impious assertions, and the great distractions among the people. The common people, unable to judge the substance of these things, stand amazed at the empty sound and multitude of words. But as this happy outcome has been much desired in similar cases before, so could it never be less hoped for than now, when our sins have breathed new life into the dead carcass of the Antichrist <note>A common post-Reformation reference to the Papacy or the Roman Catholic Church</note>. They criticize my pamphlets as being full of **unsound and absurd positions**. But the Commission has neither awarded any such sentence, and these men have done a double, notable wrong in slandering both the Commission and us.
