...he learned in the Schwenckfeldian Dove-House <note>A derogatory term for the followers of Caspar Schwenckfeld, a mystic who emphasized the "Inner Word." The "dove" refers to the Holy Spirit.</note> the third age <note>original: "seculum"</note> of the Holy Spirit; truly, his foolish "doves" and empty arguments or reasons, which he displays to beautify his cause, demonstrate and prove this quite clearly and "heaven-bright."

> 1. He says: that which was prophesied regarding the restoration of the **Israelites**, and also the forgiveness of the sins of the people in **Romans 11:15** and **Isaiah 59:16, 17**, has not yet been fulfilled. Therefore <note>original: "Ergo"</note>, the *third golden age* <note>original: "tertium aureum seculum"</note> must yet follow.

Answer to this: That the restoration of the **Israelites** consists in the grace of God and not in a new world; just as the calling of the East **Indians** to the light of the Gospel, which occurs through the **Dutch**, does not bear witness to a new third or fourth world or age, but rather to the renewal of the grace of God which has been granted to them.

> 2. **God** the Father had a time, that being the **Old Testament**: **God** the Son also a time, meaning the **New Testament**: So it must necessarily follow that **God** the Holy Spirit also has a special new world or age, and this shall be the third, golden, and entirely pure one.

Answer: Where is it written that three distinct Persons in the Godhead should have three distinct worlds or ages? Nowhere.

> 3. In the second age <note>original: "seculo"</note>, that which was prophesied of the **Messiah’s time of peace** in **Isaiah 2:4**, **chapter 11:6**, and **Micah 4:3** has not been fulfilled; therefore the third age must still be at hand, in which all such things shall be completed.

Answer: The premise is entirely false. For that all these things were fulfilled is read in **Acts 2:44 & 4:32** <note>The text likely means 4:32 regarding the unity of believers, though it prints 4:22</note>; just as it is still fulfilled to this day in the hearts of all those who hold fast to one another in the fellowship of a holy faith.

> 4. The saying of Christ in **Matthew 5** is not yet fulfilled: **Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth.** Therefore, the third age must yet come to pass, in which those people shall possess the earth.

Answer: The premise is again false: For the God-fearing truly possess the earth; that is, they use and enjoy to full satisfaction everything that is sufficient and necessary for them. And that happens not only in the New Testament, but also happened long ago in the Old Testament, as **David** reports in **Psalm 37:11** <note>The original text cites Psalm 27:2, but the quote "the meek shall inherit the earth" is from Psalm 37:11, which Jesus quoted in the Beatitudes.</note>, from which Christ’s word and saying is taken word for word.

<meta>This page continues the author's point-by-point refutation of millenarian "prophets" who claimed a new "Golden Age" of the Holy Spirit was imminent. 5. If the third age <note>original: "seculum"</note> were not to follow, then the "Our Father" and prayer would have to be
abolished, wherein we pray for the coming of the Kingdom of GOD
and the obedience of men, equal to the obedience of the Angels.

Answer: This is a blasphemous thing. For in the second petition, we do not sigh
for a Golden Age on earth, because we are certain that here on
earth, battle and strife must prevail, but the crown and reward shall follow in Heaven;
rather, we sigh and long for the coming of the Heavenly Kingdom of Honor.
In the third petition, we plead for obedience to the will of God, just as
the former Christian Church did with no less sighing and prayer
in Psalm 119. It is there that we receive and obtain from GOD the LORD
such ability to obey His Word as is pleasing to Him, even though
we must often repeat in our hearts that little song of our GREAT
Inability: **Let the grace of GOD be sufficient for you.** <note>original Latin: "Sufficiat tibi gratia Dei." A reference to 2 Corinthians 12:9.</note>

Now, it is not my intention to examine the grounds of these New Prophets at greater length;
a reasonable man can remind himself from these few points what is to be
held regarding many others, so that he knows how to guard himself against the deceit and cunning of the false prophets;
and it is certain that when God grants them one true word, they conversely
spout ten or more lies. This is attested to by the most prominent and famous
Imperial Cities in Germany. Since those of Nuremberg last year took Nahelemos <note>Referring to Paul Nagel, who used the pseudonym Nahelemos.</note> into custody; those of Bern in the year 1609, Ziegler; the Alsatians in the year 1621, Praetorius;
the Strasbourgers, Geiger; those of Erfurt, Stifel;
the Zurichers, Felgenhauer; the citizens of Ulm drove their master of accounts <term>Rechenmeister; a teacher of mathematics and accounting</term> Faulhaber out of the city,
because their false, prophetic madness could not be tolerated. So it is also not
without cause that Nagel in Meissen rejects the predictions of all other astrologers <note>original: "Astrologon"</note> (as one who
attributes to himself alone the science of the secret interpretation of the stars).
Also, Ziegler in Franconia damns Nagel’s antics; Stifel in
Thuringia wants to tilt against <note>original: "Ritter werden," literally "to become a knight against someone," meaning to challenge or attack their arguments.</note> Ziegler; conversely, Stifel against the Palatine
Praetorius; Praetorius against Felgenhauer; then Geiger in Alsace; and the one who wants to be 
master and judge of them all, the master-of-ceremonies <term>Pritchmeister; originally a figure in shooting festivals who kept order with a wooden paddle, here used to mean a self-appointed critic or mock-leader</term> of the brand-new kingdom, Wilhelm Loin-H... <unclear>possibly Loinhold</unclear>.
Whoever cannot or will not recognize the vertigo and spirit of error which so vigorously 
stirs and peeks out from such prophets, truly deserves to remain 
clinging to and deceived by a lying Golden world-hope for eternity. Written in Cologne
in the year 1623, in the month of October.
