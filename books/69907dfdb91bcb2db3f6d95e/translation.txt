Rough soles of the feet belong to those who are poor.

### Characteristics of the Shins

Now [begins the study of the shins]:

Those with shins like reeds <note>original: "nala-jangha". This refers to shins that are thin and straight, like a hollow reed or stalk.</note> do not become kings.
