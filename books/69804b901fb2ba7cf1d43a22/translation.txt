# 48
### Introduction to the True Way

*Having been earnestly requested for the sake of many friends—among whom are some of the great luminaries of Germany—I have written of these difficult matters in a humble style.*

Finally, to all of you, Most Noble Men, and specifically to these most famous members of the <term>Curious Academy<note>The *Academia Naturae Curiosorum*, known today as the Leopoldina, dedicated to the study of the natural world.</note></term>: Mr. Dr. Volkamer, primary physician of the Republic of Nuremberg; Mr. Dr. Franck, Professor at Heidelberg, my dearest friends; and Mr. Dr. Wedel, Professor at Jena and Chief Physician to the most serene Dukes of Saxe-Gotha and Saxe-Weimar, my kinsman. Extending to you my most kind greetings, I shall conclude with the following saying: Just as I do not consider it insulting to disagree with any author, however great or ancient, I will just as readily disagree with my own self if someone brings forward a more correct view—even if the person who teaches it is a <term>layperson</term> <note>original: *idiota*; here meaning someone without formal academic training rather than the modern derogatory sense</note>.

*From the study in*
*Batavia, on the East Indian*
*island of Greater Java,*
*in the last month*
*of the year 1678.*

*A Devotee of your*
*most celebrated Academy,*

*HELBIG*

->THE END.<-
