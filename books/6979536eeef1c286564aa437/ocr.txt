The upper portion contains a square buckle or mill-iron charge; the lower portion depicts a wall with three battlements (merlons) above a field of wavy lines suggesting water. 

The shield is surmounted by a barred tournament helmet with elaborate foliate mantling. The crest atop the helmet is a single eagle wing displayed, charged with the same buckle/mill-iron found on the shield. Two griffins rampant serve as supporters on either side of the shield, their heads turned outward.

An elaborate engraved frontispiece depicting the intersection of alchemy, mining, and metallurgy. At the top center, the Hebrew Tetragrammaton (יהוה) is inscribed within a radiant sun, its rays shining down upon a mountain range. Seven specific peaks are marked with astronomical symbols representing the classical planets: from left to right, the Sun (☉), Venus (♀), Mars (♂), Saturn (♄), Jupiter (♃), Mercury (☿), and the Moon (☽).

The upper landscape illustrates various stages of mining and initial processing. On the left, workers are shown entering and exiting a mine shaft with wheelbarrows and climbing wooden ladders. In the center-right, figures are washing ore in large wooden vats near a building, with a massive stone furnace situated on the far right.

In the center of the composition, a large Baroque-style cartouche with ornate scrollwork contains the book's title.

Below the main landscape are three smaller vignettes framed in decorative cartouches:
- Left vignette: A worker operates a large smelting furnace; in the adjacent room, an alchemist or assayer works at a table featuring a precision balance scale.
- Center vignette: An interior view of an ore-crushing mill powered by a large waterwheel, with workers tending to sluices and collection tubs.
- Right vignette: Workers in an industrial setting process material in large rectangular molds near tall brick kilns or furnaces.
