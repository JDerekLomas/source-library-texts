# **JOHANN MICHAEL FAUST**, Doctor of Medicine,
->Regular Physician of Frankfurt,<-
->of the Imperial Leopoldine Academy, known as “Theophilus,”<- <note>Members of the Leopoldine Academy, one of the oldest scientific societies, were traditionally given academic monikers; Faust was called "Theophilus."</note>

# **NEW ALCHEMICAL COMPENDIUM**,
->or<-

# <image-desc>Ornamental woodcut initial 'P' with foliage and scrollwork.</image-desc>**PANDORA**
->Explained & Illustrated with Figures.<-
->That is / the<-

# **Noblest Gift of God**
->Or<-

## A Golden Treasure,

->With which the ancient and modern **Philosophers** <note>In this context, "Philosophers" refers to alchemists, the "philosophers of fire."</note> improved imperfect **metals** through the power of<-
->**fire**, and have driven away all manner of harmful and unwholesome **diseases**, both internal and external,<-
->through their effects.<-
->To this **edition** is further added, alongside **many copperplate engravings**, and over **800 philosophical annotations**, a<-
->complete **New Alchemical Lexicon**, and a full index of Subjects & Words <note>original Latin: "Register Rerum & Verborum"</note>.<-
