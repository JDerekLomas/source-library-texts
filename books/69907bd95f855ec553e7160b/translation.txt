->**The Book of Healing**<-
<note>original: "Kitab al-Shifa." This is a famous 12th-century work by the Moroccan scholar Qadi 'Iyad. Its full title is "The Book of Healing by the Recognition of the Rights of the Chosen One," and it remains one of the most popular books in the Islamic world concerning the life and character of the Prophet Muhammad.</note>

Owned by the humble servant <term>al-abd al-faqir: literally "the poor servant," a traditional formula of humility used by book owners and authors to indicate their need for God's mercy</term> Muhammad, son of Idris, around the year one thousand and nineteen from the <term>Hijra: the Islamic lunar calendar. The year 1019 AH corresponds to approximately 1610 CE</term>.

A calligraphic <term>tamlika: a formal note or mark indicating legal ownership of a manuscript</term> written in black ink, featuring the name "The humble Master Muhammad" with a stylistic circular flourish and the word "Owned by him" written underneath.
