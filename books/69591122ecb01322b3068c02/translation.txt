# AUTHOR’S PREFACE.

Europe currently turns its full attention toward America, desiring to know with scholarly curiosity the diversity of its climates, the structure of its mountains, the nature of its fossils <note>In this 18th-century context, "fossils" refers to any mineral or substance dug out of the earth, not just organic remains.</note>, the form of its vegetables and its animals, the languages of its inhabitants; and in short, everything that can engage its attention in those various regions. Among these—by the testimony of the authors who write of that part of our globe—the Kingdom of Chile is one of the most considerable, not so much for its extension, as for having emerged from the hands of nature endowed with partiality and with particular care; and because, sustained and favored by the delights of the climate, nature has scattered there with prodigality her best gifts, exempt for the most part from those inconveniences that usually accompany them in other places.

# Preface

Those who have arrived at its shores at different times have not failed to speak of that Kingdom; but their reports are too brief for one to form a just idea of Chile from them. Father Louis Feuillé <note>Louis Feuillé (1660–1732) was a French Minim friar, explorer, and botanist who traveled to western South America.</note>, a member of the Minim Order and a most learned Frenchman, described with extraordinary accuracy the principal plants that grow there, and some of the animals that propagate there. He did this with descriptions so certain and so consistent with the objects they encompass, that I have not found the slightest oversight in everything that great man wrote. However, his history—printed at the King's expense with a grand display of very fine plates—has not been reprinted, and is hardly known to more than a few people.

Nor have the natives <note>The author likely refers to "Criollos," people of Spanish descent born in Chile, rather than indigenous peoples.</note> neglected to illustrate their country with their own writings.
