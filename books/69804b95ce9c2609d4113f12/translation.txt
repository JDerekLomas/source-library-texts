# ECCLESIASTICAL HISTORY OF THE <term>WALDENSIANS<note>The Waldensians (or Vaudois) were a medieval Christian movement that preceded the Protestant Reformation. They emphasized poverty and the authority of the Bible, which led to centuries of conflict and persecution by the authorities in the Alpine regions of France and Italy.</note></term>.

*The frontispiece of this volume presents a chilling visual record of the persecutions endured by the Waldensians in their mountain strongholds. In the high Alpine background, soldiers are seen throwing people headlong from steep cliffs. Below, a village is engulfed in flames as families flee for their lives. The foreground contains graphic depictions of martyrdom: one man is suspended upside down and eviscerated, another is impaled upon a wooden stake, and soldiers are shown murdering an infant. These scenes were intended to illustrate the "Great Persecutions" and serve as a testament to the faith of those depicted.*

->In Amsterdam, By Jaques Boursse, Bookseller, on the Lily Canal.<-
