## *Flos Flosculosus.*

<image-desc>
An engraved botanical illustration depicting the components of a discoid or flosculous flower head (composed of tubular florets). 
- At the top left is a complete flower head labeled 'A' at the top and 'E' on the involucral bracts. 
- To its right is a longitudinal cross-section of the same flower head, revealing the receptacle 'D' and the arrangement of florets within the bracts 'E'.
- In the center and on the far right are individual florets. Labels include 'F' for the stigma/style, 'G' and 'H' for the lobes of the tubular corolla, 'B' for the corolla tube, and 'C' for the base (ovary and pappus).
- The lower section shows dissected parts: a portion of the corolla 'Q' and 'P'; an ovary or fruit 'O'; a seed with its pappus 'M' and 'N'; and another floral part labeled 'I' and 'L'.

## *Flos Semiflosculosus.*

<image-desc>
An engraved botanical illustration depicting the components of a ligulate or semiflosculous flower head (composed of ray florets).
- At the top left, a flower head is viewed from above, labeled 'A'.
- At the top right, a longitudinal section of the flower head is shown, labeled 'A' at the florets, 'C' at the base, and 'Q' at the bracts.
- In the center are two individual florets showing the characteristic strap-shaped (ligulate) corolla. Labels include 'I' (stigma), 'G' (ligule), 'E' (base of corolla), 'K' (ovary), and 'H' (anther tube).
- On the middle left is an empty, pitted receptacle labeled '*' and '&'.
- Adjacent is a seed with a long, feathery pappus labeled 'T' and 'S'. 
- A detached style and stigma are labeled 'M' and 'N'.
- Further floret dissections are labeled 'D', 'M', 'F', 'L', 'R', 'N'.
- At the bottom right, a whole flower head is shown from a side-view perspective, labeled 'B' and 'P'.
- Small seeds/fruits are shown in detail at the bottom, labeled 'R', 'V', 'Z', and 'X'.
</image-desc>
