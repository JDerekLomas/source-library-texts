## *The Flosculous Flower*
<note>original: "Flos Flosculosus." This refers to a "tubular" or "discoid" flower head, where the center is composed of tiny, tube-shaped individual flowers called florets.</note>

<image-desc>
An engraved botanical illustration depicting the components of a discoid or flosculous flower head (composed of tubular florets). 
- At the top left is a complete flower head labeled 'A' at the top and 'E' on the involucral bracts <note>The leaf-like structures that protect the base of the flower head</note>. 
- To its right is a longitudinal cross-section of the same flower head, revealing the receptacle 'D' <note>the base where all florets are attached</note> and the arrangement of florets within the bracts 'E'.
- In the center and on the far right are individual florets. Labels include 'F' for the stigma/style <note>the female reproductive parts</note>, 'G' and 'H' for the lobes of the tubular corolla <note>the petals fused into a tube</note>, 'B' for the corolla tube, and 'C' for the base (ovary and pappus).
- The lower section shows dissected parts: a portion of the corolla 'Q' and 'P'; an ovary or fruit 'O'; a seed with its pappus 'M' and 'N' <note>the feathery bristles that help seeds travel by wind</note>; and another floral part labeled 'I' and 'L'.
</image-desc>

## *The Semiflosculous Flower*
<note>original: "Flos Semiflosculosus." This refers to a "ligulate" flower head, where the individual florets have a long, strap-shaped petal that looks like a single large petal (common in dandelions).</note>

<image-desc>
An engraved botanical illustration depicting the components of a ligulate or semiflosculous flower head (composed of ray florets).
- At the top left, a flower head is viewed from above, labeled 'A'.
- At the top right, a longitudinal section of the flower head is shown, labeled 'A' at the florets, 'C' at the base, and 'Q' at the bracts.
- In the center are two individual florets showing the characteristic strap-shaped (ligulate) corolla. Labels include 'I' (stigma), 'G' (ligule) <note>the "strap" or petal-like part</note>, 'E' (base of corolla), 'K' (ovary), and 'H' (anther tube) <note>the male pollen-bearing parts fused into a cylinder</note>.
- On the middle left is an empty, pitted receptacle labeled '*' and '&'.
- Adjacent is a seed with a long, feathery pappus labeled 'T' and 'S'. 
- A detached style and stigma are labeled 'M' and 'N'.
- Further floret dissections are labeled 'D', 'M', 'F', 'L', 'R', 'N'.
- At the bottom right, a whole flower head is shown from a side-view perspective, labeled 'B' and 'P'.
- Small seeds/fruits are shown in detail at the bottom, labeled 'R', 'V', 'Z', and 'X'.
</image-desc>

<summary>This plate provides a detailed anatomical breakdown of two types of composite flowers: the flosculous (tubular) and semiflosculous (strap-shaped) varieties. It illustrates the transition from the whole flower head to the microscopic individual florets and seeds.</summary>
