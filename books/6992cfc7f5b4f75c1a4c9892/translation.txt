On the American continent, there is the nation of the <term>United States</term> <note>original: "Nai-shi-die." A phonetic rendering of the English word "United."</note>. ### The District of Columbia

Outside of the states, there are still four territories belonging to the <term>Native Americans</term> <note>original: "Yin-di-a," a phonetic rendering of Indians.</note>.

The territory of the <term>District of Columbia</term> <note>original: "Ge-jiao-mi-a."</note> is narrow and small, situated between <term>Maryland</term> <note>original: "Ma-li-lan."</note> and <term>Virginia</term> <note>original: "Wa-zhi-ni-a."</note>.

It is located on the <term>Potomac River</term> <note>original: "Zi-bo-duo-mo."</note>, two hundred *li* <note>A Chinese unit of distance, roughly 1/3 of a mile or 500 meters.</note> from the <term>Atlantic Ocean</term> <note>original: "A-lan-di."</note>. In the year 1790 <gloss>the 55th year of the Qianlong reign</gloss>, the leader <term>Washington</term> <note>original: "Wa-shen-dun."</note> designated this location, and it was subsequently named the national capital for the purpose of issuing government orders and adjudicating legal matters.

As the central gathering place for the assembly, three cities were originally built: one is Washington, one is <term>Georgetown</term> <note>original: "Cha-zhi-dang."</note>, and one is <term>Alexandria</term> <note>original: "A-li-shan-te-li-a."</note>. These three are all part of the capital city and are not included in the count of the "tribes" <note>referring to the States.</note>.

The soil and climate vary between dry and humid, and the area possesses many beautiful sights. The river channels are accessible; regardless of whether boats are large or small, they can all reach the capital directly. Consequently, trade is extremely prosperous. <gloss>Note: This district is the national capital and is situated outside of the twenty-six states.</gloss>

**The City of Washington** <gloss>Construction began in the year 1800, during the early years of the nation. The streets are level and wide, laid out like a chessboard, with horses and carriages passing in a constant stream. In the center stands the **Capitol** <note>original: "Jia-jun-duo-er," a phonetic rendering of Capitol.</note>, a single structure used for deliberations.</gloss>
