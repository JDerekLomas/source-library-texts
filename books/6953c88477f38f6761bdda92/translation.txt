# THE MODEL OF THE MAGICAL SCHOOL.
<note>original: "Scholæ Magicæ Typus"</note>

<image-desc>A detailed symbolic engraving. At the top center, a large, rugged peak is labeled "The Invisible Mountain of the Magi." Above the mountain, clouds gather at the peak; to the left is the Sun with a human face, and to the right is a crescent moon surrounded by a field of stars. Below the mountain is a cavernous arch labeled "The Fantastic Region," where various winged hybrid creatures and demons reside. Inside the arch, on the left, stands a winged angelic figure holding a wand and a key. On the right, a blindfolded man in 17th-century dress fumbles blindly. Between them stands a stone altar with a single burning candle; arched around the flame is the text "The Light of Nature." At the base of the figures' platform, the words "The Enchanted Treasure" are inscribed. In the foreground, a dragon bites its own tail to form an Ouroboros circle. Inside this circle, a small child sits atop a heap of coins or jewels, holding a chain. Above the child is written "Only for the Little Ones." In the bottom right corner, outside the main frame, is the engraver's signature.</image-desc>
