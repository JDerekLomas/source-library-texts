And in the various operations which they perform upon the elements, He ordained that the pestilential air and water and the fruits of the earth could be corrupted and even become deadly, according to the tyranny and malice that all such things produce; they even move the individual spirits of those born <note>original: "spiritus natorum"</note> toward their own specific actions. 

Now, if the right and power of generating, corrupting, and altering has been given to them over the whole world and the greatest of creatures (such as the elements), how much more so is this true for the smallest gems and forms?
