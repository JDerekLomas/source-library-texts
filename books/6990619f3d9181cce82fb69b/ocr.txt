## *Edward Fenner, one of the Iustices of the*
## *court of her Maiesties bench.*

In these times (Right VVorshipfull) wherein euery idle wit seekes to blaze abroade their vainenesse, there ought to be no smal care for the restraining of triuiall Pamphlets, aswell to exercise the Readers in matter necessary, as to cut off the writing of thinges needles. Among other your worships care, as well for the furthering of the truth of this arraignment, being Iudge at the tryall of the malefactors, as also the crossing of whatsoeuer Pamphlets should haue beene preferd, respecting either the matter partly or confusedly: emboldens me to prefer the patronage hereof to your worship, not doubting but as you haue been carefull aswell for the tryall & iudgement of such hainous offenders, and withall taken extraordinary paines in perfecting this work for the printing to others example, you will likewise vouchsafe to pardon our bold<unclear>n</unclear>es.
