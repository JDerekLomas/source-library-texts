# Emblem 31

->It is not disturbed on high<-

<note>original: "Non perturbatur in alto"</note>

The engraving depicts a vast, allegorical landscape framed within a double border. Across the sky, a large, flowing banner carries the central motto: **It is not disturbed on high**.

Below this banner, the scene is one of dramatic contrast. Heavy, dark clouds gather over a rugged mountain range, and several jagged lightning bolts strike downward toward the lower peaks and a distant city visible on the horizon. This represents the <term>sublunary world</term> <note>The realm below the moon, which in ancient philosophy was subject to constant change, storms, and decay.</note>, where turmoil and danger are ever-present.

However, the highest mountain peak rises majestically above the densest clouds. While the lower lands are ravaged by the storm, this summit remains in perfect clarity. In the top right corner, a radiant sun emits beams of light, illuminating the peak. The foreground shows rocky terrain with sparse trees, emphasizing the isolation and height required to reach such a state of peace. The image serves as a visual metaphor for the human mind or soul which, by rising toward divine contemplation, remains untroubled by the "storms" of worldly life.

<summary>This page features an emblem illustrating that the higher reaches of the mind or soul remain peaceful and untouched by the chaotic storms of the material world.</summary>
