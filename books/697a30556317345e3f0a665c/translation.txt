## Preface.

**O**lthough I was never inclined to commit to print the special and rare **cases** <note>original: "Casus"</note> sent to me mostly by persons of noble rank, along with the written **consultations** <note>original: "Consiliis"</note> I provided regarding them; nevertheless, the reproaches of many sincere friends, as well as some honest and upright **physicians** <note>original: "Medici"</note> with whom I have had the opportunity to **confer**, have particularly prompted me to satisfy their request for now. This is all the more necessary because most people are still so captivated by a manifest blindness of false **prejudice toward authority** <note>original: "præjudicii auctoritatis"; the tendency to believe something simply because an established authority figure says it, rather than through observation.</note> from other doctors that they shamelessly contradict experience—which has been confirmed here and there by so many **examples** and proofs, and carries more weight than all **theoretical reasons** <note>original: "rationes"</note>—and consider everything false and fabricated. Hence it happens that many **patients** have allowed themselves to be misled and turned away, letting themselves be "sweet-talked" by their doctors in the meantime, until they finally realized the great harm when it was too late. There is a common proverb: *One potter hates another; likewise, one doctor envies another* <note>original: "Figulus figulum odit, item, Medicus Medico invidet"</note>. This is true enough, and it is experienced especially by those upright physicians who have actually and effectively achieved more than what **our common practice** <note>original: "praxis nostra vulgaris"</note>—even if it claims to be **methodical and rational** <note>original: "etiamſi ſit methodica, & rationalis"</note>—is capable of today. For **professional jealousy** <note>original: "Handwercks-Neid"</note> is immediately behind it, especially from those who are in fact very poor **practitioners** and yet wish to be **venerated** by everyone as great men...
