Among members of our own Society <note>The Society for Psychical Research, founded in 1882</note>, our warmest thanks go to Miss Porter for her focused, patient, and energetic assistance in every department of the work. In addition to the names listed above, the Society includes over 600 Members and Associates. The privileges and conditions of membership are defined in the Rules as follows:

Rule IV.—The Society shall consist of:

> (a) *Members*, who shall contribute no less than two guineas <note>A guinea was a gold coin worth 21 shillings, or one pound and one shilling. It was often used for professional fees and elite memberships</note> annually, or a single payment of twenty guineas. Members shall be entitled to hold any of the offices of the Society; to vote in the election of the Governing Council; to attend all meetings of the Society; to use its Reading Room and Library; to borrow books from its Library; and to receive for free any journal, transactions, or periodical publication that may be issued by the Council.

> (b) *Associates*, who shall contribute no less than one guinea annually, or a single payment of ten guineas. Associates shall be entitled to attend all meetings of the Society, except those called for business purposes only; to use its Reading Room and Library; and to receive for free the ordinary published *Proceedings* of the Society and the monthly *Journal*.
