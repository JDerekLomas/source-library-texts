## Q U E S T I O N  II. <note>original Latin: "Q V E S T I O II"</note>

Out of four I am; their war kills me, <note>The "four" refers to the four elements: Earth, Air, Fire, and Water. Their "war" refers to their opposing qualities (hot/cold, wet/dry) which cause corruption and decay.</note>

Their war destroys the green grass;

## Second Response. <note>original Latin: "Responſio Secunda"</note>

If you want to reconcile the evil four,

## Q U E S T I O N  III. <note>original Latin: "Q V E S T I O III"</note>

Before there were four; now there are only three;

One and four are five, rightly so;
