# Ode on Measuring the Heavens

High and bright, covering from above: the sun, moon, and stars. Submerged and hidden, carrying from below: the wind, rain, and dragon spirits.

"High and bright" refers to Heaven. Heaven is originally one single vital energy <term>qi (the primordial breath or energy of the universe)</term>, light and pure, which revolves and rotates above. 

"Submerged and hidden" refers to Earth. Earth is originally one single vital energy, heavy and turbid, which establishes its fixed position below. 

The Sun is the essence of Great Yang <term>Taiyang (the supreme active, masculine principle)</term>. It accumulates to form a celestial image; its light radiates outward, while its physical substance is contained within. 

The Moon is the essence of Great Yin <term>Taiyin (the supreme passive, feminine principle)</term>. It accumulates to form a celestial image; its light shines by reflection <note>Original: "映照" (mirroring/reflecting); the text correctly identifies that the moon reflects the sun's light</note>, reaching the peak of fullness before waning into deficiency.

The Stars are the essences of the Five Elements: 
*   Wood is called the **Year Star** <note>Jupiter</note>.
*   Fire is called the **Glimmering Deluder** <note>Mars; named for its reddish hue and unpredictable path</note>.
*   Earth is called the **Guarding Star** <note>Saturn</note>.
