# PREFACE

### To the

## GOD-LOVING

# READER.

1. The Very First and Greatest Commandment is **LOVE**, since God himself is Love; now it is certain and true that God alone is the First and Greatest, in whom alone everything begins and ends; therefore this Commandment is truly the first and great Commandment: so it is for the most weighty reason that God’s Children, and all who wish to be Christians, must begin here first and foremost. For there is a right true Proverb here:

> *He who starts badly at first,*
> *The end shall swallow him.*
<note>This proverb warns that a life or spiritual journey built on a poor foundation is destined for failure.</note>

Is Jesus Christ then not the Love of the Father? How then does their behavior resemble Him? Must Man not also bear once again the **Image and Likeness of God**?

<note>"Natural understanding" refers to the innate human ability to reason and perceive truth, which the author fears has been clouded by worldly living.</note>

4.
