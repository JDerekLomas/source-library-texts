->IN THE NAME OF JESUS.<- <note>original: "IN NOMINE JESU."</note>

->The<-

# Eternal Gospel /
## Concerning the Certainty of Salvation for all Penitent and Believing Children of God /

->in Christ Jesus /<-

As the unique, unshakable foundation upon which they stand secure, firm, and eternal, and—together with all the saints from the beginning—may defy, fight, and far, far overcome the devil, the world, and all the gates of hell. Romans 8, Psalms 18, 46, 118.

Composed out of watchful concern for the remaining intimidated, confused, and scattered children of God, in defiance, dread, and torment of the devil.

->By<-

**MASTER FRIEDRICH BRECKLING,** <note>Friedrich Breckling (1629–1711) was a radical Pietist theologian known for his sharp critiques of the established Lutheran church and his focus on inner spiritual life.</note>
Preacher at Handewitt in Holstein.

> 2 Corinthians 4: We do not distort the word of God, but by the manifestation of the truth, we commend ourselves to every human conscience before God.

->Amsterdam, printed by Christoffel Cunradus,<-
->for the Author, in the Year 1660.<-
