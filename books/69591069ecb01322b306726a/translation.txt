# TO THE

# QUEEN,

->*MADAM,*<-

<image-desc>Decorative initial 'A' featuring a bird perched among foliage and flowers.</image-desc>As these Volumes contain an Essay towards the *Natural History* <note>The study of organisms and the physical world through observation rather than experimentation.</note> of that Part of Your MAJESTY'S Dominions, which are particularly honoured by bearing Your AUGUST NAME, CAROLINA <note>The province was named in honor of King Charles I (Carolus), but the author here pays tribute to Queen Caroline, the wife of George II.</note>, this and Your great Goodness in encouraging all Sorts of Learning, hath emboldned me to implore Your Royal Protection and Favour to my slender Performance. I hope Your MAJESTY will not think a few Minutes disagreeably spent, in casting an Eye on these Leaves; which exhibit no contemptible Scene of the Glorious Works of the Creator, displayed in the New World; and hitherto lain concealed from the View of Your MAJESTY, as well as of Your Royal Predecessors, tho' so long possessed of a Country, inferior to none of Your MAJESTY'S American Dominions.

M. CATESBY. <note>Mark Catesby (1683–1749) was an English naturalist who published the first comprehensive survey of the flora and fauna of North America.</note>
