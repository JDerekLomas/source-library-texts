### Elisha ben Gabriel Gallico. *Perush Shir ha-Shirim*. Venice, Giovanni di Gara, 1587

A commentary on the Song of Songs, prepared and researched, according to the title-page, in Safed, the famous Kabbalistic centre in the sixteenth century, which produced such luminaries as Moses Cordovero and Isaac Luria. The mystical school of Safed was the first to interpret the Song of Songs as a dialogue between God and the soul.

Provenance: University of Haifa, 1950s.
