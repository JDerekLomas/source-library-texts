# The Complete Library of the Four Treasuries
<note>original: "四庫全書" (Sìkù Quánshū). This was the largest collection of books in Chinese history, commissioned by the Qianlong Emperor of the Qing dynasty in 1772 to encompass all known knowledge across four main categories.</note>

## Masters Division
<note>original: "子部" (Zǐ bù). This is the third of the four traditional branches of Chinese literature. It includes the works of various philosophical schools—such as Daoism, Legalism, and Mohism—as well as specialized texts on agriculture, medicine, astronomy, and the arts.</note>

<summary>This title page identifies the text as belonging to the "Masters Division" of the Complete Library of the Four Treasuries, a massive 18th-century imperial project to catalog Chinese knowledge.</summary>
