# The Art and Science
# of Personal Magnetism
<term>Personal Magnetism</term> <note>In the context of the early 20th-century New Thought movement, this refers to an individual's ability to attract, influence, and lead others through the power of their personality and mental energy—similar to what we now call "charisma."</note>

## The Secret of Mental Fascination
<term>Mental Fascination</term> <note>The practice of focusing one's mental energy to capture the attention or influence the willpower of another person.</note>

### By
## Theron Dumont
<note>Theron Dumont was a well-known pseudonym for William Walker Atkinson (1862–1932), a prolific writer and influential figure in the New Thought movement who explored themes of psychology, occultism, and personal success.</note>
