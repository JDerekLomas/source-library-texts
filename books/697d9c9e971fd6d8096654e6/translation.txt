->Watch and pray. <note>original Latin: "VIDETE VIGILATE."</note> Matthew 13.<-
->Meditate upon these things. <note>original Latin: "HÆC MEDITARE."</note> 1 Timothy 4.<-

### Golden
### Jewel
### of the Children of God
### that is,
# The true way to Christianity, translated from English into German by EMANUEL SONTHOM. <note>Emmanuel Sonthom was the pseudonym of Emmanuel Thomson, an English devotional writer whose work became immensely popular in Germany.</note> However, out of love for the book, it has been changed in many places because of several un-German and doctrinally suspicious phrases. <note>This indicates the German editor "corrected" the English original to ensure it aligned perfectly with local Lutheran theology.</note>
->With Electoral Saxon Privilege. <note>A "privilege" was an early form of copyright protection granted by a ruler—in this case, the Elector of Saxony—to prevent others from reprinting the work.</note><-
## LÜNEBURG.
->Printed and published by Hans and Henrich Stern.<-

<image-desc>
An elaborate copperplate engraving serving as the title page. At the top, two putti flank a decorative arch; the left putto holds a sword and shield, while the right putto rests on a skull with an hourglass, symbols of the "memento mori" (remember that you will die) tradition. Banners above them contain biblical citations. The central illustration below the text depicts Father Time (Chronos) as a winged, bearded man carrying a large scythe, leading a woman and a small child by the hand, signifying the passage of life. In the background, a landscape with trees and a church spire is visible. To the far left, a partial view of a scene shows figures feasting and drinking, representing worldly vanities that the pious should avoid. In the bottom right corner is a circular printer's mark or seal showing a hand from a cloud holding a key, surrounded by the Latin motto "The hand of the Lord protects us" <note>original Latin: "MANUS DOMINI PROTEGAT NOS"</note> and the initials "S H" for the Stern house. The engraver's signature "Le Blon made this" <note>original Latin: "Le Blon fecit."</note> is located at the bottom left.
</image-desc>

May God the Lord bless you,
my dearly beloved ones.

<column-break/>

Do not mourn too deeply
over my departure.
