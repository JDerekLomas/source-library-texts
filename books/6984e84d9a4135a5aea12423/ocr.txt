<image-desc>
A detailed symbolic diagram titled "Signaculum Mundi Pythagoricum" (Pythagorean Seal of the World), representing the hierarchy of the universe. At the top, a radiant sun represents the "Archetypal World" (MVNDVS ARCHETYPVS) with the name "DEVS IEHOVA" at its center. Below this, a large triangle is divided into various realms: the Angelic, Ethereal, and Elementary. Within the triangle are listed "COELVM" (Heaven), "HOMO" (Man) in a central circle, and "AER" (Air), along with their inhabitants (Angeli, Stellae, Meteores, Aves, Bestia, Pisces). The base of the triangle contains "TERRA" (Earth) and "AQVA" (Water) and their constituent parts (Sulphur, Sal, Metalla, Plantae, Lapides). The entire triangle rests on a rectangular foundation representing "CHAOS", which contains "Ignis", "Inane", "Tenebrae", and "Abyss", with a circle at the bottom center for "Malum SATAN". The diagram includes symbolic numbers 1 through 6 distributed throughout the hierarchy.
</image-desc>
