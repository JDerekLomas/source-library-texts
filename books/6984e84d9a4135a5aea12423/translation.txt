# THE
# Philosophical Epitaph of *W. C. Esquire*.
->For a *Remember Death* <note>original: Memento Mori</note> on his Tombstone.<-

With three hieroglyphical shields <note>original: Scutcheons; heraldic shields containing symbolic imagery</note>, and their philosophical mottoes and explanation; with the philosophical *mercury*, the *nature* of *seed* and *life*, and the *growth* of *metals*; and a discovery of the immortal liquid *Alkahest* <note>original: Al-chahest; a hypothetical universal solvent sought by alchemists</note>. The salt of *tartar* volatilized, and other *elixirs*, with their differences.

->A L S O,<-

## A Brief Account of the Golden Calf (the World's Idol).
Discovering the rarest miracle in nature: how, by the smallest proportion of the Philosopher's Stone, a large piece of common lead was totally transmuted into the purest, transparent gold at the *Hague* in 1666 by *Johann Friedrich Helvetius*.

->A N D,<-

## The *GOLDEN ASS* Well Managed, and *Midas* Restored to Reason; Or, *A New Chemical Light*,
Demonstrating to the blind world that good gold may be found in cold regions as well as hot, and can be profitably extracted out of sand, stones, gravel, and flints, etc., to be worked by all sorts of people. Written by *Johann Rudolf Glauber*.

->W I T H<-

# JEHIOR <note>original: Jehior; a Hebrew term often translated as "Let there be light"</note>. [*The Dawn of Wisdom* <note>original: Aurora Sapientiæ</note>], Or, the Day-Dawning or Light of Wisdom, containing the three principles or origin of all things; whereby the great and many mysteries in God, Nature, and the Elements—hitherto hidden—are now revealed.

## All Published by *W. C. Esquire* <note>William Cooper, a famous London bookseller and specialist in alchemical literature</note>.
