<term>Catalog of Manuscripts</term>
<note>In the context of 18th and 19th-century libraries, a "Catalogo dei Manoscritti" was a formal inventory of hand-written documents, often commissioned by wealthy collectors or academic institutions to organize their archives.</note>

<summary>This page shows the title and volume number gold-stamped onto the leather binding of a manuscript catalog. It identifies the book as the first volume of a larger collection of handwritten documents.</summary>
