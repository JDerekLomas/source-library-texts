The portrait is set within a complex architectural and allegorical frame. At the top, three cherubs are present: the one on the left holds a sword and scales (Justice), and the one on the right holds a mirror and a snake (Prudence). 

In niches on either side of the portrait stand two allegorical female figures. The figure on the left holds a large tablet or book inscribed with "FIDES SPES CHARI." (Faith, Hope, and Charity). The figure on the right is Temperance, shown pouring liquid from a pitcher into a cup.

At the bottom center is an ornate coat of arms featuring four quarters with various charges, surmounted by a mitre and a crozier. This is flanked by two circular medallions (emblems). The left medallion shows clouds, the Hebrew Tetragrammaton (יהוה), and a curved agricultural tool, with the motto "IN DEO MEO". Sed, vt sunt humana, superueniens nuper ex AQVILONE PROCELLA decussit flores & proturbatos suis cum plantulis thalamos euertit; vt jam, quo loco olim Mitissimæ Stirpes adoleuerant, nihil aliud supersit, quam ferox & truculenta BELLONÆ Facies; Alibi superstantes saxis minacibus aggeres; alibi præcipitati defendendis aggeribus scopuli, & fulmina per obliquas rupes prospicientia, & trunci ac prominentes cippi, & corbes rudi fabulo trementes; ac quidquid in bellantium perniciem hodierni Æui Solertia excogitauit.

Cæterùm ne Immortalis Ausî Memoria omnis Posteritati efflueret, fecit IS quem exoratus iterum DEVS Mitigandis horum Temporum Calamitatibus indulsit Reuerendissimus atq; Illustrissimus PRINCEPS AC DOMINVS D. MARQVARDVS Episcopus Eistettensis. Eius Munere ac Sumptibus habes quæ Curis hisce Secundis elaborauimus ex voto & sententia eorum, qui hanc operam desiderare visi; quales fuêre in GALLIA, BELGICA, BRITANNIA complures.

Ordinem secuti sumus, qualem prima exempla præiuêre, nisi quod superuacuum credimus in tot manipulos hanc messem digerere; Tripartita tamen Serie prodeunt, vt Prima Classis Vernas Stirpes exhibeat, Altera Æstiuas, Vltima eas quæ vix præcipitante Autumno apud Nos excoqui possunt.

Descriptiones, quas alij parùm sinceras, alij parùm scitas nec suffecturas huius Sæculi impatientiæ existimârunt, omisimus; eò etiam maximè, ne exemplo priscorum artificum peccaremus, quibus necesse fuit artis suæ simplicitati adpositis bona fide Indicibus succurrere, & admoneri scilicet Populum, Bouem esse qui spectetur, non Hominem.
