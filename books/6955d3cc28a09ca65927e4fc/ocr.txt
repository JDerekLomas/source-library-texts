## THE RIGHT HONOURABLE

# THE EARL OF ORRERY.

MY LORD,

In your <unclear>Lordship's hands</unclear>, the <unclear>most difficult parts of</unclear> *Natural History*, *CHYMISTRY* and *MEDICINE*, lose <unclear>their</unclear> *native roughness*, and *appear* with an *elegance* and *lustre* that *would give a credit* to any deterring produc-
tions of study: But the volume herein-
scribed to your Lordship has a further
right to your countenance and favour;
as not only treating of subjects,
whereof your knowledge is thorough
and profound; but, being
part of a work originally proceeding
from one, whose immortal Name adds
