# Preface.

<meta>The text is enclosed within a rectangular ornamental border composed of small linked oval motifs.</meta>

->Preface.<-

Since then (as Augustine <note>Saint Augustine of Hippo (354–430 AD), a highly influential theologian and philosopher.</note> reports) to /
wander astray anywhere is very dangerous /
to seek something is very laborious / 
but when one has found it /
such a thing is very fruitful and good:
Therefore, at the request of the
publisher, I have undertaken to take 
this **Chemical Treatise**,
which THOMAS NORTON,
an Englishman / many years ago / in 
his mother tongue / wrote in the form
of rhymes / and titled *Believe Me* <note>original Latin: "CREDE MIHI"</note> or 
*The Ordinal* <note>original Latin: "ORDINALE"</note>, which 
afterwards was translated from the 
English manuscript into Latin and 
printed by the late Doctor Michael 
Maier <note>Michael Maier (1568–1622), a famous German physician and alchemist who served as counselor to Emperor Rudolf II.</note>, Count Palatine <note>original Latin: "Com: Pal:", short for Comes Palatinus, a title of nobility.</note> / I have undertaken / 
I say / to bring this work from the 
said Latin into common German 
rhymes / especially because the said 
treatise is highly esteemed by many 
as being very good and useful.
