## Preface.

Among the countless blessings of God, which Divine mercy exercises daily toward the whole human race, and especially toward His faithful, I would believe this one to be singular: namely, the Knowledge of the malice and impiety of our enemies, both visible and invisible. Thus, once their cunning is known, we may place obstacles where they prepare and attempt their stumbling blocks. Truly, since the pride <margin>Psalm 73 <note>original: "Ps. 73." Although he reaps briars for himself, increasing his own eternal punishments and sharpening his pains, he does so provided that he may lead as great a multitude of men as possible down to Hell <note>original: "Inferna"</note>, and [destroy] the image of the Most High...
