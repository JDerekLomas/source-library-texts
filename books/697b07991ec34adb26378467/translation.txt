# TO THE READERS,
## Concerning the Contents in this VOLUME.

So that the matters which were recorded concerning LEARNING <note>original: *eruditio*</note> in the three books found in the preceding Volume might be published with greater benefit, it seemed necessary—and not without good reason—that it be shown *more specifically* which individuals ought to be solidly educated. This applies at least to the foundational principles in the three states or conditions of life which are the most significant among all: 1. namely, in the state of *infancy* <note>or early childhood</note>; 2. in a more mature state regarding external worship and the branches or societies of the *Christian Religion*, where one person argues for some and another for others (often involving both external and internal turmoil); 3. and finally, in the state of the *interior life*, for those who dedicate themselves to spiritual matters, believing this to be the primary duty of the immortal soul and the most worthy of all occupations. However, the more general method of treatment I had then set for those previous books did not allow me to discuss these specific points in that place.

Since, however, those Books are now being reprinted at this time, I considered that *those more specific desired works*, which I had published separately some time ago, could now—for the reason mentioned—be appropriately added to the preceding Books ON LEARNING, serving as a SECOND VOLUME <note>original: *Volumen Alterum*</note> to them.
