# Foreword

A pure Christian doctrine is, among all temporal gifts and blessings from God, perhaps the very greatest and most important, because without it, all other gifts of God would be of very little benefit to our true well-being. Through it, not only is the foundation for all good morality <note>original: *Sittsamkeit*—refers to a combination of character, decency, and ethical conduct</note> established, by which we may be useful and pleasing to God and our fellow human beings, and taught how we should use all of God's gifts for our salvation; but it also shows us most clearly how we may be rescued from all misery through the knowledge of God and our Savior <term>Savior: *Heilmacher*, literally 'Heal-maker' or restorer</term> Jesus Christ, and attain an eternal and supreme happiness. The Son of God himself and his apostles, driven and equipped by the power of the Holy Spirit, brought this doctrine to light and set it on its path, indicating that this good news of God should be faithfully offered and proclaimed to all people until the Last Day. All honest servants of Jesus Christ have faithfully applied themselves to this at all times, seeking to promote and spread it in every way possible.
