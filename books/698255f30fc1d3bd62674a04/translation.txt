->OR<-

->thus rendered into *English*.<-

## By ROBERT FLUDD, Esquire;
->&<-
### Doctor of Medicine <note>original: "Physick"</note>.

---

## To the Judicious and Discreet
# R E A D E R.

It is my desire, (*Judicious and Learned Reader*), that it may not prove offensive to anyone if—following the example of my medical and God-centered philosophical <note>original: "Physicall and Theo-philosophicall"</note> patron *St. Luke*—I mention and cite the testimony of *Holy Scripture* to prove and maintain the true and essential *Philosophy*. This is supported by the virtuous properties of that eternal *Wisdom*, which is the foundation and cornerstone upon which it is grounded. Was this not the primary subject of my aforementioned patron, who was as much a *Divine Philosopher* as he was a *Physician*?

If the purpose of *Jacob's Ladder* was for *souls and angels* to ascend from the *Earth* to *Heaven*, and to descend from *Heaven* to *Earth* by many steps or levels corresponding to both the *physical* <note>original: "Elementary"</note> and *ethereal* or *heavenly* nature; or (as the *Poet* speaks mystically) if the chain of *Nature* has its highest and last link fastened to the foot of *Jupiter's throne in Heaven* while the lower link is fixed on *Earth*: how is it possible for us earthly creatures—or rather, divine images housed and obscured in dwellings made of clay <note>original: "clayie tabernacles," a biblical metaphor for the human body</note>—to navigate, by ourselves, through the confusing *labyrinth* of creation <note>original: "the creature"</note> toward the bright *essence* of the *Creator*? 

That is to say, how can we search out the mysteries of the true *Wisdom* in this world and its creatures, except by penetrating with mental contemplation and practical skill into its earthly circumference or dwelling place? By doing so, we may dive or attain, little by little, to the heavenly palace—I mean the middle point or *Center* of it, which is the only place where she may be found, she who is the *Center* of all things, but whose circumference cannot rationally be imagined or thought of? 

If God, therefore, in and by his *Eternal Word or Divine Wisdom*, first made all creatures and has sustained them until this present time, how can a real *Philosopher* uncover <note>original: "enucleate"</note> the mysteries of the *Creator* within the creature, or wisely behold or express the creature within the Creator (for in him are all things), except by such rules or directions as the only storehouse of *Wisdom*—namely the *Holy Scriptures*—has recorded, and the finger of that Sacred Spirit has written for our instruction?
