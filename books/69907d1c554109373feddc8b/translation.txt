## Usage Guidelines

Google is proud to partner with libraries to digitize public domain materials and make them widely accessible. Public domain books belong to the public, and we are merely their custodians. Nevertheless, this work is expensive. To continue providing this resource, we have taken steps to prevent abuse by commercial parties, including placing technical restrictions on automated searching.

We also ask that you:

+ **Use the files for non-commercial purposes.** We designed Google Book Search for use by individuals; we request that you use these files for personal, non-commercial research.
+ **Refrain from automated querying.** Do not send automated queries of any kind to Google’s system. If you are conducting research on <term>machine translation</term> <note>The use of software to translate text from one language to another.</note>, <term>optical character recognition</term> <note>The electronic conversion of images of typed or handwritten text into machine-encoded text.</note>, or other areas where access to large amounts of text is helpful, please contact us. We encourage the use of public domain materials for these purposes and may be able to assist you.
+ **Maintain attribution.** The Google "watermark" you see on each file is essential for informing people about this project and helping them find additional materials through Google Book Search. Please do not remove it.
+ **Follow the law.** Regardless of how you use these files, remember that you are responsible for ensuring that your actions are legal. Do not assume that just because we believe a book is in the public domain for users in the United States, the work is also in the public domain in other countries. Whether a book is still protected by copyright varies by country, and we cannot offer guidance on whether any specific use of a book is allowed. Please do not assume that a book’s appearance in Google Book Search means it can be used in any manner anywhere in the world. The legal liability for copyright infringement can be very severe.
