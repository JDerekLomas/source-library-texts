# GIAMBATTISTA
## DELLA PORTA OF NAPLES
### ON REFRACTION,
### A PART OF OPTICS:
### Nine Books.

1 *On refraction <note>The bending of light as it passes from one medium (like air) into another (like water or glass).</note> and its properties.*
2 *On the refraction of a crystal sphere.*
3 *On the anatomy of the parts of the eye and their functions.*
4 *On vision.*
5 *On the properties of vision.*
6 *Why we see a single object with two eyes.*
7 *On those things which happen inside the eye, but are perceived as being outside.*
8 *On Lenses <note>original: "Specillis." In this period, the term referred to magnifying glasses, spectacles, or lenses used to correct or enhance sight.</note>.*
9 *On colors arising from refraction: namely the rainbow, the Milky Way, etc.*

For he himself, when he taught publicly, used to extol my writings (however modest they may have been) with praises to the heavens; he mentioned me quite famously in his own most learned writings.
