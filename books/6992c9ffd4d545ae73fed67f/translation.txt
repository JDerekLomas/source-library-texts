# General Rules

1. The **Section of Pervasive Perfection** <note>original: *Dongzhen bu*</note> consists of the scriptures issued by the Supreme Dharma King, the **Celestial Worthy of Primordial Beginning** <note>the highest deity in the Daoist pantheon, representing the beginning of the universe</note>. These are titled the "Scriptures of Pervasive Perfection" and represent the supreme laws of the **Great Vehicle** <note>original: *Dacheng*; referring to teachings aimed at the salvation of all beings</note>; they constitute the Way of the **Nine Saints**. This section is divided into twelve categories:

    *   **First: Primary Texts** <note>original: *Benwen*</note>. These include the writings of the **Three Origins and Eight Assemblies** <term>a primordial, celestial script from which all human writing is said to have evolved</term> and accounts of the historical origins and causes of the teachings, serving as the foundation of the scriptural doctrine.
    *   **Second: Divine Talismans** <note>original: *Shenfu*</note>. These consist of texts written in the **Dragon Script and Phoenix Seal** styles <term>highly stylized, esoteric calligraphy believed to hold celestial power</term> and characters found in numinous traces and talismanic writings.
    *   **Third: Jade Instructions** <note>original: *Yujue*</note>. These include works such as the **Heshanggong** commentary on the *Dao De Jing* <note>one of the oldest and most influential commentaries on the "Classic of the Way and Its Power"</note> and explanations of the "Golden Scripts."

*   First, the **Section of Pervasive Mystery** <note>original: *Dongxuan bu*</note>. This section was issued by the Physician-King of the Three Realms, the Supreme Lord of the Way <note>original: *Sanjie Yiwang Taishang Daojun*; a title for the Heavenly Worthy of Numinous Treasure, the second of the Three Purities</note>. These works are titled the *Scriptures of Pervasive Mystery* and represent the Medium Vehicle and Medium Method <term>Zhongcheng; a mid-level stage of spiritual practice and understanding</term>. This is known as the Way of the Nine Perfected <note>celestial beings of a high rank</note>. This section also contains twelve categories, which are identical to the categories of the previously mentioned Section of Pervasive Perfection. All teachings transmitted and spread by the Supreme Lord of the Way are attached to their respective categories. Its auxiliary branch is the **Section of Great Peace** <note>original: *Taiping bu*</note>.

*   Next, the **Section of Pervasive Divinity** <note>original: *Dongshen bu*</note>. This section was issued by the Teacher of the Way of the Ten Directions, the Supreme Lord Lao <note>original: *Shifang Daoshi Taishang Laojun*; the deified Laozi</note>. These works are titled the *Scriptures of Pervasive Divinity* and represent the Small Vehicle and Initial Method <term>Xiaocheng; the introductory level of Daoist training</term>. This is known as the Way of the Nine Immortals. This section also contains twelve categories, identical to those in the Section of Pervasive Perfection. All teachings transmitted and spread by the Supreme Lord Lao are attached to their respective categories. Its auxiliary branch is the **Section of Great Clarity** <note>original: *Taiqing bu*</note>.

Beyond these, there is also the **Section of Orthodox Unity** <note>original: *Zhengyi bu*</note>. "Orthodox Unity" is that which pervades and connects all the levels mentioned above.
