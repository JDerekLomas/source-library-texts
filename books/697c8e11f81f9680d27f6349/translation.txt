## FONTANUS HORTULANUS, <note>Latin pseudonym: "The Gardener of the Spring."</note>
## THE DESTINIES OF CHEMISTRY,
### Being a Description
#### Of
## **True and False**
# CHEMISTRY,
->With<-
## **Eight added rare little tracts and, in**
## **part, ancient, precious manuscripts,**
->Consisting of:<-

1. On the correspondence between the <term>macrocosm and the microcosm</term>. <note>The "great and small worlds"—the ancient belief that the human body reflects the structure of the entire universe.</note>
2. On the indication of where humanity derived its <term>signatures</term> <note>The "Doctrine of Signatures" is the belief that the physical appearance of plants or minerals (their "signature") reveals their medicinal or spiritual use.</note>, and through which many arts and sciences were discovered.
3. Presents magical <term>seals</term> <note>Graphic symbols or characters believed to hold spiritual power.</note> and figures, according to Biblical names and the alignment of the stars, with the signatures of the planets, and the proof of why magical images possess such great power and effect.
4. Explains chemical words that occur during laboratory work and the preparation of chemical and other medicines.
5. Teaches how to prepare several choice medicines used by the highly experienced natural philosophers of former times.
6. A treatise on old age: namely, how one can make such a state bearable, etc.
7. An archive for ladies, or secrets pertaining to human adornment and beauty.
8. An instruction for a small home-dyeing workshop.

---

->Cassel,<-
->Published by Johann Bertram Cramer. 1740.<-
