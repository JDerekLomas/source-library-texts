# CORNELIUS GEMMA
## OF LOUVAIN, PROFESSOR
## OF MEDICINE,
### ON THE CYCLOGNOMIC ART,
### THREE VOLUMES.

Presenting the entire doctrine of orders, and simultaneously the philosophy of Hippocrates, Plato, Galen, and Aristotle, in the form of a single, most universal, and circular Method. This method, which works through the triple spheres of the mind fashioned after the likeness of the celestial sphere, not only unfolds the secret mysteries of Medicine but also opens a shortcut for discovering and establishing all other arts and sciences.

# TO THE ARCHITECT-MIND OF ALL THINGS,
## THE HEART DEDICATED THIS GEM
## AS A WEDDING SONG FOR DIVINE LOVE AND PSYCHE
## IN PLACE OF A MAGIC HYMN.

<margin>Love is certainly a great spirit among the Gods: he is the most powerful and also the primary inventor of the <term>Cyclognomic art<gloss>A technical term likely referring to "knowledge of the cycle" or the science of circular/universal movements.</gloss></term>.</margin>
<image-desc>Large decorative initial A with ornate floral and foliate patterns, set within a rectangular frame.</image-desc>
The famous crowd of ancient bards, singing of arms and their leaders, struck the ears, minds, and mouths with a powerful impact: whether they unfolded the golden age of Saturn, or an offspring beautiful with silver and <term>electrum<gloss>A naturally occurring alloy of gold and silver.</gloss></term>, or Mars terrifying the lands with glowing iron. Now a greater God, a greater one drives me, seizing all the spoils, the chariots, and the ranks of the gods along with him; as soon as his fire has breathed upon me, I shall no longer marvel at the strength of the wind or the three-forked flame <note>The thunderbolt of Jupiter.</note>, nor at Bacchus, nor the ears of corn of Ceres. Nor shall I sing of the realms subjected to the cruel trident by Jupiter's command <note>The sea, ruled by Neptune.</note>, nor the riches of <term>Dis<gloss>The god of the underworld, also known as Pluto.</gloss></term> in the depths of <term>Acheron<gloss>One of the rivers of the underworld, here representing the realm of the dead.</gloss></term>:

<margin>The Mind, the architect of things and spouse of Love, was called "Psyche" by the ancients.</margin>
->But you, the great maker of the world, **MIND**, descended from highest Jupiter,<-
->diffused through the countless limbs of the universe:<-
->You, the spouse and immortal love to great **LOVE**,<-

<margin>In the marriage of Love and Psyche, the entire face of nature is contained.</margin>
<margin>The three motions of nature: Analysis, Synthesis, and Conversion.</margin>
You shall explain nature, the lineage, and the threatening weapons of your husband; in what ways he—and from what seed—first hammered out the chaos of the lands and the deep, and the unformed states of the liquid ether into the orb of beautiful light, and granted that these same things be born, and those being born be led in a fixed circuit, and having been dissolved, be brought back.

<margin>The purification of souls before the consideration of divine things.</margin>
<margin>The descent of souls.</margin>
Hide your head in the shadows, far off (alas), far off, inhabitant of dire <term>Tisiphone<gloss>One of the Furies of Greek mythology, who punished crimes of pride and malice.</gloss></term>, you whose blood has grown stiff from the wave of icy sloth and from a coldness congealing more than the Arctic; you to whom helpless envy, pride, and fierce ambition snatch away all light from the eyes at the very threshold of the heart, and [remove] the winged sandals from the feet.

<summary>This page serves as a poetic dedication and opening to a philosophical work, framing the study of nature as the "marriage" between Divine Love and the Architect-Mind (Psyche). It invokes a "greater God" than those of traditional epic poetry and warns the spiritually impure to stay away from these sacred mysteries.</summary>
