# THE HISTORY OF Dæmons, Specters, Witches, Apparitions, &c.

IN this Collection I have no respect to time when these matters were acted, so as to put them into a Chronological Order, though I shall set down the years wherein most of them were done; Neither will I divide the Histories that are Domestick from those which happened in Foreign Nations; But only relate bare matters of Fact as I find them recorded by credible Historians, without much inlarging upon Reflections or Advertisements, having already premised what may be thought worth answering in the preceding Preface, and will therefore proceed with that brevity and perspicuity which it becomes an uninterested Person to use, wherein I shall indifferently set down the Relations of Dæmons, Witches, Spirits, Apparitions, and other remarkable Accidents, without distinguishing them into particular Heads or Chapters.
