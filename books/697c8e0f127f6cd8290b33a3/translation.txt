## To the Kind Reader

What I share here, I can certainly assure you, is that these are purely true *Experiments* <term>Experimente: practical laboratory trials or tests</term> which actually deliver through *practical handiwork* <note>Hand-Arbeit refers to the physical labor performed at the alchemical furnace</note> exactly what they promise. What the title shows you, you will indeed see in this small, yet invaluable little work. The Artists <note>Artisten: a term for practitioners of the alchemical art</note> will wonder that I promise mostly *particular processes* <term>Particularia: alchemical procedures aimed at specific transmutations rather than the "Universal" Philosopher's Stone</term>, apart from the so-called *Universal Source* <note>original: "Fonte Universali"</note>, and that I do not follow the opinions of other *Adepts* <note>Adeptorum: masters who have supposedly achieved the Great Work</note>. I have already sufficiently addressed the grounds and reasons why I am able to do this in my recently published *Theoretical-Practical Theo-Philosophy* <note>Theo-Philosophia Theoretico-Practica</note>, to which I now wish to refer the valued reader. In this present little work, I likewise proceed from common things, according to the foundation...
