## To the Reader.

I have been somewhat at odds with myself as to how I might bring this little treatise <note>original: "Tractätlein"</note> into a proper order, because the subject matter is entirely contrary to the spirit <note>original: "Genio"</note> of these times, which is altogether corrupt and melancholic <term>miltsüchtig: literally "spleen-sick," referring to the humoral theory where the spleen caused gloominess or irritability</term>. I desired to keep this work to myself; but the connection <note>original: "Verwandniß"</note> which it has with my previous writings has compelled me to publish it. This is the final mirror of my thoughts: and because the previous ones were not complete, I have added this one to replace what was still lacking in them.
