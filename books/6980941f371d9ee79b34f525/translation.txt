This sentence is a chronogram, a common 17th-century puzzle where the capitalized letters (I, I, D, I, V, I, V, C, I, V, I, D, V, V, V, V) are Roman numerals that add up to the year of publication, likely 1630 or 1634, during the height of the Thirty Years' War.</note>

<summary>This is the title page of a 17th-century tract that uses the Parable of the Good Samaritan as a metaphor for Germany's devastation during the Thirty Years' War. It introduces a "Heavenly Samaritan" or "Knight of Jacob" as a symbolic savior for the war-torn nation.</summary>
