# The Editor greets the Reader. <note>original: "Editor Lectori S.", an abbreviation for the Latin *Editor Lectori Salutem*, a standard greeting meaning "The editor sends greetings to the reader."</note>

Dear, friendly reader, why I have now undertaken to bring to light the present treatise—which was written more than forty years ago (since the highly enlightened author, **Julius Sperber**, is said to have departed this world <note>original: "diese Welt gesegnet haben soll," a common euphemism for death</note> in the year 1616)—is to be mentioned briefly: namely, to achieve, if possible, a three-fold purpose.

Firstly, because the great **GOD** continues to perform His miracles before the eyes of the world, and indeed increases them in various ways as an even greater testimony; so that the children of men may be given more cause to truly open their eyes, and so that God our Merciful One need not complain so much or so bitterly about the general worldly complacency <term>worldly complacency (Welt-sicherheit): a state of spiritual apathy where people feel falsely secure and ignore divine warnings</term> (wherein everyone busies themselves only with greed, gluttony, drunkenness, gambling, and luxury, but does not heed the works of the Lord nor look upon the deeds of His hands, **Isaiah 5:12**) and then be forced to strike us without further mercy and put an end to us <note>original: "den garauss spielen," a German idiom for finishing someone off or destroying them.</note>.

But does it not happen exactly the same way among us Christians today? Whatever is prophesied for our benefit—serving a good outcome for us—that we believe steadfastly; but whatever is prophesied against us, that must be false and wrong.
