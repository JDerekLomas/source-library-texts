*On how a Mason should conduct himself in the Lodge.*

A Lodge <note>In Freemasonry, a "Lodge" refers to both the local chapter of members and the ritual space where they meet.</note> is an assembly of virtuous men, and consequently, men who are respectable. I shall not dwell upon morals; he who says Mason, says honest man <term>Honest man (honnête homme): In the 18th century, this term referred to a man of integrity, refinement, and social grace, rather than simply someone who does not lie.</term>: and all newly Initiated members must be convinced that this name is generic; that is to say, it encompasses within it the names of faithful subject, good son, good husband, good father, and perfect friend.
