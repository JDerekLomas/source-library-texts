### NEW
# CONVERSATIONS
### ON THE
## SECRET SCIENCES,
### OR THE
## COUNT OF GABALIS:

->*Renewed & expanded*<-
->WITH A LETTER ON THIS SUBJECT.<-

> To merely show what is hidden with such great effort is to destroy it. <note>original Latin: *Quod tanto impendio absconditur, etiam solummodo demonstrare, destruere est.* This quote is from Tertullian, an early Christian author; it suggests that "secret" knowledge loses its power once it is exposed to the light of day.</note>
