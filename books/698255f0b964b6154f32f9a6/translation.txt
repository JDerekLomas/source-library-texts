# HEROIC DEDICATION:
### TO THE
# MOST ILLUSTRIOUS AND POWERFUL
### PRINCE AND LORD OF THE HOLY ROMAN EMPIRE,
# LORD MAURICE,
# LANDGRAVE OF HESSE, 
-> COUNT OF KATZENELNBOGEN, DIEZ, ZIEGEN- <-
-> hain, and Nidda, etc. My most merciful Lord and Patron, <-
-> as well as most munificent Benefactor, etc. <-

<image-desc>Ornate decorative drop cap 'R' featuring floral patterns.</image-desc>

The Roman EMPIRE, to which no other was ever equal,
now weighted down by its own mass, totters on feet of clay, <note>A reference to the prophecy in Daniel 2:33, symbolizing an empire that is large but structurally weak and prone to collapse.</note>
and—painful to behold—hangs by thin fibers of iron.
That three-headed Cerberus, the wretchedly lost Antichrist, <note>A common Reformation-era polemic referring to the Papacy.</note>
stirring up hell <note>original: "Acheronta," a river in the underworld; used here to mean hellish forces.</note> once again, sows new seeds 
of German wrath across the world, and vomits dire poison from his mouth.
Powerful in sedition, he drives fierce tyrants to arms
and bloody pacts, and directs all the force of his wealth
toward the destruction of CHRIST'S flock—those who protect
His commands with sincere faith and life. He desires these souls 
perished by sword, rope, and fire, or forced to approve
the unspeakable idols of the Papacy.
Alas! Pious Religion is seen everywhere hiding her bright face,
while hideous error takes a great increase.
Harmony is removed from our midst; matters are managed by force;
and through fraud, sacred laws are silent amidst the weapons of war. <note>original: "Martia," referring to Mars, the Roman god of war.</note>
Ancient faith, integrity, and simple love grow cold.
The Muses <note>The nine Greek goddesses of inspiration in the arts and sciences.</note> flee at the rattling tumult of war,
just as trembling doves flee when an eagle approaches.
Barbarism threatens the schools, and violation the temples,
while by our dismal crimes we kindle the heat of the Lord’s wrath,
all too forgotten of His blessings and fatherly warnings.

<summary>This dedication to Maurice of Hesse-Kassel presents a dark vision of the Holy Roman Empire's decline, framing the religious conflicts of the time as an apocalyptic struggle between the true faith and the "Antichrist."</summary>

# HEROIC DEDICATION.

What care for pious Princes! What great vigilance!
And how huge a burden lies upon them! How immense the dangers
surround them on all sides in the fatal whirlwind of affairs!
But the Almighty Father, pitying their bitter lot,
places these men—protected by the defense of His salvation-bearing shield—
on a high rock, surrounds them with a wall of bronze,
and just as He guards the center of the eye from a harmful blow,
He eludes hatreds and frauds, and tears apart the snares of the wicked;
having snatched them from danger, He crowns them with majesty:
So that they may protect the laws, the sacred rites, and the good arts,
so that His glory may shine through all His lands.
You indeed shine before all as a noble example
of a beloved Leader, O great **MAURICE**, to the Thunderer <note>original: "Tonanti," a classical epithet for God (historically associated with Jupiter) used here to emphasize His power over the heavens.</note>,
O star of Europe, star of the German senate,
torch of the sacred Empire, jewel of true piety,
mirror of virtue, bright light of a blessed age:
The highest **GOD** anointed you, the chosen one, with sacred oil,
and commanded you to assist the fallen affairs of the Empire,
and like Atlas <note>In Greek mythology, Atlas was the Titan who held up the sky; the poet suggests Maurice is doing the same for the crumbling Holy Roman Empire.</note> to withstand such great ruins.
He spreads life-giving vigor through your limbs,
having granted you a sound mind in a sound body,
nor does He permit you to be submerged in the vast whirlpool of cares:
But He draws you up high, far from all dangers,
and breathes favorable success upon all your deeds,
He makes you fruitful with pious offspring, with kingdoms, and with wealth,
and compensates for most sorrowful deaths with new joy:
After the example of the King, the son of Jesse <note>A reference to the biblical King David, who was also a musician, poet, and leader who suffered many trials before his triumph.</note>, who suffered much,
**GOD** exalts you and places you in a joyful seat,
so that Virtue, having endured through hardships, might shine more brightly.
You indeed, *Prince*, live widely by the gift of the eternal King;
you yourself worship His divinity with a devoted heart,
and you take care that the citizens honor the same in beautiful order,
by **COUNSEL** and **VIRTUE** you restore the sacred state.
Under your leadership, Religion, free from all the leaven <note>The author uses "leaven" (*fermento*) in the biblical sense of a corrupting or transformative influence—here referring to Catholic doctrine.</note>
of the Popes, is now restored whole to the sacred pulpits
