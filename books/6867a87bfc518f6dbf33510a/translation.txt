### The first chapter of this Astrological Completion. <note>original Latin: "Complementi"</note>

#### Why the Prognosticon was thus written and compiled for four or five years together.

It might cause someone to wonder for what reasons a <term>Prognosticon</term> <note>A formal book of astrological predictions.</note> for five years—namely from 1620 to 1624—was prepared, composed, and thus written together by me, contrary to the common manner and custom. But let this be the answer: that such a thing did not happen without any reason, but rather out of special consideration. For I have first shown and proven in the first part of this Prognosticon how a prediction's significance in a single year—whether taken from the <term>Revolution</term> <note>The "Solar Revolution" or the return of the sun to its position at a specific moment, used to cast a chart for the year.</note>, great conjunctions, and eclipses, or other phenomena—cannot be fully demonstrated within that year. Instead, for the most part, the meaning only comes to light in subsequent years, often not until the third year, as experience has so often shown.

## The Fourth Chapter.
