<meta>This is the title page of the 1569 edition published in Venice. It introduces a commentary on the Kabbalistic work of the famous Renaissance philosopher Giovanni Pico della Mirandola.</meta>

# THE MORE SELECT
## AND MORE OBSCURE DOCTRINES
## OF THE KABBALISTS,
### LONG AGO EXCERPTED BY GIOVANNI PICO <note>Giovanni Pico della Mirandola (1463–1494) was a celebrated Renaissance nobleman and philosopher who sought to reconcile various schools of thought, including Christianity and Jewish mysticism.</note> FROM THEIR COMMENTARIES,
### AND NOW BY ARCHANGELO OF BORGONOVO
-> Of the Order of Friars Minor, for the first time illustrated with <-
-> most lucid interpretations. <-

-> *In which you will find no few things which wonderfully contribute to that* <-
-> *most beautiful and difficult method of philosophizing through* <-
-> *numbers and Mathematics.* <-

-> With a very full Index of all the more notable matters. <-

### WITH PRIVILEGE. <note>original: *Cum Privilegio*. A legal protection for the printer, similar to a modern copyright, usually granted by the Venetian Senate.</note>
