## MYTHOLOGICAL

### **History of the Greeks, Phoenicians, and Egyptians is asserted to pertain to Chemistry <note>original: "Chemiam". In 1687, this term referred to "Chymistry," a discipline that encompassed both practical laboratory work and what we now call alchemy—the search for the philosopher's stone and the transmutation of metals.</note>.**
