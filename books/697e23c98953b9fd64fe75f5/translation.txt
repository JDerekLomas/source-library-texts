# TO THE READER
## GREETINGS TO THE WELL-WISHING READER <note>original: "BENEVOLO S.," an abbreviation for *Benevolo Salutem*, a standard Latin greeting wishing the reader health and prosperity.</note>

Here for you is the *History of the Templars* <note>original: "Historiam Templariorum"</note>, perhaps recalled to memory not without purpose. It is composed more often from the words of others than my own, a method chosen neither without precedent nor without reason. For you may see the thoughts, spirit, faith, rituals, and customs of the <term>Ancients<note>In this context, the author refers to the original medieval chroniclers and witnesses of the Crusades.</note></term> more reliably in their own words than you might learn from later interpreters. I have expanded the work with ecclesiastical observations concerning the *vows of monks, the dignity of <term>patriarchs<note>High-ranking bishops in the early and Eastern church; the Patriarch of Jerusalem was a key figure in the Templars' history.</note></term>, the clothing of religious persons, the generosity shown toward <term>holy orders<note>Groups such as the Templars or Hospitallers who took religious vows while performing specific duties, like military service.</note></term>, and so forth, so that...*
