How is the arrangement performed? To derive a thing from an utterance, and an utterance from a thing; the arrangement through permutation, and the permutation through arrangement; the generalization through calculation, and the calculation through generalization. This continues until all their words are established like a fountain of flame, and the flame like a fountain, until it is beyond searching and beyond number, toward the light that hides itself within the additions of the secret darkness, bringing everything into a completed work through thirteen types of transformations.

These transformations are exchanges, and the exchanges are transformations; they are all joined back-to-back, spreading across the movements, and the movements into additions, and the additions into subtractions, and the subtractions into externalities, and the externalities into redundancies, and the redundancies into advantages, and the advantages upon the back of the *Aleph* <note>The first letter of the Hebrew alphabet, representing unity</note>.

And now, we must search for those things which are both revealed and hidden, such as the name *Adonai*. The letter *Aleph* divides into 400 heads. How so? And another explanation: Light is an act of "making," as it is written: *And God said, "Let there be light"* <note>Genesis 1:3</note>, and there is no "being" except through making and formation. Darkness did not involve "making"—that is, "making" is not written regarding it, but only "separation" and "division"; therefore, it is called "creation," as one might say "so-and-so has recovered" <note>The Hebrew root B-R-A can mean both 'create' and 'to be healthy/fattened'</note>.
