### The Results of the Moon

**House 1** When [the Moon] is in the **Ascendant** <note>original: "lagnamukhe," referring to the first house</note>, the woman is devoted to her friends and immersed in profound devotion. She is beloved by the wise and refined <note>original: "shishta-vallabha"</note>. Always "lotus-eyed" <term>Varijakshi: A poetic epithet for eyes that are wide and beautiful like the petals of a lotus flower.</term>, she possesses a face full of joy, beautiful eyes, and a lovely form. **१** 

**House 3** In the **third house** <term>Sahaja Bhava: The house of siblings, courage, and communication.</term>, she is "fawn-eyed" <note>original: "saranga-netra"</note> and becomes a giver of **prosperity and success** <term>Riddhi-Siddhi: The personification of wealth (Riddhi) and spiritual/intellectual attainment (Siddhi).</term>. She is joined by her many kinsmen and endowed with grace and playfulness. She finds enjoyment in her husband’s company, and her limbs are adorned with the beauty of gold and fine ornaments. **३**

**House 4** In the **fourth house** <term>Bandhu Bhava: The house of home, mother, and emotional foundations.</term>, the Moon produces a woman of great pleasure and happiness. She experiences the joy of abundance and gains many profits. She always finds "happiness in the home" <note>original: "veshma-saukhyam"</note>. Blessed with sons, she sees the growth and prosperity of her children; indeed, she finds great comfort in her offspring. 

**House 5** In the **fifth house** <term>Putra Bhava: The house of intelligence, creativity, and children.</term>, she is endowed with a **good intellect** and is well-refined <note>original: "samskara-yukta"</note>. However, she may suffer from gold-related troubles or "afflictions of the head" <note>original: "shirogapti-vadha," potentially referring to migraines or chronic headaches</note>. **५** Within her family, she may experience distress and the pain of "fire" <note>original: "vahnimartti," which can signify either physical burns, a sharp fever, or a volatile temperament</note>. She is often beset by a sharp digestive fire or a harsh nature, and is frequently troubled by disease.

### The First House: The Ascendant
When the "Noble King" <term>Bhadraraja: an epithet often used for Mercury in this context, signifying intelligence and noble character</term> is situated in the **Ascendant** <term>Vilagna: the 1st house, representing the self, body, and appearance</term> for a woman of the Padmini type, she becomes a seeker of spiritual truths and an enjoyer of happiness. She is beautiful and virtuous. She possesses a certain "stoutness" or healthy fullness of the body <note>original: "sthulata"</note>. ### The Influence of Saturn (Shani) in the Various Houses

When Saturn resides in the **first house** <term>Lagna: the ascendant or house of self</term>, he acts as a minister of transformation, yet he limits the intellect. He causes the loss of good reputation and makes the individual prone to jealousy, though they may possess a "divine voice" <note>original: "devavāṇī," suggesting a solemn or prophetic way of speaking</note>. **1**

In the **second house** <term>Dhana-bhava: the house of wealth and speech</term>, the presence of Saturn indicates a lack of harmony. The individual may suffer from diseases of the rectum <note>original: "gudāmayī"</note> and ailments affecting the ears. There is a persistent sense of being troubled by the elements of water and fire. **2**

In the **third house**, when the "Harsh-Rayed One" <term>Saturn is often described by his cold or piercing influence</term> is positioned there, there is a lack of stability and a loss of melodic happiness. One experiences fear and tends to wander aimlessly. A peculiar physical mark, such as a sign resembling a dog <term>Sarameya: descendant of Sarama, a dog</term>, may appear on the feet. **3**

When Saturn is placed in the **fourth house** <term>Chathurtha: the house of home, mother, and emotions</term>, he brings about a loss of honor and reputation. The body becomes afflicted by severe diseases arising from the imbalance of the wind and bile humors <term>Vata and Pitta: the biological forces of air/motion and fire/metabolism in Ayurveda</term>. Specifically, he may cause ailments of the throat and a general state of ignorance or mental heaviness. **4**

In the **fifth house** <term>Panchama: the house of children, intelligence, and legacy</term>, Saturn causes the loss of sons, friends, and wealth.
