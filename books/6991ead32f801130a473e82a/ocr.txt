<note>This page contains draft sentences likely intended for Leonardo's "Prophecies" or "Riddles" (Profetie). The text, written in mirror script, discusses sleep, dreams, and lack of consciousness. He uses various symbols (D, +, q) to categorize or link his thoughts. Abbreviations like the horizontal bar for nasal consonants (e.g., 'nō' for 'non', 'āimo' for 'animo') are frequent. 'Sūno' and 'sōno' are used interchangeably for 'sonno' (sleep) and 'sogno' (dream).</note>
